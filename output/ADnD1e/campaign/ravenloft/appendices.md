## Count Strahd von Zarovich

*(PDF p.3)*

> Strahd's full stat block, vampire abilities, spells, and DM guidance are detailed in **see region.md § Count Strahd Von Zarovich**. Below is a consolidated combat-reference summary.

### Count Strahd Von Zarovich (Vampire, Magic-User 10)

- **AC:** -1 [20]
- **HP:** 55 (HD 10)
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** 5-10 (1d6+4)
- **THAC0:** 10 [+9]
- **MV:** 120' (40') / Fly 180' (60')
- **SV:** D8 W9 P9 B10 S10 (F10/MU10, best of both)
- **ML:** — (special: genius-level tactics)
- **AL:** CE
- **S** 18/76
- **SA:** Energy Drain (2 levels per hit), charm person (gaze, save vs. spells at -2)
- **SD:** +1 or better weapon to hit, regenerate 3 hp/round
- **MR:** Standard vampire immunities
- **Intelligence:** Genius
- **Size:** M

### Magic-User Spells (10th Level)

| Level | Slots | Spells |
|------:|------:|--------|
| 1st | 4 | *comprehend languages, hold portal, protection from good, sleep* |
| 2nd | 3 | *invisibility, locate objects, mirror image, ESP* |
| 3rd | 2 | *fireball, gust of wind, suggestion* |
| 4th | 2 | *polymorph other, polymorph self* |
| 5th | 2 | *animate dead, distance distortion* |

### Vampire Abilities

- **Energy Drain:** Each successful hit drains 2 life levels from the target.
- **Regenerate:** 3 hit points per round.
- **Gaseous Form:** Can assume gaseous form at will.
- **Shape Change:** Can shape change into a large bat at will.
- **Charm Person:** By gazing into a character's eyes; target saves vs. spells at -2.
- **Weapon Immunity:** Can only be hit by magical weapons (+1 or better).
- **Summoning:** Can summon wolves and bats.
- **Cannot Enter Uninvited:** Cannot enter a building unless invited in. May knock at doors and attempt to charm characters into inviting him.

### Strahd's Vulnerabilities

- **Sunlight:** Destroyed by exposure to direct sunlight for 10 rounds.
- **The Sunsword:** +3 to hit, +10 additional damage per hit against vampires (see [Sunsword](#sunsword) below).
- **Holy Symbol of Ravenkind:** Flares with sunlight for 1-10 rounds; Strahd cannot move or attack while it flares (see [Holy Symbol of Ravenkind](#holy-symbol-of-ravenkind) below).
- **Stake through heart:** Does not kill, but holds him from movement.
- **Standard vampire weaknesses** per *Monster Manual*.

### Combat Tactics (DM Guidance)

The entire adventure centers around the vampire. Always keep in mind the motives of the vampire, how he moves about, and what his cunning plot is. Play Strahd in the same way the players play their characters.

1. **Strahd chooses when he attacks.** He is a genius — play him as one. His attacks must be timed to be most advantageous to him. He moves around during the adventure but can always be found in the place determined by Fortunes of Ravenloft (see region.md).

2. **Strahd knows when to withdraw.** If losing a battle, he becomes gaseous, *polymorphs* into a wolf or bat, and/or summons creatures to guard his retreat.

3. **Strahd's attacks depend upon his goals.** The reason Strahd lured the PCs into his kingdom is determined by Fortunes of Ravenloft. His plans for achieving his goal are listed with his goals (see region.md).

### Spy Network & Attack Timing

- Spies report **four times daily** (dawn, noon, dusk, midnight).
- **60% chance** Strahd knows the PCs' location at each report time.
- If he knows their location, he **attacks within two hours**.

### Strahd's One-Time Attack Options

Each of the following can be used **once** during the adventure:

1. **Personal Attack:** Strahd attacks a single PC for 5 melee rounds, then leaves.
2. **Zombie Assault:** Strahd calls 10-15 Strahd zombies. All must attack at the same time in the same place.
3. **Wolf Pack:** Strahd calls 3-12 worg wolves. All must attack at the same time in the same place.

## Unique Monsters

*(PDF p.31–32)*

### Barovian Villagers

**Barovian Villager** ×3-10: AC 10 [9], HD 2 (variable hp), Att 1 × weapon (1d6), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML —, AL NG; XP 20. SA nil, SD nil, MR normal.

The Barovians and their ancestors have lived in this valley for as long as anyone can remember. Their lives are deeply imbedded in their homes and their traditions. They are, by nature, very wary of strange peoples and customs, almost to the point of paranoia.

**Behavior:**
- **Stare openly** at strangers in silence, venting disapproval of everything unfamiliar.
- **Not talkative** with strangers, to the point of being pointedly rude.
- **Violent tempers** that boil through their customary silence when provoked.
- **Social cohesiveness:** Can rise up together against outsiders if one Barovian is mistreated.
- **Loyal friends:** If one wins a Barovian's trust, one has a friend for life and an ally that will never fail.

**Barovians know the following:**

1. When they attempt to leave the land of Barovia, they begin to choke. Those who do not turn back perish.
2. The Master of Ravenloft is "The Devil Strahd." Barovians believe that Strahd is a curse placed over the land because of some forgotten sin of their ancestors.
3. Vampires can be destroyed, but no one has ever succeeded. Barovians know all the tricks for destroying vampires outlined in the *Monster Manual*.

### Gypsies (Vistani)

**Gypsy** ×3-30: AC 6 [13], HD 5 (variable hp), Att 1 × weapon (1d8), THAC0 14 [+5], MV 120' (40'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML —, AL NE; XP 175. SA nil, SD nil, MR normal. Intelligence: High. % In Lair: 10%. Treasure: Individual M, A in lair.

The gypsies speak in long flowing riddles of often useless information. They tell tales of their forefathers that are almost certainly untrue. Their riddles and stories are a front, for the gypsies are a most serious people. They are quick to act when their lives or traditions are threatened. They are merciless when they feel they must be.

**Key behavior:**
- Gypsies readily tell adventurers they have a potion that protects them from the vampire. **This is a lie** — they attempt to sell their fake potion for as much money as they can get.
- Always try to appear helpful. In truth, the only information they ever give away is **misleading at best and often a lie**.
- The gypsies are **in the service of Strahd von Zarovich** and fear the consequences of disobedience.
- Only the gypsies can pass through Barovia at will.
- Their leader, **Madam Eva**, foreshadows the events that befall adventurers (see region.md § Fortunes of Ravenloft).

### Strahd Zombies

**Strahd Zombie** ×1-10: AC 8 [11], HD 4 (variable hp), Att special (1d8), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F4), ML —, AL N; XP 190. SA severed members also attack, SD turn as mummies, MR normal. Intelligence: Non. % In Lair: 80%.

Strahd zombies are a form of undead that serve Strahd von Zarovich. They were called into being through a dark magic, now forgotten even by Strahd himself. Strahd zombies were created from the long-dead guards of Castle Ravenloft.

**Special abilities:**
- **Fragile appearance:** Gray-green flesh looks soft and bones seem brittle. Any single hit of **5 points or more severs parts** of their bodies.
- **Severed limbs continue to attack.** Despite their fragile appearance, the life forces of Strahd zombies are very great.
- **Distributed life force:** Each Strahd zombie's life force lies in its total being. Damage to any part damages the whole creature. Damage to any one part in excess of a Strahd zombie's total hit points destroys all of that zombie's parts.
- **Turned as mummies.**

### Witches of Ravenloft

*(see castle-spires.md § K56)*

**Witch** ×7 (human): AC 8 [11], HD 2 (hp 13, 12, 8, 8, 7, 6, 2), Att 1 × dagger (1d4), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML —, AL CE; XP 29 each. SA spells (2 per witch).

The witches attack immediately when encountered. They are incanting in room K56. Each witch can cast **two spells**:

| Witch | Spells |

At night, the witches come through the secret trap door in K55 (after attacking with a *sleep* spell) and take a member of the party — an NPC if possible — back to their lair at K56. Their cat familiars in K54 alert them if PCs are detected nearby.

## Magic Items

*(PDF p.30–31)*

### Holy Symbol of Ravenkind

- **Type:** Wondrous item (holy symbol)
- **Appearance:** An ancient platinum medallion shaped like the sun, with a large crystal embedded in its center. Around the crystal are many holy symbols of light and truth.
- **History:** Once worn by the High Priest of Ravenloft. A powerful lawful good symbol.
- **Powers:**
  - When presented forcefully toward any undead creature, the medallion adds **+2 to the cleric's "turn undead" roll**.
  - When presented against **vampires**, it **flares with the light of the sun for 1-10 rounds**. Vampires are destroyed by exposure to direct sunlight for 10 rounds. **Vampires cannot move or attack** while the medallion flares. Other creatures are not affected.
- **Restriction:** Can only be used **once per week**.
- **Location:** Determined by Fortunes of Ravenloft (1st card; see region.md).

### Sunsword

- **Type:** Weapon (longsword, +2/+3)
- **Appearance:** A magical longsword. Its blade is a form of crystal glassteel, held by a platinum hilt and guard. It glows with dim blue light most of the time. When within 30 feet of any undead, the blade suddenly glows brilliant blue.
- **Powers:**
  - **+2 sword** against all creatures except undead.
  - **+3 vs. undead** (to hit and damage). When within 30 feet of any undead, the blade suddenly glows brilliant blue.
  - **+10 additional damage per hit** against vampires. The blade flares and sparks when it hits a vampire.
- **Special — Separated Blade and Hilt:** Ages ago, Strahd employed a powerful magician to destroy the sword. The first part of the process required that the hilt and blade be separated, then destroyed simultaneously. However, before the hilt and blade were destroyed, the magician's assistant stole the blade and attempted to flee from Barovia. Though his body was later found in the woods, the blade was not with him. When it was separated from the hilt, the blade took on the appearance of a normal longsword.
- **Reuniting the Sunsword:** The hilt remained in Castle Ravenloft; the blade was never found. Strahd has always lived in fear that the blade may be reunited with the hilt. It so happens that **one of the PCs** (a random fighter) has been unknowingly carrying the blade with a normal hilt (since without the hilt it cannot be discerned from a normal blade). If the PCs find the hilt, it will **magically leap onto the blade**, replacing the PC's hilt, becoming the *sunsword*.
- **Immunity:** Cannot be rusted by rust monsters.
- **Location (hilt):** Determined by Fortunes of Ravenloft (4th card; see region.md).

### Icon of Ravenloft

- **Type:** Wondrous item (holy artifact)
- **Appearance:** A small carving of the purest silver. 12 inches tall and 6 inches across.
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good (tremendous power)
- **Powers:**
  - Adds **+4 bonus to turn any undead**.
  - **Heals 6-21 (3d6+3) hit points** once per day when used by a cleric of good alignment.
- **Location:** Found on the altar in the **Chapel of Ravenloft (K15)** (see castle-courtyard.md § K15). A figure draped over the altar is all that remains of a mighty evil cleric who attempted to obtain the Icon. Check Fortunes of Ravenloft results for a possible additional encounter and other treasure in this room.

### Witches' Spellbook

- **Type:** Spellbook (evil)
- **Location:** Room K56, beside the kettle. Cannot be found until after **all the witches are destroyed** (see castle-spires.md § K56).
- **Protection:** The book is evil and does **3-30 (3d10) points of damage** to anyone of non-evil alignment who even touches it. The book can be handled by evil characters.
- **Contents:** All of the spells the witches know (see [Witches of Ravenloft](#witches-of-ravenloft) above) plus:
  - *fear*
  - *curse*
  - *cloudkill*
- **Value:** 42,750 gp.

## Tome of Strahd

*(PDF p.31)*

The Tome of Strahd is an ancient work penned by Strahd himself. It is a tragic tale of how Strahd came to his fallen state. The book is bound in a thick black leather cover with brass hinges and fastenings. The pages are of parchment and very brittle. Most of the book is written in the curious shorthand that only Strahd himself employs. Stains and age have made most of the work illegible. There is a **base 30% chance to read any paragraph** in the book. Read the paragraphs in order from the text below.

**Location:** Determined by Fortunes of Ravenloft (2nd card; see region.md).

### Full Text

> I am The Ancient, I am The Land. My beginnings are lost in the darkness of the past. I was the warrior, I was good and just. I thundered across the land like the wrath of a just god, but the war years and the killing years wore down my soul as the wind wears stone into sand.

> All goodness slipped from my life; I found my youth and strength gone and all I had left was death. My army settled in the valley of Barovia and took power over the people in the name of a just god, but with none of a god's grace or justice.

> I called for my family, long unseated from their ancient thrones, and brought them here to settle in the castle Ravenloft. They came with a younger brother of mine, Sergei. He was handsome and youthful. I hated him for both.

> From the families of the valley, one spirit shone above all others. A rare beauty, who was called "perfection," "joy," and "treasure." Her name was Tatyana and I longed for her to be mine.

> I loved her with all my heart. I loved her for her youth. I loved her for her joy. But she spurned me! "Old One" was my name to her — "elder" and "brother" also. Her heart went to Sergei. They were betrothed. The date was set.

> With words she called me "brother," but when I looked into her eyes they reflected another name — "death." It was the death of the aged that she saw in me. She loved her youth and enjoyed it. But I had squandered mine.

> The death she saw in me turned her from me. And so I came to hate death, my death. My hate is very strong; I would not be called "death" so soon.

> I made a pact with death, a pact of blood. On the day of the wedding, I killed Sergei, my brother. My pact was sealed with his blood.

> I found Tatyana weeping in the garden east of the Chapel. She fled from me. She would not let me explain, and a great anger swelled within me. She had to understand the pact I made for her. I pursued her. Finally, in despair, she flung herself from the walls of Ravenloft and I watched everything I ever wanted fall from my grasp forever.

> It was a thousand feet through the mists. No trace of her was ever found. Not even I know her final fate.

> Arrows from the castle guards pierced me to my soul, but I did not die. Nor did I live. I became undead, forever.

> I have studied much since then. "Vampyr" is my new name. I still lust for life and youth, and I curse the living that took them from me. Even the sun is against me. It is the sun and light I fear the most. But little else can harm me now. Even a stake through my heart does not kill me, though it holds me from movement. But the sword, that cursed sword that Sergei brought! I must dispose of that awful tool! I fear and hate it as much as the sun.

> I have often hunted for Tatyana. I have even felt her within my grasp, but she escapes. She taunts me! She taunts me! What will it take to bend her love to me?

> I now reside far below Ravenloft. I live among the dead and sleep beneath the very stones of this hollow castle of despair. I shall seal shut the walls of the stairs that none may disturb me.

## Optional Ending

*(PDF p.30)*

After Strahd is destroyed forever, the DM may provide this ending to wrap things up. The scenario is set at **dawn on the overlook (K6)**. **Ireena Kolyana must be with the PCs.**

### Read Aloud

> Thick clouds are overhead. Through the chilly morning mists, the lands of Barovia are visible far below. There is a peacefulness here. Rest has come to the valley for the first time anyone can remember. There is sleep without fear.
>
> A light flashes brilliantly behind you. Wheeling around, you see a shining stately man in white armor and cape. His rugged features show great strength of will, yet the forcefulness of his presence is tempered by his calm, sad eyes. His features are those of Strahd, yet subtly different.
>
> His voice is calm and peaceful. "My name is Sergei von Zarovich." He turns to Ireena, "Tatyana, the time is at hand to rest. Come, my love and wife." He stretches forth his hand.
>
> Ireena Kolyana's questioning eyes suddenly open with recognition and knowledge. Forgotten memories rush upon her. "Sergei!" she cries, springing to him with the grace of a doe. They embrace.
>
> Ireena turns to you. "I am Ireena Kolyana, but in my past I was Sergei's beloved Tatyana. Through these many centuries we have played out the tragedy of our lives. Now, with our deepest gratitude to you, that tragedy is over. It is time for joy to begin again."
>
> Shimmering light surrounds Ireena and Sergei. Hand in hand, they walk east toward the edge of the overlook. Their feet do not touch the ground, they tread a path beyond this mortal world. Their invisible road takes them beyond the eastern precipice, their glow illuminating and thinning the clouds above Barovia. The clouds suddenly break open, letting glorious shafts of sunlight flood through. In the valley below, the strange fog dissolves, loosing its power. Barovia is free once more.
