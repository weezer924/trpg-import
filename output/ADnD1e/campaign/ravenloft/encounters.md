### Nightly Raids (Outdoor Camps)

*(PDF p.6)* — Each night spent outside Castle Ravenloft, Strahd sends his forces. They withdraw after 5 rounds.

- **Worg Wolf** x4-16 (4d4): AC 6 [13], HD 4+4, Att 1 x bite (2-8), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 180' (60'), SV F4.
- **Bat** x10-100 (10d10): AC 6 [13], HD 1d4hp, Att 0, THAC0 19 [+0], MV 40' (13') / Fly 180' (60'), SV NH. Interfere with spell casting (Dex check for material components).
- Wolves never attack Ireena Kolyana. Strahd cannot enter buildings uninvited.

## Encounter Difficulty Notes

### Lethal Encounters (Potential TPK)

1. **Iron Golems (K78)** — 80 HP each, AC 3, 4d10 damage, require +3 weapons. Most parties at level 5-7 will lack +3 weapons entirely. The hourglass puzzle is the intended solution: retrieve stones without triggering the 5-round combat cycle. Avoid a straight fight.

2. **Count Strahd von Zarovich** — AC -1, 55 HP, energy drain 2 levels per hit, regeneration 3 hp/round, 10th-level MU spells (*fireball*, *sleep*), charm at -2. A direct fight without the Sunsword and Holy Symbol is near-suicidal. The Sunsword (+10 damage vs. vampires) and Holy Symbol (stuns him 1-10 rounds) are essential.

3. **Ghost, Crypt 4** — Aging 10-40 years on sight (save vs. spells to avoid panic). *Magic jar* while ethereal. Anyone killed by the ghost is **dead forever** (no raise dead). Approach only with strong saves and a plan.

4. **Banshee, Crypt 21 (Patrina)** — Keening in total darkness = save vs. spells or instant death. 47 HP, drain 2 levels. If exploring catacombs, keep light sources ready at all times.

5. **Transpositional Teleport Traps (Crypts 37/38)** — PCs approaching Strahd's crypt on foot are swapped with wights and deposited alone in coffins in Crypt 14 with 15 wights. The intended route to K86 is through Crypt 32's east alcove teleport.

### Very Dangerous Encounters

6. **Shadow Demon (K72)** — AC 1, half damage, +2 to hit in darkness. Torchlight drops it to AC 5 and normal damage. A *light* spell deals 1d6 per caster level. Parties that rush in without light will suffer.

7. **Helga the Vampire (K32)** — Poses as an innocent maid. Energy drain 2 levels when she strikes. The deception is the true danger; she waits to isolate a lone PC.

8. **Red Dragon Statues (K7)** — 3-30 bite damage. Only trigger when PCs try to **leave** the castle through the east doors. Parties must find another exit or deal with 4 simultaneous attacks.

9. **Gargoyles (K8)** — 8 gargoyles requiring +1 weapons, attacking in darkness (they extinguish torches). Only on return visit, so first entry is safe.

10. **Trapper (Crypt 31)** — HD 12, 62 HP, disguised as floor. Smothers victims. High damage output. Easy to walk into unprepared.

### Moderate Encounters

11. **Witches (K56)** — 7 witches with 14 spells including *magic missile*, *charm person*, *burning hands*. Individually weak (HD 2), but dangerous in numbers. At night they raid K50 to kidnap sleeping PCs.

12. **Strahd Zombies (K76)** — 6 zombies rising from water. Dismembered limbs continue attacking. Turn as mummies (harder than normal zombies).

13. **Guardian of Sorrow (K20)** — 6 halberds attacking per round, Dex checks to avoid falling. The heart is 240 feet up. Low HP (10) but very hard to reach.

### Suggested Encounter Order for DMs

- **First session:** Barovia village (NPCs, information gathering) → Tser Pool (Fortunes reading) → approach castle
- **Early castle:** K7-K10 (first visit safe), K15 chapel (Icon of Ravenloft), K20 tower
- **Mid castle:** K30 (accountant intel), K35-K41 (treasury route), K56 (witches)
- **Late castle:** K61-K72 (larders, shadow demon), K73-K78 (dungeon, iron golems)
- **Endgame:** K84 catacombs (navigate teleport traps, find Crypt 32), K86 (Strahd's coffin), final confrontation at Fortunes location
