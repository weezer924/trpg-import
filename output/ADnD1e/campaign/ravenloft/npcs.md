## Barovia NPCs

### E1. Bildrath's Mercantile

#### Bildrath (Barovian Merchant, Fighter 4)

- **AC:** 8 [11]
- **HP:** 30
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** 1-8 (sword)
- **THAC0:** 16 [+3]
- **MV:** 120' (40')
- **SV:** D13 W15 P14 B16 S16 (F4)
- **AL:** LN
- **S** 15 **I** 10 **W** 12 **D** 14 **C** 11 **Ch** 13
- **Notes:** Trades with the gypsies. Sells anything on the AD&D shopping list except religious items, livestock, and transport — at **10x normal price**. Never bargains.

**Role:** The only merchant in Barovia. Serves himself and promises no sanctuary. If given trouble, calls Parriwimple.

**Key Information:** None useful. He knows nothing beyond basic Barovian common knowledge (see appendices.md § Barovian Villagers).

#### Parriwimple (Barovian Stock-Boy, Fighter 9)

- **AC:** 2 [17]
- **HP:** 50
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** 5-12 (1d8+4)
- **THAC0:** 11 [+8]
- **MV:** 120' (40')
- **SV:** D8 W10 P9 B10 S12 (F9)
- **AL:** LN
- **S** 18/80 **I** 3 **W** 9 **D** 17 **C** 14 **Ch** 8
- **Notes:** Bildrath's nephew. Extremely simple-minded but enormously strong. Dedicated to his uncle and will not follow the PCs. Knows that crossing the master of the castle is death.

**Role:** Bodyguard for Bildrath. Responds to violence against his uncle with overwhelming force. His visible musculature should warn PCs not to start trouble.

### E2. Blood of the Vine Tavern

#### Arik (Barovian Barkeep, Normal Human)

- **AC:** 8 [11]
- **HP:** 3
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** 1-4
- **THAC0:** 19 [+0]
- **MV:** 120' (40')
- **SV:** D16 W18 P17 B20 S19 (NH)
- **AL:** CN
- **S** 9 **I** 4 **W** 7 **D** 13 **C** 8 **Ch** 10
- **Notes:** Mindlessly cleans glasses, one after the other. When they are all clean, he starts over. Takes orders for drinks in a dull, hollow voice. After serving drinks, returns to cleaning glasses. Ignores all questions.

**Role:** Atmospheric NPC. Provides no information. Embodies the despair of Barovia.

#### 3 Tavern Gypsies (Tavern Owners, Fighter 4)

**Gypsy** x3: AC 4 [15], HD 4 (32hp, 30hp, 24hp), Att 1 x weapon (2-7, 1d6+1), THAC0 16 [+3], MV 120' (40'), SV D13 W15 P14 B16 S16 (F4), ML --, AL LN.

**Role:** Own the tavern. Ensure customers pay their bills. Otherwise disinterested in PCs.

#### Ismark the Lesser (Barovian, Fighter 2)

- **AC:** 7 [12]
- **HP:** 18
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** 1-6 (longsword)
- **THAC0:** 18 [+1]
- **MV:** 120' (40')
- **SV:** D14 W16 P15 B17 S17 (F2)
- **AL:** LG
- **S** 15 **I** 12 **W** 13 **D** 10 **C** 16 **Ch** 13
- **Notes:** Son of the Burgomaster. Speaks fair common speech. Greets the PCs and takes them to the Burgomaster's home to aid his adopted sister, Ireena Kolyana.

**Role:** Primary quest-giver in the village. Guides PCs to Ireena at E4.

**Key Information:**
- Tight-lipped unless the subject deals with Ireena or Strahd.
- Knows everything the other villagers know.
- For some unknown reason, Strahd is attracted to Ireena more than anything else.
- His father, the Burgomaster, has been dead for over **10 days**.
- The great holy symbol that provided their strongest protection from the vampire was stolen.
- Ireena is unprotected and helpless in the house.
- If shown the forged letter, Ismark confirms the Burgomaster did attempt to send a letter of warning. The PCs' letter, however, was **certainly not written by the Burgomaster.** He recognizes the handwriting is not the Burgomaster's.

### E4. Burgomaster's Home

#### Ireena Kolyana (Barovian, Fighter 4)

- **AC:** 10 [9]
- **HP:** 6
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** 1-4 (dagger)
- **THAC0:** 16 [+3]
- **MV:** 120' (40')
- **SV:** D13 W15 P14 B16 S16 (F4)
- **AL:** LG
- **S** 13 **I** 10 **W** 14 **D** 17 **Ch** 18
- **Notes:** Burgomaster's adopted daughter. Bitten twice by Strahd. Does not remember her past.

**Role:** The central figure of Strahd's obsession. She is the reincarnation of Tatyana, the woman Strahd loved in life. She will accompany the PCs and aid them as best she can. Not a hapless victim -- she has a strong will and a good arm.

**Key Information:**
- She has been bitten twice by the vampire and can no longer be protected in the house.
- The townspeople are afraid of her and avoid her.
- She does not remember her early past, does not know how she came to Barovia or where she came from.
- **DM info:** Kolyan Indirovich found her as a girl at the edge of the Svalich Woods near the Pillarstone of Ravenloft. She had no memory of her past.

### E6. The Church

#### Donavich (Barovian Priest, Cleric 2)

- **AC:** 10 [9]
- **HP:** 10
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** 1-4
- **THAC0:** 18 [+1]
- **MV:** 100' (33')
- **SV:** D10 W14 P13 B16 S15 (C2)
- **AL:** LG
- **S** 9 **I** 13 **W** 16 **D** 14 **C** 14 **Ch** 15
- **Spells (1st Level):** *protection from evil, sanctuary, detect evil, remove fear*
- **Notes:** Has been praying and chanting throughout the night. His voice is hoarse and weak. Spent this night, as every night, warding off evil from his church.

**Role:** The only active force of resistance against Strahd in Barovia. His nightly prayers keep the church sacred.

**Key Information:**
- Only nightly prayers have kept the church a place of cleanly sacredness in Barovia.
- There is a **book in the library of Ravenloft** that might help destroy Strahd. Strahd kept meticulous notes on all that he did or said. Perhaps some weakness may be found there. *(Refers to the Tome of Strahd.)*
- Ireena Kolyana was **not the natural daughter** of Kolyan Indirovich. Old Kolyan found her at the edge of the Svalich Woods near the Pillarstone of Ravenloft when she was a girl, with no memory of her past. He adopted her as his own.

### G1. Madam Eva's Tent

#### Madam Eva (Gypsy, Cleric 10)

- **AC:** 10 [9]
- **HP:** 22
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** 1d4
- **THAC0:** 10 [+9]
- **MV:** 90' (30')
- **SV:** D6 W10 P9 B12 S11 (C10)
- **AL:** CN
- **S** 15 **I** 10 **W** 17 **D** 15 **C** 13 **Ch** 8
- **Notes:** This old woman may seem crazed and mad but she is, in fact, quite cunning and sharp of mind. She is never fooled by adventurers and is very neutral. She serves Strahd as long as that benefits her and her troupe. She never gives aid and never needs any.

**Spells:**

| Level | Spells |
|------:|--------|
| 1st | *darkness, protection from evil, cause fear, command, cure light wounds* (x2) |
| 2nd | *augury, chant, hold person, know alignment, speak with animals, silence 15' radius* |
| 3rd | *glyph of warding, curse* (x2), *locate object* |
| 4th | *cause serious wounds, detect lie, divination* |
| 5th | *true seeing, raise dead* |

**Role:** Performs the Fortunes of Ravenloft card reading that determines key treasure locations and Strahd's goals. Leader of the Tser Pool gypsy encampment (10 gypsies, F-4 each; see barovia.md § G).

**Key Information:** She speaks the name of each PC and makes reference to a past deed of each. If asked, she performs the Fortunes reading. Her answers are cryptic but the card reading results are binding on the adventure.

### Court of the Count & Rooms of Weeping (K25-K46)

#### Lief Lipsiege (Human Accountant, F-0) — K30

- **AC:** 10 [9]
- **HP:** 5 (HD 2)
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** 1-4
- **THAC0:** 19 [+0]
- **MV:** 90' (30')
- **SV:** D16 W18 P17 B20 S19 (NH)
- **AL:** CE
- **Notes:** Pressed into service by Strahd ages ago. Keeps all the books for Strahd, recording his riches and conquests. Chained to the desk. Grumpy because the Count does not allow him to know about all of the treasures.

**Role:** Information source. If treated with kindness, reveals the **exact location of the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind** (per Fortunes results) and draws a crude but geographically accurate map. His map does **not** avoid traps or dangers along the route.

**Danger:** If threatened, he pulls a rope that sounds a loud gong. Within 1-10 minutes, a random monster from Table 6 (see region.md) appears and attacks the PCs.

#### Helga (Vampire) — K32

- **AC:** 1 [18]
- **HP:** 36 (HD 8+3)
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** 5-10 (1d6+4) + drain 2 levels
- **THAC0:** 11 [+8]
- **MV:** 120' (40') / Fly 180' (60')
- **SV:** D10 W12 P11 B12 S13 (F8)
- **AL:** CE
- **SD:** +1 weapons or better needed to hit her

**Role:** One of the most dangerous deceptions in the module. Disguised as a maid, she claims to be the daughter of a villager cruelly forced into Strahd's service. She will plead to be saved, join the party if asked, and play the innocent female perfectly. She only reveals her true nature when she attacks -- targeting isolated PCs.

**Key Detail:** She is actually the daughter of a townsperson but **chose** a life of evil with Strahd. She attacks only when she can isolate a single PC, or when commanded by Strahd. Full vampire abilities including energy drain.

#### Gertruda (Barovian, F-0) — K42

- **AC:** 10 [9]
- **HP:** 4
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** 1-4
- **THAC0:** 19 [+0]
- **MV:** 120' (40')
- **SV:** D16 W18 P17 B20 S19 (NH)
- **AL:** NG
- **S** 8 **I** 8 **W** 4 **D** 16 **Con** 13 **Ch** 16
- **Notes:** Mad Mary's daughter. Sheltered her entire life. Extremely naive. Believes in a fairytale view of life.

**Role:** Innocent victim. Found in Strahd's bedchamber (K42), oblivious to any danger. Strahd has **not yet bitten her** -- he is saving her for later. When faced with decisions, she almost always makes the most simplistic choice. She is a danger to herself and others through naivety.

**Connection:** Her mother, Mad Mary (E3), is inconsolable over her disappearance.

### Larders of Ill Omen (K61-K72)

#### Cyrus Belview (Human, Fighter 0) — K62

- **AC:** 8 [11]
- **HP:** 8
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** club (1-4)
- **THAC0:** 18 [+1]
- **MV:** 120' (40')

**Role:** Castle servant. Obviously crazy. Has served Strahd for uncounted years and is totally devoted to him. Will not attack first. - In K66 (Butler's Quarters), he fingers the PCs' equipment -- he has been collecting gear from dead adventurers for years and looks forward to adding to his collection.

### Dungeon & Catacombs (K73-K88)

#### Werewolf (Disguised Villager) — K75

**Werewolf** x1: AC 5 [14], HD 4+3 (17hp), Att 1 x bite (2-8), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 150' (50'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F4), ML --, AL CE. SD can only be hit by silver or +1 or better weapons.

**Role:** Appears as a villager locked in a dungeon cell, begging for rescue. Claims to have been locked up by Strahd for disobedience. In truth, the werewolf is anxious to prove its worth to Strahd and will try to join the party, then attack at the first good opportunity.

#### Sasha Ivliskova (Vampire) — Crypt 20

**Vampire** x1: AC 2 [17], HD 8+3 (47hp), Att 2 x strike (1d6+4 + drain 2 energy levels), THAC0 11 [+8], MV 120' (40') / 180' (60') flying, SV D8 W9 P10 B10 S11 (F8), ML --, AL CE.

**Role:** An old wife of Strahd's. Once a townsperson, now under his control. Her crypt is inscribed "Sasha Ivliskova: Wife." She attacks when her crypt is opened.

#### Patrina Velikovna (Banshee) — Crypt 21

**Banshee** x1: AC 0 [19], HD 7 (47hp), Att 2 x touch (1d6+2 + drain 2 energy levels), THAC0 12 [+7], MV 150' (50'), SV D8 W9 P10 B10 S11 (F7), ML --, AL CE. SA keening -- save vs. spells or **die** (once per day, only in total darkness). Sight of her acts as a *fear* spell unless a save is made.

**Role:** A gypsy elf maiden who learned much of the black arts and was nearly a match for Strahd's powers. She desired to become one of his wives. Strahd agreed, but before the final draining could take place, **her own people stoned her to death** in mercy. Strahd demanded and received the body; she became the banshee found here. Her crypt reads "Patrina Velikovna: Bride."

## Key Antagonist: Count Strahd von Zarovich

> **Full stat block:** See appendices.md and region.md for complete data, spell lists, vampire abilities, combat tactics, and DM guidance. Below is a quick combat reference only.

### Quick Combat Reference

- **AC:** -1 [20]
- **HP:** 55 (HD 10)
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** 5-10 (1d6+4) + **drain 2 levels** per hit
- **THAC0:** 10 [+9]
- **MV:** 120' (40') / Fly 180' (60')
- **SV:** D8 W9 P9 B10 S10 (F10/MU10, best of both)
- **AL:** CE
- **S** 18/76

### Key Abilities

- **Energy Drain:** 2 levels per hit
- **Regenerate:** 3 hp/round
- **Charm Person:** Gaze, save vs. spells at -2
- **Shape Change:** Gaseous form or large bat at will
- **Weapon Immunity:** +1 or better to hit
- **Summoning:** Wolves and bats
- **Cannot Enter Uninvited:** Must be invited into buildings

### Spell List (MU-10)

| Level | Spells |
|------:|--------|
| 1st | *comprehend languages, hold portal, protection from good, sleep* |
| 2nd | *invisibility, locate objects, mirror image, ESP* |
| 3rd | *fireball, gust of wind, suggestion* |
| 4th | *polymorph other, polymorph self* |
| 5th | *animate dead, distance distortion* |

### Vulnerabilities

- **Sunlight:** Destroyed after 10 rounds of direct exposure
- **Sunsword:** +3 to hit, +10 additional damage vs. vampires
- **Holy Symbol of Ravenkind:** Flares with sunlight 1-10 rounds; Strahd cannot move or attack while it flares
- **Stake through heart:** Holds from movement (does not kill)

### Spy Network

- Spies report **4x daily** (dawn, noon, dusk, midnight)
- **60%** chance he knows PC location at each report
- If he knows, he **attacks within 2 hours**

### One-Time Attack Options

1. **Personal Attack:** Attacks a single PC for 5 rounds, then leaves
2. **Zombie Assault:** 10-15 Strahd zombies, all at once
3. **Wolf Pack:** 3-12 worg wolves, all at once

> **Reference:** See region.md for full Strahd behavior rules, Fortunes of Ravenloft goals, and Playing Strahd principles. See appendices.md for the complete stat block and combat tactics.

## NPC Relationship Map

### Central Relationships

- **Strahd** → **Ireena Kolyana**: Obsessive love. Ireena is the reincarnation of Tatyana, the woman Strahd murdered his brother Sergei to possess. He has bitten her twice and intends to make her his vampire bride (Crypt 18 is inscribed "Ireena Kolyana: Wife" and awaits her).
- **Strahd** → **Sergei von Zarovich** (deceased): Brother. Strahd killed Sergei on his wedding day to Tatyana out of jealousy. Sergei's tomb is K85.
- **Strahd** → **Tatyana** (deceased): The original woman Strahd loved. She flung herself from the castle walls rather than submit to him. Ireena is her reincarnation.

### Village Relationships

- **Ismark the Lesser** → **Ireena Kolyana**: Siblings (Ireena is adopted). Ismark seeks to protect her and enlists the PCs' aid.
- **Ismark** → **Kolyan Indirovich** (deceased Burgomaster): Son. The Burgomaster died of natural causes (heart failure from nightly attacks on his house). Dead for 10+ days.
- **Kolyan Indirovich** → **Ireena**: Adopted father. Found her as a girl at the edge of the Svalich Woods with no memory.
- **Mad Mary** → **Gertruda**: Mother and daughter. Mary sheltered Gertruda her entire life. Gertruda escaped and ended up in Strahd's bedchamber (K42).
- **Bildrath** → **Parriwimple**: Uncle and nephew. Parriwimple is his bodyguard and stock-boy.

### Strahd's Servants

- **Strahd** → **Madam Eva**: Alliance of convenience. She serves Strahd as long as it benefits her and her troupe. She is the gypsy leader and performs the Fortunes of Ravenloft reading.
- **Strahd** → **Helga** (K32): Vampire servant. She chose a life of evil with Strahd. Disguises herself as an innocent maid.
- **Strahd** → **Cyrus Belview** (K62): Devoted servant. Crazy but loyal. Unwittingly (or wittingly) directs PCs into danger.
- **Strahd** → **Lief Lipsiege** (K30): Forced servant. Chained to his desk as Strahd's accountant. A potential ally for the PCs if treated with kindness.
- **Strahd** → **Witches** (K56): Seven witches in his service. They raid K50 at night to kidnap sleeping PCs.
- **Strahd** → **Gypsies**: The Vistani serve Strahd out of fear. They are given a potion that lets them pass through Barovia's fog freely. They mislead adventurers and sell fake anti-vampire potions.

### Strahd's Former Brides (Catacombs)

- **Strahd** → **Sasha Ivliskova** (Crypt 20): Former wife, now a vampire under his control.
- **Strahd** → **Patrina Velikovna** (Crypt 21): Would-be bride, a gypsy elf maiden. Her own people stoned her to death before Strahd could complete the draining. She became a banshee.

### Other Castle Connections

- **Werewolf** (K75) → **Strahd**: Disobedient servant locked in the dungeon, trying to regain Strahd's favor by betraying the PCs.
- **Donavich** → **Strahd**: The village priest is the only active resistance. His nightly prayers protect the church.

## NPC Roleplay Notes

### Ireena Kolyana
**Voice:** Quiet but resolute. Polite, slightly formal. Speaks with a hint of sadness but not self-pity.
**Personality:** Sweet but troubled. Strong-willed beneath a mild exterior. She is no damsel in distress -- she will fight alongside the PCs. She is confused about her past and disturbed by Strahd's attention. She does not understand why he is obsessed with her.
**Key trait:** She does not remember her early past. Any mention of "Tatyana" should provoke no recognition from her.

### Ismark the Lesser
**Voice:** Earnest, direct, slightly desperate. Speaks fair common speech.
**Personality:** Dutiful son trying to protect his sister after his father's death. Tight-lipped about most things but opens up about Ireena and Strahd. He carries the weight of his father's failure and the village's despair.
**Key trait:** He is called "the Lesser" -- a name that suggests he lives in his father's shadow and knows it.

### Madam Eva
**Voice:** Crackling, dry weeds. Her tone soars and falls like the wind. Prone to sudden cackling laughter.
**Personality:** Seems crazed and mad but is cunning and sharp. She has seen many adventurers come and go. She is truly neutral -- she serves Strahd when it benefits her and her troupe, but she does not go out of her way to harm the PCs either. She never gives aid and never needs any.
**Key trait:** She knows the PCs' names and past deeds before they introduce themselves. Her card readings are theatrical and dramatic.

### Bildrath
**Voice:** Flat, mercantile, unapologetic. No warmth.
**Personality:** A pure capitalist. He will not bargain, ever. *"If you want it badly enough, you'll pay for it -- because you certainly won't be taking your business elsewhere."* He sees the PCs as marks, nothing more.
**Key trait:** Completely self-interested. No loyalty to anyone except profit (and his nephew).

### Cyrus Belview
**Voice:** Giggly, scattered, prone to non sequiturs and bad jokes at the worst times.
**Personality:** Obviously crazy. Devoted to Strahd in a way that is more pathetic than threatening. He is not malicious -- he genuinely thinks he is being helpful by sending PCs to "their room in the tower." He has been collecting dead adventurers' gear for years and looks forward to adding the PCs' equipment to his pile.
**Key trait:** Comic relief with a dark edge. Everything he suggests benefits Strahd, whether he knows it or not.

### Lief Lipsiege
**Voice:** Grumpy, put-upon, resentful.
**Personality:** Chained to his desk for ages. Resentful of Strahd for not letting him know about all the treasures. He can be won over with kindness -- he is the most reliable information source in the castle, knowing the exact location of the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind.
**Key trait:** His loyalty is to himself. He will share information if treated well, but will summon a monster (via the gong rope) if threatened.

### Helga (the Vampire Maid)
**Voice:** Pleading, desperate, tearful. The perfect innocent victim.
**Personality:** A consummate actress. She will beg, plead on her hands and knees, and play every note of the helpless maiden. She maintains this facade until the moment she attacks. The key to playing her is that **she never breaks character** until she strikes. There should be no "tells" for the players.
**Key trait:** She chose evil. She is not a tragic figure -- she is a predator wearing a mask.

### Donavich
**Voice:** Hoarse and weak from nightly prayer. Gentle but exhausted.
**Personality:** The last bastion of goodness in Barovia. He has devoted his life to holding back the darkness, night after night. He is knowledgeable about Strahd's history and Ireena's origins but speaks of these things with the weariness of someone who has carried the burden too long.
**Key trait:** He is the one NPC who can provide genuine, truthful information about the Tome of Strahd and Ireena's true nature.

### Mad Mary
**Voice:** Moaning sobs. Barely acknowledges anyone.
**Personality:** Completely broken by grief. She cannot be reasoned with or comforted. She exists to set the tone of Barovia -- a land where even love cannot protect you from loss.
**Key trait:** She is the emotional heart of the village's despair. Play her grief as genuine and overwhelming.

### Arik (the Barkeep)
**Voice:** Dull, hollow. Monotone.
**Personality:** Mindless. Cleans glasses endlessly. Takes drink orders and serves them, then returns to cleaning. There is nothing left inside him. He is a shell.
**Key trait:** Do not try to make him interesting. His emptiness IS the point.
