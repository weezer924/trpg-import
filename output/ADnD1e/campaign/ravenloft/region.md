## Count Strahd Von Zarovich

*(PDF p.3)*

### Stat Block

**Count Strahd Von Zarovich (The First Vampyr)**

- **AC:** -1 [20]
- **HP:** 55 (HD 10)
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** 5-10 (1d6+4)
- **THAC0:** 10 [+9]
- **MV:** 120' (40') / Fly 180' (60')
- **SV:** D8 W9 P9 B10 S10 (F10/MU10, best of both)
- **ML:** — (special: see Playing Strahd below)
- **AL:** CE
- **S** 18/76
- **Frequency:** Unique
- **% In Lair:** 90%
- **Treasure Type:** F
- **SA:** Energy Drain (2 levels per hit), charm person (gaze, save vs. spells at -2)
- **SD:** +1 or better weapon to hit, regenerate 3 hp/round
- **MR:** See vampire abilities below
- **Intelligence:** Genius
- **Size:** M
- **Psionic Ability:** Nil

### Vampire Abilities

- **Strength 18/76:** Standard vampire strength (+2 to hit, +4 damage in melee per AD&D exceptional Str table — the listed 5-10 damage is standard vampire base damage).
- **Energy Drain:** Each successful hit drains 2 life levels from the target.
- **Regenerate:** 3 hit points per round.
- **Gaseous Form:** Can assume gaseous form at will.
- **Shape Change:** Can shape change into a large bat at will.
- **Charm Person:** By gazing into a character's eyes; target saves vs. spells at -2.
- **Weapon Immunity:** Can only be hit by magical weapons (+1 or better).
- **Summoning:** Can summon wolves and bats (see Attack Options below).
- **Invitation Required:** Cannot enter a building unless invited in. May knock at doors and attempt to charm characters into inviting him.

### Strahd's Spy Network & Attack Timing

Strahd has a variety of spies and servants. They report to him **four times each day** (at dawn, noon, dusk, and midnight). There is a **60% chance** that Strahd knows the PCs' location at each of these times.

If Strahd knows their location, he **attacks the PCs — wherever they are — within two hours**. Strahd chooses the time and method of his attacks carefully.

Although Strahd can be encountered in many places, he is **always encountered in the place indicated by the Fortunes of Ravenloft results** (see below), unless he has been forced to his tomb.

### Strahd's Attack Options (One-Time Each)

Strahd can attack in each of the following ways **once** during the adventure:

1. **Personal Attack:** Strahd attacks a single PC for 5 melee rounds, then leaves.
2. **Zombie Assault:** Strahd calls 10-15 Strahd zombies to attack the party. All zombies must attack at the same time in the same place.
3. **Wolf Pack:** Strahd calls 3-12 worg wolves to attack the party. All wolves must attack at the same time in the same place.

### Playing Strahd: Three Core Principles

1. **Strahd chooses when he attacks.** Strahd is supposed to be a genius — play him as one. Whenever he is aware of the PCs' positions, he is allowed to make an attack how and where he wants. His attacks must be timed to be most advantageous to him. To do that, Strahd must move around during the adventure. However, he can always be found in the place determined in Fortunes of Ravenloft. It is the DM's responsibility to see that the vampire uses his abilities to his greatest advantage.

2. **Strahd knows when to withdraw.** He knows when he is in over his head. If he is losing a battle he becomes gaseous, *polymorphs* into a wolf or bat, and/or summons other creatures to guard his retreat.

3. **Strahd's attacks depend upon his goals.** There is a reason why Strahd lured the PCs into his little kingdom. This reason is determined in Fortunes of Ravenloft. Strahd's plans for achieving his goal are listed with his goals.

## Fortunes of Ravenloft

*(PDF p.4–5)*

Fortunes of Ravenloft is an imitation ancient gypsy card reading. It determines Strahd's goals and the placement of important treasures. This card reading makes *Ravenloft* different when you play it again.

**You must run this card reading before playing this module.** If, during the adventure, the PCs have their fortunes read at the gypsy camp (Area G1), run this card reading again out loud. Substitute the new results for the old ones. Be sure to write down the results.

### Card Reading Procedure

1. Find a regular 52-card deck. Remove all **2, 4, 6, 8, 9, and joker** cards. You now have 4 suits × 8 cards each (3, 5, 7, 10, J, Q, K, A). Shuffle.
2. **Alternative:** Roll 1d8 for card value, 1d4 for suit. Re-roll duplicates.
3. **Deal the top five cards face down.**
4. Reveal each card in order, reading the flavor text aloud:

| Card # | Determines | Table |
|-------:|-----------|-------|
| 1st | Location of **Holy Symbol of Ravenkind** | Table 1 (card value) + Table 2 (suit) |
| 2nd | Location of **Tome of Strahd** | Table 1 (card value) + Table 2 (suit) |
| 3rd | Location where **Strahd can always be found** | Table 1 (card value) + Table 2 (suit) |
| 4th | Location of **Sunsword hilt** | Table 1 (card value) + Table 2 (suit) |
| 5th | **Strahd's goal** | Table 3 (card value) |

**Read-aloud text for each card:**

- **1st card (Holy Symbol):** *"This card is a symbol of great power. It is a holy symbol of great hope and protection against the forces of darkness. This card tells where to find it."*
- **2nd card (Tome):** *"This card tells of history. Knowledge of the ancient may help you understand a foe."*
- **3rd card (Strahd):** *"This is the object of your search! Ah! I see darkness and evil behind this card! It is a powerful man whose enemy is light, and whose powers are beyond mortality!"*
- **4th card (Sunsword):** *"This card is good for you. It is a card of power and strength. It tells of a weapon of vengeance: a sword of light."*
- **5th card (Goal):** *"And here is the root card. Out of darkness and chaos, this card finds the reason and foundation for darkness and chaos. This card shows the purpose of all things. It is the key to life and death and else beyond."*

### Table 3. Fortunes of Ravenloft: Strahd's Goals

---

#### 3 or 5 — Strahd Seeks a New Identity

> *Not yet, but soon, one who appears to be your friend will become your enemy.*

Strahd will try to be alone with a charmed PC. When he is alone with a charmed PC, Strahd casts *polymorph other* on the PC, turning the PC into a vampire. The spell operates as detailed in the AD&D Players Handbook with the following clarifications:

- The PC takes the form of a vampire and radiates a magical aura, regardless of whether the PC survives a system shock check or not.
- A PC that survives a system shock check has all of the abilities of a vampire as detailed in the AD&D Monster Manual, regardless of whether that PC passes a mental check or not.
- Undead cannot drain levels from undead. Thus, if a polymorphed PC vampire retains his own mind, he and Strahd can fight, but neither can drain levels from the other. They can both, however, drain levels from non-undead.

After turning the PC into a vampire, Strahd casts *polymorph self* and turns into the likeness of the PC. Strahd puts the new vampire PC into his own coffin and attempts to join the party, masquerading as the PC. Strahd will try to persuade the party that he found a way out of Barovia. Strahd can, after all, open the gates. Strahd wants to move to another country using his new identity. The gypsies will carry dirt from his crypt to his new home.

#### 7 or 10 — Strahd Wants to Make a Magical Sphere of Darkness

> *This card tells of a tool of evil. Darkness surrounds and protects this tool, giving comfort to black hearts and protection from good.*

Strahd is trying to assemble a magical item that casts a continuous sphere of darkness. Such an item would greatly extend the range of his travels. Over the centuries he gathered the pieces of the sphere one by one, until now he is missing only one piece, a **black opal**.

Strahd mistakenly believes that one of the PCs has a black opal stone. Strahd will use his natural *charm person* abilities to charm lone PCs. Strahd will send those charmed PCs back to the party, asking, "Do you have the black opal?" When Strahd discovers that none of the PCs have a black opal, he will attempt to destroy them.

#### Jack or Queen — Strahd Wants to Win the Love of Ireena Kolyana

> *The darkness loves a light and desires it. Great subtle plans are in motion about you; plans that the dead may find warmth from the living.*

Strahd will attempt to charm all of the PCs, and make them attack Ireena. When they attack, Strahd will swoop down and save her from the PCs. Strahd hopes that the rescue will turn Ireena's heart to him. He wants Ireena to love him willingly rather than by force.

#### King or Ace — Strahd Wants the Sunsword

> *Here is a high and noble card. One of you carries a weapon that is stronger than any other against the evil in this land. Only one part is missing from this weapon. That part is found in the evil one's lair.*

See appendices.md for details about the *sunsword.* Strahd wants to destroy the *sunsword.* He believes (correctly) that one of the PCs (a random fighter) has been unknowingly carrying the *sunsword* hilt for some time. If the sunsword hilt is found and reunited with the blade, Strahd could be in real trouble.

## Lands of Barovia

*(PDF p.6)*

A perpetual rolling blackness of thunderclouds casts a gray pall over the land. The darker silhouette of Castle Ravenloft looks over the valley from its 1,000 foot pillar of rock.

### Geography

Castle Ravenloft dominates the small village of Barovia. The castle stands high atop a natural pillar of rock against the cliffs to the north of the village. The dark Svalich woods surround the village and cliffs. Use Map 1 to see the relative locations of the town and Castle Ravenloft.

There is a deathly stillness in the dark Barovian woods. Yet the woods are patrolled constantly by the wolves and serving beasts of Count Strahd.

The poor villagers of Barovia have been terrorized for centuries by "the devil" Strahd. Only the town priest and the few survivors of the second ruling house of Barovia have the will to offer more than just personal resistance to Strahd. No villager has left Barovia for centuries. Those who tried never returned, dying from the vile snapping teeth of the Barovian wolves and the choking deadly fog.

### The Gates of Barovia

The road to the village and lands of Barovia goes through ancient iron gates, controlled by the will of Count Strahd. There are only **two gates**: one east of the village and one west of the castle crossroads up the mountain.

- The gates **open** when the PCs arrive but **close** after they enter Barovia.
- The gates **will not open** for the PCs again until Strahd is destroyed.

### The Fog of Barovia

No one has left Barovia for centuries. This is because of the **trapping fog** that exists everywhere in Barovia.

- Once breathed, the fog infuses itself around a character's vital organs as a **neutralized poison**.
- The fog does not taste or smell any different than normal fog.
- It does **not harm** characters as long as they continue to breathe the air in Barovia.
- When they **leave Barovia**, the poison becomes active. Characters must **save vs. poison** or start to choke.
- Unless choking characters **reenter Barovia within 24 hours**, they die.
- The choking stops as soon as they breathe the fog again.

**Gypsy Immunity:** The gypsies were given a potion by Strahd that cancels the effects of the fog. This potion is jealously guarded by Madam Eva, who buried it in a secret place. It is impossible for the PCs to discover the potion.

**Destruction:** The fog is magically produced by Strahd and disappears entirely upon his destruction.

### Travel & Encounter Checks

- Every **3 turns**, check for a random encounter.
- **Outdoors, daytime** (not in Castle Ravenloft): Use **Table 4**.
- **Outdoors, nighttime** (not in Castle Ravenloft): Use **Table 5**.
- **Inside Castle Ravenloft**: Use **Table 6** (p.12).

## Nightly Raids

*(PDF p.6)*

Each night the PCs stay **anywhere other than in the castle** (Area K), Strahd attacks with his wolves and bats. His attacks are intended more to frighten the PCs than to damage them. Strahd just toys with them. After **5 melee rounds**, Strahd and his creatures withdraw.

**Nightly Attack Force:**

**Worg Wolf** ×4-16 (4d4): AC 6 [13], HD 4+4, Att 1 × bite (2-8), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 180' (60'), SV D13 W15 P14 B16 S16 (F4), ML —, AL NE.

**Bat** ×10-100 (10d10): AC 6 [13], HD 1d4hp, Att 0, THAC0 19 [+0], MV 40' (13') / Fly 180' (60'), SV D16 W18 P17 B20 S19 (NH), ML —, AL N. Bats fly into rooms, getting into the way of spell casting. Characters must make **dexterity checks** to cast spells with material components.

**If the PCs are indoors:** Every turn Strahd attacks, wolves try to break through a window or a door. A roll of **1 or 2 on 1d6** means a wolf breaks through. Once a wolf gets in, the remainder of the wolves and bats come in.

**Important:** The wolves and Strahd **never attack Ireena Kolyana.** After 5 melee rounds, all creatures flee into the night, leaving only Strahd's hollow laughter in the distance.

**Invitation Rule:** Strahd may knock at the door, but he **cannot enter** the building until someone invites him in. Strahd tries to *charm* characters into inviting him in.
