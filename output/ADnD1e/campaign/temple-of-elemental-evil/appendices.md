## Appendix A: Deities & Demigods

*(PDF p.120–122)*

Before the start of this scenario, you or the players should determine the characters' deities. A list of the greater and lesser gods of the World of Greyhawk Fantasy Game Setting, with active demigods, is given hereafter.

Remind your players that the local folk tend towards the Druidical faith (Ehlonna, Obad-Hai, and most of all Beory), or follow St. Cuthbert, or serve some deity of Evil (in the case of unsavory elements).

Because of the plots of Iuz and various demons and evil elemental types, St. Cuthbert has become actively aware of events, and has indirect assistance from Beory (who resists elemental destruction). The enmity between Iuz and St. Cuthbert may result in direct confrontation!

### Standard Divine Abilities

The following spell-like powers are common to all deities in this setting. Each is usable at will, up to once per round, and is activated by brief concentration alone:

Astral travel, comprehend languages, continual darkness, continual light, cure blindness, cure deafness, cure disease, cure insanity, detect charm, detect evil, detect good, detect illusions, detect invisibility, detect lie, detect magic, detect traps, ethereal travel, geas, feeblemind, infravision, know alignment, levitate, mirror image, polymorph self, read languages, read magic, teleport (no error), tongues, and ultravision.

### Iuz (The Old)

*(PDF p.121–122)*

**Demigod** (Oppression, deceit, wickedness, pain)

- **AC:** -4 [23] (-8 [27] with cape)
- **HP:** 165
- **#AT:** 1 or 2, **D:** by weapon type or 2-5/2-5 (+9 strength bonus)
- **MV:** 180' (60')
- **MR:** 45% (65% with cape)
- **SZ:** M
- **AL:** CE
- **S** 21 **I** 18 **W** 20 **D** 18 **Co** 18 **Ch** 18 (-4)
- **Classes:** 16th level Cleric / 16th level Assassin
- **Psionic Ability:** Class III, Attack/Defense Modes: All/all
- **Symbol:** Grinning human skull
- **Plane:** Prime Material (Oerth)
- **Worshippers' Alignment:** Evil (any)
- **SD:** +1 or better weapon to hit

Whether Iuz is a human who has become demon-like through the centuries, or whether he is a semi-demon, a cambion (as some suggest, a by-blow of Orcus), no mortal knows. He is, however, the first known godling of Chaotic Evil, whose wickedness and treachery are infamous throughout the Flanaess.

**Appearance:** This cruel being can appear as either a massive (7' tall) fat man with demoniac features, reddish skin, pointed ears, and long steely fingers, or as an old and wrinkled manikin scarcely 5 feet in height. Either might be Iuz' true form — or even both.

**Combat:**
- In his **demoniac form**, Iuz prefers to wield a great two-handed sword +3, though he can use other weapons. In any guise, Iuz can use his exceptionally long and sinewy digits to claw, strike, and strangle. If he strikes twice, the victim of his strangling grasp will die in 2-5 rounds unless freed.
- In his **manikin-like form**, Iuz can generate a disgusting spittle which he can expectorate once per round. This substance ages the victim struck by 1-6 years (no saving throw), and withers the area hit, numbing one member and making it useless for 2-5 rounds. His wrinkled visage projects a negative Charisma (-4), which causes Horror to all those of 6 or fewer HD, for a -55% reaction penalty.

**Soul Object:** Iuz is rumored to have a Soul Object secreted on the Abyssal Plane dominated by the demoness Zuggtmoy, with whom he is known to consort. He is thus free to roam outside his domain without fear of permanent harm.

**Cape:** He is also said to wear an old, short cape which boosts his Magic Resistance by 20%, and serves as a cloak of protection +4 as well.

**Clergy of Iuz:** Clerics of Iuz dress in rusty black (their master's favorite color) or white streaked with rust-red bloodstains. At 3rd level each gains the ability to *change self* (as the illusionist spell) once per day. Ceremonies include burning foul-smelling substances, beating of great drums and clangor of bronze bells, and blood sacrifice whenever possible. Places of service must be old, filthy, and dark. Altar services are typically of bone, including many skulls.

**Standard Demigod Powers:**
- As often as desired: *finger of death*, *invisibility*, *phantasmal force*, *protection from good 10' radius*, *remove fear*, summon followers (1-2 creatures of same type and alignment, up to 20 HD total), *wall of force*
- 3×/day: *cure light wounds*, *raise dead*
- 2×/day: *dispel good*, *dispel illusion*, *dispel magic*, *true seeing*
- 1×/day: *anti-magic shell*, *command* (2 round duration), *gate*, *heal*, *unholy word*, *limited wish*, *symbol*

Great enmity exists between Iuz and St. Cuthbert. This is carried on by the servants of the two, as well.

### St. Cuthbert (of the Cudgel)

*(PDF p.122)*

**Lesser God** (Wisdom, common sense, truth, forthrightness, dedication)

- **AC:** -3 [22] (-8 [27] in plate mail)
- **HP:** 224
- **#AT:** 2, **D:** by weapon type (+8 strength bonus)
- **MV:** 210' (70')
- **MR:** 80%
- **SZ:** M
- **AL:** LG(N)
- **S** 20 **I** 10 **W** 23 **D** 19 **Co** 25 **Ch** 19
- **Classes:** 22nd level Cleric / 8th level Druid / 7th level Monk
- **Psionic Ability:** Class VI, Attack/Defense Modes: All/all
- **Symbol:** Wooden Billet, Starburst, Crumpled Hat
- **Plane:** Arcadia
- **Worshippers' Alignment:** Lawful Good / Lawful Neutral
- **SD:** +3 or better weapon to hit

**Appearance:** St. Cuthbert appears as a stout red-faced man, with a drooping white moustache and flowing white hair. He wears a simple plate mail of +5 enchantment. Atop his helmet is a crumpled hat, and a starburst of rubies set in platinum hangs on his chest.

**Weapons:**
- **Left hand — Bronzewood Cudgel:** Actually a +3 weapon equal to a morning star in St. Cuthbert's grasp. Any human touched must save vs. spells or be beguiled for 5-20 turns.
- **Right hand — Mace of St. Cuthbert:** A mace of disruption +5. On a natural 20, the victim loses 1 point of Intelligence (no save, no MR). Additional powers: *bless* (by tapping touch), *know alignment* (1/day), *tongues*, *exorcise* (1/month), *remove curse* (7/week).

**Aspects:** St. Cuthbert hates Evil, but his major interest is in Law and Order, and the dual work of conversion and prevention from backsliding of "true believers." He seldom leaves his plane, unless some great duty brings him forth. When on the Prime Plane, he sometimes appears as a manure-covered yokel, or as a wanderer clad in brown and green robes, or as an elderly and slight tinker. He can gate to or from Arcadia or the Prime Material Plane at will.

**Priesthood (Three Orders):**

| Order | Dress | Role | Special Ability |
|-------|-------|------|-----------------|
| Chapeaux | Varying garb, all wear crumpled hats | Seek to convert people to the faith | *Shillelagh* 1×/day at 3rd level |
| Stars | Dark green robes, starburst holy symbol (copper/gold/platinum) | Retain doctrinal purity among the faithful | *ESP* 1×/day at 4th level |
| Billets | Simple brown and russet garments, oaken/bronzewood billet holy symbol | Ministers and protectors of the faithful | *Friends* 1×/day at 2nd level |

Followers found mainly in the central Flanaess, around Greyhawk, the Wild Coast, Urnst, and Verbobonc. A great rivalry exists between those who follow St. Cuthbert and the followers of Pholtus.

**Standard Lesser God Powers:**
- As often as desired: *control temperature 20' radius*, *improved invisibility*, *improved phantasmal force*, *remove curse*, *remove fear*, summon followers (1-3 creatures of same type and alignment, 25 HD total)
- 4×/day: *dispel evil*, *dispel illusion*, *dispel magic*
- 3×/day: *cure serious wounds*, *resurrection*, *true seeing*
- 2×/day: *anti-magic shell*, *gate*, *heal*, *holy word*, *polymorph others*, *protection from evil 20' radius*, *symbol*
- 1×/day: *command* (3 round duration), *death spell*, *minor globe of invulnerability*, *quest*, *restoration*, *trap the soul*, *wish*

### Zuggtmoy

**Demoness Lady of Fungi**

- **Frequency:** Very rare (unique)
- **AC:** 0 [19] (body) or -2 [21] (head)
- **HP:** 222 (49 HD)
- **#AT:** 2+2 or 2
- **D:** 2-8 each (×4 pseudopods) or 2-12/2-12 (trample)
- **MV:** 150' (50'); 60' (20') on walls/ceilings; 120' (40') in human form
- **MR:** 85%
- **Intelligence:** Genius
- **AL:** CE
- **SZ:** L (M in human form)
- **Psionic Ability:** 222, Attack/Defense Modes: All/all
- **SD:** +2 or better weapon to hit
- **Regeneration:** 1 hp/round (double if standing on loose soil)
- **Strength:** 19
- **Weight:** Over 2,000 pounds
- **XP:** 45,170 (×10 if slain permanently); currently bound, award is 28,770 (see dungeon-level-3.md area 353 for revised values if partially freed)

**Combat (Natural Form):**
- 4 pseudopods, each dealing 2-8 damage. Up to 2 hits on a single opponent; if both hit, additional 5-8 crushing damage (total 9-24 for two blows).
- May choose to **trample** instead (prone or small creatures under 4' only): 2 attacks on one opponent, each 2-12 damage; if both hit, victim also stunned for 1-4 rounds.

**Combat (Human Form):**
- Appears as an old and feeble crone. Can only attack with weapons or spell-like powers (at 22nd level of magic use).

**Spell-Like Powers (at will):**

*anti-plant shell*, *astral spell*, *charm plants*, *continual darkness*, *detect good*, *detect magic*, *entangle*, *fear 15' radius*, *improved invisibility*, *hold plant*, *levitate*, *locate plants*, *plant door*, *plant growth*, *pass plant*, *pollute food & drink*, *programmed illusion*, *read languages*, *read magic*, *silence 15' radius*, *snare*, *speak with plants*, *telekinese* (10,000 gp weight), *tongues*, *transport via plants*, *trip*

**Additional Powers:**
- Ethereal 2×/day
- *Limited wish* 1×/day
- *Gate* 1×/day (70% success, up to 1×/round attempts but succeeds only once): 01-50 = 1-4 Type I demons, 51-75 = 1-3 Type II demons, 76-90 = 1-2 Type III demons, 91-00 = 1 babau demon
- *Summon fungi* 1×/day (see table below)

**Summon Fungi Table:**

| d6 | Fungi Summoned |
|---:|---------------|
| 1 | 1-2 ascomids |
| 2 | 5-8 phycomids |
| 3 | 4-6 zygoms |
| 4 | 3-6 ustilagors |
| 5 | 2-4 basidironds |
| 6 | 5-8 violet fungi |

When in her lair, she is accompanied by all creatures on the summoning table (in maximum numbers), as well as poisonous toadstools, molds (brown, russet, and yellow), and a host of other dangerous and disgusting growths.

**Description:** Zuggtmoy's natural form is bulbous. She resembles a puffball mushroom with a toadstool growing on top. Four elephantine legs with suckered bottoms support the spherical body. The globular torso is flattened at the bottom and bulges at its equator, so that the legs reach to a bit over 3-foot height, the body another 30 inches higher, but with a horizontal diameter of nearly five feet. The mushroom-stem neck is two feet long, capped by a head which appears to have squashed humanoid features. The eyes are round, black, and blank-looking.

Zuggtmoy can extrude a pair of three-foot long pseudopodial arms from either hemisphere of her body, at will. These growths can extend opposing digits if desired.

Body coloration is variable, as Zuggtmoy is able to change it to suit her surroundings, though she is typically of a pale grayish-white or brownish-white color, the head slightly darker. Color can be moss green, dull brown, red-orange, mottled purple, etc. Body texture and odor are obviously fungoid. Blotchy patches of mold, smut, and blight cover her body.

### Juggernaut

*(PDF p.124–125)*

A creature related to the mimic, appearing as a large stone object with wheels, runners, or some other source of mobility.

- **Frequency:** Rare
- **AC:** 2 [17]
- **HD:** 10-13
- **#AT:** Up to 6, **D:** 2-12 each
- **MV:** 10'-120' (see below)
- **MR:** Standard
- **Intelligence:** Animal to Average
- **AL:** N(E)
- **SZ:** L (8-20' long)
- **Psionic Ability:** Nil
- **SD:** Immune to all fire
- **XP:** X/3,000 +14/hp

**Pseudopods:** Just as the mimic can project a pseudopod, so can the juggernaut produce several growths — slim toothy heads, pincers, rams, and so forth. It can make up to 6 attacks per round depending on shape limits. It can change form slowly; once pseudopods are cut off or reabsorbed, up to a month may be needed to re-grow them. Heads on pseudopods may permit noises of many sorts, and some few can even speak (though poorly).

**Movement:** A juggernaut with wheels or rollers cannot turn sharply or reverse directions unless it first comes to a complete halt. For every 10 feet traveled it can turn about 3 feet; it must travel 30 feet to make a 90° turn. It is also slow to start:

| Round | Movement Rate |
|------:|:-------------|
| 1 | 10'/round |
| 2 | 20'/round (2") |
| 3 | 30'/round (3") |
| ... | +1"/round |
| 12+ | 120'/round (12" max) |

**Crush Attack:** A juggernaut may attack by rolling over a victim, crushing everything in its path. Normal "to hit" roll is made. If it hits, all the victim's items must be checked vs. crushing blow. The victim takes up to **10-100 (10d10)** points of damage if the juggernaut is large and heavy. The victim may make a saving throw vs. death ray for half damage, but success does not negate the crushing effect on items.

### Gargoyle Cloak

**GP Sale Value:** 6,000 | **XP Value:** 1,000

When donned, this garment polymorphs the user into a gargoyle, with effect as the *polymorph self* spell. No command word is used. Items carried are absorbed within the gargoyle form, unusable. Immunities, flying ability, and all other gargoyle characteristics are bestowed. AC is modified by the user's true Dexterity, magical effects, and protective devices worn or carried (but excluding modifications from armor and shield).

The cloak's effects may be produced up to **3 times per day**. The polymorph occurs within 1 segment after the cloak is donned, without the sparkling lights usually accompanying a polymorph.

**Risk:** After the first full turn of use, there is a **10% chance per turn (cumulative)** of the wearer being mentally changed into a gargoyle as well. If this occurs, the user either attacks or flies off. Only a *wish* or death can return the victim to normal form.

The cloak is easily damaged by any edged weapon, fire, and other hazards. If the wearer takes 20 or more points of damage from such attacks, consider the cloak destroyed. It may be repaired magically (via *limited wish* or similar enchantment) but not by normal means.

**Note:** Other cloaks of polymorphing may be found. Each is designed to change the user into one specific creature, with similar benefits and hazards.

### Ichor of Intoxication

**GP Sale Value:** 900 | **XP Value:** 500

A colorless syrup found in half-ounce vials. It can be mixed with a liquid, applied to food, or simply ingested. The ichor dries when exposed to air, hardening in 1 turn.

When a vial is consumed, the user becomes greatly intoxicated within 1 round. A half-vial produces mild intoxication; lesser doses have little or no effect. More than one vial causes a comatose state. One vial can treat one apple-sized foodstuff (~10 square inches) or any beverage.

**Recovery time:** Mild = 2-4 hours, Great = 4-6 hours, Comatose = 7-10 hours (requires sleep).

**"The Answerer"**

**GP Sale Value:** 90,000 | **XP Value:** 18,000 (plus scabbard: 35,000 gp)

This hefty steel broadsword bears a mighty enchantment. It was originally fashioned for a demigod of elsewhen, and eventually passed into the World of Greyhawk setting. It has been in the hands of the minions of Chaotic Evil for a decade or more, resting in hiding in the Temple of Elemental Evil, guarded by the worst of elemental grues.

**Alignment:** Chaotic Good

**Alignment Restrictions:**

| Wielder Alignment | Effect |
|-------------------|--------|
| Lawful | 1-6 damage, falls senseless for 1-10 rounds |
| Neutral | 1-3 damage, swoons for 1-4 rounds |
| Evil | No "to hit" bonus whatsoever |

| **Chaotic Good** | **Full function: +4 bonus always operates** |

**Description:** The sword has a hilt of silver and gold wire, most cunningly wrought. Its guard and pommel are set with 7 perfect emeralds (corundum), total value 65,000 gp. Its scabbard is trimmed with precious metal and decorated with gems of green and golden hue, worth 35,000 gp.

### Lens of Transformation

**Artifact** | **GP Sale Value:** 22,500

*(See dungeon-level-4.md for placement)*

This device is a sheet of cloudy crystal, 7 feet broad and tall, 3 inches thick. It was created by Iuz, who placed it in the dungeon for his own evil purposes.

- The crystal appears **cloudy** from one side, but **clear** from the other.
- No *detect* spell works within 20 feet, except *detect magic* (reveals a powerful dweomer, and if activated, a lesser magic within 20 feet).

**Activation:** Whenever any life force (living or undead) approaches within 3 feet of the clear side, the cloudy side brightens, producing a *continual light* effect within one round. Viewed from the cloudy side, the users (those on the clear side) appear as the **purest opposites** of their actual forms. A paladin would appear demonic; a CE monster might appear as a paladin or a deva. Additional visual effects (flames, smoke, sky, fluffy clouds) are produced as appropriate background. Subtle feelings (fear, peace, etc.) are projected to viewers of the illusion, allowing **no saving throw** (though innate total immunity to ESP negates emotional effects).

**Durability:** Protected by a powerful and permanent force field. Impervious to harm by anything less than a godling. Even *passwall* and *glassee* are useless. Cannot be removed by a power lesser than Iuz', except for a *wish* (and even that has a 25% chance of shattering the lens). A final dweomer lets Iuz know if the lens is destroyed, and who did it.

### The Orb of Golden Death

**Artifact** | **GP Sale Value:** 75,000

*(Campaign-critical item — see also elemental-nodes.md)*

Also known as: **Yellowskull**, **Goldenskull**, or the **Death Orb**.

Because of its nature and aura, no paladin will touch it willingly, nor will any Good cleric; they feel its immense innate Evil.

**Appearance:** A gold sphere 3 inches in diameter, shaped to resemble a human skull sans lower jaw. Atop the crown is a raised diadem with depressions designed to accept four large gems, one representing each element.

#### Base Powers (Without Gems)

The possessor can, at will:
- *Detect lie*
- *Know alignment*
- *Poison* (as reverse of *neutralize poison*)

The possessor learns from the Orb full details of usable powers, but **no information** about: Charisma effects, potential powers from gems, unfavorable effects, or the Orb's protection ability.

#### Protection

Neither Zuggtmoy nor Iuz can harm the possessor of the Orb, nor can either be the agent employing another to cause harm. However, both Zuggtmoy and Iuz know the identity and exact location of any possessor (their detection ability overpowering any protections).

#### Throne Knowledge

If the possessor gazes upon the great throne in the upper Temple (area 3, see temple-exterior.md), he or she immediately knows how to operate it. The Throne can raise and lower itself between the upper Temple works and dungeon level 3 (area 340).

#### Charisma Effect (CE Creatures Only)

| State | Charisma | Reaction Adj. | Max Henchmen | Loyalty Base | Special |
|-------|---------|--------------|-------------|-------------|---------|
| Normal | 20 | +45% | 25 (all CE) | 60% | Awe (≤2 HD/Levels) |
| Angry | -3 | -50% | — | — | Horror (≤4 HD/Levels) |

Those affected by Awe or Horror are stunned into inaction (no save), and cannot initiate any action other than physical defense. CE creatures only.

#### Gem Powers (When Mounted)

When one of the elemental power gems is placed in its diadem mounting, the possessor gains these abilities (and immediately realizes each):

| # | Power |
|---|-------|
| 1 | Noted spell power, usable 1×/week |
| 2 | Travel to/from the corresponding Elemental Plane at will (as *teleport without error*) |
| 3 | Comfortable existence in the corresponding element or Elemental Plane (no environmental damage) |
| 4 | *Conjure elemental* 1×/week (from appropriate Plane; 8 HD, max hp 64; no mental control needed while Orb held; not possible while ON the same Elemental Plane) |
| 5 | Summon and control a demon of the noted type, up to 3×/day (never more than 1×/hour) |

**Elemental Gems Table:**

| Element | Gem | Spell (1×/week) | Demon Summoned |
|---------|-----|-----------------|----------------|
| Air | Smoky quartz | *Wind walk* | Babau |
| Earth | Carnelian | *Earthquake* | Type I |
| Fire | Garnet | *Flame strike* | Type II |
| Water | Aquamarine | *Wall of ice* | Type III |

The four gems are hidden in the Elemental Nodes (see elemental-nodes.md). Before the final assault on the Temple, Zuggtmoy hid the gems in their corresponding Nodes and entrusted the gemless Yellowskull to her priesthood.

**Loose Gem Transport:** When touched, a loose gem immediately transports all creatures within 50 feet to the corresponding Node (no save). The gem travels with the victims. No effect occurs if touched while in its corresponding Node. A container (ideally the Orb) must be used to prevent involuntary travel.

#### Ill Effects

No Good ever results from the Orb's powers, except on a very short-term or temporary basis. If evil can result from any use, it occurs alongside whatever was actually done. For example, a conjured elemental fighting evil creatures will do so in a way that allows maximum harm to the conjurer's associates.

Each time the **Awe Power** is brought forth, the user loses 1 point of Charisma (with respect to non-CE creatures only).

Zuggtmoy and/or Iuz can attempt to persuade or coerce the possessor to hand over the Orb (though they cannot take it by force). The possessor is forced to react cooperatively, the degree corresponding to Charisma loss from Awe use — a **10% chance of persuasion per point lost**. If the character does not naturally cooperate, roll d% to check.

Any character who actually **gives** the Death Orb to either Zuggtmoy or Iuz becomes the **hopeless thrall** of that Evil being ever afterwards (permanently removes the character from PC play).

#### Destroying the Orb

The Death Orb can only be destroyed if **all four gems are properly inset**. It must then be subjected to the following effects, in quick succession and in the proper order:

1. A wind of 50+ mph force
2. The strike of a maul made from a solid piece of granite
3. A very hot flame (1,000°F)
4. Immersion in very cold water (32°F)

**Consequences of Destruction:**
- The Elemental Nodes collapse
- The Greater Temple (Dungeon Level 4, area 417) collapses, and the dungeon levels above as well
- Zuggtmoy takes **111 points** of physical damage, is unable to use any powers for **4 days**, and cannot leave her own Abyssal Plane for **40 years**
- Iuz loses the services of evil elemental creatures for **4 years**
