## Part 3: Dungeons of Elemental Evil

*(PDF p.44–45)*

The Temple dungeons are a busy place, for the underground is still alive with followers of the defeated cult. They are engaged in various struggles amongst themselves, forgetting the common enemy Good. A few individuals and groups still strive to unite the factious adherents of Zuggtmoy and Elemental Evil; most, however, seek to place themselves at the head of the conjoined group. Because of this rivalry and internal strife, adventurers can fairly easily penetrate the Temple dungeons, whether disguised or not.

Characters wearing the robes of one elemental faction will likely be attacked by the other three groups (if they perceive the party to be weak) or by other bands dwelling beneath the Temple.

### Standard Corridors

- 10' wide corridors: gothic arches, peaking at ~17' height. Sconces at 10' intervals.
- 20' and 30' passages: roman arches, ~30' tall. Cressets at 30' intervals.
- Sconces/cressets staggered left-right, unlit unless specified.
- Doors: oak, hornwood, or bronzewood, ~3" thick, bound with bronze, ring on each side.

### Stairs

All stairways steep, one-foot risers. Large ones polished stone (area 148 et al.), small ones hewn from solid rock.

### Ventilation

Many small vents and air shafts throughout. Rooms warmed by braziers. Torch smoke does not linger. Strange odors and odd noises borne on gusts of air.

### Map Numbering

First digit = Dungeon Level. Area 1 of Level 1 = 101; area 29 of Level 2 = 229; etc.

### Prisoners

When human and demi-human prisoners are found and rescued (taken out of dungeon), their XP value is applied to party's total as if defeated monsters. If prisoners are slain, party loses **triple** XP values. If simply turned loose in dungeon, no XP award, no deductions.

### Treasure

Base value given for each gem; roll 1d10 for increase/decrease (DMG p.26). Jewelry checked using 1d8 (same page).

### Recruiting

All factions recruit actively. Any group of humans or small humanoids can replace losses at 1 per day. Larger humanoids (3+1 HD or more) replace 1 per 3 days; monsters 1 per week. Once leaders eliminated, neither they nor their forces can be replaced.

### Zuggtmoy Bound

The demoness is imprisoned in a room of Dungeon Level Three. Four huge bronze gates exist at areas 1, 145, 210, and 340. If gate 340 destroyed → Zuggtmoy free to roam areas 339-353. Gate 210 also opened → free on levels 2-3. Gate 145 sundered → also levels 1 and 4. All gates destroyed → completely freed.

### Clues

- Area 216 contains a scroll with cryptic poem referring to area 334 and Fragarach.
- The Orb of Golden Death (Appendix D) is key to final success against Zuggtmoy. A clue poem may be obtained from Jaroo (Hommlet druid) by Neutral PCs, or from Church of St. Cuthbert by Lawful Good PCs. See `appendices.md` for the Orb of Golden Death details.

## Room Key

### 101. Armory

*(PDF p.46)*

> This plain chamber is filled with wooden racks for various spears, pole arms, and smaller weapons. The walls have many pegs indicating where other weapons and shields once hung. All that remains now are broken weapons, sundered shields, and ruined coats of mail. Several skeletons, both human and gnoll, lie scattered here and there, as well as odd skulls and bones. Cobwebs hang in places; if any creatures have been here, visits are not frequent.

Close examination of bones reveals teeth marks. Many bones are cracked, and marrow chewed out. No treasure, nothing of value.

### 102. Armory

See area 101 for general description. In the southwest corner, under several splintered wooden shafts, is a quiver of **17 usable arrows**.

### 103. Pillared Hall

*(PDF p.46)*

> This 70'×30' chamber appears deserted. It has probably served as a privy, for the place has a foul fecal odor and filth is scattered here and there on the floor. The columns which support the ceiling 40' overhead are thick, four or five feet in diameter. A few skulls and bones lie around the pillar bases.

100 pounds of pressure on the floor 5' in from the entry triggers a delayed-action mechanism (X marks on map). One round later, a heavy barred grate drops and seals the doorway.

Two **harpies** roost atop the two central eastern pillars, in special niches 34' above the floor (90% undetectable). When the grate crashes down, the harpies start singing. After one round, they cease singing and fly around, attacking any creature that makes a hostile gesture. If sounds of fighting occur, occupants of area 104 arrive 1 round later. If no turmoil, they arrive 5 rounds after the grate's descent.

**Harpy** ×2: AC 7 [12], MV 60' (20') / Fly 150' (50'), HD 3, hp 16, 12, Att 3 × claw/claw/weapon (1-3/1-3/1-6), THAC0 17 [+2], SV D13 W15 P14 B16 S16 (F3), ML 7, AL CE; XP 193, 181. SA singing + charm.

The secret door in the northern portion of the west wall cannot be opened from this area except by magic.

### 104. Room, 30' × 20'

> This dark, filthy chamber is the prison in which—no. The door to the west of harpy hall leads to this place. No exits are apparent. The place reeks of carrion, and filth and bones litter the floor. Heaps of old cloth along the walls indicate where the occupants once nested. Old garments hang from pegs in the wall.

Six **ghouls** move from here into area 103 if any noise is heard therein. These creatures dwell in harmony with the harpies and others to the north (areas 105-107). Harpies get the "fun" of killing; most corpses then taken by ghouls for feasting.

**Ghoul** ×6: AC 6 [13], MV 90' (30'), HD 2, hp 15, 13, 12, 10, 8, 6, Att 3 × claw/claw/bite (1-3/1-3/1-6), THAC0 18 [+1], SV D14 W16 P15 B17 S17 (F2), ML 9, AL CE; XP 95, 91, 89, 85, 81, 77. SA touch paralyzes.

**Treasure:** Under bedding — 187 cp, 81 sp, 5 ep, 61 gp. A suit of **elf-sized elfin chainmail** hangs under an old cloak on south wall (may be sold for up to 500 gp; not normally available for purchase).

### 105. Room, 30' × 30'

Four **ghouls** lair herein; ignore all noise in area 103. Attack any humans or demi-humans not clad in Temple robes.

**Ghoul** ×4: AC 6 [13], MV 90' (30'), HD 2, hp 14, 13, 10, 9, Att 3 × claw/claw/bite (1-3/1-3/1-6), THAC0 18 [+1], SV D14 W16 P15 B17 S17 (F2), ML 9, AL CE; XP 93, 91, 85, 83. SA touch paralyzes.

### 106. Room, 10' × 20'

The only items of value are four "pewter" (actually tarnished silver) plates, each worth **30 gp**.

### 107. Room, 20' × 20'

> The south wall bears a bas relief of a hideous head — a human-like face with squashed features, the face low upon a mushroom-shaped cranium. Various humanoids, monsters, and demoniac creatures prance in the background. The remains of a sandstone altar lie in fragments before this sculpture. All furnishings are gone, but six heaps of old clothing remain. A wooden box is near the north door. The west door has a peep hole, usable from inside the room.

Two **ghasts** lair here, directing the ghouls. If wounded and hard-pressed, they try to escape via secret door in east wall → area 115. Secret exit works by raising a wall sconce (5' wide pivoting wall section). One-way; cannot be worked from outside.

**Ghast** ×2: AC 4 [15], MV 150' (50'), HD 4, hp 23, 20, Att 3 × claw/claw/bite (1-4/1-4/1-8), THAC0 15 [+4], SV D13 W15 P14 B16 S16 (F4), ML 9, AL CE; XP 282, 270. SA touch paralyzes, stench 10' radius (save vs. poison or -2 to hit).

**Treasure:** Under bed — loose stonework block hides a large gold cup (worth 450 gp) containing 112 gp and 3 onyx gems (each 50 gp).

### 108. Garbage Room

> The whole room reeks horribly. Bones are tossed here and there, and a partially eaten orc carcass lies on the floor near the south wall. No exits are visible, save the normal door to the north.

If party disturbs the trash, they arouse 20 giant rats. If 10+ slain, rest retreat (50% per round).

**Giant Rat** ×20: AC 7 [12], MV 120' (40') / Swim 60' (20'), HD 1/2, hp 3 each, Att 1 × bite (1-3), THAC0 19 [+0], SV D16 W18 P17 B20 S19 (NM), ML 8, AL N; XP 10 each.

### 109. Frescoed Corridor

*(PDF p.47)*

> This passage is evidently one of importance, for it is plastered and painted with the evil scenes typical of the Temple.

The east-west 10' passage slopes gradually downward. The 20' wide corridor gently descends towards Dungeon Level Two. Gradients noticed only by dwarves (75%) or gnomes (80%); others notice only if spread over 40+ feet (10% per 10' of spread).

If the wide corridor is taken, refer to area 201 (Dungeon Level Two).

### 109a. Stairway

Corridor ends in stairway diagonaling upward to a vestry in the Upper Temple (area 4 east, area 5 west).

### 110. Storage Chamber

> This chamber is obviously no longer in use, for it contains sacks of moldering grain, barrels of rotting flour, and heaps of broken containers and spilled contents in a jumbled mess.

Nothing of use or value.

### 111. Storage Chamber

> From the stale reek in this room, you can determine that much beer, wine, and ale was poured out here. The floor is covered with a dark residue, and broken tuns, barrels, casks, and kegs are strewn everywhere.

Careful inspection: one small barrel with contents intact — vinegary wine, so bad that anyone sampling it loses **1-4 STR for 2-12 turns** due to nausea.

### 112. Armory

See area 101 for general description. Anything of value smashed or removed. A usable **mace** remains, found only if a full turn is spent searching.

### 113. Armory

> This inner room was evidently the antechamber of some minor functionary, for its walls are plastered and covered with obscene painting. The remains of upholstered furnishings — a couch and four chairs — are still in the room. Some old and battered pewter dishes are heaped upon a small table. Normal doors are placed in the south and east walls.

See area 101 for general description. Thoroughly vandalized. On a small ledge of one supporting arch, 10' above floor, lies a **crossbow bolt +3**. Detect magic easily reveals it; without magic, only 5% chance per thorough search. If anyone reaches 9+ feet above floor, discovery is 75% likely.

### 114. Secured Door

*(PDF p.48)*

Door breaks only after **50 points of damage** (2 hits/round; consider Large size). Noise draws all occupants from area 115 next round.

> This dark, filthy chamber is the prison in which captives are held, for eventual sacrifice at the Earth Temple (area 145) or for feasting of ghouls and ghasts (areas 115-118).

**11 human prisoners** currently here; all naked, males chained to wall. All from Nulb area:
- 2 females (wife and servant of a merchant)
- 2 merchants (XP 13 each)
- 3 servants (XP 12 each)
- 1 river sailor (Level 0, hp 6, XP 16)
- 4 men-at-arms (Level 0, hp 4 each, XP 14 each)

Prisoners know nothing of the Temple. Sailor was brought by Dick Rentsch. Merchants/wife/servants ask to be shown the exit. Sailor and men-at-arms serve with 100% loyalty if supplied with clothing, armor, arms, and paid normally.

### 115. Room, 20' × 30'

> This place is plastered and wainscoated in some (now ruined) wood. It was evidently some form of trophy or museum room, and several broken cases are shoved against the walls. A serviceable but moldy shield hangs on the west wall. Two great heaps of old cloth and pillows serve as beds.

Six **ghouls** dwell here. Pass through eastern door to investigate noise from corridor (area 114). If intruders discovered, five attack while one hastens to alert area 116.

**Ghoul** ×6: AC 6 [13], MV 90' (30'), HD 2, hp 13, 12, 11, 10, 9, 8, Att 3 × claw/claw/bite (1-3/1-3/1-6), THAC0 18 [+1], SV D14 W16 P15 B17 S17 (F2), ML 9, AL CE; XP 91, 89, 87, 85, 83, 81. SA touch paralyzes.

**Treasure:** Box contains old cloak (no value), 263 cp, and a 6" tall finely carved **ivory statuette** (worth 85 gp).

These creatures, like their fellows, are allowed free run of this sector as a first line of defense. (Temple also hopes to build a contingent of ghoul hordes for future war on Good.)

### 116. Room, 30' × 20'

> A ten-foot alcove is in the northeast corner. Bones are heaped in a pile in the alcove, and a disgusting odor lingers. Torn cloth and old cushions form two tangled nests of bedding near the south door. A large iron-bound chest with a heavy padlock stands in the southeast corner.

Six **ghouls** reside herein; move to aid fellows as needed. If necessary, one goes to area 117 for aid while five attack.

### 117. Room, 20' × 20'

A battered table and two stools occupy the center. Mounds of cloth line the walls. A water barrel is near the south door, and a full sack near the north door.

Two **ghasts** live here. Tend to flee from a lost cause or join a winning fight. When fleeing, head for area 107 (via areas 103-106).

### 118. Hall, 10' × 30'

*(PDF p.48–49)*

**Giant Poisonous Snake**: AC 5 [14], MV 150' (50'), HD 4+2, hp 21, Att 1 × bite (1-3 + poison), THAC0 15 [+4], SV D13 W15 P14 B16 S16 (F4), ML 8, AL N; XP 495. SA bite = save vs. poison; 3d6 damage if made, death if failed. Attack range 8'.

### 124. Room, 20' × 20'

*(PDF p.50)*

> This place appears to have been a visitors' chamber. Plastered walls are broken and smoke-stained. Several couches shoved together form a huge bed in NE corner. Empty sacks on floor, nearly full barrel of ale near west door. Large table and rude chair in NW corner; big yellowish cheese and hard sausages atop table, beside an empty gallon jug.
