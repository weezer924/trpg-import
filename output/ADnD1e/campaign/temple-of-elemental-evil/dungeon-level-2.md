## Room Key

### 201. Great Bronze Gate

*(PDF p.59)*

> The passage is barred by a huge sheet of bronze, the surface covered with bas relief faces of evil leering creatures. It is evidently placed to bar further progress north, though 10' wide corridors lead east and west. This portal is too massive to even attempt to raise by brute strength.

Reached from Dungeon Level One by descending the ramp in area 109. Can be opened by no less than **3 consecutive knock spells** — one each for locks, bars, and gate. Can be raised normally from the northern side using a windlass (see area 211). Not opened in several years.

**Note:** This is NOT one of the four gates protected by the permanent antipathy effect binding Zuggtmoy.

### 202. Domed Chamber

*(PDF p.59)*

> This 40' diameter area has an arched ceiling some 30 feet high. Its polished stones indicate it was once meant for some special purpose, lost now under litter of refuse, bones, and dung. A huge metal ring cemented into the center of the floor, with a heavy iron chain of about 8 feet length fastened to the ring.

A five-headed **hydra** guards this chamber, bound by a leg chain. If any visible creature tries to enter, the hydra hisses loudly and strikes, bringing its keeper from area 203 in 1 round. No treasure.

**Hydra, 5-headed**: AC 5 [14], MV 90' (30'), HD 5, hp 40 (8/head), Att 5 × bite (1-6 each), THAC0 14 [+5], SV D11 W13 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 11, AL N; XP 365.

### 203. Old Storeroom

*(PDF p.60)*

> Boxes, crates, and barrels line the walls of this 30'×20' chamber. Spilled contents — flour, grain, broken crockery — are mixed with old bones, broken furniture, splinters, rags, and ordure. The whole area stinks of troll.

The hydra's keeper, a **troll**, dwells here on orders from the priest of the Fire Temple. Grabs its military fork and investigates any noise from the beast. Those with proper identification (Fire Temple robes or eight-pointed amulet) can pass — troll shoves hydra aside and stops its heads. Otherwise allows beast to attack and aids it.

If enemies close, discards fork and attacks normally (tooth and claw). Wears a **ring of fire resistance**.

**Troll**: AC 4 [15], MV 120' (40'), HD 6+6, hp 42, Att 3 × claw/claw/bite (5-8/5-8/2-12), THAC0 13 [+6], SV D11 W13 P12 B13 S14 (F6), ML 10, AL CE; XP 861. SA military fork (range 10', D 3-10, +2 STR damage). SD regenerates 3 hp/round (starting 3 rounds after damage). Wears **ring of fire resistance**.

**Treasure:** Small cask of 2 gallons fuel oil (~16 gp/gallon), box of 20 fresh torches.

### 207. Pearlescent Room

*(PDF p.62)*

> Floor of white marble, walls and vaulted ceiling sheathed in pearly alabaster. A "right feel" — a place of safety in a storm of evil. Bare and clean. A sheet of cloudy crystal substance set into the northern portion of the east wall begins to brighten! Two manlike creatures with wings of snowy feathers and radiant faces, robed in purest white, appear amidst golden-tinted clouds. A pale blue sky behind them. One speaks in dulcet tones, asking what fair folk are doing in this foul place.

No detect spell other than detect magic works here. The whole room radiates dim magic; a more powerful dweomer from the crystal sheet.

The angelic figures demand the party place all weapons, holy symbols, magic items, silvered items, and magical herbs before the crystal ("Window of the Planes of Weal"), then step out the west door and count to 77. They claim a "Ray of Just Might" will make all items +1, recharge them by seven, or double their efficacy — but flesh would be blasted and eyes blinded.

**This is a deception.** The crystal is a one-way mirror placed by **Iuz** (see Appendix C). Protected by permanent force field — impervious to anything less than a godling.

### 210. Octagonal Chamber (Air Temple)

*(PDF p.63)*

> Illumination seems to come from everywhere — ceiling, walls, floor — a milky radiance giving dim eerie glow. Floor is 15' below normal dungeon level, with short broad stairways. Ceiling vaults to 40'. Walls and floor of polished gray stone with mica whorls; floor partially obscured by swirling, eddying, softly glowing mist.
>
> Center: great pierced square of bronze, 10' on a side. A dome pierced in the northern ceiling, circular shaft ~20' wide over a pit of same diameter and 5' depth. South of pit: alabaster block (2'×4'×8') with two knives and a bowl of finest crystal. Flanking pit: two crystal braziers on silver-chained tripods, emitting faint sickly-sweet perfumed smoke.
>
> Doors to north are bronze, sealed with iron, chained, barred, with warning runes.

If bronze doors in area 145 (Level 1) are sundered, stairs descend to this room, but entry blocked by another set of like doors in north wall (with antipathy effect).

**Altar/Crystal service** protected by glyph of warding — requires Air Temple symbol worn and glyph name "**whah-duh**" spoken. Failure causes whirlwind (pit to altar), 12 damage per creature within 10' (save vs. spells for half), plus alarm chiming that summons the guardian (area 210a).

Two crystal knives worth 250 gp each; bowl worth 1,250 gp.

**Braziers:** If disturbed, pour clouds of dense choking smoke (20,000 cu.ft./round for 11-20 rounds). Heavier than air, builds from floor up. Treat as wall of fog; 5-8 damage/round from smoke inhalation. After 10 rounds, any creature in the pit is **teleported to the Air Node (see `elemental-nodes.md`)**. If holy water poured in: a **Type I demon** summoned, attacks everyone. Other liquid: double smoke volume and damage for 1 round. Each brazier worth **10,000 gp** and functions normally once removed.

Secret door to west opens to steps → area 301 (Level Three).

North doors bear antipathy runes (save vs. spells at -2 or be affected, as with main Temple entrance).

### 212. The Temple of Fire

*(PDF p.64–65)*

> This huge hall of red granite, lit by scores of flambeaux, pervaded by heated metal and sulfur. Great doors of beaten brass reflect leaping flames. Tapestries adorn walls depicting fire in evil form. Air carries motes of rust-colored luminescence; streaks of blue and mica in polished granite appear to leap and dance as if flames.
>
> A brass tube (20'×2') suspended from ceiling by chains. Just north: a shallow fire pit. To either side: low brass stands with many small charcoal blocks. East and west beyond pillar lines: great copper cauldrons flanking the main Temple. Before each vessel: octagonal brass table with 16 bowls and a copper dipper.
>
> At south end: translucent block of golden apatite (3'×3'×9'). Skulls grin from its sides, inlaid with hammered gold. Atop the altar: the eight-pointed fire symbol in wrought gold. Before altar: diamond-shaped fire pit (10'×8'), several feet deep, bottom covered with glowing hot coals and tiny cavorting forms.

If anyone enters by bronze doors, a permanent **unseen servant** slams them shut.

**Charcoal incense in fire pit** → bursts aflame, brass tube (great bell) sounds loudly, summoning priests from area 205.

**Apatite altar (south):** 6 skulls (100 gp each). Wrought gold design pried off (500 gp weight).

**Fire pit:** Anyone entering takes 4-24 fire damage/round (min 1-6 even for touch), no save. With magical protection: no damage 1st round, 1-6/round thereafter (no save).

**Copper cauldrons:** Amber liquid = fine scented oil with continual light. If any material other than copper touches the oil, a **salamander** appears in the cauldron, igniting oil (2-12 fire damage to each creature within 5'), then attacks the defiler.

**Altar touch (before oil poured):** Sheet of flame expands 5' in all directions, 10 fire damage (no save). Magic mouth on central skull bellows "WHO DARES DEFILE THE SACRED ELEMENTAL FIRE?!"

**Oil poured on altar:** Magic mouth commands "faithful now offer major sacrifice." Flames leap higher as miniature salamanders. Must depart or sacrifice (500+ gp treasure or living creature in fire pit). Failure → **16 salamanders** grow to full size in 4 rounds. Only 4 are real; 12 are **mirror images**.

**Salamander** ×4: AC 5/3 [14/16], MV 90' (30'), HD 7+7, hp 44, 41, 40, 37, Att 2 × spear/constriction (1-6/2-12), THAC0 12 [+7], SV D10 W12 P11 B12 S13 (F7), ML 10, AL CE; XP 1265, 1235, 1225, 1195. SA 1-6 heat damage added to each attack. SD +1 or better weapon to hit.

**Hidden treasure (under coals):** Locked iron box (5-8 rounds searching to find, detect magic notwithstanding). **Scythe blade trap** — when any item lifted from bottom, blade cuts through anything (no save; hand removed, only regenerate can replace). Contains: 2 **potions of fire resistance**, **ring of fire resistance**, and a **frost brand longsword +3/+6 vs. fire creatures** (LG, Int 16, Ego 17; speaks Common, fire giant, red dragon, salamander; detect evil, detect magic, detect shifting walls/rooms, levitation).

**Fire mechanics:** Water → only steam/fog (1 round/gallon). Holy water quenches 1 cu.ft. (flames return 1 turn later unless all quenched). Pyrotechnics: coals save at 20 minus caster level. Affect normal fires (enlarge): burns brightly 4 rounds, cool 4 rounds, then reignites.

### 213. Hall of Verdigris (Water Temple)

*(PDF p.65–66)*

> This huge hall (40'×90') with arched ceiling 30' overhead. Floor and walls covered in polished azurite-malachite. Soft cloudy greenish luminosity seeps from walls and floor, making chamber appear deep underwater. Bronze doors, fountain, all covered with verdigris. Damp air smells of the sea.
>
> Middle of west wall: great bronze bas relief of underwater vista. Fish-like head projects, emitting stream of water into tiered series of 4 basins (fourth never overflows — drain system).
>
> Near mid-portion: bronze altar with marine creatures in bas relief, ~4' high, round, 10' diameter. Concave top with shallow basin ~2' deep filled with water — coins, gems, small seashells at bottom.
>
> South of altar: idol of serpentine stone — writhing mass of fish, eels, octopi (~8' high, nearly as broad). Behind it: 20' wide alcove, back wall covered by drapery of "seaweed and water."
>
> Each corner: triangular plinth extending ~20' up, each topped by a gargoyle-like bronze statue coated with verdigris.

Whole place radiates dim magic, pervaded with evil. Proper ritual: dip offerings in fountain basins (highest to lowest) before placing on/in altar. Failure → gargoyles give chase.

**Altar basin (B):** Salt water. Treasure: 42 cp, 37 sp, 60 ep, 51 gp, 23 pp, 39 gems (total 1,920 gp: 17 agates 10 gp each, 3 bloodstones + 6 zircons 50 gp each, 4 amethysts + 3 red garnets + 6 tourmalines 100 gp each). If defiled or anything removed, guardian idol animates.

If sacrifice properly prepared and placed, magic mouth on idol summons area 214 occupants (arrive 2-5 rounds). If improperly handled, idol rolls toward offender, hooting and hissing.

**Juggernaut (idol C)**: AC 2 [17], MV 10'-120' (3'-40'), HD 10, hp 66, Att 4+1 × tentacle/roll (2-12 ×4 and 10d10), THAC0 10 [+9], SV D10 W12 P11 B12 S13 (F10), ML 12, AL CE; XP 3924.
