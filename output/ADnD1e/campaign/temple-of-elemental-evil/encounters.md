## Encounter Difficulty Notes

### Lethal Encounters (Potential TPK)

1. **Zuggtmoy (L3: 353)** — 222 HP, AC 0/-2, 85% MR, regeneration, extensive spell-like powers, gate demons. The campaign's final boss. Without the four power gems or the Orb of Golden Death, the party has almost no chance. Even bound, her corridor traps (blade, poison darts, lightning bolt, green slime) are deadly.

2. **Hedrack (L4: 417)** — AC -4, 66 HP, Cleric 9 with wand of fear, rod of smiting, Daern's instant fortress. The supreme commander fights intelligently and can deploy the fortress offensively. He can summon Iuz's attention via the ebony skull.

3. **Will-o-Wisps (L3: 311)** — Four at once, AC -8, immune to most spells. Only magic missile and a few other spells work. Each hits for 2-16. Combined with 9 statue traps including cloak of poisonousness and dust of sneezing/choking.

4. **Earth Elementals (L1: 145)** — Four 16 HD elementals (69-87 HP each) requiring +2 weapons to hit. Present in the Earth Temple itself. Most low-level parties cannot damage them.

5. **Ooze/Fungi Garden (L3: 349)** — 53+ oozes, slimes, molds, and fungi in one room. Multiple save-or-die effects. Yellow mold spores, green slime conversion, black pudding acid. Avoidance is the correct play.

6. **Pool Creature (L2: 216)** — 100 HP, acid immersion 5-20/rd, suggestion power, limited wish. Extremely dangerous if engaged unprepared.

7. **Falrinth + Smigmal + Kriitch (L3: 336-337)** — Combined MU 8 + F7/A7 + quasit. Explosive runes, chlorine gas trap, Orb of Golden Death. Falrinth holds one of the most important artifacts.

### Scaling Notes

- **Levels 1-3:** Hommlet → Moathouse. The moathouse upper level is appropriate for level 1-2; the dungeon for level 2-3. Lareth (area 35) is a serious boss fight for a level 3 party.
- **Levels 3-5:** Nulb → Temple Exterior → Dungeon Level 1. The Earth Temple forces on Level 1 are appropriate for a level 3-5 party. The 4 earth elementals (area 145) are a level 5+ encounter.
- **Levels 5-6:** Dungeon Level 2. Four rival factions can be played against each other. The salamanders (area 212), juggernaut (area 213), and pool creature (area 216) are the hardest encounters.
- **Levels 6-7:** Dungeon Level 3. Significantly tougher: will-o-wisps, roper, banshee, and the ooze garden. Smigmal + Falrinth are dangerous.
- **Levels 7-8:** Dungeon Level 4 → Elemental Nodes. Hedrack and the gate traps are the main threats. The nodes themselves have dragon-level encounters (HD 10-16 creatures).
- **Level 8+:** Zuggtmoy confrontation. Requires multiple power gems and/or the Orb of Golden Death.
