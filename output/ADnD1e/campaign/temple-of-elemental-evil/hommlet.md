### Area 1. Prosperous Farm Cottage and Large Barn

> The large goodwife is friendly, greeting all who call, while her four children look on. Inside, a young girl and her old granny do various chores. The lintel over the front door is carved with acorns and oak leaves.

**Faith:** Old Faith (druidical). The family will say so if politely asked.

The head of house and his two full-grown sons work in the fields. All three are **militia members**. The elder has a bill-guisarme and ring mail jack; sons have leather armor, shield, flail and axe respectively. Each carries a dagger at all times. They take arms only in self-defense or when called by the village elder. Two farm dogs attack intruders if family is threatened.

**Farm dogs** ×2: AC 7 [12], HD 1+1 (hp 7, 5), Att 1 × bite (1-4), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL N; XP 34, 30.

**Farmer:** AC 7 [12] (ring mail); Level 0 (HD 1-1); hp 6; Att 1 × bill-guisarme (2-8); THAC0 19 [+0]; MV 120' (40'); SV D16 W17 P14 B17 S18 (NM); ML —; AL N; XP 16.

**Sons** ×2: AC 7 [12] (leather & shield); Level 0 (HD 1-1); hp 5, 4; Att 1 × flail (1-8) or axe (1-4); THAC0 19 [+0]; MV 120' (40'); SV D16 W17 P14 B17 S18 (NM); ML —; AL N; XP 15, 14.

**Treasure:** 57 pp hidden in mattress; blue spinel (500 gp) in farmer's pouch.

Nothing else of interest to adventurers. No goods for sale or trade.

### Area 2. Modest Farmhouse and Barn

> Clean but slightly run-down buildings indicate this farm is not very prosperous. However, the stock appears quite healthy and plump. An active lad of 12 or so is doing chores.

An elderly couple (master and mistress) and a servant boy. They have nothing to trade. The elders say their son **Elmo** would be interested in talking with strangers — he's at the inn having a jack of ale. Their other son, **Otis** (they sadly shake their heads), took service with some gentlemen and is away seeking his fortune.

The elderly farmer is a **retired fighter (Level 4)**, captain of the local militia (he will say nothing of it). Equipment: scale mail, shield, longsword, light crossbow in a chest.

**Treasure:** Under rusty nails in a keg in the back shed: 172 ep, 51 gp, 20 pp. Silver service in cupboard worth 1,300 gp.

**Elmo** may (50%) return while party is here, (25%) be encountered on the road. Otherwise found at the Inn.

### Area 13. Trading Post

> Outside the door, a shield and lantern are suspended from chains. The shield is painted, showing a sword and a cheese.

See Map 4 and [detailed description below](#area-13-trading-post-detailed).

Run by **Rannos Davl** (slow, fat, clumsy, placid) and his partner **Gremag** (tall, thin, sharp features, protruding eyes, tends to dither).

Animals for sale in rear barn: mules, draft horses, light riding horses, ponies (2-5 of each), and one medium war horse. War horses take 7-30 days to obtain.

**Pricing:** Sell at minimum 110% of list price (asking more first). Buy at 25% of value, barter up to 40-70%.

A groom and man-at-arms work here (both militia, spying for shopkeepers). They hire out at 2 gp/day if chain mail is purchased here.

### SECRET: Temple Agents

**Rannos and Gremag are servants of the Temple of Elemental Evil.** They report activity in Hommlet to a brigand courier. They aid all evil creatures in Temple service. They desire their agent to accompany the party; if not hired, they track party activities by any means, following personally if warranted. They have a stock of magical devices hidden away.

**C 3. Altar and Sanctuary:** Circular chamber screened by deep green velvet drape. Life-sized statue of St. Cuthbert — smiling, cudgel held high, left hand beckoning. Growing ferns behind the statue. Tree stump pulpits. Bronzewood altar with billets, starbursts, and the Crumpled Hat.

Carved wall panels, painted murals of St. Cuthbert's marvels. Band of holy sayings above for divination (throw sticks on altar):
- "Square corners can be pounded smooth."
- "Thick heads are not made of glass."
- "Salvation is better than smart answers."
- "Evil which cannot be removed must be eliminated."
- "Foolishness can be beaten."
- "Preach quietly, but have a large cudgel handy."

**C 4. Study and Audience Chamber:** Entrance room. Hard benches for visitors, padded chairs by fireplace. Religious tracts and scrolls. Poorbox (emptied after each visitor). Calmert steps out 2-12 minutes, then returns. Contributions expected: ~1-10 gp per level of strongest character. 50% chance chief cleric is unavailable (return later, same routine). Injured/sick/needy not subjected to this.

**C 5. Vestry:** Rest and dressing room for clerics. Steps to upper floor.

**C 6. Kitchen:** Serving woman and husband prepare meals.

**C 7. Meditation Room.**

**C 8. Exercise Room:** Club practice daily.

**C 9. Sacred Trophies Gallery:** Dented helmets, maces, clubs, knobbed cudgels of fame.

**C 10. Cells:** For needy, laity, acolytes, criminals, offenders. Peg, pallet, pail. Barred window, heavy iron-bound door bolted from outside.

#### Upper Floor

**C 11. Church Library:** Many religious and legal works. Hidden in *Legal Affairs in Veluna, 213 to 312 CY*: **scroll of seven clerical spells** (one per level 1-7) and **amulet of life protection**.

**C 12. Upper Hall:** Upholstered chairs and couches for visiting clerics during sermons.

**C 13. Assistant Cleric's Chambers:** Calmert's quarters. Two cots, brazier, furnishings. **C 14. Chief Priest's Chamber:** Comfortably furnished. Fireplace mantle contains hidden cache (known only to Y'dey — see Nulb area 3): bronze neck chain (six rubies 1,000 gp each), amber cudgel (2,500 gp) on chain (7,500 gp), mace +1, scroll of protection from demons. Terjon keeps church funds in locked iron coffer under bed: 546 cp, 317 sp, 88 ep, 102 gp, 16 pp. Of minor contributions, 30% is removed first (20% Terjon, 10% Calmert).

**C 15. Balcony:** About 15' above hall floor, for sermons and addresses.
