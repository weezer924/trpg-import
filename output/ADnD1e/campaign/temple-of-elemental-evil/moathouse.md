## Background

*(PDF p.21–22)*

> A scrub of thorns, thistles, weeds, and shrubs grows thickly along the edge of the track which leads to the ruins. Even the track is mostly overgrown and cluttered with fallen branches and trees. Some game evidently still follows the pathway. Going is slow — over an hour on horseback, two hours on foot. Double the time for the first trip (considerable hacking needed). After two miles, the land sinks and becomes boggy. Tall marsh plants, cattails, and tamaracks grow thickly. Off to the left — the jagged silhouette of the moathouse.

This place was once the outpost of the Temple of Elemental Evil — watchtower and advance base for raids, looting, and destruction. Servants of the Temple were to bring Hommlet and surrounding lands into subjection, then use conquered folk as slaves to build more fortresses.

The moathouse was ignored during the main battle against the Temple. Afterward, a detachment of horse and foot with a siege train came to lay the castle low. The common folk from miles around came to help. The moathouse was surrounded, cut off, and battered into extinction.

The place is now shunned by the people of Hommlet. The track continues past the ruins for seven leagues to Nulb.

### Upper Level Encounter Key

### 1. Pool

The following encounter should challenge a party of 5-8 characters of 1st or 2nd level. Adjust numbers if party is smaller.

Six giant frogs lurk in tall weeds near the pool edge. They surprise on 1-4 (d6) and leap (max range 12") to attack. They are voraciously hungry.

**Mounted characters:** 90% chance horse panics (10% for warhorse). If panic: 50% chance each of throwing rider or plunging into marsh and becoming mired.

**Tongue attack:** Two larger frogs have 12' tongues; smaller frogs have 6' tongues. At 5'+ range, tongue attack at +4 to hit (no damage). Hit pulls victim in (half speed if victim weighs more than frog, gaining second attack on tongue). Small frogs weigh ~100 lbs; larger frogs ~200 lbs.

**Swallow:** On natural 20, frog swallows elf, halfling, or small human. Victim has three tries to escape (edged weapon, hit on 18+). After three failures, victim dies.

**Giant frogs, average** ×2: AC 7 [12], HD 2 (hp 13, 10), Att 1 × bite (1-6), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 30' (10') / Swim 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML —, AL N; XP 70, 64. SA 12' tongue.

**Giant frogs, small** ×4: AC 7 [12], HD 1 (hp 7, 6, 4, 4), Att 1 × bite (1-3), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 30' (10') / Swim 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML —, AL N; XP 29, 28, 26, 26. SA 6' tongue.

**Treasure:** The largest frog has swallowed an amethyst (100 gp).

### 2. Rotting Drawbridge

When the moathouse was taken, part of the drawbridge was battered through. The chains were broken and it fell; it has remained thus since. Strong enough for a man on foot. Mounts may break through: horse 50%, mule 30%, pony 20%. Injury reduces mount to 2/3 hp (permanent unless magically cured), movement and encumbrance to half.

### 3. Broken Gates

> The door is hanging open on one great hinge; the other is splintered and holed but still in position, wedged and shored closed from the inside.

Careful examination of the ground reveals boot prints heading for the building steps (area 5). 50% chance a pair of brigands (from area 7) are here, see the party, and retreat to give warning. Party is unaware unless using invisible scouting.

### 4. Tower

> The upper portion of the tower has collapsed, and the interior is dark.

Even if an arrow slit is peered through, infravision will reveal nothing. The occupant lurks above. A scattering of husks and a few bones lie on the floor, possibly giving warning to the wary, but the gleam of coins therein may tempt investigation.

A great wolf spider crouches on remains of an upper floor (~15' overhead). It leaps (range 3") and bites as soon as anyone enters. Surprise on 1-5 (d6).

Amidst the rubbish: 71 cp, 38 sp, and an ivory box about the size of a large book (50+ gp).

**Huge spider** ×1: AC 6 [13], HD 2+2 (hp 14), Att 1 × bite (1-6 + poison, +1 bonus to save), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 180' (60'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML —, AL N; XP 187.

### 22. Torture Chamber

**Secret door in pillar:** Exacting care inspecting the floor reveals blood drops leading south (trail of a sloppy ghoul). Without this clue, 90% likely the secret door goes undiscovered (check once per character). If trail found, normal secret door chances apply (1 in 6, 2 in 6 for elves).

### 24. Large Room — Lubash the Ogre

Devours the slain; tosses wounded/prisoners into room 25.

**Ogre (Lubash)** ×1: AC 5 [14], HD 5+1 (hp 21), Att 1 × bardiche (7-13, i.e. 2d4+5), THAC0 14 [+5], MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 10, AL CE; XP 195.

### 25. Oak Door — Lubash's Pantry

Heavily barred door. Contains two human merchants (mildly tortured) and a badly beaten gnome (intended as Lubash's next meal).

**If rescued:** Merchants promise large rewards — four weeks after release, a caravan delivers 100 sp per merchant to rescuers at Hommlet.

**XP:** 14 per rescued merchant (deduct 50 XP per merchant if slain). Gnome XP: 20.

**Gnome's gift:** A plain iron ring, given to the first person who agrees to free him. Recognized 70% of the time by gnomes within 100 miles as a sign the bearer is an ally (+25% friendliness).

### 26. Undetected Trap

A huge iron grate could fall from 15' above — painted grayish brown to blend with stone ceiling. **Two triggers** are the false doors described as area 28.

### 29. Odd Side Room — Gnolls

Nine gnolls recruited by the New Master (area 35). Attack intruders fiercely, fighting to the death if necessary. **However, 75% likely to stop and listen to a parley offer** — they are dissatisfied (lost six members raiding, unhappy with loot, irate about bugbears' greater status).

### 35. Chamber of the New Master — Lareth the Beautiful

### Lareth the Beautiful (The New Master)

- **Race/Class/Level:** Human Cleric 5
- **AC:** -1 [20] (plate mail +1)
- **HP:** 44
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** mace (1-6, left hand, -2 to hit as secondary) or staff of striking (right hand; staff +3, D 4-9 per charge, 1 charge/hit, or 2 for double, 3 for triple; 20 charges remain)
- **THAC0:** 16 [+3]
- **MV:** 60' (20')
- **SV:** D9 W10 P13 B16 S14 (C5)
- **AL:** CE
- **S** 18 **W** 18 **D** 17 **Co** 16 **Ch** 18
- **Equipment:** plate mail +1, mace, staff of striking (20 charges), phylactery of action (immune to paralysis incl. all hold spells/powers)
- **SD:** immune to paralysis (phylactery of action)
- **Spells:**
  - 1st: command, cure light wounds (×2), protection from good, sanctuary
  - 2nd: hold person (×2), know alignment, resist fire, silence 15' radius
  - 3rd: cause blindness, continual darkness
- **Treasure:** 9 pp, black opal (1,000 gp)
- **XP:** 770

**Tactics:** Aids guards' melee with spells while keeping a defensive posture and commanding troops. Melees if necessary, probably after using spells. Wields mace in left hand, staff of striking in right.

**If seriously threatened:** Offers all non-magical treasures as ransom for his life.

**Aftermath if slain:** Cautious, discreet inquiries in Hommlet by servants of evil. If any of Lareth's possessions turn up, the slayers are revealed. An **assassin of 10th level** (speaks alignment languages of individuals and allies) comes to the village in 5-20 days to eliminate the offenders. Jaroo and Terjon will aid those attacked. If the assassin is killed, nothing further is attempted.

> Lareth the Beautiful is the dark hope of chaotic evil — young, handsome, well endowed in abilities and aptitudes, thoroughly wicked, depraved, and capricious. Whomever harms Lareth had best not brag of it in the presence of one who will inform the Demoness Lolth!

Lareth has been sent to rebuild a force of men and humanoid fighters to gather loot and restore the Temple to its former glory. He is but one of many so charged, but is looked upon with special favor. He and his minions raid far from this area (never nearer than 3-4 leagues), travelling on foot or in traders' wagons from Hommlet. No victims are ever left alive; no trace of men, mounts, goods, or wagons is ever found.

If a situation appears doubtful, he uses bribery and honeyed words. Will gladly accept adventurers into the ranks (but continually tests them). Those who arouse suspicion or show too much promise will be quietly murdered in their sleep.
