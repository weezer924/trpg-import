## Hommlet NPCs

### Temple Agents (Hostile)

#### Rannos Davl (Human Thief 10)

- **AC:** 2 [17] (leather +1, ring of protection +1)
- **HP:** 48
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** shortsword +1 (1-6+1) or dagger +1 (1-4+1)
- **THAC0:** 16 [+3]
- **MV:** 120' (40')
- **SV:** D11 W12 P11 B14 S13 (T10)
- **AL:** CE
- **S** 8 **I** 14 **W** 9 **D** 18 **C** 15 **Ch** 7
- **Equipment:** leather armor +1, ring of protection +1, shortsword +1, dagger +1, medallion vs. crystal balls and ESP
- **Notes:** Co-owner of the Trading Post (area 13). Reports adventurer activity to brigand couriers who relay to the Temple. Presents as an honest, friendly merchant. Will not fight openly unless cornered — prefers to manipulate and inform.

#### Gremag (Human Assassin 7)

- **AC:** -1 [20] (shirt of chain mail +3)
- **HP:** 39
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** dagger of venom (1-4 + save or die)
- **THAC0:** 16 [+3]
- **MV:** 90' (30')
- **SV:** D11 W12 P11 B14 S13 (T7)
- **AL:** CE
- **S** 15 **I** 12 **W** 7 **D** 18 **C** 15 **Ch** 7
- **Equipment:** shirt of chain mail +3, dagger of venom, 3 pinches of dust of disappearance
- **Notes:** Rannos' partner. Quieter and more dangerous. Will assassinate troublesome PCs if given the opportunity (disguised as robbery). The dagger of venom is his preferred method.

#### Zert (Human Fighter 2)

- **AC:** 4 [15] (scale mail + shield)
- **HP:** 12
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** longsword (1-8)
- **THAC0:** 18 [+1]
- **MV:** 60' (20')
- **SV:** D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (F2)
- **AL:** CE
- **S** 16 **I** 13 **W** 9 **D** 15 **C** 11 **Ch** 10
- **Equipment:** scale mail, shield, sword, dagger; medium warhorse and lance in stable
- **Notes:** Temple spy staying at the Inn (Room I 6). Will enthusiastically offer to join the party. Plans to betray them at the worst possible moment — reporting their plans, sabotaging gear, or leading them into ambushes. The traders (area 13) know his identity.

#### Turuko (Human Monk 3)

- **AC:** 8 [11]
- **HP:** 11
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** open hand (1-6)
- **THAC0:** 18 [+1]

- **AL:** LE
- **S** 15 **I** 9 **W** 15 **D** 15 **C** 11 **Ch** 5
- **Equipment:** hand, quarterstaff, dagger
- **Notes:** Stays at Inn Room I 14 with Kobort. Plans to waylay PCs returning from the Moathouse, splitting any treasure. Manipulates Kobort through superiority and promises.

#### Kobort (Human Fighter 2)

- **AC:** 0 [19] (splint + shield, DEX bonus)
- **HP:** 20
- **#AT:** 1, **D:** longsword (1-8)
- **THAC0:** 18 [+1]
- **MV:** 60' (20')
- **SV:** D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2)

- **Notes:** Strong but dim (INT 6). Fell in with Turuko and follows his lead. Not inherently evil — just easily manipulated. Has a heavy warhorse in the stable.

### Key Relationship Notes

1. **Elmo ↔ Otis**: Brothers serving the Viscount of Verbobonc. Elmo in Hommlet, Otis in Nulb. Together they are the party's most important intelligence sources.

2. **Y'dey ↔ Terjon**: Y'dey is the original chief cleric of St. Cuthbert; Terjon replaced her. She's running intelligence from Nulb.

3. **Alrrem ↔ Ashrem**: Brothers. Alrrem (Fire Temple) betrayed Ashrem (Air Temple). Ashrem is now trapped in the nodes, burning for revenge.

4. **Falrinth ↔ Lareth**: Connected through Lolth. Falrinth's quasit Kriitch was a gift from Lolth. Lareth in the moathouse also serves Lolth. This is a secondary power play within the Temple.

5. **Iuz ↔ Zuggtmoy**: Iuz uses the Temple as a power base while Zuggtmoy is imprisoned beneath it. They are nominal allies but Iuz benefits from her continued imprisonment.

6. **Four Temple Factions**: Earth, Fire, Water, Air constantly compete for resources and influence. PCs can exploit these rivalries by playing factions against each other.
