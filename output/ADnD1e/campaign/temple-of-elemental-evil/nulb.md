## Interlude: Hommlet to Nulb

*(PDF p.27–30)*

### Players' Background

After clearing the Moathouse and its evil inhabitants, the party learns that the Temple of Elemental Evil itself remains the source of evil. Nulb lies about half a day's walk east along the High Road (about two hours' ride), and the Temple is hidden in the hills nearby.

The upper works of the Temple were thrown down in the final siege, but the conquerors did not raze the Temple proper — instead sealing its great doors and placing mighty wards. Even the great army could not cleanly deal with what lurked below.

> "The lure of such adventure beckons to you, drawing your party as surely as a lodestone draws iron."

### Players' Historical Notes

*(PDF p.27–28)*

The cult of the Temple was based on the premise that elemental forces are Chaotic and opposed to mankind:

- **Fire** was regarded as the first elemental evil — its penchant for Chaos fit the cult's premise
- **Water** was worshipped as an even more powerful Chaotic Evil force (floods, storms, raging seas)
- **Air and Earth combined** was the epitome — represented by blackness corresponding to the demonic Abyss, regarded as complete negation of matter

The cult grew powerful and rich. Captives were sacrificed (children to fire, men to water, women to the blackest Evil) or enslaved. A deep labyrinth was constructed beneath the Temple. A demon took up abode in the deepest level.

Eventually the neighbors formed a mighty host. Two battles were fought; the Temple was besieged, fell, and its inhabitants put to the sword.

### DM Notes

*(PDF p.28–29)*

- Hommlet is about 30 miles west-southwest of Nulb
- The only ford of Imeryds Run is at Nulb (next nearest: 6 leagues upstream)
- Characters may base in Nulb — buy a deserted structure for 500-800 gp (initial asking price double, slow bargaining)
- A party based in Nulb attracts attention if obviously Good or well-heeled
- Bringing booty to Nulb while weakened invites pirates, brigands, and militia to band together
- Only four points in Nulb are detailed; DM should develop the remainder

### WORLD OF GREYHAWK Setting

The epic adventure formed the basis for a mini-campaign within the larger Greyhawk Campaign. Many veterans began adventuring around Hommlet about ten years ago. Lord Robilar eventually entered the Temple depths, freeing Zuggtmoy. Tenser and associates (including Otis, Burne, Rufus) pursued, but the final result was a draw with a strategic edge for Chaotic Evil.

## Secret History of the Temple

*(PDF p.29–30)*

When the demoness **Zuggtmoy** initially conceived her plan to establish rule on a portion of the Flanaess, she decided to use "Elemental Evil" (which had broader appeal than a cult of fungi). The quadrate system was obvious: four types of Elemental Evil would appeal to a wider base, and the competing temples would check each other while Zuggtmoy retained all real power.

**Iuz** perceived the Temple as a tool for his own ends:
- Help the Temple grow → draw forces of Good away from his own border
- If Good triumphed, Iuz would be unharmed in Dorakaa
- If a draw, Iuz could move his armies down to create a vise of Evil
- If the Temple triumphed, Zuggtmoy would be in his debt

**Zuggtmoy** saw Iuz's magic as useful for expanding her domain. After learning of Lolth's sharp check, she accepted Iuz as an almost-equal partner.

The alliance formed and actually worked — but saved not the Temple. **Zuggtmoy was bound in the dungeons**, but **Iuz remained free** (as he had planned). Iuz occasionally returned hoping to free Zuggtmoy (who would then owe him greatly).

### Nulb Militia

The NE populace can muster militiamen (equivalent to bandits, Level 0):

| Trigger | Number |
|---------|--------|
| Village threatened with destruction | 60 |
| Possibly over 1,000 gp loot | 40 |
| Minor loot and easy battle | 30 |
| Village leaders demand service | 20 |
| Target party is Good | 10 |

Additionally: 5-30 actual brigands in Nulb (turn out if lucrative). 2-12 additional pirates (regardless of whether Tolub's crew is present).

### Coordinated Efforts

Nulb is not isolated groups acting randomly. Serious combat at one place attracts everyone nearby — Waterside Hostel, citizenry (10-40 militia), and Otis/Y'dey/Murfles. The latter three usually do **not** help even Good individuals in public (to protect their cover).

### Mercenaries

5-8 mercenaries present each time a PC enters the Waterside Hostel. All Evil or Chaotic Neutral.

**Table 1 (Normal):** 1-3 CE, 4-5 NE, 6 CN.

**Table 2 (Party in need):** 1 Bandits, 2-3 NE mercenaries, 4-6 N mercenaries.

Led by Level 2 Fighter; if 6+, also a Level 1 "serjeant." Each group has: 2 light crossbowmen, remaining spearmen, leader + serjeant are swordsmen. All carry 1-2 daggers.

**Armor:** Leader/serjeant: chain + shield (AC 4 [15]). Spearmen: ring mail + shield (AC 6 [13]). Crossbowmen: leather + shield (AC 7 [12], AC 8 [11] while firing).

### Assistance and Training

If Otis requests aid, **Burne** (at Hommlet) assists MUs with spells (1,000 gp/spell level):
- 1st: burning hands, magic missile, protection from evil, shield, spider climb, ventriloquism
- 2nd: audible glamer, continual light, levitate, ray of enfeeblement, web
- 3rd: dispel magic, fireball, suggestion, tongues

If Otis reveals the Temple's true power, Burne summons powerful characters from Veluna and Verbobonc. Training time cut 50%.

## Nulb Encounter Key

*(PDF p.32–36)*

> The village of Nulb is filthy, ramshackle, and still fairly active despite the destruction of the Temple. The buildings are of turf, mud brick, crudely hewn logs, and old timbers from boats. The inhabitants are boatmen, fishers, herdsmen, and farmers. No one manages to make much of a living in Nulb.

A small amount of commerce moves along the Low Road and via Imeryds Run (wide and deep enough for small river vessels).

### Area 1. The Waterside Hostel

One of the more substantial buildings — field stone foundation, sawn timber walls, but nonetheless scurvy. Main trade is the tavern room. Rich guests have been known to disappear mysteriously.

Prices are half those of the Welcome Wench (round down).

**Daytime:** 2-3 local folk (Level 0).
**Night:** 14-34 (4d6+10) roistering folk (25% villagers, 25% boatmen/bargefolk, 25% transient bandits/mercenaries, 25% river pirates/Temple spies).
