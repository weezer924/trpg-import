## Introduction

*(PDF p.5)*

Welcome to the WORLD OF GREYHAWK fantasy setting. This adventure unfolds in a small part of that world, at the foot of the Kron Hills not far north of the great Azure Sea. The villages of Hommlet and Nulb squat in the vales between the Archclericy of Veluna, the kingdom of Celene, and Furyondy.

**Starting Characters:** 1st or 2nd level. The group should include at least one of each of the four major classes. Experienced players should start new 1st level characters.

**Required Materials:** AD&D Players Handbook, Dungeon Masters Guide, dice, monster books. The DM should read and become roughly familiar with the entire adventure before starting. If starting before reading the whole, limit the first game session to Hommlet itself.

**Appendix A** gives a list of deities found in the WORLD OF GREYHAWK setting. The DM should review those notes with the players either before starting or at some time early in the characters' careers.

## Player's Background

*(PDF p.5–6)*

### Geography

The Village of Hommlet is situated in the central part of the Flanaess, the known and civilized portion of western Oerik Continent. It lies some 30 leagues southeast of the town of Verbobonc, on the fringe of the territory controlled by the Viscount of Verbobonc, at a crossroads.

- **North:** The mighty Velverdyva River; along its south bank runs the Lowroad. Many days' travel east, on the shores of the Lake of Unknown Depths (Nyr Dyv), lies the great walled city of Dyvers. The village of Sobanwych lies about halfway along the route.
- **Southeast/East:** Miles and miles of the Gnarley Forest, beyond which is the Wild Coast, Woolly Bay, and the Sea of Gearnat.
- **South:** The road forks a league beyond the community — one branch meanders toward the Wild Coast, the other rolls through the lower Kron Hills to the village of Ostverk and eventually southward into the elven kingdom of Celene.
- **West:** Into the gnomish highlands, passing through Greenway Valley about a day's travel distant, onwards to the Lortmil Mountains.

### History of Hommlet

Hommlet grew from a farm or two, a rest house, and a smithy. The roads brought sufficient travelers and merchant wagons to attract tradesmen and artisans. The resthouse became a thriving inn. Prosperity was great, the lord taxed but little, and the land was untroubled by war, outlaws, or ravaging beasts — too much so, in the eyes of some.

### The Rise of Evil

Whether the evil came west from Dyvers or crept up out of the forestlands bordering the Wild Coast, come it did. First a few thieves and odd groups of bandits molesting merchant caravans. Then small bands of humanoids — kobolds, goblins — raiding flocks and herds. Local militia and foresters of the Waldgraf of Ostverk checked but did not stop the spread of outlawry.

A collection of hovels and their slovenly inhabitants formed the nucleus for the troubles. A wicked cleric established a small chapel at **Nulb**, 6 miles distant from Hommlet, on a small river flowing into the Velverdyva. The thickets and marshes around Nulb became lair and hiding place for bandits, brigands, and all sorts of evil men and monsters. The chapel grew into a stone temple as its faithful brought in their ill-gotten tithes.

In but three years, a grim and forbidding fortress surrounded the evil place, and swarms of creatures worshipped and worked their wickedness therein. The servants of the **Temple of Elemental Evil** made Hommlet and the lands for leagues around a mockery of freedom and beauty. Commerce ceased, crops withered; pestilence was abroad.

### The Battle of Emridy Meadows

The leaders of this cancer sought to overthrow the good realms to the north. A great battle was fought. The forces of good won such a slaughter that the walled stronghold of the Temple fell within a fortnight, despite the aid of a terrible demon. The place was ruined and sealed against further return by powerful blessings and magic.

### Current Situation

Life in Hommlet returned to normal. For five years the village and surrounding countryside became richer and more prosperous than ever before. Adventurers came to seek out caches and remote lairs — some found wealth, some never returned. After a time, adventurers stopped coming.

**But then, a year ago, the bandits began to ride the roads again.** The good folk of Hommlet sent word to the Viscount. The news has attracted outsiders to the village once again. Who and what these men are, no one can be quite sure. All claim to be bent on slaying monsters and bringing peace — but lies cloak the true purposes of the malevolent.

## Notes for the Dungeon Master

*(PDF p.5–7)*

### Character Levels and Gear

- Designed for beginning-level play (1st–2nd level)
- Experienced players should start new 1st level characters
- Sharply limit gear and treasure brought to the village
- If the group has had exceptional luck, engineer minor encounters on the way to reduce excess resources

### NPC Agents and Information Flow

> There are wheels within wheels in the Village of Hommlet and the lands around. Behind each person lurks another, the circles growing wider and the figures shadowy but very powerful.

- NPCs noted as agents of various powers send a continuous stream of information to their superiors
- All agents are cautious in their actions, unlikely to be duped or maneuvered
- If agents participate in adventures, or are imprisoned or badly treated, their masters **will** seek to redress matters according to their alignments, personalities, and ultimate goals

### Village Details

- Only some villagers are named; the DM should name the others
- Most local folk are of the **Old Faith** (druidical) and consider the Druid of the Grove (area 24) their spiritual caretaker
- Many inhabitants are **zero-level types**; treat as 1-1 Hit Dice for combat
- Floor plans for most buildings are not given; design them as needed
- Change the map of Hommlet as the game progresses — note destroyed places, add new ones

## Other Adventures

*(PDF p.6–7)*

### Hommlet and Nulb

Develop Nulb in the manner of Hommlet for many natural developments. NPC adventurers will arrive with regularity — some as hirelings or mercenaries, some with rival parties. As the local situation changes, noteworthy events may occur:

- Temple factions may decide on a premature foray into the countryside
- Someone might arrive to replace **Lareth the Beautiful** (of the ruined moathouse)
- Evil elemental forces could be sent forth from the dungeons as a probe
- The King of Furyondy might send reinforcements
- The druidical leaders might counter with their own elemental powers

### Kron Hills

Nearly 20,000 gnomes live here. They were instrumental in driving humanoid hordes from the Lortmil Mountains and in the overthrow of the Temple a decade ago. The enclaves should not be invaded, but other caves and ruins are scattered in the region — a natural setting for side dungeons.

### Velverdyva River

The river flows eastward from the Yatil mountains through Veluna, past Verbobonc and quite near Nulb (fed by the Imeryds Run), continuing through the Gnarley Forest into the Nyr Dyv. One of its sources is in the far northern Burneal Forest, in the land of Blackmoor itself. A boating jaunt can encounter river pirates and aquatic encounters. Low-level characters should be discouraged; level 5+ might find other arms of Chaos and Evil lurking in the waters.

### Verbobonc

A city of 11,600 (Viscounty total pop. 35,000). Source of new adventurers, better equipment, greater information. The spies of the rising Temple are here also, following those who seem to be on their mistress's trail.

## Secret History of the Temple

*(PDF p.28–29)*

When the demoness Zuggtmoy initially conceived the plan to establish her own rule on a portion of the Flanaess, she decided to use as many "tools" as possible, just as did her competitor (Lolth). Consulting some of her clerical servants, Zuggtmoy decided that Elemental Evil would have more appeal than a cult dedicated to her beloved fungi. Recruiting was not difficult, and soon the rudiments of the Temple of Elemental Evil were flourishing.

### The Quadrate System

The beauty of the quadrate system was obvious to Zuggtmoy; each type of Elemental Evil would appeal to a broader base of servants and followers, and certain ones would actually serve her directly. The four separate elemental temples would also compete, spreading evil more effectively and also serving to check one another if any grew too powerful — Zuggtmoy, of course, desiring to retain all real power in her own grasp.

### The Alliance with Iuz

At this crucial juncture, Iuz himself requested an audience with Zuggtmoy. The Old One, long ruling a realm of his own, perceived the Temple of Elemental Evil and Zuggtmoy as tools to be used for his own ends. His reasoning:

- Help the Temple grow and ravage the lands around → draw hosts of Good away from his own borders
- If Good triumphed, Iuz would not be harmed — he could retire to Dorakaa, none the worse
- If the battle were a draw, Iuz could move his own armies into Furyondy to put Good in a vise
- If the Temple triumphed, Iuz could call upon Zuggtmoy to repay his aid
- A strong Chaotic Evil ally against the growing power of the Horned Society could be most useful

Zuggtmoy in turn saw the advantage of having Iuz's magic to aid her in building her fortress and expanding her domain. She would encourage Iuz to crush the Horned Society, leaving herself as the great Evil power in the Flanaess, ready to absorb the Wild Coast and Pomarj. And so the alliance formed, and actually worked, but saved not the Temple.

### After the Fall

Zuggtmoy was bound in the Temple's dungeons, but Iuz remained free (just as he had planned). After the mighty magicks had been wrought to bind Zuggtmoy — powers which most certainly involved great wizards, high priests, and deities — Iuz determined to occasionally return to the Temple in hopes of gaining advantage. If he could somehow free Zuggtmoy, she would certainly be much in his debt and seething with a lust for revenge against those who imprisoned her.

It was Iuz who helped the remnants of the Temple's followers to remain viable, but he lacked the power to forge them into a useful tool. The weakened elemental temples fought more amongst themselves than with the forces of Good pressing their advantage above. Some followers remained faithful to the demoness despite not knowing where she was or how to free her. Some actually turned to **Lolth** in desperation, for many drow visited the Temple after its fall. Lareth was one who sought to serve both the Temple and Lolth.

### The Seven Factions

The Temple is thus factioned into no less than **seven competing groups**:

1. **Earth Temple** — serves Elemental Evil (earth)
2. **Air Temple** — serves Elemental Evil (air)
3. **Fire Temple** — serves Elemental Evil (fire)
4. **Water Temple** — serves Elemental Evil (water)
5. **Iuz's followers** — serve the Old One's interests
6. **Lolth's followers** — drow and their allies (weakened since Lolth's defeat)
7. **Zuggtmoy loyalists** — still faithful to the imprisoned demoness

Woe to the disloyal should the Lady of Fungi ever regain her power!

## Main Quest Flow

1. **Hommlet (Levels 1-2):** Arrive at the village. Explore, gather information, recruit hirelings. Discover rumors about the ruined Moathouse. Uncover hints of Temple agents operating in the village.
2. **Moathouse (Levels 2-3):** Explore the ruined outpost. Fight bandits, monsters, and undead. Confront Lareth the Beautiful in the dungeon level. Find evidence of the Temple's resurgence.
3. **Interlude:** Return to Hommlet. Report findings. Learn of Nulb and the history of the Temple. Advance to higher levels through training.
4. **Nulb (Levels 3-5):** Explore the evil village. Deal with hostile NPCs, thieves, and Temple supporters. Gather intelligence on the Temple.
5. **Temple Exterior (Levels 4-5):** Approach the ruined Temple. Clear the Broken Tower, Building Ruins, and Temple upper works.
6. **Dungeon Levels 1-4 (Levels 4-8):** Delve progressively deeper. Navigate factional conflicts between Earth, Air, Fire, and Water temples. Face increasingly powerful enemies.
7. **Elemental Nodes:** Enter the four demi-planes. Survive hostile environments. Gather the means to confront Zuggtmoy.
8. **Finale:** Confront the demon queen Zuggtmoy. Potentially interact with Iuz and St. Cuthbert. Resolve the fate of the Temple.
