## Players' Description

*(PDF p.35–36)*

> The road leading from Nulb to the Temple is rutted and spotted with rank weeds — thistles, burrs, thorns, nettles. Some foot and animal traffic has been using the track, but it is not a busy thoroughfare. (Most traffic avoids using the road, so as not to leave a discernable path.)
>
> As you approach the Temple area, the vegetation is disconcerting — dead trees with a skeletal appearance, scrub growth twisted and unnaturally colored, all unhealthy and sickly looking or exceptionally robust and disgusting. The ruins of the Temple's outer works appear as dark and overgrown mounds of gray rubble and blackish weeds. Skulls and bones of humans and humanoids gleam white here and there.
>
> A grove of stunted, unhealthy usk trees still grows along the northern end of the former compound, and a stump of a tower juts up from the northeast corner of the shattered wall. The leprous gray Temple stands intact, its arched buttresses somehow obscene with their growth of climbing vegetation.
>
> Everything surrounding the place is disgusting. The myriad leering faces and twisting, contorted forms writhing and posturing on every face of the Temple seem to jape at the obscenities they depict. The growth in the compound is rank and noisome. Thorns clutch, burrs stick, and crushed stems either emit foul stench or raise angry weals on exposed flesh.
>
> Worst of all is the pervading fear which seems to hang over the whole area — a smothering, clinging, almost tangible cloud of vileness and horror. Sounds seem distorted. Your eyes play tricks — darting movements at the edge of vision, but nothing when you look.
>
> There is no doubt; you have come to a place of ineffable Evil. Still, it is most certainly a place for high adventure and untold treasures.

## Exterior Notes

*(PDF p.36)*

- All ground in and around the place is overgrown by weeds; observation restricted to 15' in any direction for objects 6' or shorter, except on road/path remains
- **Uskfruit:** Pale blue, small, misshapen, splotched with red patches. **1 in 6 is poison** — consumer grows sick and dies in 24 hours. First effect: lose 1 STR about 2 hours after eating; lose 1 STR each hour thereafter; 0 STR = coma

Three areas of the Outer Works are described:
- **A. The Broken Tower** — northeast corner, brigand stronghold
- **B. Building Ruins** — northwest, concealed by woods
- **C. The Temple** — the main edifice, still standing

## A. The Broken Tower

*(PDF p.36–39)*

### Tower Exterior

Located at the northeast corner of the Upper Works.

> One heavy door can be seen, barred and chained shut from the outside. Arrow slits are visible on either side of the door, but peering in reveals only blackness beyond.
>
> The jagged stub of a large tower stands up from the razed walls. Two small black birds are perched on the tower top, about 25 feet up.

An **illusion** on the tower top makes all creatures thereon appear smaller. The "birds" are actually two of 16 **giant ravens** which serve as guards and sentries for the brigands. Most ravens roost on timbers where the third storey collapsed.

The bar on the door is a ruse — it can be disengaged from inside. From outside, one must break the lock, free the chain, and slide the bar.

### General Notes

- Four guards on duty at all times, each at an arrow slit
- Two ground-level slits screened by heavy black felt (peering in shows only darkness)
- Low stone walls inside channel entrants through an 8' wide passage
- Tower cannot be entered from above (2nd/3rd storeys collapsed)
- Interior lit by a dozen torches in sconces
- **26 brigands reside here**

### Tower Garrison

**Giant ravens** ×16: AC 4 [15]; HD 3+2; MV 30' (10') / Fly 180' (60'); Att 1 × beak (3-6); THAC0 16 [+3]; SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F3); ML 8; AL N(E); XP 60+4/hp. Croak warning to guards if intruders sighted.

**Footmen** ×8: AC 6 [13]; Level 0; hp 6 each; MV 120' (40'); Att 1 × spear (1-6) or throwing spear or longsword (1-8); THAC0 19 [+0]; SV D16 W18 P17 B20 S19 (NM); ML 7; AL CE; XP 20 each. Front rank (4): ring mail, spear + throwing spear. Rear rank (4): studded leather + shield, spear + longsword.

**Crossbowmen** ×4: AC 6 [13] (scale mail); Level 0; hp 4 each; MV 120' (40'); Att 1 × heavy crossbow (2-5) or battle axe (1-8); THAC0 19 [+0]; SV D16 W18 P17 B20 S19 (NM); ML 7; AL CE; XP 18 each.

**Archers** ×4: AC 5 [14] (chain mail); Level 0; hp 5 each; MV 90' (30'); Att 2 × shortbow (1-6/1-6) or 1 × shortsword (1-6); THAC0 19 [+0]; SV D16 W18 P17 B20 S19 (NM); ML 7; AL CE; XP 19 each.

**Guards** ×5: AC 5 [14] (chain mail); Level 0; hp 5 each; MV 90' (30'); Att 2 × shortbow (1-6/1-6) or 1 × shortsword (1-6); THAC0 19 [+0]; SV D16 W18 P17 B20 S19 (NM); ML 7; AL CE; XP 19 each.

**Sergeants** ×2: AC 4 [15] (chain + shield); Level 1 Fighter; hp 11, 9; MV 90' (30'); Att 1 × throwing spear (1-8) or longsword (1-8) or dagger (1-4); THAC0 19 [+0]; SV D14 W16 P15 B17 S17 (F1); ML 8; AL CE; XP 25, 23.

**Lieutenants** ×2: AC 2 [17] (plate mail, DEX 15); Level 3 Fighter; hp 22, 17; MV 60' (20'); Att 1 × heavy crossbow (2-5) or morning star (2-8) or spear (1-6) or dagger (1-4); THAC0 18 [+1]; SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F3); ML 8; AL CE; XP 116, 101.

### Defense Strategy

If party approach is seen (by guards or ravens):
1. All but two torches immediately extinguished
2. Missile-armed brigands cover the door
3. Shield-and-spear wall forms two ranks deep, 5' NE of entryway
4. Behind missiles: one 1st and one 3rd level brigand on each flank
5. Behind ranks: 6th level leader and one 1st level brigand stand ready
6. Stairway guards remain at arrow slits for targets of opportunity

**When door is attacked:**
- Remaining torches extinguished
- Lanterns in interior rooms remain lit (not detectable)
- When door opens: arrows, bolts, and spears fired at illuminated/silhouetted targets

**Combat Advantages (if party was observed):**
- Only intruders can be surprised
- Brigands gain automatic initiative in round 1
- Brigands get +2 to hit with all missiles vs. illuminated/silhouetted targets (6 arrow shots, 2 heavy crossbow bolts, 4 light crossbow bolts, 10 spears per round)
- -1 penalty to all intruder attack rolls until interior illuminated; +1 bonus for brigands (familiarity with dark area)

**Rockfall trap:** If seriously threatened, the leader moves to south-central pillar and releases a chain, triggering several hundredweight of rocks. Each victim in the entry area takes 1-12 damage, is stunned 1 round, and blinded by choking dust (settles in 2 rounds). Leader then flees to area 3 and the escape tunnel.

### Tower Area Descriptions

#### A1. Main Room

Little besides rude tables, benches, chairs, stools. Heaps of old blankets and brown cloaks. Sacks of foodstuffs. Bottles of cheap wine, knucklebones, dirty plates/mugs. Smoked meats and sausages in rafters. Provisions for one full week.

Under the spiral staircase: five large barrels (two water, two beer, one ~40 gallon nearly full of cheap wine). Bale of blankets stuffed under stairs (merchant loot).

#### A2. North Room

Quarters for two lieutenants and three sergeants. Five rough beds, round table, three stools, chest of drawers with lantern (old clothes inside).

**Padlocked iron-bound oak chest:** potion of healing, sack of 300 ep, jeweled dagger (250 gp), four bolts of silk (10-60 gp each).

#### A3. Northeast Room (Leader's Chamber)

Comfortable bed with quilts and pillows, table, two chairs, small desk and stool, chest of drawers, padlocked iron box.

**On table:** Pewter dishes, bottle of good brandy, slightly wrinkled apples.

**Desk:** Quills, inkwell, parchment. One sheet lists possible victims (including the jeweller at Hommlet). Another sheet, held to strong light, reveals a **map showing the secret tunnel** out of the tower — and into Dungeon Level Three (area 335).

**Chest of drawers:** Shortsword with topaz pommel (500 gp), suit and hooded cloak of brown velvet trimmed with fur (200 gp), normal clothing.

**Iron chest (poison needle trap):** 384 cp, 556 sp, 106 ep, 277 gp, 91 pp.

**Secret trap door:** One flagstone lifts to reveal a bronze ring. The pivot is undetectable by normal sight; flagstone locks in place. Mechanically linked to counterbalanced trap door beneath. Shaft ~3' square, bronze rungs lead 30' down to a 10'×15' chamber containing:

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Small flat silver box | Worth 125 gp, contains jeweled necklace (2,400 gp) |
| Cloak of elvenkind | Hanging on peg by passage entrance |
| Large shoulder pouch | 50 tiny pearls (10 gp each), leather bag with 50 pp |
| Longbow + quiver | Nine arrows +1 |
| Locked bronze coffer | Potions of speed, extra healing, and water breathing |
| Locked iron box | 800 gp and 200 ep |

**Fleeing leader:** Immediately dons/takes all but the last two items. 75% chance drinks extra healing; 75% drinks speed if he hears pursuit; 75% ignores water breathing. Flees via west passage, visits Nulb at night, then leaves the area on horse.

#### A4. Escape Tunnel

5' wide passage hewn from bedrock, following a natural fissure. Bears generally west with many curves; width varies 4-8' in unworked sections. About 540' along, opens into a small natural cave (40' oval chamber).

**North wall:** 3' wide opening → leads north then gradually west, ascending ~200'. Turns north for 100' more. Limestone gives way to clay walls shored with timbers for 50'. Exit: side of an abandoned dry well. Small heavy door painted dull brown, barred from inside. Ladder set up inside shaft to ascending ledges.

Near the exit: deserted cottage and ramshackle barn (screened by trees). Barn contains **six light horses** belonging to brigand leaders (fed and exercised each night).

**South wall (secret):** Balanced, pivoting stone slab conceals 3' wide passage leading 600' south, slanting steeply downward to **Dungeon Level Three, area 335**.

## B. Building Ruins

*(PDF p.38)*

Northwest of the Temple, concealed by dense woods.

> The outlines of this place are discernable even though the whole is tumbled, burned, and broken. Broken bricks, charred beams, and broken plaster heaped in the shell of the lower storey. Parts of a narrow outside stairway on the west side can be seen through the rubble, leading to a dark, small opening.

No evidence of heavy traffic; only a few rat tracks. The stair leads to a cellar. Anyone crawling in is attacked by 12 giant rats.

**Combat restrictions:** All shield and DEX protection lost (area too cramped). Rats attack crawling victims at +4 to hit. If retreat is immediate, rats don't follow. Otherwise, additional waves (3-18 rats per wave, 2 hp each) attack on rounds 3, 7, and 12.

**Giant rats** ×12: AC 7 [12]; HD 1/2 (hp 3 each); Att 1 × bite (1-3 + disease 5%); THAC0 19 [+0]; XP 10 each.

**Cellar:** Nothing but litter and filth. Old mat in NE corner covers a trapdoor to a **hidden wine cellar** (20' square room):
- Dozens of barrels of cheap (sour but drinkable) wine
- 68 stoneware containers of very excellent wine (1-6 gp each, or 3 gp each in bulk)
- Keg of very ancient brandy (400 gp in Hommlet or anywhere except Nulb)

## C. The Temple

*(PDF p.39–42)*

### First Impressions

The oddly peaked and gabled roof sets the viewer's teeth on edge. Gruesome visages glare from every surface — projecting ornaments, supports, bas-reliefs.

**Dimensions:**
- North-south: over 450'
- East-west: nearly 450' at widest
- Walls: ~30' high, supported by arched buttresses and pilasters
- Roof peak: ~20' above walls
- Total height: ~50'
- Only three doors visible, all at the south end
- Narrow windows, barred with bronze

### Sealed Doors

*(PDF p.39–40)*

Four pairs of huge bronze doors exist within the Temple:
1. **Grand Entrance** (Upper Works area 1)
2. Dungeon area 145
3. Dungeon area 210
4. Dungeon area 352

Each pair is held fast by huge iron chains with all cracks sealed in soft iron. Graven upon each portal are **magical runes which glow with silvery radiance**.

**These portals are the binding forces confining the demoness Zuggtmoy to Dungeon Level Three.** If one or more pairs are sundered, Zuggtmoy gains freedom and additional powers (see area 353 and Appendix B).

**Antipathy effect:** Any character approaching within 10' must save vs. spells at -2. Failure: cannot approach within 10' of those doors (or any similar portals in the dungeon) and cannot bear to look upon the runes. Success: must save again each time approaching within 10' of the same or similar portals.

**Breaking the doors:** Impossible for normal strength or most magic. Requires STR 20+:

| STR | Chance to Open |
|-----|---------------|
| 20 | 1-3 on 1d6 |
| 21 | 1-4 on 1d6 |
| 22 | 1-4 on 1d6 |
| 23 | 1-5 on 1d6 |
| 24 | 1-7 on 1d8 |
| 25 | 1-9 on 1d10 |

### Temple Area Descriptions

#### 1. Grand Entrance

> The bronze doors of the entry (23' high, 20' wide) are held fast by huge iron chains, and all cracks are sealed with soft iron. Graven upon these massive valves are runes. The writing glows and seems to burn with silvery radiance, making your eyes teary.

Side doors (bronzewood, east and west): heavy and thick but can be broken. Each is locked and barred; 50 points of damage effects entry. Windows are high, narrow (no passage for armored man), barred and spiked with bronze.

#### 2. Vestibule

> The floor is reddish brown slate-like stone squares, each about 2' square. The walls are plastered and painted with scenes befitting the Temple's nature — acts of evil, depicted in disgusting detail. Evil is flaunted and lionized. Dim light filters through stained glass windows, casting revolting colors upon the greenish stones of the floor to the north. The nave of the Temple can be seen ahead. The pillars to either hand are of a pinkish mineral, shot through with worm-colored veins.

The arches lead to unremarkable lesser side aisles. Columns and arches worked in bas relief with vile scenes. This area was reserved for the lowliest worshippers.

#### 3. Central Altar

Pillars of white marble veined with ugly red. Altar block of pinkish white marble, roughly oval (7'+ long × 5' wide). Top has a hollowed depression resembling a human form (legs apart, arms away from body), stained darker.

**Well:** Circular, marble-lined pit 20' diameter, just north of altar. Shards of broken crystal vessels scattered about. Crystal knife with broken blade atop the stone block.

Careful examination reveals a 1' wide ledge beginning 15' down. It circles the northern face, leading to narrow steep steps spiraling clockwise from the east. **Shaft is 85' deep.** Leads to Dungeon Level One (area 131) and Dungeon Level Two (area 210).

#### 4. East Altar

Thick stone columns of deep green with blood-red striations. Paving blocks of mossy green. Circular dais ~20' diameter, two-tiered (each tier 18" high; inner tier 14' diameter). Lower disc: greenish black stone. Upper: blackish green. Center depression ~8' across, filled with **scummed-over black liquid** (dirty water, 6' deep). Several pieces of smashed shells and a broken bronze knife scattered about.

If pool is dragged: small, delicate skeleton discovered at bottom (the last sacrifice).

#### 4a. East Vestry

Broken rhondite bowl and ewer in a corner. Under a thrown-down wardrobe: **three moss-green robes** lined with black satin, each with cowl. Soiled but in good condition, of little worth. Flight of stairs descends to Dungeon Level One, SE corner (area 109a).

#### 5. West Altar

Sandstone supporting pillars on red slate floor. Bits of broken pottery and sharp rock cover the floor (walking is risky). Stump of granite monolith with chunks of brownish-red rock at its base — altar was violently assaulted and destroyed. A few links of bronze chain, twisted manacle, and bronzewood maul with snapped haft.

#### 5a. West Vestry

Pieces of broken altar service, broken trident, pieces of torn scorched moss-green robes. Smashed benches, broken table. **Two brown robes** (frayed but intact). Normal candles (~3" long). Steps lead down to Dungeon Level One, SW corner (area 109a).

#### 6. Small Door (Vestry)

Pieces of broken glass and crystal splinters. Fire remnants in far corner (charred wood/cloth, blackened rafters). Pile of soiled ivory-colored robes on floor.

If rafters are examined (10' above): an intact **ivory robe** with cowl, edged and embroidered with reddish-pink whorls, lined with pale blue satin.

#### 7. Grand Staircase

> A flight of steps, 20' wide and each step broad and tall, delves down to the north. The stone is dull gray but flecked with color — white, blue, red, green, and black. North of the staircase is a stone railing with supports of white, brown, and green stone alternating; its upper portion is cinnabar. The floor beyond is paved with 3' squares of highly polished red granite. Square columns of yellowish stone are carved in bas relief, painted to show scenes of fire and suffering with demonic creatures leering on.

At the bottom: a 10' landing and a pair of massive double doors duplicating those at the entrance (area 1). **Sealed with soft iron, chained, padlocked, bearing silvery glowing runes.** Same antipathy effect applies.

These doors lead to **Dungeon Level One**.
