## The Keep — Key NPCs

### Castellan
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, 6th level fighter, commander of the Keep
- **Location:** Area 27 (Castellan's Chamber) / Area 24 during day
- **Attitude:** Clever, sometimes hasty, absolutely brave and honest
- **Key Info:** Most powerful person in the Keep. AC −3 [22]. Sword +2, dagger +1, elven cloak and boots. Will answer questions if they don't compromise Keep security. May assign special missions. Can send military support (up to sergeant + 12 men-at-arms).
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 27

### Curate
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, 5th level cleric, spiritual leader
- **Location:** Area 17 (Chapel)
- **Attitude:** Influential, devout, protective
- **Key Info:** Most influential after Castellan. AC 0 [19]. Snake staff + mace +1. Spells: cure light wounds, detect magic, bless, hold person. Guards potions of healing and other magic items in secret compartment under offering box. Distrusts the Evil Priest (7b) — 50% chance of revealing this to a friend.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 17

### Evil Priest (Spy)
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, 3rd level cleric, **chaotic evil spy**
- **Location:** Area 7b (Private Apartments) / sometimes in Tavern (10% chance)
- **Attitude:** Jovial, outspoken against evil (facade), excellent listener
- **Key Info:** **TRAITOR.** Poses as friendly priest. Two silent "acolytes" are also evil. Will eagerly join expeditions to the Caves, then betray the party during a crucial encounter. Has cause light wounds (offensive) and light (blinding) spells. Plate mail +1, shield +1, mace +1 (AC −1). Carries bribe money. The Curate distrusts him; the Chapel Acolytes think highly of him.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 7b

### Captain of the Guard
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, 3rd level fighter
- **Location:** Area 18 (Inner Gatehouse)
- **Attitude:** Very kind, friendly, excellent leader
- **Key Info:** AC 0 [19]. Plate mail +1, shield +1, sword +2, spear +1. Sometimes disguises as a mercenary in the Outer Bailey. 150 gp gem in dagger pommel.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 18

### Sergeant of the Guard
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, 2nd level fighter, Str 17
- **Location:** Area 18 (Inner Gatehouse) / sometimes in Tavern (10% chance)
- **Attitude:** Hard fighter, loves to drink and brawl
- **Key Info:** AC 2 [17]. Chain mail, shield +1, ring of protection +1. Sword +1, dagger +1. +2 to hit, +2 damage from Strength. Potion of healing hidden in chest.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 18

### Captain of the Watch
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, 3rd level fighter
- **Location:** Area 12 (Watch Tower) / sometimes in Tavern (10% chance)
- **Attitude:** Dutiful
- **Key Info:** AC 2 [17]. Dagger +1, sword +2. Silver flagon and tankard worth 750 gp. Carries 20 pp.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 12

### Corporal of the Watch
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, 2nd level fighter
- **Location:** Area 3 (Entry Yard) / sometimes in Tavern (10% chance)
- **Attitude:** Grouchy, low charisma, admires brave fighters, easily taken in by pretty girls
- **Key Info:** AC 2 [17]. Sword +1.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 3

### Advisor (Elf)
- **Species/Identity:** Elf, 3rd level
- **Location:** Area 26 (Central Towers)
- **Attitude:** Dutiful, well-appointed
- **Key Info:** AC 0 [19]. Plate mail +1, Dex 16. Ring of fire resistance. 10 arrows +1. Spells: charm person, read magic, web. Jeweled pendant (1,000 gp). Valuable tapestry (500 gp) in room.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 26

### Scribe (Cleric)
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, 2nd level cleric
- **Location:** Area 26 (Central Towers)
- **Attitude:** Austere
- **Key Info:** AC 2 [17]. Plate + shield. Scroll: hold person. Spell: light (blinding). Gold holy symbol (150 gp). 48 gp in purse.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 26

### Bailiff
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, 3rd level fighter, superintendent of Outer Bailey
- **Location:** Area 6 (Bailiff's Tower) / sometimes in Tavern (10% chance)
- **Attitude:** Dutiful, authoritative
- **Key Info:** AC 1 [18]. Magic plate mail +1 or sword +1 (has one). 3 arrows +1. 50 gp hidden in old boots.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 6

## The Keep — Service Providers

### Jewel Merchant
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, normal man
- **Location:** Area 7a (Private Apartments)
- **Attitude:** Commercial, awaiting caravan
- **Key Info:** Buys gems at 60–90%, sells at 110–140%. 200 pp + 100 gp in iron box. 10 gems (100 gp each) in belt. Wife has valuable jewelry (2,100 gp total). Two F2 guards with war dogs.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 7a

### Taverner
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, normal man
- **Location:** Area 15 (Tavern)
- **Attitude:** Hospitable, talkative (for a drink)
- **Key Info:** Shares stories (1 drink each, 50% true). Hates small beer, loves honey mead. 82 cp, 29 sp, 40 ep, 17 gp hidden in old crock.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 15

### Smith/Armorer
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, 1st level fighter
- **Location:** Area 8 (Smithy)
- **Attitude:** Practical
- **Key Info:** Repairs armor, makes weapons, shoes animals. 11hp. 27 ep hidden in bedroom.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 8

### Provisioner
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, normal man
- **Location:** Area 9
- **Attitude:** Practical
- **Key Info:** Sells standard equipment. Buys at 50%. 100 gp in strongbox.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 9

### Trader
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, normal man, with two sons
- **Location:** Area 10
- **Attitude:** Interested in furs
- **Key Info:** Sells armor/weapons. Buys furs at full value. 500 gp + 1,110 sp hidden under floorboards.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 10

### Banker
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, retired 3rd level fighter
- **Location:** Area 11 (Loan Bank)
- **Attitude:** Professional
- **Key Info:** Exchanges money (10%), stores wealth, makes loans at 10%/month. Has a 2nd level MU clerk (sleep, ventriloquism) and an F1 mercenary guard. Extensive vault with traps.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 11

### Guild Master
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, normal man
- **Location:** Area 16 (Guild House)
- **Attitude:** Very influential
- **Key Info:** His favor/dislike reflected in treatment by fortress personnel. 712 gp in strongbox. 4 fanatical men-at-arms guards.
- **Stats:** See `the-keep.md` Area 16

## Wilderness NPCs

### Mad Hermit
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, 3rd level thief, insane
- **Location:** Wilderness Area 4, hollow oak
- **Attitude:** Deceptive — feigns holiness, then attacks from behind
- **Key Info:** AC 4 [15] (leather, ring of protection +1, Dex 17). +2 to hit and damage from madness. Backstab: +6 to hit, double damage +2. Pet mountain lion lurks in tree above. 31 gp, 164 sp, potion of invisibility, dagger +1 buried under "bed."
- **Stats:** See `wilderness.md` Area 4

### Raider Leader
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, 2nd level fighter, chaotic
- **Location:** Wilderness Area 3, forest camp
- **Attitude:** Hostile, spying on the Keep
- **Key Info:** Commands 11 others. AC 5 [14], chain mail. 2d6 gp plus 3d6 sp.
- **Stats:** See `wilderness.md` Area 3

## Caves of Chaos — Leaders

### Kobold Chieftain
- **Species/Identity:** Kobold, unusually large
- **Location:** Cave A, Area 5
- **Attitude:** Aggressive
- **Key Info:** HD 2, 8hp. Fights with battle axe (2d4). Large gem on golden chain (1,200 gp). Has key to food storage (Area 3).
- **Stats:** See `caves-of-chaos.md` Area 5

### Orc Leader (Cave B)
- **Species/Identity:** Orc, very large
- **Location:** Cave B, Area 12
- **Attitude:** Defiant, fights to the last
- **Key Info:** HD 4, 15hp. Chain mail + shield +1, mace. +2 damage. Ring with gem (700 gp). Secret meeting room with Cave C leader. If slain, all orcs flee to Cave C.
- **Stats:** See `caves-of-chaos.md` Area 12

### Orc Leader (Cave C)
- **Species/Identity:** Orc, very large
- **Location:** Cave C, Area 16
- **Attitude:** Cunning, prepared
- **Key Info:** HD 3, 16hp. Plate mail + shield, sword, magic hand axe +1 (thrown). Silver belt (160 gp). Rope of climbing in chest. If slain, survivors flee to Cave B.
- **Stats:** See `caves-of-chaos.md` Area 16

### Goblin Chieftain
- **Species/Identity:** Goblin
- **Location:** Cave D, Area 20
- **Attitude:** Cunning, uses allies
- **Key Info:** HD 3, 11hp. Chain mail + shield. 18 gp, 2 pp. Can summon ogre (via Area 18). Hidden tapestry worth 900 gp. Goblins flee east to hobgoblins if defeated.
- **Stats:** See `caves-of-chaos.md` Area 20

### Ogre
- **Species/Identity:** Ogre
- **Location:** Cave E, Area 22
- **Attitude:** Mercenary, greedy
- **Key Info:** HD 4+1, 25hp. AC 4 [15] (hide + bearskin). Club 3–12 damage. Mercenary for hire. Can be bribed (≥20 gp, 90% success). Sits on bag of 7 treasure sacks. Hidden: 6 arrows +1, potion of invisibility, cleric scroll.
- **Stats:** See `caves-of-chaos.md` Area 22

### Hobgoblin Chief
- **Species/Identity:** Hobgoblin, great and ugly
- **Location:** Cave F, Area 30
- **Attitude:** Aggressive, well-organized
- **Key Info:** HD 5, 22hp. Plate mail + shield (AC 2). Damage 3–10. Belt (600 gp). False-bottom iron box with treasure. Wand of paralyzation (7 charges) hidden near fireplace.
- **Stats:** See `caves-of-chaos.md` Area 30

### Bugbear Chieftain
- **Species/Identity:** Bugbear, tough old
- **Location:** Cave H, Area 36
- **Attitude:** Cunning, resourceful
- **Key Info:** HD 4+1, 18hp (equal to ogre). 29 pp, 3×50 gp gems. Hand axe +1 (thrown). Knows secret door (escape to Cave I minotaur). Gray chest on high ledge: 1,462 sp, statue (200 gp), 2 potions of healing.
- **Stats:** See `caves-of-chaos.md` Area 36

### Minotaur
- **Species/Identity:** Minotaur, fiendishly clever
- **Location:** Cave I, Area 45
- **Attitude:** Territorial, relentless
- **Key Info:** HD 6, 35hp. Chain mail coat (AC 4), spear +1. Direction confusion spell in labyrinth. Knows area perfectly. Massive hidden treasure behind stone slab: staff of healing, plate mail +1, potions, jewelry.
- **Stats:** See `caves-of-chaos.md` Area 45

### Gnoll Chieftain
- **Species/Identity:** Gnoll
- **Location:** Cave J, Area 50
- **Attitude:** Strong, commanding
- **Key Info:** HD 3, 17hp. Pieces of plate mail (AC 3). Damage 4–10. Silver armbands (100 gp total). 39 gp. Hidden pot: 200 cp, 157 sp, 76 ep, 139 gp. Secret door to Shrine (unknown to gnolls) — dead thief with 12 gems and elven boots.
- **Stats:** See `caves-of-chaos.md` Area 50

### Evil Priest (Shrine)
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, 3rd level cleric, head of the Shrine of Evil Chaos
- **Location:** Cave K, Area 59
- **Attitude:** Calculating, evil, will flee if overwhelmed
- **Key Info:** AC 0 [19] (−1 vs. good). Plate mail +1, shield +1, amulet of protection from good. Snake staff (+1 hit, 2–7, constricts). Spells: cause light wounds, cause fear. Scroll: detect magic, hold person, silence 15' radius. Potion of gaseous form (escape). Gold ring with black gem (1,400 gp). 51 pp. Escape route through wardrobe → secret doors (500 gp + 50 gems spill to delay pursuit).
- **Stats:** See `caves-of-chaos.md` Area 59

## Caves of Chaos — Special NPCs

### Merchant (Prisoner)
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, normal man, plump
- **Location:** Cave F, Area 24 (prisoner #1)
- **Attitude:** Grateful if rescued
- **Key Info:** Scheduled to be eaten. If rescued and returned to Keep: 100 gp reward from Guild, honorary Guild status, 1 year fee exemption.

### Merchant's Wife (Prisoner)
- **Species/Identity:** Human female, normal
- **Location:** Cave F, Area 24 (prisoner #4)
- **Attitude:** Grateful if rescued
- **Key Info:** Also slated for feast. Will reward rescuers with **dagger +1** from her room at the Keep.

### The "Hero" (Prisoner)
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, 4th level fighter, **evil**, berserk
- **Location:** Cave H, Area 41 (prisoner)
- **Attitude:** Dangerous, treacherous
- **Key Info:** Str 18, 24hp. +4 to hit, +3 damage. In combat: 50% chance per round of attacking allies. After battle madness fades: will try to kill the party for treasure, or steal and sneak off if party is too strong.
- **Stats:** See `caves-of-chaos.md` Area 41

### Rebel Bugbear (Prisoner)
- **Species/Identity:** Bugbear, rebel
- **Location:** Cave H, Area 41 (prisoner)
- **Attitude:** Hates his own kind
- **Key Info:** HD 3+1, 14hp. Will fight against all cave inhabitants as long as party stays. Useful temporary ally.

### The Medusa (Prisoner)
- **Species/Identity:** Medusa, recently captured
- **Location:** Cave K, Area 64 (cell)
- **Attitude:** Deceptive — promises to help, plans to petrify
- **Key Info:** Appears as scantily clad maiden needing rescue. HD 4**, 20hp. Poison bite + petrification gaze. Has **potion of stone to flesh** (6 uses) but won't share willingly. If freed, tries to petrify rescuers.
- **Stats:** See `caves-of-chaos.md` Area 64
