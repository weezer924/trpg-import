## Adventure Background

*(PDF p.8)*

The Realm of mankind is narrow and constricted. Always the forces of Chaos press upon its borders. Bold adventurers from the Realm set off for the Borderlands to seek their fortune, carrying the battle to the enemy in a testing ground where only the fittest will return.

The party has journeyed to the **Keep on the Borderlands** — a stronghold of civilization at the edge of the wilds. Using the Keep as a home base, they will equip themselves and venture forth to find the **Caves of Chaos**, where fell creatures lurk.

**Character Levels 1–3.** Designed for 6–9 PCs of 1st level. If fewer than 6 players, the party should hire men-at-arms at the Keep and may receive advice to "stay near the beginning of the ravine area, and enter the lower caves first."

**Module Design:** Sandbox structure — no fixed storyline. The Keep is a safe base; the Caves of Chaos are the primary adventure site. The wilderness between contains additional encounters. Players choose which caves to explore and in what order.

## Factions & Tribal Alliances

*(PDF p.16)*

The Caves of Chaos contain multiple humanoid tribes in an uneasy network of alliances and rivalries.

| Faction | Cave | Relationship |
|---------|------|-------------|
| **Kobolds** | A (Areas 1–6) | Try to be forgotten by all; lowest in the pecking order |
| **Orcs — Tribe B** | B (Areas 7–12) | Rival of Orc Tribe C; secret meetings with C's leader in Area 13 |
| **Orcs — Tribe C** | C (Areas 13–16) | Rival of Orc Tribe B; loose alliance with Gnolls |
| **Goblins** | D (Areas 17–21) | Allied with Hobgoblins; pay the Ogre for mercenary service |
| **Ogre** | E (Area 22) | Mercenary — usually serves Goblins, can be bought off by anyone |
| **Hobgoblins** | F (Areas 23–31) | Allied with Goblins; secretly steal Goblins' supplies via secret door |
| **Shunned Cavern** | G (Areas 32–34) | Gray ooze + owl bear; all other inhabitants avoid this cave |
| **Bugbears** | H (Areas 35–41) | Pick off stragglers from all sides; keep slaves from other tribes |
| **Minotaur** | I (Areas 42–45) | Independent; may ally with Bugbears at cost of one human slave per 3 days |
| **Gnolls** | J (Areas 46–50) | Loose alliance with Orcs |
| **Shrine of Evil Chaos** | K (Areas 51–64) | Evil clerics commanding undead; secret passage to Gnoll lair |

**Tribal Warfare:** Goblins and Hobgoblins fight against Orcs (sometimes with Gnoll allies). Kobolds hope to be forgotten. Bugbears pick off stragglers. PCs may exploit these rivalries to set tribes against each other — a useful tactic for small parties with clever players.

**The Evil Priest at the Keep (Area 7b):** A chaotic spy posing as a jovial priest. He will accompany parties to the Caves, then betray them during a crucial encounter. The Curate (Area 17) distrusts him, but the Acolytes think highly of him. See `the-keep.md` Area 7b.

## Travel Rules

*(PDF p.14)*

**Movement rates on the Area Map:**
- **Searching:** 1 square per hour
- **Walking (road/clear):** 3 squares per hour
- **Walking (forest):** 2 squares per hour
- **Walking (fens):** 1 square per hour

**Provisions:** Characters should bring food and water. They are adventuring, not hunting. If food runs out, hunting success is 1-in-6 per attempt to catch food for 1 day for d6 men.

**Leaving the Map:** If the party tries to leave, redirect them with signs, wandering strangers, talking magpies, or other "helpers."

## Camping Outdoors

*(PDF p.14)*

Nothing will bother the party unless camped within **6 squares** of a numbered encounter area.

| Distance (squares) | Encounter Chance |
|:-------------------:|:----------------:|
| 6 | 1-in-6 |
| 5 | 2-in-6 |
| 4 | 3-in-6 |
| 3 | 4-in-6 |
| 2 | 5-in-6 |
| 1 | 6-in-6 (automatic) |

- If no guards posted → monsters surprise automatically (party sleeping)
- If fire lit → monsters are never surprised (but party may be)

## DM Notes — Caves of Chaos

*(PDF p.16)*

### General Features

- **Woods:** Thick growths, tangled; movement in single file. Possible encounters with stirges, humanoids from nearby caves.
- **Underground:** Caves/passages on different levels; passages slope between contours. Areas roofed by ≥5' of solid rock.
- **Interiors:** Natural or cut from living rock. Rough surfaces, easy for thieves to climb. Small ledges, cracks, holes.

### Ransoming Prisoners

Organized tribes may take PCs prisoner, freeing one to bring ransom (10–100 gp or a useful magic item per prisoner). If ransom is paid:
- Tribe gains 2–12 additional members (permanent)
- Tribe watches for returning adventurers for 1–4 weeks

### Monsters Learning from Experience

- If PCs use flaming oil → monsters will use oil when they find some
- If PCs sneak → monsters set warning devices
- If PCs run from large numbers → monsters use noise ruses to frighten intruders

### Emptied Areas

Cleared areas stay empty for 1–4 weeks, then former inhabitants return or new monsters move in.

## Adventure Flow

This is a sandbox module with no fixed storyline. Typical progression:

1. **Arrive at the Keep** — establish base, buy equipment, gather rumours at the Tavern
2. **Hire men-at-arms** — available at the Tavern (50% chance of 2–5 mercenaries present)
3. **Explore the wilderness** — find the Caves of Chaos (and possibly encounter areas 1–4)
4. **Caves of Chaos** — explore lower/easier caves first (A, B, C, D), work up to harder ones
5. **Return to Keep** — rest, resupply, sell loot, heal
6. **Repeat** — gradually clear caves, potentially setting tribes against each other
7. **Shrine of Evil Chaos (K)** — the ultimate challenge, with evil clerics and undead
8. **Inner Bailey access** — earned through heroic service; Castellan may send the party on missions

**Gaining Access to the Inner Bailey (Areas 18–27):**
- Perform a heroic act for the Keep
- Bring back an exceptional trophy or valuable prisoners
- Contribute a valuable magic item or ≥1,000 gp
- If the Castellan likes the party, he may assign a special mission
- Success grants passes to the Inner Bailey and potential military support
