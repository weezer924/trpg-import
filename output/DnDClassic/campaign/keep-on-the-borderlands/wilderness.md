## 1. Mound of the Lizard Men

*(PDF p.14–15)*

Streams and pools of the fens. Tribe of exceptionally evil lizard men. Nocturnal — unknown to Keep residents. Will not bother daylight travelers unless they set foot on the mound.

Males emerge one by one from the marked opening to attack intruders.

- **Lizard Men (males)** ×6: AC 5 [14], HD 2+1 (12hp, 10hp, 9hp, 8hp, 7hp, 5hp), Att 1 × crude weapon (2–7), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 12, AL C.

If all 6 males killed, remainder hides in lair. The largest male wears a **necklace worth 1,100 gp**.

**The Lair:** Inside the mound.
- 1 additional male: AC 5 [14], HD 2+1 (11hp), stats as above.
- 3 females: equal to males but attack as 1+1 HD monsters (8hp, 6hp, 6hp).
- 8 young: 1hp each, do not attack.
- 6 eggs.

**Lair Treasure:** Hidden under nest with eggs: 112 cp, 186 sp, gold ingot worth 90 gp, **potion of healing**, **potion of poison**.

**Tactics:** First person crawling into lair always loses initiative to the remaining male and largest female, unless a torch is thrust well ahead of the body.

## 2. Spiders' Lair

*(PDF p.15)*

Two black widow spiders have spun webs amongst the trees.

- **Black Widow Spiders** ×2: AC 6 [13], HD 3* (11hp, 10hp), Att 1 × bite (2d6 + poison), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 60' (20') / 120' (40') in web, SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 8, AL N. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

**Treasure:** Under a pile of leaves nearby — skeleton of a hapless elf. Everything rotted except a filthy shield (appears worthless; cleaning and oiling reveals **shield +1**).

## 3. Raider Camp

*(PDF p.15)*

A dozen chaotic fighters camped close enough to spy on the Keep, far enough to avoid patrols.

- **Leader:** AC 5 [14] (chain mail), HD 2 (12hp), Att 1 × bow (1d6) or spear (1d6), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 10, AL C.

- **Lieutenant:** AC 6 [13] (leather + shield), HD 1 (7hp), Att 1 × spear (1d6) or sword (1d6), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 9, AL C.

- **Bowmen** ×2: AC 7 [12] (leather), HD 1 (4hp each), Att 1 × bow (1d6) or dagger (1d4), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL C.

- **Spearmen** ×8: AC 6 [13] (leather + shield), HD 1 (5hp each), Att 1 × spear (1d6) or dagger (1d4), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL C.

**Treasure:** Each has 3d6 sp; lieutenant +d6 gp; leader +2d6 gp. Bowmen have extra quiver of 20 arrows. Cask of good wine on a tree stump. Several cleaned game animals (edible, portable).

## 4. The Mad Hermit

*(PDF p.15)*

A solitary hermit who has haunted this forest for many years, becoming progressively wilder and more dangerous. Home is a huge hollow oak, entrance concealed by thick bush. Inside: mound of leaves, crude furniture — all worthless.

- **Mad Hermit:** AC 4 [15] (leather armor, ring of protection +1, Dex 17), HD 3 (15hp), Att 1 × weapon (3–8, +2 to hit, +2 damage from madness), THAC0 19 [+0] (effective 17 [+2] with madness bonus), MV 120' (40'), SV D13 W14 P13 B16 S15 (T3), ML 10, AL C. 3rd level thief. Move silently 30%, hide in shadows 20%. Backstab: +6 to hit, double damage +2. No carried treasure (except ring of protection +1).

**Tactics:** May approach party on friendly terms, claiming to be a holy man. Suddenly turns, striking from behind and calling his pet.

- **Mountain Lion:** AC 6 [13], HD 3+2 (15hp), Att 3 × claw/claw/bite (1d3/1d3/1d6), THAC0 16 [+3], MV 150' (50'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 8, AL N. Always attacks first each round. Leaping from tree: +2 to hit on all attacks that round. If unengaged, climbs tree to leap again next round.

**Buried Treasure:** Small chest under a few inches of dirt under the leaves of his "bed": 31 gp, 164 sp, **potion of invisibility**, **dagger +1**.
