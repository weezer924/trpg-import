## Return to Sukiskyn

*(PDF p.28)*

After exploring Xitaqa, the party returns to Sukiskyn. Stephan is reunited with his family. The magical needle and thread found in Golthar's room (X12) is touched to the tapestry in the homestead hall (S4), revealing a map to the **Lost Valley of Hutaaka** — the next stage of the adventure.

---

## The Joyous Feast

*(PDF p.28)*

Pyotr celebrates the party's success. Each adventurer receives a **100gp gem** as a reward. Stephan proposes the following plan:

1. Take the white horses to **Rifllian** (elf village) for sale — avoiding Kelven where the Iron Ring has agents
2. Route via **Misha's Ferry** crossing the Volaga river
3. **Taras** (or another family member) accompanies the party to Rifllian, then returns to Sukiskyn with Pyotr's share of the proceeds
4. After Rifllian, continue north to **Threshold** to purchase supplies before heading to the Lost Valley

---

## Iron Ring Response

*(PDF p.28)*

The Iron Ring mobilizes in response to events at Xitaqa:

- **If Golthar escaped Xitaqa:** He fled to Kelven to warn the Iron Ring
- **If Golthar was killed:** Karllag (another Iron Ring Master) arrived at Xitaqa hours after the party left

**Forces deployed:**

| Force | Composition | Destination |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Bors & Hermann (Reavers) + 7 Hounds | 2 fighters + 7 thugs | Misha's Ferry — ambush |
| Aksel (Reaver thief) + 2 Hounds | 1 thief + 2 thugs | Toward Selenica, link up with slavers |
| Jolenta & Gactis (Reavers) + 2 Hounds | 2 rogues + 2 thugs | North toward Threshold |
| Other Reavers | Various | Krakatos and Specularum |

## W18. Misha's Ferry (Revisited)

*(PDF p.28–29)*

### Early Arrival (within 2 days of returning to Sukiskyn from Xitaqa)

If Misha's bear is still alive from the party's first visit, it has returned looking for Misha. The bear only attacks in self-defence and flees at 12hp or fewer.

- **Cave Bear**: AC 5 [14], HD 7 (30hp), Att 2 × claw / 1 × bite + hug (if both claws hit) (2d4/2d4/2d6 + 2d8), THAC0 13 [+6], MV 120' (40'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F4), ML 11, AL N, XP 450. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

### Late Arrival (Iron Ring Ambush)

If the party arrives later, the Iron Ring ambush is in place. Surprise on 1–3. Reavers flee on failed morale; Hounds fight to the death.

- **Bors & Hermann** (Reavers) ×2: AC 4 [15] (chain mail & shield), F3, hp 17 each, Att 1 × sword (1d8+2, inc. Strength bonus), THAC0 15 [+4] (inc. Strength bonus), MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F3), ML 10, AL N, XP 50 each. **Treasure:** 45gp each; one carries rare perfume (75gp).
- **Hounds** ×7: AC 7 [12] (leather), HD 1+1 (6hp), Att 1 × short sword or 1 × arrow (1d6), THAC0 16 [+3] (inc. ferocity bonus), MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 12, AL N, XP 19 each. R 50/100/150. No treasure.

## WE6. Loshad Sighted

*(PDF p.29)*

One hour after crossing the Volaga. The party spots **Loshad** (in stallion form) with 3 horse bodyguards in the distance. They gallop away. Half an hour later, Loshad returns in **centaur form**, non-threatening.

Loshad tells the party about a **slavers' camp** nearby with chained humans and horses. He wants the party to free the horses (and the humans). If the party is reluctant, Loshad:
- Reminds them that the humans are being beaten
- Hints that the party will need the consent of the chevall to sell the white horses at Rifllian

For Loshad's stats, see `south-of-the-river.md` WE4.

## WE7. Slavers' Camp

*(PDF p.29–30)*

### Camp Composition

- **19 slavers:** Rucker (C5 Reaver), Sydnor (F4 Reaver), Sgagast (bugbear Reaver), 8 Hounds, 8 Yellow-fang goblins
- **34 slaves:** 19 humans (NM, 1hp each), 15 Red-blade goblin slaves (1hp each)
- **11 riding horses** (hobbled, 3hp each)

### Camp Conditions

| Time | Guards | Other |
|------|--------|-------|
| Day | 1 Hound on each of 3 paths (3 total) | Goblins asleep; Sydnor whipping a horse |
| Night | 2 goblins on each of 3 paths (6 total) | All humans asleep without armour |

### Slavers' Stats

- **Rucker** (Reaver cleric): AC 5 [14] (chain mail), C5, hp 19, Att 1 × mace (1d6), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 90' (30'), SV D8 W9 P10 B13 S12 (C5), ML 10, AL C, XP 300. **Spells:** 1st — *cure light wounds*, *darkness*; 2nd — *blight*, *hold person*. **Treasure:** staff of healing, 2 holy water, 3 gems (250gp each), 75gp.

- **Sydnor** (Reaver fighter): AC 4 [15] (chain mail & shield), F4, hp 25, Att 1 × sword (1d8+2, inc. Strength bonus), THAC0 15 [+4] (inc. Strength bonus), MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F4), ML 10, AL C, XP 125. **Treasure:** 5 gold ingots (150gp each, Iron Ring stamp), 50gp.

- **Sgagast** (bugbear Reaver): AC 5 [14], HD 3+1 (16hp), Att 1 × battle axe (1d8+1), THAC0 16 [+3], MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F3), ML 9, AL C, XP 75. **Treasure:** gold chain (350gp), 35gp.

- **Hounds** ×8: AC 7 [12] (leather), HD 1+1 (6hp), Att 1 × short sword or 1 × arrow (1d6), THAC0 16 [+3] (inc. ferocity bonus), MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 12, AL N, XP 19 each. **Treasure:** 2d20gp.

- **Yellow-fang goblins** ×8: AC 6 [13], HD 1-1 (4hp), Att 1 × short sword or 1 × arrow (1d6), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 90' (30'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 8, AL C, XP 5 each. R 50/100/150. **Treasure:** 2d6gp each.

### Aftermath

After the slavers are defeated:
- Freed human prisoners attempt to kill any surviving goblins unless the party restrains them
- Sydnor has the **keys to the chains**
- **Loshad** appears and requests the horses be released. He allows them to carry prisoners back to Sukiskyn first, after which the horses become unmanageable and run free

## WE8. Merchants

*(PDF p.30–31)*

Where Misha's Ferry trail meets the Kelven–Selenica trail. Merchant caravan of **Ahiktos**: 20 wagons (spices, silks, velvets), 45 mounted fighter guards, 20 drivers.

### Key NPCs

- **Ahiktos**: AC 9 [10], F3, hp 15, Att 1 × sword (1d8), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F3), ML 8, AL N. Male, 58, overweight. Sells information for a base price of 90gp or one white horse.

- **Guard Leaders** ×5: AC 4 [15] (chain mail & shield), F4, hp 22, Att 1 × sword (1d8), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 90' (30') / 240' (80') mounted, SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F4), ML 10, AL L.

- **Guards** ×40: AC 5 [14] (chain mail), F1, hp 6, Att 1 × sword or 1 × arrow (1d8 or 1d6), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 90' (30') / 240' (80') mounted, SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 9, AL L. R 50/100/150.

- **Drivers** ×20: AC 9 [10], NM, hp 3, Att 1 × short sword (1d6), THAC0 20 [-1], MV 120' (40'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 7, AL N.

### Horse Trading

Ahiktos offers a base price of **75gp per white horse**.

### Intelligence for Sale

Ahiktos sells the following information (base price 90gp or one white horse):
- Golthar (yellow robes) has appeared in Kelven
- A tall dark woman has been asking about the party
- A rumour about the Duke's daughter
- Bandits reported on the road ahead

## Rifllian

*(PDF p.31–32)*

Small elven village, 170 inhabitants, **Callarii elves**. Outpost and trading post on the western bank of the Windrush river.

### Iron Ring Watchers

**Jolenta** and **Gactis** (see PSV for full stats) + 2 Hounds arrived one day before the party. They watch from a wooded knoll. When they see the party arrive, they wait until the party leaves, then release a **carrier pigeon**. Jolenta heads north to organize brigands (WE12). Gactis follows the party (WE11).

### R1. Arrival

The Duke's Galley is departing as the party arrives. A brass bell on the east bank calls the ferryman.

| Service | Base Price |
|---------|----------:|
| Ferry (per person) | 1gp |
| Ferry (per horse) | 2gp |

The ferryman offers **135gp** (base) for one white horse.

### R2. Silver Swan Inn

Run by **Stubbs Plattermann** (halfling), H5, hp 17. Clean, homely. Prices 20% above standard.

Stubbs knows Stephan and mentions that a **"strange tall dark woman with blue eyes"** was asking about Stephan yesterday, accompanied by 2 silent male companions.

### R3. The Trader

**Prestelle** (E3) offers a base price of **120gp per white horse**. If an elf PC bargains: +1 bonus to the bargaining roll. Individual elves in the village offer **150gp** base (1d20 elves interested, one horse each).

General supplies and weapons available at **20% above standard prices**. Stephan buys chainmail here.

### Taras' Departure

Taras returns to Sukiskyn via Kelven carrying half the money from the horse sale.

### Callarii Elf Stats (Generic)

- **Callarii Elves**: AC 5 [14] (chain mail) or 9 [10], E1–4, hp 4–16, Att 1 × dagger / 1 × sword / 1 × longbow arrow (1d4 / 1d8 / 1d6), THAC0 19 [+0] (E1–2) or 17 [+2] (E3–4), SV E1–4, ML 9, AL N.

## On to Threshold

*(PDF p.32)*

### By Boat

Weekly passenger vessel **"Mudlark"**, fare 16gp per character, voyage approximately 2 days. No horses allowed.

- **Captain Scylla**: AC 7 [12] (leather), F3, hp 11, Att 1 × sword (1d8), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F3), ML 8, AL L.
- **Crewmen** ×10: AC 9 [10], NM, hp 4, Att 1 × short sword (1d6), THAC0 20 [-1], MV 120' (40'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 8, AL N.
- **Mudlark:** 30' long, 19' beam, 2' draft, MV 60'/round, Hull 35hp, AC 8.
- **Cargo:** 10 crates of clothes/boots, 4 chests of lumber axes.

### By Road

Trail north from Rifllian. Terrain becomes broken/hilly — **half normal movement rate**. The river trail maintains normal speed.

## Events on the Road

*(PDF p.32–33)*

### WE9. Gentle Reminder

If the PCs ride the white horses northward, **Loshad** appears and reminds them the horses are to be sold to the elves, not ridden further.

### WE10. Victim of the Scange

A female human corpse killed by three **black-feathered arrows**. Found by the trail or floating in the river. Killed by Scange brigands. No treasure.

### WE11. Follower Behind

PCs glimpse a mounted figure on the skyline — **Gactis** (T4 Reaver, see PSV for full stats) following at a distance. If they double back, he avoids them. If they set an ambush, he may ride into the trap (DM's discretion).

### WE12. Trouble Ahead (Scange Brigands)

*(PDF p.32–33)*

Scange brigands: 18 at the ambush site + 8 at the camp = **26 total**. Hired by **Jolenta**.

#### Ambush Leaders and Stats

- **Hrothgar** (brigand leader): AC 1 [18] (plate mail +1 & shield), F3, hp 14, Att 1 × sword (1d8+1, inc. Strength bonus), THAC0 18 [+1] (inc. Strength bonus), MV 60' (20') / 120' (40') mounted, SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F3), ML 9, AL C, XP 35. **Treasure:** jewelled necklace (500gp), gold ring (175gp), key in boot (opens chest in camp).
- **War Horse**: AC 7 [12], HD 3 (16hp), Att 2 × hooves (1d6/1d6), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 9, AL N.
- **Scange Archers** (×variable): AC 7 [12] (leather), F1, hp 4, Att 1 × short bow (1d6) or 1 × sword (1d8), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 9, AL C, XP 10 each. **Treasure:** 30gp each.
- **Scange Swordsmen** (×variable): AC 6 [13] (leather & shield), F1, hp 4, Att 1 × sword (1d8) or 1 × dagger (1d4), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 9, AL C, XP 10 each. **Treasure:** 30gp each.

#### Ambush on the River (by boat)

Scange boat (25' long, Hull 30hp, AC 8) intercepts the Mudlark. 8 brigands grapple the vessel; 10 fire arrows from the bank.

#### Ambush on the Trail (by road)

Hidden in bushes with a **rope across the trail**. Surprise on 1–3: the rope is pulled taut, lead horses stumble, riders must **Save vs. Wands** or be thrown (1d6 damage). 8 swordsmen leap from trees onto horses (need 17+ on 1d20 to land). Hrothgar charges from the front.

#### Scange Camp

8 more Scange at camp (4 archers, 4 swordsmen). 2 archers serve as lookouts (crow calls as signal). 6 hide tents around a cooking fire.

**Camp Treasure:**
- One tent: 200 black-flight arrows, 6 short bows, 18 daggers, 15 swords
- Hrothgar's tent: fine rug (150gp), keg of brandy (200gp), locked chest (key in Hrothgar's boot): silver drinking cup (200gp), **potion of heroism**, **5,000gp**

### WE13. Flying Carpet

Day 2 after leaving Rifllian. A **flying carpet** heading north over the hills east of the river. It belongs to **Cardia** the elf (carrying Golthar and Sligh to Threshold). Captain Scylla can identify the carpet if the party is travelling by boat.
