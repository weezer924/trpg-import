## Sukiskyn — Pyotr's Clan

*(Pull-out Sheet III, PDF p.60–61)*

### Pyotr — Clan Head

- **Species/Identity:** Human male, age 40, tall, red-haired with beard
- **Location:** Sukiskyn (S4 Hall)
- **Attitude:** Fatherly, brave, honest, decisive
- **Key Info:** Clan head, pivot of negotiations. Asks party to search for Stephan. Good horseman. AL N.
- **Stats:** AC 9 [10] / 3 [16] (chain + shield +1), F5, hp 32, Att 1 × sword +1 (1d8+3) or 1 × arrow +1 (1d6+1), THAC0 14 [+5] / 17 [+2] (16 [+3] with +1 arrows), MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 10. S16 I11 W15 D9 C13 Ch12. Gear: sword +1, long bow, arrows, 5 arrows +1.

### Darya — Pyotr's Wife

- **Species/Identity:** Human female, age 38, medium height, black hair
- **Location:** Sukiskyn (S4/S6)
- **Attitude:** Slightly haughty but down-to-earth; loyal
- **Key Info:** Excellent cook, good horsewoman. Good at understanding cryptic codes. Ensures adventurers are cared for. AL L.
- **Stats:** AC 8 [11] / 6 [13] (leather), F2, hp 11, Att 1 × dagger (1d4) or 1 × arrow (1d6), THAC0 19 [+0] / 18 [+1], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 8. S9 I15 W10 D13 C11 Ch12.

### Taras — Pyotr's Elder Son

- **Species/Identity:** Human male, age 20, tall, red hair, long moustache
- **Location:** Sukiskyn (S7)
- **Attitude:** Enthusiastic, good humour; befriends fighters, suspicious of elves/MUs
- **Key Info:** Excellent horseman, good area knowledge. Encourages action against goblins. Goes with party to recover horses and to Rifllian. Becomes clan head if Pyotr dies. AL N.
- **Stats:** AC 8 [11] / 5 [14] (leather +1), F3, hp 15, Att 1 × battle axe (1d8+1) or 1 × arrow (1d6), THAC0 18 [+1] / 18 [+1], MV 120' (40'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F3), ML 9. S15 I9 W12 D14 C10 Ch11.

### Alfana — Taras' Wife

- **Species/Identity:** Human female, age 19, fair-haired
- **Location:** Sukiskyn (S7)
- **Attitude:** Gentle, sensitive, intelligent
- **Key Info:** Only person who can re-tame horses made wild by Loshad (1d4+1 weeks). AL L.
- **Stats:** AC 9 [10], NM, hp 3, Att 1 × dagger (1d4-1), THAC0 20 [-1], MV 120' (40'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 7. S6 I17 W10 D9 C11 Ch10.

### Irina — Pyotr's Daughter (Cleric)

- **Species/Identity:** Human female, age 17, plump, plaited red hair
- **Location:** Sukiskyn (S11)
- **Attitude:** Perceptive, inquisitive
- **Key Info:** Cleric, heals characters. Can normally (70%) tell when someone is lying. AL L.
- **Stats:** AC 6 [13], C2, hp 9, Att 1 × sling (1d4), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 120' (40'), SV D11 W12 P14 B16 S15 (C2, +2 ring), ML 8. S10 I11 W16 D14 C9 Ch12. Gear: sling, ring of protection +2, holy symbol. **Spells:** 1st — *cure light wounds*.

### Matvey — Pyotr's Younger Son

- **Species/Identity:** Human boy, age 10, untidy red hair
- **Location:** Sukiskyn (roaming)
- **Attitude:** Secretive, quiet; prefers animals to people
- **Key Info:** Can hide in shadows (15%) and move silently (25%). Potential source of mischief — may need rescuing. AL N.
- **Stats:** AC 9 [10], NM, hp 3, MV 120' (40'). S6 I12 W9 D12 C10 Ch12.

### Kuzma — Pyotr's Mother (Cleric)

- **Species/Identity:** Human female, age 66, short, thin, grey-haired
- **Location:** Sukiskyn (S11)
- **Attitude:** Stern with adults, kind to children and halflings; suspicious of non-lawful clerics
- **Key Info:** Excellent knowledge of legends (Loshad summoning, area history). Heals if asked. AL L.
- **Stats:** AC 9 [10], C4, hp 12, MV 60' (20'), SV D9 W10 P13 B16 S14 (C4), ML 8. S8 I14 W18 D9 C9 Ch10. **Spells:** 1st — *cure light wounds* ×2; 2nd — *bless*. Gear: holy symbol, scroll (*bless*, *cure light wounds* ×2), scroll (*cure disease* — works on lycanthropy).

### Masha — Hakos' Widow

- **Species/Identity:** Human female, age 18, tall, blonde, pale; carries baby Garina
- **Location:** Sukiskyn (S15)
- **Attitude:** Distraught; likes poetry/songs; fascinated by elves; loathes violence
- **Key Info:** Sings beautifully, plays balalaika. Urges non-violent solutions. AL L.
- **Stats:** AC 9 [10], NM, hp 2, MV 120' (40'), ML 6. S9 I10 W11 D10 C9 Ch14.

### Stellios — Servant

- **Species/Identity:** Human male, age 63, bald, plump, one arm
- **Location:** Sukiskyn (S5)
- **Attitude:** Eccentric; long memory
- **Key Info:** Cannot ride. Expert knowledge of gems — can identify and value gems/jewellery. Alternative source of legends. AL L.
- **Stats:** AC 9 [10], NM, hp 2, Att 1 × sling (1d4) if needed, THAC0 20 [-1], MV 90' (30'), ML 7. S10 I11 W9 D9 C11 Ch9.

## Miscellaneous Allies

### Stephan — Pyotr's Brother

*(Pull-out Sheet IV, PDF p.61)*

- **Species/Identity:** Human male, age 37, tall, red-haired
- **Location:** Kelven (start) → captured by goblins → Xitaqa (X10) → party companion
- **Attitude:** Friendly, brave, honest; becomes obsessed with finding the Lost Valley
- **Key Info:** Recruits party at start. Familiar with trail to Threshold. Can negotiate with NPCs. After rescue, driven to find the Lost Valley before Golthar.
- **Stats:** AC 6 [13] (leather), F5, hp 26, Att 1 × sword or 1 × arrow (1d8+1 or 1d6), THAC0 16 [+3], MV 120' (40'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 10. S15 I10 W9 D14 C12 Ch15. R 50/100/150. Gear: sword, short bow, arrows.

### Loshad — Chevall

*(Pull-out Sheet IV, PDF p.61)*

- **Species/Identity:** Chevall (shapechanging horse/centaur), male
- **Location:** Open lands east/north of Kelven (roaming)
- **Attitude:** Concerned only with horse welfare; blood enemy of werewolves
- **Key Info:** Can talk to/command horses. Once/day summon 1d3 war horses (arrive in 1d4 rounds). Only hit by silver/magical weapons. Has ring of invisibility (earring).
- **Stats (centaur form):** AC 5 [14] (1 [18] if invisible), HD 7* (46hp), Att 2 × hooves / 1 × arrow (1d6/1d6/1d6), THAC0 13 [+6], MV 180' (60'), SV D8 W9 P10 B13 S12 (F7), ML 9, AL N, XP 850. R 50/100/150.
- **Stats (stallion form):** AC 2 [17], MV 270' (90'), Att 2 × hooves / 1 × bite (1d6/1d6/1d4), ML 11.

### Kalanos — Boatman

- **Species/Identity:** Human male, age 40, short, black receding hair
- **Location:** Kelven riverboat (start) → Ilyakana (W4, berserk)
- **Attitude:** Initially helpful; goes berserk after witnessing Ilyakana massacre
- **Key Info:** Transports the party upriver from Kelven. After Ilyakana attack, his mind is affected — goes berserk at the sight of any armed creature. Madness can be cured by rest.
- **Stats (sane):** AC 7 [12], F5, hp 22, Att 1 × dagger (1d4), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 12, AL L. Heavy brown robe over leather armour, 2 gold arm bands (60gp each).
- **Stats (berserk):** AC 7 [12], F5, hp 22, Att 1 × battle axe or 1 × arrow (longbow) (1d8 or 1d6), THAC0 15 [+4] (inc. berserk +2), MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 12, AL N. R 70/140/210. Bonuses: +4 vs. mind-affecting spells (insanity), +2 to hit (berserk). Leather armour, 2 gold arm bands (60gp each).
- **See:** `sukiskyn.md` (Leaving Civilisation Behind), `south-of-the-river.md` (W4)

### Fyodorll — Bandit Leader

- **Species/Identity:** Elf (female), bandit horse-trader
- **Location:** W3 Bandit Camp
- **Attitude:** Prefers to trade; fights if negotiations fail
- **Key Info:** Bought 24 horses from the Vipers (paid 15gp each, claims 50gp each). Uses *ventriloquism* to distract, then attacks with bandits (backstab if possible).
- **Stats:** AC 1 [18], E4, hp 17, Att 1 × sword or 1 × arrow (1d8 or 1d6), THAC0 17 [+2] melee / 14 [+5] missile, MV 120' (40'), SV D8 W9 P10 B13 S12 (E4), ML 9, AL N, XP 175. R 70/140/210. S9 I15 W10 D18 C11 Ch16. Blue robe over chain mail +1, ring (210gp), 25pp. **Spells:** 1st — *sleep, ventriloquism*; 2nd — *ESP, levitate*. **Spellbook:** detect magic, light, sleep, ventriloquism, ESP, knock, levitate. Scroll: floating disc ×2.
- **See:** `sukiskyn.md` (W3)

### Babushka — Prisoner

- **Species/Identity:** Human female, elderly
- **Location:** W16f Cell (Wolfskull Lair)
- **Attitude:** Terrified inside the lair; talkative once freed
- **Key Info:** Captured during raid on Cherkass (W6). Provides critical intelligence about Stephan's location — prisoners taken to Xitaqa by Yellow-fang goblins. Left behind as "too scrawny."
- **Stats:** NM, hp 1.
- **See:** `south-of-the-river.md` (W16f, Babushka's Tale)

### Vissaryon — Gnome Mine Owner

- **Species/Identity:** Gnome male, mine owner
- **Location:** W12 Gold Mine (Miners' Quarters)
- **Attitude:** Honest but overfond of gold
- **Key Info:** Owns gold mine with brother Tekaryon. Offers 800gp (half their mined gold) to kill the shroud spider. If Tekaryon and dwarves returned alive, also gives a necklace worth 3000gp.
- **Stats:** AC 5 [14], HD 3 (12hp), Att 1 × war hammer or 1 × crossbow (1d6), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 60' (20'), SV D8 W9 P10 B13 S12 (D3), ML 10, AL N. R 60/120/180. Green cloak over chain mail.
- **See:** `south-of-the-river.md` (W12)

### Tekaryon — Gnome Miner

- **Species/Identity:** Gnome male, Vissaryon's brother
- **Location:** W12i Food Store (captured by shroud spider)
- **Key Info:** Found web-shrouded and paralysed in the spider's food store alongside 2 dwarf miners. Revives after 2 rounds if webs removed.
- **See:** `south-of-the-river.md` (W12i)

### Goriidel — Vyalia Elf Leader

- **Species/Identity:** Elf (Vyalia clan), leader of 5 elves
- **Location:** W11 Lake of Lost Dreams

### Ahiktos — Merchant

- **Attitude:** Mercenary; sells information for gold or horses
- **Key Info:** Caravan of 20 wagons (spices, silks, velvets), 45 mounted fighter guards, 20 drivers. Sells intelligence about Golthar and Iron Ring movements for 90gp or one white horse. Offers 75gp base price per white horse.
- **Stats:** AC 9 [10], F3, hp 15, Att 1 × sword (1d8), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F3), ML 8, AL N.

### Stubbs Plattermann — Halfling Innkeeper

## The Iron Ring

### Golthar — Master of the Iron Ring

*(Pull-out Sheet V, PDF p.62)*

- **Species/Identity:** Human male, age 32, tall, gaunt, deep-set eyes, piercing stare
- **Location:** Xitaqa (X10–X12) → Threshold (Fogor Isle) → Lost Valley
- **Attitude:** Controlled menace; unpredictable, violent temper

Gear: throwing daggers, ring of protection +2, jade ring (controls Xitaqa living statues), pouch with 5000gp in gems.

## Threshold

*(Pull-out Sheet V, PDF p.62)*
