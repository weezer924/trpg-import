## General Features

*(PDF p.10)*

- **Construction:** All oak, including roof shingles; tower and ground floors of kitchen/forge are stone
- **Palisades:** 8-foot-high log palisades join buildings
- **Gates:** Heavy oaken gates; gatehouse overlooks bridge
- **Windows:** No ground-floor windows
- **Doors:** Oak, require 30 hp damage to smash; can be barred from inside (+5 to +15 extra hp to smash when barricaded)
- **Tower:** Stone-built refuge with battlements and weapon/provision stores

## Leaving Civilisation Behind

*(PDF p.7)*

The party departs Kelven on Kalanos' riverboat on Moldain the 7th of Thaumont. Crew of nine (including eight rowers). Unknown to Kalanos, one rower is an **Iron Ring Reaver** (thief) planted to aid an ambush upriver.

The river is flanked by rolling downs at first; a few miles upriver, forest closes on the southern bank. The boat strikes a **chain stretched across the river** — an Iron Ring trap.

### River Ambush

The Iron Ring squad automatically gains surprise. Bowmen fire from the wooded right bank (~80 feet away). After round 1, the crew (including Kalanos) hide in the bottom of the boat. Hounds with daggers swim toward the boat; takes 1 round to clamber in (no attack that round). The disguised Reaver on the boat attacks simultaneously, backstabbing if possible.

**Cover:** Boat gives complete cover below gunwales, +2 AC for those showing themselves. Water gives +1 AC to swimmers.

**On the bank:**

- **Reaver of the Iron Ring** ×1: AC 4 [15] (chain mail & shield), F2, hp 14, Att 1 × sword or 1 × arrow (1d8+1 or 1d6), THAC0 18 [+1] (inc. S bonus), MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 10, AL N, XP 25. R 50/100/150.
- **Hounds (bowmen)** ×8: AC 7 [12] (leather), HD 1+1 (6hp), Att 1 × arrow (1d6), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 12, AL N, XP 19 each. R 50/100/150.
- **Hounds (boarders)** ×12: AC 9 [10], HD 1+1 (6hp), Att 1 × dagger (1d4), THAC0 16 [+3] (inc. ferocity bonus), MV 120' (40'), swim 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 12, AL N, XP 19 each.

**On the boat:**

- **Kalanos** (boatman): AC 7 [12], F5, hp 22, Att 1 × dagger (1d4), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 12, AL L. Appearance: Male, age 40, short, black receding hair. Heavy brown robe over leather armour, 2 gold arm bands (60gp each).
- **Ordinary crewmen** ×7: AC 9 [10], NM, hp 3 each, Att nil, SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 7, AL N.
- **Reaver (thief)** ×1: AC 6 [13] (inc. D bonus), T4, hp 12, Att 1 × dagger +1 (1d4+1, double for backstab), THAC0 19 [+0] (15 [+4] for backstab), MV 120' (40'), SV D13 W14 P13 B16 S15 (T4), ML 10, AL N, XP 125.

**Treasure:** Hounds have nothing. Impostor rower has 3gp. Bank Reaver has 30gp mixed coins.

**Clues:** Iron Ring brand (two manacles linked by chain) on left forearm + manacle scars on Hounds' wrists/ankles. Kalanos does not recognise the marks.

## W1. Misha's Ferry

*(PDF p.7–8)*

Misha's Ferry stands where a trade route crosses the Volaga river — first crossing point upriver from Kelven. Kalanos continues upstream to the Ilyakana lumber camp (W4).

Misha was attacked the previous night by Vlack's vampire bats (event WE5) and drowned. Her cave bear was wounded and fled.

The cabin door is unlocked. Nothing seems disturbed. Only treasure: 45gp mixed coins in a jar on a shelf.

Shortly after the party arrives, the wounded bear returns and charges anyone it sees.

- **Cave Bear** ×1: AC 5 [14], HD 7 (30hp), Att 2 × claw / 1 × bite + hug (if both claws hit) (2d4/2d4/2d6 + 2d8), THAC0 13 [+6], MV 120' (40'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F4), ML 11, AL N, XP 450. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

If the party takes refuge in the cabin without attacking, the bear claws at doors/windows then wanders off. Feeding it will not pacify it.

## Journey to Sukiskyn

*(PDF p.8)*

Night falls about an hour after arrival at Misha's Ferry. Party should stay in the cabin overnight (howling wolves encourage this). Venturing out at night: attacked by 2–5 wolves (optional encounters) and lose their way, returning after 2 hours.

Next day is fine and sunny. Sukiskyn is ~18 miles from Misha's Ferry — a full day's travel. Party should arrive just after sunset, as the goblins attack at dusk.

## Flames at Dusk

*(PDF p.8)*

When the party is ~500 yards from the homestead:

> It is past sunset. A twilight hush has settled over the forest. Then the silence is broken. A faint cry drifts on the evening breeze which also brings a whiff of woodsmoke. Ahead, flames rise beyond the trees. Shouts of men mingle with harsh, guttural war-cries and the clash of battle, while all the time the flames grow higher.

**Ambush at the Bridge:** A squad of 8 Wolfskull goblins (in wolf-skin cloaks) ambushes from undergrowth. Surprise on 1–4 on 1d6. First round: spears. After that: axes.

When the fight is nearly over, 7 more Wolfskull goblins mounted on dire wolves charge along the riverbank to cut the party off. If the party runs for the gate within 2 rounds, they get through ahead of the riders.

**Inside the gate:** Alfana holds the gate open, then bars it. Five Red-blade goblins are rushing from a breach in the palisade. Three others already killed; three survivors have 2hp each. They ignore Alfana and concentrate on characters in metal armour.

The woman is **Alfana**, the man is **Taras**. They lead the party to the main building.

## The Long Night

*(PDF p.9)*

Once the party reaches the main building, surviving Red-blade in the yard fall to defenders' arrows. Then a lull (~3 turns) as goblins wait for the barn, gatehouse, and northern palisade to burn down.

**Pyotr's briefing:**
- Two goblin clans attacking: Red-blade (Gnhasska) and Wolfskull (Kloss-lunk, who ride wolves)
- A third clan, the Vipers (Jaggadash), stole the horses and fled, killing Novannes and Hakos
- Barn, gatehouse, and northern palisade are burning; main building and stables intact
- With the party's help, they can hold until dawn when daylight forces goblins back
- Leaving before dawn would be suicidal
- This is one of several recent goblin attacks on homesteads

**Defender Positions at Start:**
- Hall (S4): Pyotr, Darya, Matvey, Masha (weeping), baby Garina, Stellios
- Tower room (S11): Kuzma and Irina
- Stable block (S16): Bodies of Novannes and Hakos

**Moonlit night:** Defenders can see attackers in the open. Shadows hide creatures in the woods. Goblins chant, beat drums, and light numerous small fires to exaggerate their numbers.

**If adventurers leave the homestead:** Attacked by one of the war parties (3d6 missiles/round in the open; full war party in the woods).

## Siege Events

*(PDF p.8–12)*

### SE1. Ominous Silence

*(Repeatable, DM's discretion)*

The goblins interrupt drumming and chanting with periods of ominous silence to frighten defenders.

### SE2. Snipers

*(Repeatable, DM's discretion)*

Groups of 1d4+2 Red-blade goblins sneak to the wood's edge and fire slingstones at windows and battlements. Goblins have **-4 AC bonus** (concealment) against missiles from the homestead. They retreat after 1d6+8 rounds or if 2+ are killed.

### SE3. Foray

*(~3 hours before midnight)*

**Western attack (bridge):** 2 Wolfskull goblin bodyguards mounted on dire wolves + 10 Wolfskull goblins (on foot) charge across the bridge, through the ruined gatehouse, to hack at the northern door into the kitchen.

**Eastern support:** 8 Red-blade goblins rush across the northern clearing, take cover in the barn ruins (-2 AC bonus), and give covering fire with slings.

**Door mechanics:** Up to 2 goblins can hack at once, hitting automatically. Door breaks at 30+ hp damage. If the door breaks, Wolfskull pour in, followed by Red-blade. Dire wolves will not enter the building.

**Retreat:** Either group retreats if it fails a morale check or loses half its numbers.

### SE4. Decoy

*(~Midnight)*

Defenders hear screams from the woods east of the clearing — a "woman" in a yellow dress being dragged by goblins. This is a trick: the figure is a hobgoblin in a stolen dress, the voice a female goblin warrior. Screams continue for 2 turns.

Pyotr sees through it. Darya will be upset — her friend Katarina from Cherkass homestead (W6) has a yellow dress. If PCs venture out, treat as breaking out (see The Night).

### SE5. Aerial Attack

*(~2 hours after midnight)*

Vlack sends 5 giant vampire bats against defenders on the battlements or in open areas. Otherwise bats squeeze through windows to attack indoors.

- **Giant Vampire Bats** ×5: AC 6 [13], HD 2* (7hp), Att 1 × bite (1d4 + paralysis), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 180' (60') flying, SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL N, XP 25 each. Any character bitten: Save vs. Paralysis or fall unconscious for 1d10 rounds. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

### Just Before Dawn

The **Wolfskull** give up and leave (pursuing the Viper goblins). All goblin drums go silent.

**Red-blade final assault:** Gnhass leads his remaining troops in a final charge across the eastern clearing. Gnhass + 2 bodyguards leap over the burnt northern palisade and hack at the hall's northern door. 2 other bodyguards lead remaining Red-blades against the eastern wall of the hall with 5 crude ladders.

**Door:** Same mechanics as SE3 (30hp to break). If broken, Red-blades rush in and fight to the death (no morale check).

**Ladders:** 2 goblins per round per ladder; no attack that round, lose initiative next round. PCs can topple a ladder with a hit roll vs. AC 6 [13] (bare hands, Str bonuses apply). Falling goblins take 1d6 damage.

**Retreat:** Eastern wall goblins retreat if morale check fails or half killed. If Gnhass is killed, all surviving Red-blade flee.

During the final attack, **Vlack and his retinue slip away unnoticed** (encountered later in event WE5).

## The Day After

*(PDF p.11–12)*

### Reading the Signs

The clearing reveals carnage: 4 dead Red-blades (north), 3 dead Viper goblins (pens), plus any killed by the party. Goblins stripped bodies in woods/west bank but abandoned the rest.

**Forest search reveals:** camp fires, stripped goblin bodies, chewed bones, fruit rinds, rubbish; numerous goblin and dire wolf footprints; 30% chance of noticing larger hobgoblin prints. Among the rubbish: a **silver amulet** in the shape of a ruined tower (50gp) — the symbol of the **Yellow-fang** (Faz-plak) goblins, dropped by one of Vlack's bodyguards.

Vlack's trail cannot be found. Scattered Red-blade tracks lead in many directions but cannot be followed far. The **Viper trail + stolen horses** is very clear, heading east.

### Captives

Goblin captives can provide information (see Sources of Information, `south-of-the-river.md`).

## Recovering the Horses

*(PDF p.12–13)*

The Viper goblins headed toward a bandit camp (W3) with 41 stolen horses. En route, they were overtaken by Wolfskull riders (W2). Some Vipers reached the bandits and sold surviving horses.

Taras tries to persuade the party to pursue:
- Horses easy to track (many prints)
- Goblins can't ride — mounted PCs will catch up
- Vipers are cowardly, less fight in daylight
- Pyotr offers 20gp reward per horse recovered

Adventurers may borrow riding horses from stables (S17) but must take care — each is personal property.

### W2. Scene of Battle

*(PDF p.12–13, no map)*

Following the Viper trail east, tracks veer suddenly into the forest. ~200 yards in:

> In the muddy clearing, the goblins have been attacked and massacred. Stripped and bloody bodies of a score of goblins lie mixed with torn carcasses of horses. The obese corpse of a tall goblin hangs by his feet from a tree. Six large beetles feast on the remains — and scuttle towards you.

- **Oil Beetles** ×6: AC 4 [15], HD 2* (8hp), Att 1 × bite + oil squirt (1d6 + blisters: -2 to hit for 24 hours), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL N, XP 25 each. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

A female **Viper goblin** hid in a tree during the battle. When fighting starts, she attempts to flee. If captured: describes the battle willingly, may give other information.

- **Viper Goblin** ×1: AC 6 [13], HD 1-1 (3hp), Att 1 × war hammer or 1 × thrown dagger (1d6 or 1d4), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 90' (30'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 6, AL C, XP 5.

**Dead:** 22 Viper goblins (cheek tattoos), 3 Wolfskulls, 1 dire wolf — all stripped. 17 of the 41 horses killed. Clear tracks of remaining horses head SE. Wolfskull tracks (heading SW) peter out.

### W3. Bandit Camp

*(PDF p.13, no map)*

The Viper trail follows an overgrown trackway to the bandit camp. Sign nailed to a tree: *"Miss L. Fyodorll — Equine Entrepreneur — Dealer in Fine Horses."*

- **Fyodorll** (bandit leader): AC 1 [18], E4, hp 17, Att 1 × sword or 1 × arrow (1d8 or 1d6), THAC0 17 [+2] melee / 14 [+5] missile, MV 120' (40'), SV D8 W9 P10 B13 S12 (E4), ML 9, AL N, XP 175. R 70/140/210. **Abilities:** S9, I15, W10, D18, C11, Ch16. Blue robe over chain mail +1, ring (210gp), 25pp. **Spells:** 1st — *sleep, ventriloquism*; 2nd — *ESP, levitate*. **Spellbook:** detect magic, light, sleep, ventriloquism, ESP, knock, levitate. Scroll: floating disc ×2.

## A Muted Feast

*(PDF p.14)*

After the refugees arrive, Pyotr holds a sombre feast in the hall. During the feast, **Darya** draws Pyotr's attention to the **tapestry** on the hall wall (S4) — the one with geometric patterns. She has noticed that it resembles a Hutaakan artefact described in old legends Kuzma once told her.

Pyotr asks the adventurers to:
1. **Search for Stephan** — who was taken by the goblins and may still be alive
2. Take care — goblin territory is dangerous
3. Report back to Sukiskyn with any news

The party is expected to explore the wilderness south and east of Sukiskyn, searching for Stephan's captors (the Wolfskull tribe). See `south-of-the-river.md`.

## Homestead Description

*(PDF p.10)*

### S1. Bridge (f)

Survives the siege. Spans the Syereb River to the homestead gate.

### S2. Gatehouse (f)

Small wooden tower over the gate. **Burnt down** during the siege.

### S3. Barn (f)

**Burnt down** during the siege. Provides cover (-2 AC bonus) for snipers in its ruins.

### S4. Hall (f)

Heart of the homestead. Long table, benches, Pyotr's large chair. Wall decorations: stuffed eagle, wolf's head, old hunting horn, two tapestries. One tapestry shows wild running horses; the other has a **geometric pattern** — this is the **Hutaakan tapestry** (see `region.md`). Gallery at upper floor level with windows. Adventurers sleep here (straw mattresses).

**Overlooks:** N & S Yards, Pens, E Clearing.

### S5. Side Room (f)

Dark room for brewing, salting, washing, storage. Stellios sleeps here. Contents: tubs, salted meat (ceiling), ale casks, food stores, oil lamps, oil, Stellios' bed.

### S6. Kitchen (f)

Large hearth and bread-oven. Women often gather here. Contents: table, stools, pots, pans, utensils, food, herbs, spices, Darya's pewter dinner service.

### S7. Taras' and Alfana's Room (fff)

Contents: bed, table, chairs, two chests of clothes. **Taras' collection:** 15 ornate daggers and 10 antique pole-arms (on wall).

**Overlooks:** N Yard.

### S8. Pyotr's and Darya's Room (fff)

Contents: bed, table, chairs, mirror, portrait of Pyotr's father. **Hidden:** Locked secret compartment by hearth — iron-bound chest containing 2500gp. **Trap:** Cunningly trapped base (-10% detect); lifting the sack fires a poison dart (THAC0 10, 1d4 damage, Save vs. Poison or die).

**Overlooks:** N & S Yards, W River.

### S9. Store (ff)

Emergency food and drink supplies, plus forgotten items including **one elven boot** (useless alone — the other is in Dark Web Orcs' Lair, W12(m)).

### S10. Armoury (ff)

Contents: 2 short bows, 2 longbows, 1 battle axe, 2 small shields, 1 long sword, 1 rusty mace, 1 dwarf-sized plate armour, 1 broken crossbow, 3 slings, 16 spears, 120 arrows, 200 slingstones.

**Overlooks:** N Yard, N & E Clearing.

### S11. Kuzma's and Irina's Room (fff)

Smells of incense and old books. Contents: 2 small beds, 2 desks, chairs, lectern, bookshelves, scrolls, 1 chest of clothes. **Rack of 8 bottles:** 4 herbal horse medicine (no effect on man or horse), 2 vials of holy water, 2 potions of healing.

**Overlooks:** N Yard, N & E Clearing.

### S12. Battlements (f)

**Overlook:** N Yard, N, E & S Clearings.

### S13. Forge (f)

Novannes' workshop. Contents: hearth, fuel, anvil, tools, iron rods, horseshoes, nails.

### S14. Tack Room (ff)

Contents: saddles, harnesses, ropes, straw, oats, grooming items, an old tent.

### S15. Hakos' and Masha's Room (fff)

Contents: bed, cot, chest of clothes, table, mirror, baby toys, balalaika (stringed instrument) on wall.

**Overlooks:** W River, S Yard, Pens.

### S16. Novannes' Room (fff)

Contents: bed, desk, leather chair, chest of clothes, intricate wrought-iron candlestick, small painting of Novannes' wife, stuffed goblin's head (on wall).

**Overlooks:** W & S River, S Yard.

### S17. Stables (f)

**11 riding horses:** AC 7 [12], HD 2 (11hp), Att 2 × hooves (1d4/1d4), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 240' (80'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 7, AL N. Each horse is personal property of a clan member.
