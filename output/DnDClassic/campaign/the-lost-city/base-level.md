## General Features

*(PDF p.17)*

Tier 5 is the lowest fully detailed level. This tier, including the pyramid's main entrance, is buried under the sand. Hidden in the main entry chamber (room 50) is a secret door to a passage leading to the underground city (see `underground-city.md`).

**Monster Notes:**

- **Tiger Beetle:** 4' long with striped tiger-skin carapace. Carnivorous, attack if hungry. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

Hit by a thoul: **save vs. Paralysis or be paralyzed** (like ghoul touch, but thouls are not undead). **Regenerates** 1 hp per round after first hit. Serve the Priests of Zargon — hunt for prisoners. ## 41. Storage Room

*(PDF p.17)*

Shelves along the walls with items used in religious ceremonies (candles, incense, clerical robes, etc.). Three huge human-like creatures wearing animal skins have smashed open a box of candles and are eating them.

- **Monsters:** Ogre ×3: AC 5 [14], HD 4+1 (19hp), Att 1 × club (1-10), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F4), ML 10, AL Chaotic, XP 125. Not very bright — thought candles looked good to eat. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Treasure:** Three huge leather sacks: 500 gp, 500 gp, 400 gp. **Ring of fire resistance.** Magic-user/elf scroll: continual light, levitate, read languages.

## 42. Cleric's Quarters

*(PDF p.17)*

Three bunk beds. Humans in bright yellow robes lying on them, wearing boar, tiger, and wolf masks.

- **Monsters:** Doppelganger ×3: AC 5 [14], HD 4* (18hp), Att 1 × strike (1-12), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 90' (30'), SV D6 W7 P8 B8 S10 (F10), ML 10, AL Chaotic, XP 125. Took the forms of three Cynidiceans, then killed the originals. Will try to join the party, choosing one victim each. When they get a chance to act secretly, they change shape to look like the victim, then try to kill and replace them. **Immune to sleep and charm.** See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Treasure:** 9 gems worth 1,500 gp total.

## 43. Treasure Room

*(PDF p.18)*

A slight breeze when the door opens. A large padlocked wooden chest near the north wall. Behind the chest, a huge tapestry of a desert scene.

- **Monster:** Polymar ×1: AC 9 [10], HD 10* (45hp), Att 3 × strike (1-6/1-6/1-6), THAC0 11 [+8], MV 60' (20'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 10, AL Chaotic, XP 1,600.
  - **Module-unique monster.** Semi-intelligent many-celled creature that can reshape its body to look like any creature of 10 HD or less, or any object up to 10' × 10' × 10'. Gains only the shape — no special abilities.
  - **Detection:** Change is physical, not magical — detect magic will not work. Characters have the same chance to detect the polymar as to find secret doors (1-in-6; 2-in-6 for elves). The tapestry will not move in the breeze because the polymar is much heavier than a real tapestry.
  - **If not detected:** Attacks with surprise.
- **Treasure (locked chest):** 3,000 ep, 5,000 gp, **potion of invisibility**, **chain mail +1**, **dagger +1**.

## 45. Bedroom

*(PDF p.18)*

Bed, table, chair, lantern, wooden holy symbol of two intertwined snakes. Furniture is wooden. A hooded white robe draped over the chair.

- **Backstory:** Once the bedroom of **Demetrius**, a 6th level cleric and elder in the cult of Usamigaras. His twin brother **Darius** was a 6th level cleric in the cult of Zargon. Demetrius vowed to destroy the cult of Zargon and especially his evil brother, but was assassinated before he could begin. His dying wish was that his spirit live on until Darius was destroyed.
- **Spirit Possession:** The spirit of Demetrius rests in the white robe. Any character touching the robe must **save vs. Dragon Breath or be possessed** by Demetrius. He will only possess the character long enough to kill Darius (room 58).
- **Possessed character stats:** Though outwardly unchanged, becomes a 6th level Lawful cleric. Uses Demetrius's WIS 16, INT 10, and hp 31. Saving throws: D9 W8 P12 B14 S10 (C6).
- **Spells:** 1st: cure light wounds, detect evil. 2nd: bless, hold person. 3rd: striking (Expert rules only). 4th: cure serious wounds (Expert rules only).
- **Knowledge:** Demetrius knows the way to room 58 including secret doors. Does **not** know about traps on this level.
- **Behaviour:** As long as the party moves by the quickest route to Darius, Demetrius may help defeat monsters. He will depart once Darius is dead, and the white robe disintegrates. The possessed character is magically restored to full hp.
- **If reduced to 0 hp:** The possessed character dies.
- **DM tip:** Let the player whose character is possessed play Demetrius. Take the player aside and explain the situation.

## 46. Kitchen-Dining Room

*(PDF p.18)*

Abandoned kitchen and dining room. Dusty cupboards, table, and chairs. Near the table, two large snakes with brown and yellow scales coiled around the body of a woman wearing a rabbit mask.

- **Monsters:** Rock Python ×2: AC 6 [13], HD 5* (23hp), Att 2 × bite/constrict (1-4/2-8), THAC0 14 [+5], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F3), ML 8, AL Neutral, XP 300. If bite hits, python coils around victim and constricts in the same round for 2-8 damage. Thereafter, constriction hits automatically each round. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Treasure (on dead Cynidicean):** Necklace worth 700 gp. Full water bottle and small pouch of food concealed in robes.

## 47. Living Room

*(PDF p.19)*

- **Monsters:** Living Iron Statue ×2: AC 2 [17], HD 4* (18hp), Att 2 × strike (1-9/1-8 + special), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 30' (10'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F4), ML 11, AL Neutral, XP 125. **Will attack only if the party enters and allows the door to swing shut.** While door is held/jammed open, they will not attack. ## 49. Consultation Room

*(PDF p.19)*

Wood-paneled room, dusty carpet. Large heavy desk in the centre with several wooden chairs to the north. Plush chair behind desk. Mounted lion's head on south wall. Four large painted urns in corners. Stone pedestals with stone statues of winged beasts (claws, fangs, horns) on both sides of the north doorway.

- **Monsters:** Gargoyle ×2: AC 5 [14], HD 4* (18hp), Att 4 × claw/claw/bite/horn (1-3/1-3/1-6/1-4), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 90' (30') / fly 150' (50'), SV D8 W9 P10 B10 S12 (F8), ML 11, AL Chaotic, XP 125. If the party expects gargoyles, roll initiative normally; if not, party is surprised. Attack as soon as party enters. **Can only be hit with magical weapons.** Not affected by sleep or charm. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Treasure (desk cabinet — locked):** 2,000 ep, **mace +1**, **poison potion**.
- **Treasure (plush chair cushion):** 18 gems (100 gp each) hidden inside.
- **Urns and lion head:** Nothing of value.

### 49a. Pit Trap

Hidden 10' deep pit filled with spikes. Triggered on 1–2 on 1d6 for each character entering the area. **2-12 (2d6) damage.** Note: Demetrius does not know about this pit.

## 50. Main Entry Chamber

*(PDF p.19–20)*

High arched ceiling supported by a double row of pillars — each carved into a statue of a king or queen of Cynidicea. Mosaic pictures on the walls starting in the northeast corner and continuing clockwise. Pair of huge stone doors in the centre of the north wall. Sand partially covers the floor at the base of the doors.

**Mosaics (clockwise from northwest):**

1. Squat, heavy-browed humanoids with crude stone weapons worshipping a lizard-headed god.
2. Tall, black-haired people battle the humanoids and take their hunting grounds.
3. A village rises on the battle site. Surrounding fields of grain, herds of cattle and sheep.
4. A great leader gathers villagers into an army that destroys snake-headed humanoids.
5. The victorious leader is crowned king, founding the line of rulers of Cynidicea.
6. The stone city of Cynidicea is built.
7. Scenes of the kingdom's splendour at its height.
8. The births of King Alexander and Queen Zenobia.
9. The great pyramid is built.
10. Slaves digging under the pyramid uncover foundations of a prehistoric temple.
11. The death and burial of King Alexander and Queen Zenobia.
12. Worship of the old gods replaced by worship of a large many-tentacled humanoid.
13. Life becomes a year-round carnival for the Cynidiceans. All are now masked.
14. The Cynidiceans meet a barbaric, golden-haired people (ancestors of the Heldann tribes from module X1).
15. (Not a mosaic — a crudely painted sketch of tribesmen looting Cynidicea.)

There is room on the wall for more pictures, but the space is blank. The huge doors open outward but are **blocked by sand and cannot be opened**. No treasure.

### 50a. Secret Entrance to the Underground City

Secret trap door leading to a passageway to the underground city (area S on Underground City map). **Party can find this door only if the DM is continuing the adventure past the upper pyramid.**

## 51. Chapel

*(PDF p.20)*

Large place of worship. Rows of high-backed wooden benches along a broad centre aisle. Narrow wall aisles past ornate mosaics to two small fountains set into the walls. Large dais at the southern end with a stone altar. Three large toppled statues (matching those on top of the pyramid). A man and a woman near the statues talk softly. They wear brightly coloured clothing and small bronze fox masks. Both have red hair and look related.

- **Monsters:** Werefox ×2: AC 9 [10] (human) / 6 [13] (fox), HD 3+2* (16hp), Att 1 × weapon or bite (by weapon or 1-6) + charm, THAC0 16 [+3], MV 120' (40') / 180' (60') as fox, SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F3), ML 8, AL Neutral, XP 75.
  - **Module-unique lycanthrope.** Foxes that can change into human form (fox-like red hair in human form). Can charm a person of the opposite sex once per round (like charm person, but lasts only 1 day). In fox form, can charm animals instead. Up to **3 charms per day**. Save vs. Spells to resist.
  - Disguised as Cynidiceans to hunt for treasure and supplies. May bargain or try to charm the party. Will try to trick the party out of as much treasure and magic as possible. Avoid unnecessary risks. Rely on wits to survive. If attacked, change to fox form to fight or escape. If hard pressed, may return to human form and surrender. Will lie, cheat, and bribe to survive.
- **Treasure:** 3 pieces of jewellery (3,000 gp total), **sword +1 (casts light on command, 30' radius)**, **helm of alignment change**, potion (unknown to them — **potion of levitation**). Female uses the magical sword in combat if forced to fight in human form.
- **Fountains:** Fed by the underground lake. Provide all the water the party needs.
- **Note:** This room was once the main chapel of Cynidicea, seldom used now.

## 52. Trap Room

*(PDF p.20)*

Entire room is a large sunken pool filled with bubbling liquid. In the centre, a stone box on a pedestal, lid about a foot above the surface, locked with a large iron padlock.

- **Acid pool:** Destroys any material except stone. **2-8 damage per round** to creatures that fall in. 6' deep.
- **Sinking pedestal:** If any weight is placed on the box, pedestal sinks at ½' per round. If weight removed, rises at same rate (never higher than 1' above surface).
- **Treasure (in stone box):** 20,000 sp, clerical scroll: purify food and water, remove curse.

Racks of wine bottles along the walls. Large casks and barrels along the west wall. Several casks smashed open and drained. From behind a stack of barrels, a huge bear-like creature with the head of a giant owl staggers toward the party.

## 55. Party Room

*(PDF p.21)*

Nine men and women in bright costumes and fancy masks drinking, talking, and dancing. Appears to be a masked ball. Most dance with invisible partners. Conversations make no sense — people talking to themselves.

- **Inhabitants:** Cynidicean ×9: AC 9 [10], NM (4hp), Att 1 × short sword (1-6), THAC0 19 [0], MV 120' (40'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NH), ML special, AL Chaotic. In their own dream world. Will assume characters are part of the dream. No treasure.

## 56. Crashing Walls

*(PDF p.21)*

Hidden pressure plate in the centre of the hall triggers this trap. The walls ahead crash together and fly apart. Automatically triggers the first time the party passes.

- **Purpose 1:** Alerts Darius in room 58 that someone is coming.
- **Purpose 2:** Dangerous hazard if the party leaves room 56a and forgets it's here. On the way out: triggered on 1–2 on 1d6, **3-18 (3d6) damage** to characters behind the triggering character. Pressure plate easily seen and avoided if looking for it.

### 56a. Lobby

Ten Cynidiceans in dark robes and fearsome monster masks sit in a circle staring straight ahead at nothing.

- **Inhabitants:** Cynidicean ×10: AC 9 [10], NM (4hp), Att 1 × short sword (1-6), THAC0 19 [0], MV 120' (40'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NH), ML special, AL Chaotic. In nightmare dream worlds. React to party actions, may attack, but **never speak** even if wounded. May scream at random from unseen terror (screaming doesn't match reality — won't scream if actually touched or attacked).

## 57. Gambling Room

*(PDF p.21)*

A dozen brightly robed men and women in animal masks playing games of chance. Card games at one table, dice games at another. A large wheel of fortune in the centre. Silver and gold coins, gems, and jewellery changing hands as people win and lose.

- **Inhabitants:** Cynidicean ×12: AC 9 [10], NM (4hp), Att 1 × short sword (1-6), THAC0 19 [0], MV 120' (40'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NH), ML 8, AL Chaotic. Sharing a common dream — act more normally than those in rooms 55 and 56a. Continue gambling unless physically attacked. May invite party to join. If attacked, fight back (assume gang of robbers). If party tries to take money without winning, Darius and his hobgoblins from room 58 enter through the secret door in the south wall and attack.
- **Gambling rules:** DM and player each roll 1d20; highest wins, player loses ties. Wagers: minimum 50 gp, maximum 1,000 gp. If characters win large sums, Darius will attack (considers all money his as "taxes").
- **Possessed character note:** A character possessed by Demetrius will immediately go to the proper wall section and start looking for the secret door. He knows his enemy is very close.
- **Treasure:** 3,000 sp, 5,000 gp, 26 gems (3,100 gp total), 6 pieces of jewellery (6,000 gp total).

## 58. Cleric's Chamber

> **Note:** This is Tier 5, Room 58 (Darius's Chamber). There is also a Room 58 on Tier 6 (see `deep-levels.md`).

*(PDF p.21)*

Contains the evil cleric Darius and his hobgoblin guards. They will be ready for the party but are more likely to come charging out through the secret door as the party tries to take treasure from room 57. If the party tried to steal treasure, the Cynidiceans in room 57 join Darius; otherwise, the Cynidiceans do nothing unless attacked.

- **NPC:** **Darius** (evil cleric): AC 5 [14], C6 (22hp), Att 1 × mace (1-6+1), THAC0 16 [+3] (C6 base 17, +1 STR 13), MV 60' (20'), SV D9 W10 P12 B14 S12 (C6), ML 10, AL Chaotic, XP 275. S 13, I 9, W 16, D 9, C 13, Ch 14.
  - **Spells:** 1st: cure light wounds, light (reverse to darkness if using Expert rules). 2nd: bless (reverse to blight if using Expert rules), silence 15' radius. 3rd: striking (Expert rules only). 4th: cause serious wounds (Expert rules only).
  - **Suggested spell use:** Before melee: bless hobgoblins (or blight party). Round 1: silence a party magic-user. Round 2: striking on his own mace. Round 3+: spells as appropriate. Darius always uses spells as intelligently as possible.
- **Monsters:** Hobgoblin ×6: AC 6 [13], HD 1+1 (5hp), Att 1 × weapon (1-8), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL Chaotic, XP 15.
- **Potential allies:** Cynidicean ×12: AC 9 [10], NM (4hp), Att 1 × short sword (1-6), THAC0 19 [0], MV 120' (40'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NH), ML 8, AL Chaotic. Only join if party tried to steal from room 57.
- **Treasure:** Nothing of interest in this room (the treasure is in room 57).
