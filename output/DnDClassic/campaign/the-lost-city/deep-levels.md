## General Notes

*(PDF p.22)*

Tiers 6–10 were dug after the main pyramid was built. Tier 6 housed slave workers and their guards. The lower tiers (7–9) were to be a series of chambers showing the journey of the spirit after death, each with a painting of another stage. Tier 10 (where Zargon now lives) was to have a painting of the Cynidicean paradise — never completed.

The Cynidiceans do not usually venture into the lower tiers — these are the haunt of Zargon. Cynidiceans found here are most likely Zargon's prey or Priests of Zargon.

**These rooms are outline-only** — the DM is expected to add details. On Tier 6, room titles suggest what to add. On Tiers 7–9, all rooms are cave-like chambers with paintings on walls and ceilings.

**Warning:** If all characters are low level, these monsters will be very dangerous. Low-level parties should adventure here only with a larger-than-normal group and should expect casualties.

### 100. Lair of Zargon

*(PDF p.24)*

Sliding into a large, slime-covered chamber. Floor littered with bones. Rustling noise from the north wall — a huge humanoid figure rises from the slime, standing 15' tall. Head of a giant lizard. A black 2' long horn curves above its single red eye. Sharp teeth fill its mouth. Six tentacles on each side instead of arms and legs — arm tentacles end in razor-sharp talons.

- **Monster:** **Zargon** ×1: AC 0 [19], HD 12* (80hp), Att 7 × claw/bite (6 × 1-8 / 3d8), THAC0 10 [+9], MV 90' (30'), SV D6 W7 P8 B8 S10 (F12), ML 12, AL Chaotic, XP 1,900.
  - **Module-unique monster.** Ancient but not a god — discovered its "godhood" makes getting victims easier. Was worshipped by primitive peoples, retreated underground, hibernated for ages, awakened when Cynidiceans dug under the pyramid.
  - **Regeneration:** Can regenerate its body as long as its great horn is not destroyed. Regeneration from the bare horn takes years. **Does not regenerate during combat** (too slow). At full strength whenever encountered.
  - **Destroying the horn:** Must be cast into volcanic fire — the Eye of Zargon (area Q on Underground City map). This permanently destroys Zargon.
  - **Escape:** Party can climb back up the slide with difficulty (if Zargon lets them).
- **Treasure (in the slime):** 58,000 sp, 9,000 pp, **chain mail +2**, **shield +2**, **spear +2**, cursed scroll (turns reader into a 3' tall miniature Zargon without regeneration: AC 9 [10]; hp as character; #AT 7; D 6×1-2/1-6), **wand of illusion** (7 charges), **elven cloak and boots**.
