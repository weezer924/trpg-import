## Cynidicean Encounter Table

*(PDF p.27–28)*

When a Cynidicean encounter is rolled on any wandering monster table, roll 1d12 or choose one of the following encounters. The DM may also create new ones.

**1. The Werewolf.**
One character in a wolf mask (AC 9 [10]; NM; 3hp; MV 120' (40'); #AT none; ML 7; AL C). Believes he is a werewolf. Drops to all fours, howls, sniffs, circles, behaves like a wolf. May try to bite if approached too closely. Thinks he cannot be harmed — only afraid of magic or silver weapons.

**2. The Priests.**
Three characters in dark robes and devil masks, carrying incense burners. Think they are powerful clerics (AC 5 [14]; C3/C2/C1; hp 13/9/4; MV 90' (30'); #AT 1 × mace; D 1-6; SV D11 W12 P14 B16 S15 (C1–3); ML 8; AL C). See the party as demons and try to drive them off with loud cries and foul-smelling smoke. Leader: **Brother Theodeus**.

**3. The Gift.**
One Cynidicean in bright green robes and bird mask (AC 9 [10]; NM; 3hp; MV 120' (40'); #AT 1; D 1-6; ML 11; AL C). Walks slowly up to a party member, hands them a small pouch, smiles, and walks on. Ignores all attempts to talk. Pouch contains strange powder smelling like cloves — **no special properties**.

**4. The Raiders.**
Five Cynidiceans in brightly coloured robes and human-face masks. Think the party is the same barbarian raiders who looted the city centuries ago. **Flee in panic** as the party approaches.

**5. The Invisible Snakes.**
Six Cynidiceans in striped robes and camel masks zig-zag down the hall in single file, avoiding "invisible snakes" on the floor. Try to warn the party to walk around the snakes. **There are no snakes.**

**6. The Bee Keepers.**
Four Cynidiceans in insect masks carrying large clay jars, humming like bees. Ask the party to drink from the jars. Anyone who does is **healed 1-4 hp** (jars contain killer bee honey). If attacked or someone tries to drink twice, they smash the jars and **attack berserkly** (AC 9 [10]; NM; 3hp; MV 120' (40'); #AT 1 at +2; D 1-6; ML 12; AL C).

**7. The Sick Gourd.**
Four Cynidiceans wearing animal masks (Hawk, Fish, Cat, Fox — also their names). Carrying a stretcher with a gourd on it. Rushing their "sick friend" (the gourd: Dog) to a cleric. If the party has a cleric, **insist the cleric cure their friend**.

**8. The Royal Court.**
Six Cynidiceans in bright yellow robes and human masks. Stare at the party, then "recognise" a party member as the King or Queen of Cynidicea. Follow the character, sing songs of praise, wait on them hand and foot. Smother them with attention. **Will not fight** — more likely to grab the character and rush them to safety. (AC 9 [10]; NM; 4hp; MV 120' (40'); #AT 1; D 1-6; ML 7; AL C). Spokesman: **Policrates the Herald**.

**9. The Birds.**
Four Cynidiceans in feathered robes and bird masks. On seeing the party, "fly" (run) forward, flapping their arms and squawking. Invite the party to fly along — lead on a merry chase through the pyramid. If refused, insult the party as poor creatures who cannot fly ("Thou land-bound slugs!"), circle three times, and "fly" away. If attacked (AC 9 [10]; NM; 4hp; MV 120' (40'); SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1)), they "fly" away.

**10. The Undertakers.**
Six Cynidiceans with bodies painted black, wearing vulture masks. Carry long wooden boards. Seem friendly and normal. Follow the party, waiting for something to be killed. When it is, they rush over and start building a coffin. When done, **demand 10 gp payment**. Attack if not paid (AC 9 [10]; NM; 4hp; MV 120' (40'); #AT 1; D 1-6; ML 8; AL C).

**11. The NPC Party.**
Five Cynidiceans who think the player characters are a type of monster (roll on Level 2 Wandering Monster table in D&D Basic rules to determine what they think the PCs are). They are an organised party:
- F3: AC 5 [14]; hp 14; MV 60' (20'); Att 1 × sword +1 (1-8+1); SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F3); ML 8; AL C.
- F2: AC 5 [14]; hp 10; MV 60' (20'); Att 1 × sword (1-8); SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2); ML 8; AL C.
- F2: AC 5 [14]; hp 8; MV 60' (20'); Att 1 × sword (1-8); SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2); ML 8; AL C.
- M2: AC 9 [10]; hp 6; MV 120' (40'); Att 1 × dagger or spell (1-4); SV D13 W14 P13 B16 S15 (M2); ML 8; AL C. Spells: hold portal, magic missile.
- C2: AC 5 [14]; hp 8; MV 60' (20'); Att 1 × mace (1-6); SV D11 W12 P14 B16 S15 (C2); ML 8; AL C. Spell: cure light wounds.

**12. The Priest of Zargon.**
A Priest of Zargon (AC 5 [14]; C3; hp 12; MV 60' (20'); #AT 1 × mace; D 1-6; SV D11 W12 P14 B16 S15 (C3); ML 8; AL C; Spells: cure light wounds ×2) with **twelve fanatic followers** (AC 9 [10]; F1; hp 4; MV 120' (40'); #AT 1 × sword; D 1-8; SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1); ML 12; AL C). They are on a holy quest and **insist the party come along** (the priest will try to trick the party and take them prisoner).
