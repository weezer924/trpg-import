## Adventure Background

*(PDF p.3)*

The Lost City is a long-term adventure designed to be completed in several sessions. The party, lost in a desert, finds an ancient ruined city. A step-pyramid is the largest building in the ruins. The basic adventure is the exploration of the upper five tiers of the pyramid (rooms 1–58). An expanded adventure outlines the lower pyramid (tiers 6–10) and an underground city beneath the desert.

- **Party Size:** 6–10 characters of levels 1–3.
- **Advancement:** Successful characters will likely reach 3rd level by the time they finish the third dungeon level (Tier 5).
- **Supplies:** The characters have no way to replace equipment or supplies except by finding items in the Lost City. They begin with empty waterskins and no rations.
- **Pacing:** The DM should not move the action too quickly; give the players time to rest and regain spells between sessions.
- **Interaction:** The three factions and their plots provide opportunities for character interaction beyond simple combat. The DM should plan how faction members react to the party's approaches — talking, attacking, or waiting.

## History — The Fall of Cynidicea

*(PDF p.4)*

Centuries ago, **Cynidicea** was the capital of a rich and fertile kingdom. Its people reclaimed much land from the desert, especially during the reign of **King Alexander** — the last and greatest king of Cynidicea. Upon his death, a huge step-pyramid was raised in his honour.

The fall began when workers, digging under the pyramid, discovered the lair of **Zargon** — a roughly humanoid creature larger than most humans, with twelve tentacles in place of arms and legs, the head of a giant lizard, and a large black horn in the middle of its forehead.

Zargon killed most workers and hunted prey through the streets at night. The city guard could not kill it. The rulers began sending criminals from the jails into the pyramid as food.

A **cult of Zargon** arose that worshipped the monster as a god. The worship of the old gods — **Gorm**, **Usamigaras**, and **Madarua** — was forsaken. The cult members sought oblivion in rare wines and bizarre drugs. Workers no longer repaired the irrigation ditches. Rich land turned to desert. The army lost its discipline.

**Barbarian warriors** stormed over the walls and destroyed the city. The only survivors were those who had fled underground to the vast catacombs beneath the city. There, led by the Priests of Zargon, they rebuilt a smaller city around a huge underground lake fed by channels cut through solid rock.

Above, drifting sands covered the original city, and Cynidicea was lost in the vastness of the desert.

## The Cynidiceans

*(PDF p.4)*

Generation after generation have lived underground. Though still human, their skin has become very pale and their hair is bone-white. They have developed infravision and attack with **-1 to hit in full daylight** (like goblins).

Every Cynidicean wears a stylized mask (animal or human face — made of wood, papier-mâché, or metal, decorated with beads, bones, feathers, and jewels). Most wear fancy clothes, flashy jewellery, and carry short swords. Some paint their bodies with bright colours.

**They are a dying race.** Each generation is smaller than the last. Most Cynidiceans have forgotten that an outside world exists, living most of their lives in strange dream worlds. The times when they seem normal are becoming fewer as the dreams replace reality.

**Population:** About 1,000 adult Cynidiceans live in the underground city.

## Factions

*(PDF p.4–5)*

A few Cynidiceans are nearly normal. They are trying to restore the worship of the old gods and stop the slow death of their society. The three factions do not get along well — each is sure that only its members know the proper way to restore the lost greatness of Cynidicea. When members of different factions meet, they often argue or fight. Cooperation is rare but possible.

### Brotherhood of Gorm

- **God:** Gorm — god of war, storms, and justice
- **Alignment:** Lawful
- **Members:** Male fighters
- **Appearance:** Golden masks (long-haired, bearded man with stern gaze), iron chain mail over blue tunics, steel helmets
- **Symbol:** Small blue lightning bolt tattooed on right shoulder
- **Beliefs:** Justice tempered by mercy. Worship on the 4th day of each week. Lightning storms are holy.
- **Membership:** Full — male fighters, dwarves, halflings, and elves (Lawful). Lesser — any character.
- **Leader:** Kanadius, Grand Master (see `npcs.md`)
- **Locations:** Rooms 11, 12 (Tier 2), Room 24 (Tier 3), Stronghold L (Underground City)

### Magi of Usamigaras

- **God:** Usamigaras — god of healing, messengers, and thieves
- **Alignment:** Neutral
- **Members:** Magic-users
- **Appearance:** Silver masks (smiling winged child), silver daggers, rainbow-coloured robes
- **Symbol:** Silver star lines on right palm
- **Beliefs:** Stars and planets determine holy days. The Magi record celestial positions.
- **Membership:** Full — magic-users, elves, clerics, and thieves. Lesser — any character.
- **Leader:** Auriga Sirkinos, Chief Mage (see `npcs.md`)
- **Locations:** Rooms 14–16 (Tier 3), Room 19a (food stores), Stronghold M (Underground City)

### Warrior Maidens of Madarua

- **God:** Madarua — goddess of birth, death, and the changing seasons
- **Alignment:** Neutral
- **Members:** Female fighters
- **Appearance:** Bronze masks (beautiful woman), bronze chain mail over green tunics
- **Symbol:** Small sickle-shaped scar on inside of left wrist
- **Beliefs:** Special holy day at the beginning of each of the four seasons.
- **Membership:** Full — female fighters, dwarves, halflings, and elves. Lesser — any character.
- **Leader:** Pandora, Madarua's Champion (see `npcs.md`)
- **Locations:** Rooms 21–23 (Tier 3), Stronghold N (Underground City)

### Faction Interaction

- Characters showing signs of belonging to one faction (wearing masks/necklaces) will find other factions unfriendly or hostile.
- **Reaction Roll (2d6):**
  - 9–12: Party will be asked to join
  - 6–8: Party may join, but will not be asked
  - 2–5: Attacked or driven off as enemies/spies
  - **Modifier:** -3 if showing signs of another faction; +2 if party proves they have defeated another cult.
- Faction rooms can serve as "safe" areas (free from wandering monsters; rest, rememorize spells).
- Faction supplies (masks, robes, etc.) are kept in underground city strongholds; takes about half a day for a messenger to fetch them.

## Priests of Zargon

*(PDF p.5)*

A fourth faction. Found mainly in areas outside the basic adventure (lower pyramid, underground city). They serve the evil monster Zargon and control the underground city. The Temple of Zargon (area K) is the largest building in the underground city.

## Players' Background

*(PDF p.5)*

Days ago, the party joined a desert caravan. Halfway across the desert, a terrible sandstorm struck, separating them from the rest of the caravan. When the storm died down, the caravan was nowhere in sight and the desert was unrecognizable.

They headed east (the same direction the caravan had been going). Days passed. Their mounts died and they drank the last of their water. Two days after their water ran out, they stumbled upon stone blocks sticking out of a sand dune — the remains of a ruined city.

In the centre of the city towered a **step-pyramid** with five tiers, each 20' high. The bottom tier was almost completely covered with sand. On top of the highest tier were **three 30' tall statues:**

| Position | Statue | Represents |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| Left | Strong, bearded man holding a balance and a lightning bolt | **Gorm** |
| Centre | Winged child with two snakes twined about its body, holding a wand and coins | **Usamigaras** |
| Right | Beautiful woman holding a sheaf of wheat and a sword | **Madarua** |

On the south side, a ramp with stairs leads from the ground to the top tier. A secret door was found in the side of the ramp, level with the floor of the top tier.

## Adventure Flow

*(PDF p.3–4)*

1. **Entry (Tier 1):** Party enters through secret door on top of pyramid. Room 1 has gas trap and crossbow trap. Three bronze cylinders lead up to statues (speaking tubes, levers) and down to Room 6.
2. **Exploration — Tier 2 (Rooms 2–12):** Storage rooms, creatures, first faction contact (Brotherhood of Gorm in rooms 11–12). Party must find food and water soon.
3. **Faction Diplomacy — Tier 3 (Rooms 13–24):** Headquarters of all three factions. Revolving passage connects areas. Characters may join a faction for safe rest, supplies, and equipment.
4. **Burial Chambers — Tier 4 (Rooms 25–40):** Tombs of King Alexander (room 34, guarded by banshee), Queen Zenobia (room 32, now a wight), and nobles. Major treasure and danger. Trap door at 27a leads down to Tier 5.
5. **Base Level — Tier 5 (Rooms 41–58):** Main pyramid entrance (buried under sand). Spirit of Demetrius (room 45) possesses a character to hunt his evil twin Darius (room 58). Chapel with fountains (room 51) provides water. Gambling room (room 57) + final confrontation.
6. **Expanded Adventure (Tiers 6–10):** Lower pyramid with increasingly dangerous monsters. Tier 10 (room 100) is Zargon's lair.
7. **Underground City:** Further adventures — unite the factions, destroy Zargon's horn, raid the Temple of Zargon, and more.

## Further Adventures

*(PDF p.26)*

After the pyramid has been explored, the DM may create further adventures in the underground city. Suggested adventures (all working toward rebuilding Cynidicean civilization):

1. **Uniting the Cynidiceans:** Find a way to bring order to the underground city — unite the factions, defeat all but one, or take over directly.
2. **Destroying Zargon's Horn:** Zargon can regenerate from its horn. The horn must be dropped into the lava pit at the Eye of Zargon (area Q) to permanently destroy the creature.
3. **The Ambush:** The Priests of Zargon (possibly aided by goblins/hobgoblins) ambush the party.
4. **Raid on the Temple of Zargon:** Rescue a kidnapped character, or join a faction raid on the temple.
5. **The Prophecy:** A relic of great power lies hidden on the Island of Death (area B). A parchment prophecy provides clues to the proper path.
6. **The Antidote:** A cure for the Cynidiceans' dream-state requires a small white flower found only in the lower catacombs. A mysterious guardian tests the party's worthiness.
7. **The True King:** A descendant of the royal line still lives in hiding. The party must protect and advise the heir until it is safe to take the throne.
8. **Leaving the Desert:** Wilderness adventure to find the way out of the desert.
