## Background 背景

Baron Bruno（布鲁诺男爵）, a nobleman of the Kingdom of Alania（阿拉尼亚王国） in the northeastern part of Lodoss Island, received a royal command to head to the capital Alan to suppress a rebellion. However, he had a reason why he could not leave Norvis（诺尔比斯） with peace of mind. What worried him was the possibility that his young wife Alda（阿尔达）'s heart might change.

Six months ago, the Baron used a love potion to steal Alda away from her knight husband, but because the potion was imperfect, **Alda would fall in love with the first man she saw each morning**. So the Baron decided to confine his wife to a room in his separate villa while he was away.

> **GM 摘要**：男爵半年前用不完美的爱情药水夺走他人未婚妻 Alda；药水副作用是「每天早上对第一眼看到的男人坠入爱河」。男爵被王命召往首都平叛，担心妻子被人看到，遂雇 PC 看守。

## Introduction 序章

The adventurers are staying in the town of Norvis, looking for suitable work. In town, there are quite a few jobs suitable for adventurers, such as bounty hunting and caravan escort work, but it's hard to find employment because the whereabouts of the wanted criminals are unknown, or the escort jobs have poor conditions. One morning, when they wake up with their money almost running out, they find that overnight, posters have been put up all over the town of Norvis.

The content of the poster reads:

> **"Trustworthy adventurers urgently needed. Meals included. High pay guaranteed— Baron Bruno."**

To respond to the recruitment, they need to go to the Baron's mansion, but if they gather information about the Baron beforehand, they can hear the following.

Baron Bruno is an elderly man, close to 50 years old, and the Baron's family is of a very prestigious lineage. Moreover, he manages his territory well and is wealthy. However, he has strong preferences when it comes to women, and people were worried that he had no heir because he hadn't married until this age. Then, six months ago, he married a woman named Alda who was about 20 years old. The Baron dotes on her so much that he apparently keeps her from public view out of his deep affection.

This information is something that anyone who has lived in Norvis for a long time would know, and can be obtained at suitable places like taverns. Here, the GM should have the characters gathering information make a **Magic Skill check（魔法技能检定）**. If successful, they can hear the following story from a middle-aged woman who looks very much like a gossip enthusiast:

> The Baroness Alda apparently had a young lover originally. That man was a knight of Alania, but he apparently caused some scandal and was expelled from the knight order. The Baron seemingly comforted the heartbroken Alda and made her his wife.

**The latter half of this rumor is misinformation spread by the Baron himself.** Alda certainly did have a knight lover, but the truth is that when he learned she had been stolen from him with a potion, he left the knight order and has been waiting for an opportunity to get her back.

## At the Baron's Mansion 男爵的宅邸

The Baron's mansion is located in the southeast of town. Adventurers arrive without incident. There are two guards at the gate, and when they state your business, they are directed to the back entrance. As they are led into a large room, they see shabby-looking men being driven out through a door at the far end.

When the PCs are shown into the back room, an elderly man is seated behind a desk. The man is **Drahn（德拉恩）, the Baron's loyal steward**, and the Baron is watching through a peephole in a painting hanging on the wall. When Drahn sees the PCs, his face shows relief, and he mutters things like "finally, someone who looks decent has arrived" and "there have been so many people just looking for a free meal." The Baron must depart the following morning, so there is no time to take his time selecting candidates. He simply looks at the PCs' appearance, taps on the wall, and sends the signal of approval. Drahn immediately begins to explain the details of the request.

The employer **does not explain the true circumstances** and states that the content of the request is to protect Baroness Alda while the Baron is away. The reason given is that there is a man who has an unrequited love for her and has been targeting her all along.

### Job Terms 委托条件

| Item               | Detail                                                                                  |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Duration           | From the suppression of the rebellion until the Baron returns (probably about a month) |
| Location           | The Baron's villa, half a day's journey north of Norvis                                |
| Payment            | **20 gold per day**                                                                     |
| Special Conditions | If Alda is abducted, PCs must do everything in their power to bring her back; bonus considered |

When the PCs accept the request, they are told to stay at the mansion that night and attend a dinner party. That evening, they dine with the Baron while wearing luxurious clothes lent to them. Alda is also present. The Baron is an amiable man approaching 50 years of age who entertains the PCs. Alda is (naturally) a beautiful woman who makes a show of her good relationship with the Baron (after all, he falls in love with her anew every morning). When the meal is finished, the Baron and Alda bid them goodnight and take their leave.

## The Baron's Villa 男爵的别墅

The next day, you depart for the Baron's villa. In addition to the PCs and Alda, **Drahn and five servants** accompany the PCs. They witness a scene of Alda bidding a reluctant farewell to the Baron. Alda and the servants ride in a carriage, while the others travel on horseback (they will provide horses if you don't have any).

After half a day's journey, PCs safely arrive at the villa. The villa is a single-story wooden building, with rooms arranged as shown in the diagram.

### Drahn's Security Instructions 守卫指令

At the villa, Drahn gives the following instructions regarding security:

1. **Alda must not step outside her room**, and PCs must not enter it either.
2. The **five servants** will take care of meals and other needs.
3. The **key to Alda's room** is held by Drahn and the head servant.
4. The building must be guarded inside and out **around the clock**. However, the method is left to the PCs' discretion.

### The First 12 Days 前 12 天

For a while, no particular incidents occur. During this time, **they never see Alda**. The servants seem to be keeping her company. Drahn never enters Alda's room either.

### The Storm — Day 12 第 12 日的暴风雨

On the morning of the 12th day, the weather deteriorates, and a storm begins. The storm continues for a full day. **That night**, anyone in the hallway in front of Alda's room must make a **Magic Skill check**. If successful, they faintly hear a thud and a scream from inside the room.

The knight who lost his beloved Alda has come to take her back. His name is **Richardson（理查森）**, and he was a knight of Alania. After losing his beloved, he left the knightly order and learned that Alda had been drugged. His actions since then will become clear later, but during that time he obtained earth powers, which he used to dig a tunnel and infiltrate from beneath the floor.

If the PCs don't have the key and try to break down the door to enter, they must succeed at a **Fighting Skill check（战斗技能检定）**.

### Inside Alda's Room 进入房间后

Upon entering the room, there is a hole in the center of the floor, and an earth elemental stands beside it. If PCs succeed at a **Magic Skill check** here, they notice that it is considerably smaller than a normal earth elemental. **This earth elemental is a Lesser Gnome summoned using an item.** It has been commanded to attack anyone attempting to enter the hole.

When its LP reaches zero, the gnome vanishes, leaving behind a **copper talisman** on the floor. This talisman summons a Lesser Gnome that obeys commands when the keyword is spoken. When LP reaches zero or **one hour** passes, it returns to being a charm. **This talisman can only be used 2 more times.**

On the surface of the talisman is depicted a stylized wolf crest. If a sorcerer succeeds at a **Magic Skill check**, they will recall the sorcerer who uses this crest — a sorcerer named **White（怀特）** who is quite famous in Alania, especially in Norvis. If they succeed at another Magic Skill check here, they will remember that he should have set up residence in **a cave in the mountains about 3 days north of Norvis**. This information can also be learned at the **sorcerers' guild in Norvis**.

The hole in the floor continues for **50 meters** before emerging above ground. From there, the hoofprints of a horse remain clearly visible in the soil softened by rain. Richardson is heading toward the cave where White lives, but since it is a journey of more than 2 days, **they cannot track him unless they succeed at a Thief Skill check at -20**.

Drahn orders the PCs to search for Alda's whereabouts and bring her back, then withdraws from the villa and returns to Norvis.

## The Sorcerer White's Cave 魔术师怀特的洞窟

The sorcerer **White is a high-level sorcerer** who lives a life of research deep in the mountains north of Norvis. His reputation in Norvis is that of an **eccentric but excellent sorcerer**. The cave can be found without difficulty.

### The Lava Corridor 熔岩走廊

Torches are hung here and there from the entrance, so no light is needed. As you proceed, the temperature gradually rises. The reason becomes immediately apparent. **You pass through a location where half of the corridor is exposed lava.** In this area, the temperature approaches 40 degrees. If you succeed at a **Magic Skill check**, you recall that the mountains in this area are not volcanic.

Hidden in the lava is a monster called a **Corey（科瑞）** that White uses as a guardian. A Corey is a humanoid creature made of molten lava, **2 meters tall**, which White captured from a volcano 10 years ago. When the PCs attempt to pass by the lava pool, it leaps out from the lava and blocks their path.

> ⚠️ **Level 1 characters cannot stand against this monster.** The GM should be careful not to kill the PCs with Corey. After fighting for a few rounds, when the PCs realize Corey's power and panic, **White appears**.

### White Intervenes 怀特登场

The sorcerer stops Corey's movement and admonishes the PCs:

> "This is not a goblin cave but my residence. You must not enter without permission when you have no business here."

If the PCs explain the situation or show the talisman, **White invites them inside**. The PCs are led into a room that looks like a laboratory. It appears the owner is slovenly by nature, as the room is quite cluttered. White offers cold water from a pitcher, saying it must have been hot, and then answers the PCs' questions.

Richardson obtained the sorcerers' cooperation on the condition that he become a **test subject for a certain experiment**. The mage lent him the talisman and agreed to prepare an antidote if he brought Alda. Therefore, the mage White knows all the circumstances.

### Richardson Arrives 理查森抵达

When the conversation with the PCs settles down, the back door opens, and **Richardson enters**. He says, "I finally found it," and notices the PCs. White introduces Richardson and receives the map he brought, confirming, "This is it, this is it." This map shows the location where the **Lesser World Tree（小世界树）** grows, and the leaves of this tree are necessary to dispel the effects of the love potion.

### The Pitcher Mishap 水壶意外

Richardson looks at the water the PC was drinking and points out:

> "Wasn't what was in that pitcher the love potion with the same effect that I made as a trial?"

**The unlucky White had brought out the wrong one.**

> *If a PC has drunk the love potion, of course, they will wake up due to the potion's power and fall in love with the first person of the opposite sex they see. Naturally, this will cause complications, but it's **not powerful enough to make them try to kill a romantic rival**. The GM should handle the resulting confusion appropriately at their discretion.*

### Negotiation 谈判

Even if the PC hadn't drunk the water, **Richardson asks to be let off the hook**. Furthermore, while he cannot offer as much money as the Baron, he proposes that if they cooperate, he will give them his **family heirloom sword and ring** and **teach them the keyword for that talisman**.

Regardless of how the negotiations turn out, **White will not permit fighting inside the cave**. He demonstrates his power as a high-level sorcerer to stop them by force.

If negotiations are successful, White will explain about the Lesser World Tree:

> It is said that in the **Forest of No Return（无归森林）**, there is a giant **World Tree（世界树）**, but besides that, there are also great trees called **Lesser World Trees**. Both are protected by the forest elves, and it is said that those who try to harm the trees cannot approach them.

The antidote this time can also be made from World Tree leaves, but the World Tree is deep in the forest, and only the forest elves know its exact location. Moreover, it will be heavily guarded. So they decided to substitute with **Lesser World Tree leaves** instead. Even among elves, few remember the Lesser World Trees, and even the elf among the PCs has only heard of them. White remembered that there was a map in his library and had Richardson search for it.

According to the map, the **Lesser World Tree is located at the northern edge of the Forest of No Return**. From here, it will be **about a week's journey one way**.

In another room of the cave, **Alda has been put to sleep with medicine**. She has already fallen in love with Richardson, but since it's just the power of the potion, they decided to have her sleep while they're away.

## Lesser World Tree 小世界树

The last day of the week-long journey is spent traveling through the **Forest of No Return**. It's a maze-like forest, but thanks to the map, they don't get lost. On the seventh day, they discover a giant tree that must be **50 meters tall**. This is the **Lesser World Tree**.

The trunk is **nearly 10 meters in diameter**, and around it a **Wood Golem（木魔像）** slowly circles at a constant speed. The Wood Golem completes **one circuit in 3 rounds**. Current elves do not use sorcerer magic and cannot create golems, but whether they used it in the distant past or had connections with the ancient kingdom, this golem has been set to guard the Lesser World Tree.

### Climbing Procedure 攀爬流程

To climb to the height where the Lesser World Tree's leaves are, they must succeed at **3 Thief Skill checks, taking 3 rounds to climb**. They must either defeat the golem or climb while evading its watch, but **if evading, they must hurry and climb while the golem is on the opposite side**.

If they fail the first check, the golem spots them and combat begins. Others can fight as decoys, but **the golem will not leave the Lesser World Tree**. Since trees grow densely close by, **ranged weapons cannot be used**.

## Ending 结局

If PCs bring back the leaves of the Lesser World Tree, they can make an antidote. **If they bring back extras, White will buy them for 1000 gold.** If they drink the medicine that White makes for them after a day, the love potion's effect will disappear.

If they return to Norvis, they will learn the fact that **the Baron died in battle**. Since there is no heir, the mansion will be closed. Therefore, **PCs cannot receive their reward** here.

> **GM 提示**：男爵死讯让 PC 拿不到合约工资（20 g/day × ~一个月）；但若多采世界树叶卖给 White，可得 1000 g 补偿。Richardson 答应给的家传剑和戒指仍兑现，护符关键词亦传授。

## Experience Points 经验值

In addition to the points from defeated monsters, **mission points** will be awarded:

- **300 points** if you were able to save Alda and Richardson
- **100 points** if you coldly brought Alda back（即按男爵原合同把 Alda 抓回去交差）
