## Background 背景

The accursed island of Lodoss was once part of the mainland in ancient times. It was separated and became an island before the establishment of the ancient kingdom. **Points that respond to force fields** were installed on the island to move it. Though this magical technology has long been forgotten, the **force field points placed in four locations** on the island remain.

While it's no longer possible to move the island, **if you apply a slight amount of force to the points, it's still possible to devastate the region with earthquakes**.

> **GM 摘要**：罗德岛曾是大陆的一部分。古代王国之前，岛上被设置 4 个"力场点（Point）"用于移动整座岛。如今技术失传，但仍可微弱激发它来制造大地震。本场冒险中，反派意图利用其中一个 Point 摧毁阿拉尼亚。

## Introduction 序章

The scenario begins in a town somewhere in **Alania**. The wizard **White** speaks through the character with **the lowest IN among the PCs**. The GM should choose the timing for this at the most entertaining moment. There are many situations that could create confusion, such as in a tavern or a town square.

White's message is as follows:

> "It's been a while. You gave me quite a bit of entertainment last time, and I thank you for your efforts. As a token of my gratitude, I thought I'd introduce you to a simple job."

The job White offers is to **help him move**. He wants them to transport his belongings from **the cave north of Norvis（诺尔比斯北方的洞窟）** to **another cave north of Tarba（塔尔巴北方的洞窟）**.

### Compensation 报酬

Since there may be greedy characters (and players, too?), let's start with the compensation. White lets each person choose between **cash or an item**:

| Choice | Detail                                                                                       |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cash   | **500 GP** per person                                                                        |
| Item   | One magical item from those White designates (junk that no longer has value to him); no guarantee that the desired item is available |

The scene of negotiating with the character White is possessing should be quite a sight—it will look like someone talking to themselves in two different voices.

If the PCs accept, White tells them to **pick up a wagon at a certain shop in Norvis** before coming. The shop has been contacted in advance, and the shopkeeper will hand over the wagon with an uneasy look. How the contact was made is left to your imagination.

## Packing 打包

They arrive at the cave without incident. White comes out to greet them so they won't be attacked by **Corey**（that lava monster from Episode 1）. On the way into the depths of the cave, White summons Corey and **seals it in a jar**. He hands the jar to the PCs, saying it's also part of the luggage.

> "If you open the jar, Corey will emerge and attack those nearby. If you're lucky, it might remember your faces," — White

Indeed, if they fall into danger during the journey and open the jar, Corey will fight their enemies for them. **It remembers the PCs' faces and won't attack them, but it cannot be controlled and will depart to parts unknown.** If that place happens to be a forest, it will cause a wildfire.

### The Sealed Black Box 密封黑箱

The laboratory appears to have been cleaned up to some extent (though it only looks even more cluttered!), and in the center of the room sits a **black box one meter square**. White says that **it contains important items and must never be opened**. This box is sealed with magic and **cannot be opened without using the Unlock spell**. The contents are all chemicals of unknown use.

Those who chose an item as their reward may take **one thing other than this box**. The item selection may be made at any time.

### Damon the Mouse Guide 老鼠导游达蒙

White doesn't stay with them the whole time. On the contrary, he has important business to attend to and **leaves the cave first, saying he'll meet them at the destination**. At that time, he gave them a mouse to serve as a guide.

It's a **white mouse wearing small glasses, named Damon（达蒙）**. After White leaves, this mouse directs the packing. Damon is a mouse with an attitude that doesn't match its small body, and it criticizes the PCs' every action.

Damon is a highly intelligent mouse, but physically, it can only do what a mouse can do. **It has only 1 LP and will die if injured even slightly.** During the adventure, about all it can do is call for help or gnaw through rope.

### Cave Layout 洞窟布局

White's cave is divided into three areas: **the laboratory, the storeroom, and the living quarters**. The laboratory and storeroom contain the following items.

> **价格说明**：Each has a price attached, which is the price **if sold in a large city** such as Norvis or Alan. However, **if you fail a Magic Skill check, it becomes half**. The prices here are **selling prices, not the prices at which they would be displayed in shops**. Damon pompously reveals the items' abilities.

## Journey to the New Home 前往新家

It's about a **10-day journey** from the cave north of Norvis to the new home north of Tarba (which is also a cave). The journey will be made while protecting the wagon.

### Encounter with Bandits 山贼遭遇

About **two days away from Tarba**, on a night when the terrain is starting to become mountainous, while the PCs are camping, **they hear a faint scream in the distance**. The scream is the voice of a young woman, and when approaching in that direction, a young woman is being chased by **two bandit-looking men**.

All PCs who approach make a **Thief Skill check**. If even one person fails, the bandits notice the PCs and flee in a panic. Even if they don't notice, **they will flee after being attacked for 1 round**.

#### Priss the Decoy 诱饵普莉丝

The girl introduces herself as **Priss（普莉丝）from Tarba Village**. She claims she came to gather medicinal herbs, got lost, and has been wandering for two days now—but in reality, **she's in cahoots with bandits** and plans to act in coordination with them the following night when the PCs are asleep.

Her specific action will be to **hide the PCs' weapons** shortly before the bandits attack. Any PC who is awake, such as one standing watch, may attempt a **Magic Skill check at -10%** to notice her actions. Characters who fail the check do not notice their weapons being quietly hidden.

#### The Night Attack 夜袭

That night, **four bandits attack, not counting Priss** (if any were defeated the previous night, the number is reduced accordingly). They are **armed with short swords and leather armor and carry no cash**. In the end, they are part of a bandit group that attacks caravans heading to Tarba.

## The Wizard White's New Residence 怀特的新居

The cave that Damon guides them to is located **nearly a full day's walk north of Tarba**. According to the people of Tarba Village, **a band of goblins appears in that area, so the villagers stay away**. They are also told that **some of the goblins can use magic**.

If they ask whether anything unusual has happened, even if it seems unrelated, they'll be told that there was a **fairly large earthquake yesterday**. Earthquakes happen maybe once a year in this area, so the villagers are somewhat excited about it. They'll share various stories—how such-and-such house collapsed, how the cows got spooked and won't give milk, and so on.

### Cave Entrance 洞窟入口

The cave entrance is located **midway up a sheer cliff face**. In front of the entrance lie **three goblin corpses**. The wounds on the bodies are of two types: those made by **swords** and those made by **blunt weapons**. There are signs of a struggle in the area, with many footprints mixed together.

If successful on a **Thief Skill check**, one can determine from the footprints that the group that attacked the goblins consisted of **4 or 5 people**.

### The Intruders 入侵者

The ones who killed the goblins and entered the cave are a party of four led by a **Priest of Phalaris（法拉利斯神官）**. The priest learned through a method different from White's that a **force field point exists in this cave**, and arrived two days ago.

They slaughtered all the resisting goblins and goblin shamans, reaching the point in the deepest part of the cave. There, the priest is conducting various trial-and-error attempts to control it. **Yesterday's earthquake occurred as a result of this.**

> **关于 Phalaris**：罗德岛战记中黑暗神 Phalaris（法拉利斯）的信徒——与至高神 Pharis 法利斯（光明神）拼写极相近但完全相反阵营。本书译作 Phalaris 处即指黑暗一方。

### Cave Layout 洞窟布局

The cave continues deeper while curving gently to the left. The passage is **about 5 meters wide**. After walking for a while, the passage splits left and right into a **T-junction**, where goblin corpses lie scattered.

There are **six bodies**, and **5 meters down the right passage is the corpse of a goblin shaman**. The goblin shaman's cause of death is wounds from the **Wounds spell**, which is immediately apparent to a priest.

#### Room ① — Goblin Lair（右转）

Turning right at the T-junction leads to **Room ①**, where the goblins live. Here, **goblin children and females have been massacred**. There is nothing particularly noteworthy in this room.

#### Second T-Junction（左转后）

Turning left at the T-junction and proceeding for a while, you come to **another T-junction with a door visible straight ahead**.

This door is a **false door**—opening it reveals only a wall. Moreover, the spot where one would stand in front of the door to open it is a **pit trap**. The damage is **9D2**, and armor can reduce the damage.

> 译者注：Yes, nine D2, it's not a translation mistake.

#### Room ② — Goblin Treasure Hoard（第二个 T 字路口右转）

Going right at the second T-junction, there is a **wooden door**. The door is half-open, and beyond it is **Room ②**. Here, treasure plundered by the goblins is stored. The treasure consists mostly of gold coins and gems, placed in several boxes.

There are **signs of people having entered, but the treasure has been completely untouched**. The total treasure in this room amounts to:

- **500 gold coins**
- **1000 gold worth of gems**

#### Room ③ — Stone Chamber with Ancient Script（第二个 T 字路口左转）

When PCs proceed left at the second T-junction, they'll find another half-open door here as well. **Room ③** beyond it is different from all the previous rooms, with **stone walls forming a rectangular chamber**. At the far wall of this room is a **metal double door**, while the other walls are **densely covered with ancient script**.

If PCs succeed at a **Magic Skill check**, they can determine that the walls describe the method used to **separate Lodoss Island from the continent**. However, **nothing is written about how to control the Point**.

The lock on the far door has already been unlocked. However, to guard against intruders, the Priest of Phalaris has installed a **new alarm trap**. If they examine the door, make a **Thief Skill check** — if they fail, they'll trigger the trap. This will create a loud rattling sound. If they trigger this trap, the Priest of Phalaris in the area beyond will be **fully alert and ready to engage the PCs**.

#### Room ④ — The Point Chamber 力场点之间

When PCs open the far door, a corridor continues straight ahead for a while. A **pale blue light** is leaking from Room ④. This is because **a Point in the form of a massive crystal formation is glowing**.

Room ④ has a height of **about 20 meters from floor to ceiling**, and the corridor continues at around the **10-meter height**. To descend to the floor from here, you'll need to use the **installed ladder**. **PCs can descend 5 meters per round.**

If PCs haven't triggered the alarm in Room ③, the Priest of Phalaris is in the middle of trial and error with the Point, while their minions are resting nearby.

### Combat at the Point 力场点之战

Priest of Phalaris will fight using the following tactics:

| Role        | Tactic                                                                                                  |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Priest**  | Use **Mind Blast** on PC characters who appear to be magic-users, then heal allies' wounds until approached |
| **Thieves** | Shoot arrows at characters climbing down the ladder. Characters hit and wounded must make a **Thief Skill check at +20** — if they fail, they fall |
| **Warriors**| Always fight to **protect the priest**                                                                  |

In the priest's possession is a **report to the temple**. It states that they have succeeded in controlling the Point and that it's possible to cause the collapse of all of Alania (this is his bluff—he was planning to report after succeeding in the control).

## Conclusion 结局

In the end, **White arrives two days later**. He, of course, knows about the Point and was planning to move here to study it. He was delayed because he needed to gather materials necessary for his research, and also because **he figured some animals or something would probably be living there anyway, so he thought he'd have the adventurers clean them out, too**.

White will look at the state of the cave and give each PC a **bonus of 200 GP**. However, if the PCs have stolen anything without permission or taken two or more items, **he will strongly demand their return** (don't forget that **Damon witnessed everything**).
