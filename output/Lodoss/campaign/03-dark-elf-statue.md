## Background 背景

The strongest race on Lodoss Island might very well be the **dark elves**（黑暗精灵）. The reasons they cannot replace humans are their **low fertility rate and lack of cooperation**, which prevents them from forming large groups.

Even such dark elves were once unified during the time when the ancient kingdom still flourished, under the dark elf hero **Fazan**（法赞）. The dark elves, having become a large force, grew to threaten the existence of the ancient kingdom.

However, this was not due to Fazan's personal power, but rather the power of an item called **Fazan's Statue**（法赞之像）. Therefore, when the statue was stolen by a human thief, the unity collapsed within a few years.

And now, there is an ambitious dark elf who has learned of the legend of Fazan's Statue. He has led his clan and hobgoblins into **Alania**（阿拉尼亚）.

> **GM 摘要**：远古时代黑暗精灵英雄 Fazan 借「法赞之像」之力统一族人，威胁古王国。塑像被人类盗贼偷走后联盟瓦解。如今一名野心勃勃的黑暗精灵率族人潜入阿拉尼亚地区，意图寻回此像、重启黑暗精灵霸权。

## Introduction 序章

The party is traveling from **Alania to Kanon**（卡农）（if the location is inconvenient for use in the middle of a campaign, please set it to an appropriate position at the GM's discretion）, and just as the sun is setting, they come upon a village called **Koh**（柯村）. When they stop at the village seeking lodging, the village seems somehow bustling. Young men appear to have gathered in the village square for a discussion.

When they notice the party, an elderly man approaches and speaks to them. The man is the village chief of Koh and introduces himself as **Raguru**（拉古鲁）. When he asks about the party's destination, he frowns and says that **a village ahead seems to have been occupied by monsters**, so it would be better not to proceed. He recommends that they stay in this village until the army arrives, since he's sent a messenger to the city. Of course, it's because having adventurers around would be reassuring if they're attacked by monsters.

Even if the party is reluctant, he insists, saying *"please, please,"* and has a feast prepared. While alcoholic beverages are not served much (since they can't have you fight while drunk), they are treated to quite a lavish meal. In addition, the village chief proposes that **if they protect the village until the army arrives, he'll pay a daily wage of 10 GP per day**. Of course, meals are all-you-can-eat.

## The Story of a Man Named Kazak 卡扎克的故事

If the party accepts, after the meal is finished, the village chief will tell them the details. The story goes as follows:

Not even half a day's journey from the village of Koh lies a somewhat larger village called **Filcario**（菲尔卡里奥）. In Filcario, a large market is held twice a week. Food and fodder are gathered from neighboring villages, and many people congregate there. **This morning, everyone who went to that market failed to return—except for one person.** The night before, Filcario had been occupied by monsters. A man named **Kazak**（卡扎克）, who was lucky enough to escape despite being seriously wounded, dragged his injured body back in the evening, driven by a sense of duty to warn the village. He was treated by a priest at the village's Pharis church, and his life was saved.

For information beyond this, they are told to ask Kazak himself and are guided to the church. In the center of the village stands a church so magnificent it seems out of place in such a rural village. It was built by a priest named **Greosias**（格雷奥西亚斯）, who was rumored to be destined to become the **Archbishop of Pharis in Alan**, but who shunned advancement and came to the countryside instead. Inside the church, Greosias is nursing Kazak, who lies in bed. When the village chief introduces the PCs, Greosias gives permission for a short visit.

Kazak has had the following experience. **It would be interesting to present this information not by having Kazak simply narrate it, but rather in the form of answers to questions.**

### What Kazak Has Seen 卡扎克所见

Kazak has seen several types of monsters. However, the only type he can identify with certainty is **goblins**; for the other monsters, he can only describe their appearance. Besides goblins, there are two other types: **large goblins (hobgoblins)** and **dark-skinned creatures with long ears (dark elves)**. As for numbers, based on what he saw, there were:

| Monster        | Count   |
| -------------- | ------: |
| Dark Elves     |       3 |
| Hobgoblins     |       1 |
| Goblins        | about 10 |

### The Legend of Fazan's Statue 法赞之像的传说

The thief who stole Fazan's Statue tried to sell it to a governor. The governor, fearing the statue's power, killed the thief and seized the statue. He then ordered a warrior to **hide it somewhere on the island**. The warrior's name was **Granada**（格拉纳达）. He left the city of **Kuudo**（库多）and headed east. The statue should be guarded by his descendants through the generations.

If the PCs ask whether there is a family named Granada, they are told that no such family line exists in this village. However, an old man informs them that **about 10 kilometers away lies a village that fell into ruin some 100 years ago, and that village's name was supposed to be Granada**. In fact, that village was named after the warrior, and Fazan's Statue is enshrined in the basement of the village church.

Regarding the monsters' activities, Kazak heard that they had gathered the villagers and were **interrogating them about whether they knew of a black statue resembling the dark elf himself**. After hiding and observing for a while, he overheard them extracting information about nearby villages, and **among the several villages mentioned, the name Koh came up**, so he felt he had to warn them and fled.

This is all he witnessed, but when the story of the dark elf statue comes up, please make a **magic skill check（魔法技能检定）** to see if anyone has any knowledge of the legend of Fazan's Statue. If successful, they recall the legend above.

## Information for the GM 给 GM 的形势整理

Let's organize the situation for the GM here.

In the village of **Filcario**, located less than half a day's distance from the village of **Koh** where the PCs are, there are **Dark Elves, Goblins, and Hobgoblins**. They intend to raid nearby villages one after another in search of the Statue of Fazan.

### 时间表

| Day   | 事件                                                                                       |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Day 1 | PCs 抵达 Koh 村                                                                            |
| Day 2 | **深夜**：黑暗精灵夜袭 Koh 村                                                              |
| Day 3 | **深夜**：若放任不管，黑暗精灵从 Granada 废墟取得 Fazan 之像                               |
| Day 4 | 阿拉尼亚军队抵达——但已为时已晚                                                            |

### Three Player Choices PC 的三种选择

1. **前往 Granada 废墟取得 Fazan 之像**：若清晨出发，取像后能在当夜赶回 Koh 村抵御袭击。若巧妙运用塑像，可在与黑暗精灵的战斗中获得优势（见 Granada 章节描述塑像的能力）。
2. **留在村中等待夜袭**：将于 Day 2 深夜（颇为「幸运」地）遭袭。若击退此次进攻，剧情基本就此解决。但若未对塑像采取行动，本地区终将再度被听闻传说的黑暗精灵威胁。
3. **奇袭 Filcario 村**：乍看冒险，但其实并不算坏选项。黑暗系生物白天会睡觉（且若在 Day 2 夜攻，黑暗精灵正外出袭击邻村，村中是空的）。

The situation **changes subtly depending on the PCs' actions**. In other words, if they are not in the village on the night of Day 2, the village of Koh will be attacked, and **all the villagers will be massacred**. Combat with the Dark Elves will also unfold completely differently depending on whether they possess the statue or not. The GM should always keep track of the Dark Elves' actions and pay attention to where they will encounter the PCs.

## The Dark Elves' Night Raid 黑暗精灵的夜袭

In the middle of the night on **Day 2 (midnight)**, the Dark Elves come to raid the village of Koh. Having raided other villages between Filcario and Koh without encountering much resistance, they are **completely off their guard**. Therefore, if traps have been set around the village, they will be quite effective.

### Traps 陷阱

Traps could include **pitfalls or ones using ropes**. Whether the traps are effective depends on how cleverly they were set. Have the character who took command (**a Scout would probably be best**) make a **thief skill check（盗贼技能检定）**, and if successful, **reduce the Dark Elves appearing hereafter by 1, Hobgoblins by 1, and Goblins by 3**. Of course, they fell into the traps and were rendered unable to fight, if not killed. If the skill check fails, the traps were discovered and unfortunately had no effect.

### Fences and Ranged Defense 栅栏与远程防御

Building fences and such would also be effective. In such cases, there would be effects like being able to **attack with ranged weapons for several rounds before close combat begins**. The GM makes these judgments, but please give adequate effects to appropriate ideas.

Fearing the monsters' night raid, the villagers have gathered at **the temple of Pharis** to sleep after sunset. The only one who can fight in the village is Priest **Greosias**.

The other young people cannot even defeat a goblin. However, they can hold out:

## Granada 格拉纳达废村

Granada was a village located **about 10 kilometers west of Koh**. It was a hidden village pioneered in the frontier by Granada, a warrior of the ancient kingdom, but when the ancient kingdom fell, and people came to live throughout all of Lodoss, it ended up being the oldest village in this area. However, **about 100 years ago, a mysterious plague spread**, and people abandoned the village, so now it is in ruins. The cause of the plague was **contaminated well water and was not actually a disease**, so it did not spread to the surrounding villages.

In the ruins, only **two buildings still retain their original form**: the **village chief's mansion** and the **temple**. The other buildings have crumbled away over the course of 100 years, leaving nothing but scattered rubble.

From the legend, the PCs cannot know where the statue of Fazan is hidden. Therefore, they will likely have to **search the ruined village thoroughly**.

### The Village Chief's Mansion 村长宅邸

Even though it's called the village chief's mansion, the PCs have no way of knowing that. Inside, most of the walls have collapsed, and it appears to be just one large room. The room is littered with the remains of furniture. **At the back is a fireplace.** If a **Detection** spell is used in the room, the area around the fireplace will glow.

When anyone approaches within 1 meter of the fireplace, **flames suddenly ignite**. White smoke rises, and the figure of a man materializes within it. The man is the village's last chief, and his name is, naturally, **Granada**. He is bound by a mission to protect the statue, which is why he remains in this place even as a ghost.

#### Granada's Power 格拉纳达的能力

The power he possesses is limited. It is the ability to **possess and control those who are willing to accept him**. This ability exists so he can relocate the statue if dark elves come to steal it. However, he requires the subject's consent each day, and if refused, he must withdraw. Even so, he can possess the statue itself, so he will follow it anywhere. **If someone carelessly tries to sell it, he will appear and interfere with the transaction.** However, there's no real harm in it.

#### The Negotiation 谈判

Granada questions the PCs about their intentions for visiting. If they mention the statue and that dark elves seeking it have come nearby, he falls into thought for a while. After some time, he nods to himself and says that **this place has finally been discovered, and the statue must be moved**. He then requests the PCs' cooperation. If he can move it to a safe place, his mission will be complete, and he can rest in peace.

As compensation, he proposes to **reveal the hiding place of a treasure sword that belonged to his ancestors** (he cannot offer anything more than this).

If the PCs agree, he explains that the statue is kept in the temple's basement, but that **a password must be spoken to open the final door**, so he asks to **possess one of the PCs**. If they absolutely refuse, he reluctantly tells them the password.

> **The password is "Kuudo（库多）."** It was probably chosen by his ancestors, who were overcome with homesickness.

He then tells them that if they **dig in the fireplace**, they will find a magic sword buried there. **This sword is a Longsword +2.**

When the PCs head to the temple, Granada gives them a final warning. There are **traps** set by his ancestors in the dungeon, and there are **guardians** as well. He doesn't know the locations of the traps, but he can guess about the guardians: since they've been protecting the statue for all these years, they're likely **golems or undead**. His ancestors apparently also conducted research on golems and undead, and there used to be quite a few books on those subjects.

### The Temple 神殿

This village's temple **does not belong to any existing religious sect**. Rather than a religious building, it was a structure built to **hide the statue**. Like the village chief's mansion, the walls have crumbled away. With Granada's guidance or by using a **Detection** spell, the location of the secret door can be easily found.

The secret door is in the floor, and when opened, it reveals a descending staircase. The stairs go straight for **15 meters (descending 3 meters)** and lead to **Room ①**.

### Room ① — 龙首机关

Looking into the room from the front, there is a **statue shaped like a dragon's head with its mouth open** on the far wall. The floor just inside the entrance is a switch, and when stepped on, **red arrows fly from the dragon's mouth**.

- **伤害**：D10+5
- **触发条件**：每次踏入即射出
- **库存箭数**：50 支

If **Detection** is used, the area from the entrance to **1.5 meters in** will glow. To **jump over** this requires a successful **thief skill check（盗贼技能检定）**.

#### Armor Modifiers to Jump Check 跳跃检定的护甲修正

| -------------------- | -------: |
| Robes / Leather      |        0 |

### Room ⑦ — Statue of Fazan 法赞像

This is the room where the **Statue of Fazan** is enshrined. The center of the room is raised one step, and on it are placed **5 dark elf statues and 1 red arrow**, arranged as shown in the diagram.

This does not mean there are 5 Statues of Fazan—**4 of them are fakes**. The statues and arrow correspond to the dungeon structure, and **the real statue is the one marked with a ★, representing the Dragon Tooth Warrior**, which is the only **non-undead** monster.

> **判断要点**：5 像 + 1 箭对应地宫的 5 守卫房（②③④⑤⑥）+ Room ① 的箭机关。Room ⑥ 的龙牙战士是唯一的非不死生物，因此对应的塑像即为真品。

If you touch a fake statue, an **electrical shock** runs through you, dealing **D10 damage（armor does not stop it）**.

#### Effects of the Statue of Fazan 法赞之像的效果

Now that PCs have obtained the Statue of Fazan, its effects are as follows:

- **Dark Elf 持有时**：可**无条件命令**其他黑暗精灵。
- **Human 持有时**：使黑暗精灵恐惧并行动迟缓。具体而言，**看见此像的黑暗精灵在偶数轮无法行动**。
- **Elf 持有时**：黑暗精灵会陷入狂怒，**全力杀向该精灵**。此时它们**不会使用魔法**。

## Filcario 菲尔卡里奥村

Unfortunately, **all the villagers here will be slaughtered**. The Dark Elves will use this as their base and raid nearby villages one after another. Since they prefer darkness, they are **active at night and rest inside the village buildings during the day**. Therefore, if you attack during the day, there is a chance they might be sleeping.

The types and numbers of monsters here are as described in the **Dark Elf night raid section**: **3 Dark Elves, 2 Hobgoblins, and 15 Goblins**.

### Daytime Surprise Attack 白天奇袭

Here, **one representative from the PCs rolls D100**, and if the result is **equal to or less than twice their Luck**, then, fortunately, **1 Dark Elf, 2 Hobgoblins, and 10 Goblins are fast asleep**.

Consequently:

- **前 5 轮**：仅与醒着的怪物战斗。
- **第 6 轮**：睡着的怪物醒来加入战斗。
- 若有余裕，**可按每轮 3 只的速度无条件击杀沉睡的怪物**（由玩家指定击杀对象）。

The treasures possessed by the monsters are the same as in the Dark Elf night raid section.

> **GM 摘要**：白天奇袭判定 = D100 ≤ Luck × 2 → 命中则减员（1 Dark Elf + 2 Hobgoblins + 10 Goblins 在睡）；失败则全员清醒。睡眠期间每轮可秒杀 3 只睡着的怪物。

## Ending 结局

Were PCs able to defeat the dark elves? Did they obtain the **Statue of Fazan**? The ending will be completely different depending on this.

### 1. 击败黑暗精灵 + 取得 Fazan 之像

The threat of the dark elves has passed, but unless they store the Statue of Fazan in a safe place, **the ghost of Granada will persistently appear and warn that it will eventually fall into the hands of the dark elves**, and if that happens, the world will be conquered by them.

Even if they try to sell it, **the ghost will appear, so no shop will buy it**.

On Lodoss Island, the only safe places would be:

- **The Pharis temple in Roid（罗依德的法利斯神殿）**
- 或 **a dragon's belly under the dragon**

The GM can continue the journey as a **new mission**.

### 2. 击败黑暗精灵但未取得 Fazan 之像

They won't be haunted by the ghost, and the villagers will be grateful. However, **if they're tempted by curiosity and go to investigate Granada's village in the future, the ghost of Granada will not cooperate**. On the contrary, **it will obstruct them by giving false information or possessing the characters**.

### 3. Fazan 之像落入黑暗精灵之手

Once they obtain the statue, they will return to gather their comrades. Of course, before long, **groups of dark elves will begin to operate in the shadows and a dark age will come**.
