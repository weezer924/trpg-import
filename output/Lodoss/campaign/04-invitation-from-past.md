## Background 背景

Two hundred years ago, a man approached **Zabeg**（扎贝格）, who was running a small street vendor business in **Alania**（阿拉尼亚）, with a lucrative proposition. The man, who introduced himself as the wizard **Lutzkoff**（鲁兹科夫）, proposed a certain condition in exchange for this deal.

Lutzkoff was researching how to **transfer his mind into another person's body in order to gain eternal life**, and the condition was that **once the research was complete, Zabeg would provide his body**.

Lutzkoff died before completing his research, but in truth, he had **become undead and continued his research beneath the graveyard**. And now, **200 years later**, having finally completed his research, he sent an invitation to Zabeg's descendants.

> **GM 摘要**：200 年前魔术师 Lutzkoff 与摆摊小贩 Hall Zabeg 缔约——以肉身换繁荣商运。Lutzkoff 表面身故，实际化为不死生物在墓园下继续研究。如今研究终于完成，他要求扎贝格家后代履行契约。

## Introduction 序章

One invitation arrives at the home of **Zabeg, a wealthy merchant in Alania**. The contents read:

> **"The time has come to fulfill our contract. Please come to Jones Cemetery at midnight one week from now."**
>
> — Lutzkoff

Having no recollection of any such agreement, Zabeg ignored the invitation, thinking it was just a prank or harassment. However, after a week had passed, **suspicious figures began lurking around the mansion at night**. Then **the guard dog was killed**, and **the garden was vandalized**. And then **another invitation arrived**. It was practically an ultimatum, saying he would **wait just one more week**, and **if they didn't come, he had his own plans**.

The panicked Zabeg hired a large number of guards. And he wants to hire adventurers to go to the cemetery at the appointed date and time to investigate the truth. If the PCs agree, the adventure begins.

### Compensation 报酬

The terms Zabeg offers are, befitting a merchant, in cash:

| Item                | Detail                                                                            |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cash                | Up to **1000 GP per person**（如交涉，可加至 **1200**）                            |
| Items in lieu       | He will sell them at **half price**（however, **he has no magic items**）          |

How the PCs meet should be decided by the GM according to the campaign.

## Information That Can Be Discovered Through Investigation 调查所得情报

The PCs do not need to stay at the mansion the entire time until the day they are invited to the cemetery, and may act freely. Therefore, they can gather information about Lutzkoff, the cemetery, and so on.

### About the Zabeg Family 关于扎贝格家族

For this matter, it would be best to ask Zabeg himself. The Zabeg family used to run a modest street vendor business, but became wealthy merchants dealing in a diverse range of products after **Hall Zabeg**（霍尔·扎贝格）, the head of the family **seven generations ago**, succeeded in business. Zabeg tells them the following story, first noting that the story of that success has become something of a legend and has been passed down with considerable exaggeration.

#### Hall Zabeg 的发家传说

One day, Hall Zabeg sold off all his business tools and disappeared from the city. **Half a year later**, he returned to the city carrying a large, dirty burlap sack. The sack was filled with **many fist-sized black stones**, and he began selling them to wealthy people as **good luck charms**. For some reason, the rich competed with each other to buy these dirty stones.

After acquiring a considerable sum of money, Zabeg started various businesses all at once. It was rumored that he would fail immediately since it was amateur business and his funds were spread thin, but each business steadily ate into the market share of established shops and expanded.

It wasn't that Zabeg's business acumen was exceptional; rather, it looked like **the established merchants were making major mistakes and destroying themselves**. And without exception, **the established merchants all possessed Zabeg's good luck charms**.

He tells them this story, which is a bit unorthodox for a success story, but the **key item—the charms—do not remain in the Zabeg household**. The fallen wealthy would have had to give them up as well. After passing through whose hands, **some of the charms now lie dormant in the Thieves' Guild's "magic item repository"**（though their power has already faded）.

#### Hall Zabeg 的日记

Hall Zabeg's diary remains in the Zabeg family library. It can be found if the library is carefully searched. However, **if you don't know that Hall Zabeg is connected to this incident, you won't notice the relevant pages unless you're extremely lucky**. Specifically:

> **检定**：roll D100—if it's **not** equal to or below your **Luck**, you'll overlook it.

The diary describes his contract with the wizard. While he was pleased with his business success, he apparently regretted the contract. It goes on and on about how he tried everything to get out of it, but the other party was a wizard, and there was nothing he could do. However, as you read further in the diary, it says that **since Lutzkoff died, he can now live in peace**. Ironically, **Hall Zabeg himself died of illness just one month after the wizard's death**.

### About Lutzkoff 关于鲁兹科夫

Lutzkoff is, of course, a person's name, and PCs can find it in the **old roster of the Sorcerer's Guild**. Naturally, only sorcerers can use the guild's facilities.

| 项目                          | 费用                                                              |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 询问公会前辈                  | **免费**                                                          |
| 使用图书馆查阅                | **100 GP / 天**（否则人人都会窝在图书馆里做研究）                 |

The GM should ask the players what kind of documents they want to examine. His name can be found in the roster and in his book **"On the Immortal Spirit"**（《论不朽之灵》）.

According to the roster, he died **about 200 years ago**. Additionally, he was **quite high-level and one of the guild's caretakers**, but he seems to have **disappeared about 10 years before his death**.

If you read his book "On the Immortal Spirit," you'll learn that he was researching how to **transfer only the mind to another body**. At the time this book was written, **it was already possible to transfer the mind, but the subjects went insane**, so the experiment could not yet be called a success.

### About Jones Cemetery 关于琼斯墓园

**Jones Cemetery** is located on a hill on the outskirts of the city. This cemetery is for the **wealthy, nobility, and famous wizards**, with well-maintained, impressive graves lined up. **Right next to the cemetery stands a hut where the groundskeeper lives.**

If PCs go to talk to the groundskeeper, he says nothing unusual has happened. However, in reality, **several graves have been desecrated, and bodies have been dug up**, and he knows that **suspicious figures have been wandering the cemetery night after night**. He's hiding this because he'll be held responsible if it becomes public.

PCs need a permit to enter the cemetery, but if they ask Zabeg, he'll naturally give them one (since the Zabeg family tomb is there too). If they investigate the cemetery, make a **thief skill check（盗贼技能检定）**, and if successful, they'll discover signs of desecration. It appears to have been **desecrated several months ago**, but there are also signs that someone tried to cover it up by throwing soil over it. **Of course, the bodies inside the graves are gone.** If they tell the groundskeeper about this, he asks them to keep it confidential and promises to cooperate as much as possible.

#### Lutzkoff's Empty Grave 鲁兹科夫的空坟

Lutzkoff's grave is toward the edge of the cemetery. Compared to the impressive graves around it, it's rather modest, but still quite respectable. If they examine the grave, they'll find that **the body is missing**. However, **there are no signs that the grave was recently disturbed**. There are no hidden doors or anything in the grave pit. **He is not here.**

#### The Real Hideout 真正的巢穴

In fact, **Lutzkoff is hiding beneath the Zabeg family tomb**. Since the Zabeg family tomb contains the bodies of successive heads of the family, the burial chamber is a **large room 20 meters square**. **Seven stone coffins** are placed in that room. The Zabeg name is carved on each coffin. The innermost coffin belongs to **Hall Zabeg**.

If PCs move this coffin, **there's a trapdoor underneath**. To move the coffin, they must succeed at a **combat skill check（战斗技能检定）**. However, **there's a +20 modifier for each person helping**.

#### Sneaking In Early 提前潜入的奖励

Beyond that point, refer to the "**Lutzkoff's Hideout**" section. However, if they discover this location **before the invited date and sneak in secretly**, have everyone make a **thief skill check** when they move the coffin.

> **若全员都成功**：可在 Lutzkoff 毫无察觉的情况下潜入。在此情况下，**Lutzkoff 没有时间使用镜面布置反 Turn Undead 的对策**——可以**忽略地宫中的该机关部分**。同时，**他也无法放下铁栅门**。

## Lutzkoff's Hideout 鲁兹科夫的巢穴

On the day of the invitation, **a zombie is waiting at the entrance to Jones Cemetery to greet PCs**. The groundskeeper is too frightened to come out of his hut. When the zombie sees the PCs, it slowly begins walking as if to say, **"follow me."**

The zombie enters the Zabeg family tomb. The interior of the tomb is as previously described, but **the trapdoor is open**. The zombie indicates the door, then moves to a corner of the room and stops moving. **If attacked at this point, it will not fight back.**

### The Underground Chamber 地下密室

From the door, **a ladder continues straight down for 10 meters**. The place where PCs descend the ladder is at the end of a corridor, and if they proceed about **5 meters**, they'll reach a **large room**.

The room is **divided into two sections by walls**, as shown in the diagram. When the PCs enter the room, **the entrance is sealed by an iron grate that falls from above**. The iron grate **cannot be raised without using the winch located in the back room**.

### The Mirror Trick 镜中影像

**Lutzkoff's figure can be seen in the back room, but this is an image reflected in a mirror.** Lutzkoff has become an undead with the appearance of a skeleton through magic. Because he is ashamed of his appearance, **he wears a black robe over his head. Only his eyes glow an eerie blue.** Having become undead, he fears the **Turn Undead** spell and has created this setup.

> **关键规则**：**对镜像施法无效。** A PC who sees Lutzkoff's figure must make a **Magic skill check at −20**, and if successful, will realize it's an image reflected in a mirror.

Upon seeing the PCs, Lutzkoff lets out an eerie laugh and mutters that Zabeg himself didn't come after all—**it seems the cowardly nature runs through the generations**. If the PCs haven't yet uncovered the truth, **he will narrate the outline of the incident here**（provided the PCs don't attack him right away, of course）.

He then explains that to transfer one's spirit, **it must be done with someone who made the formal contract, or their descendant, and that the PCs won't serve as substitutes**. **Zabeg must fulfill the contract, even by force.** So he wants the PCs to bring him here. Of course, to be trustworthy, the PCs will have to **become undead as well**, he says, **snapping his fingers (with skeletal fingers!)**.

### The Ambush 伏击

Then, **6 zombies and 2 ghouls that had been hiding in the shadows of the walls attack the PCs**.

Lutzkoff will **remain in the back room until all his servants are annihilated**. **That's how much he fears the Turn Undead spell.** Once his servants are completely wiped out, **he reluctantly comes out and fights the PCs**.

## Conclusion 结局

Eternal life seems to be the greatest dream for humans, especially for magicians, and many have challenged this proposition in the past. The ancient kingdom's magician **Karla**（卡拉）was one of them. **She achieved it by sealing herself in an item.** Lutzkoff challenged it through a different method, but **he succumbed to his own madness**. Considering Karla's case as well, **perhaps eternal life only brings about tragedy.**
