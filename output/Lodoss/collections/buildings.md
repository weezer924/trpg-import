# Building Collection 建筑集

> 来源：Record of Lodoss War Companion I（1989, Group SNE）PDF p.89–92
> 译本：英文同人翻译版

When adventuring on Lodoss Island, you'll encounter buildings and towns that are quite unfamiliar in the real world. A respectable GM can't always just say, *"Before you stands a building called such-and-such."* Describing things as vividly as if you've seen them yourself is an important part of a GM's job. Also, since the places featured here are all famous landmarks on Lodoss, it wouldn't be a bad idea to use this as a tourist guide for a stylish tour around Lodoss.

## Towns 城镇

### The Town of Alan（阿兰）

The townscape is modeled after the architecture of the ancient kingdom, so there are many geometric and avant-garde buildings. *It's completely unknown just how many dwarven architects were needed to build this city.*

### The Town of Raiden（雷登）

This town prospers from maritime trade with the continent, so there are many continental-style buildings, creating a streetscape unfamiliar to Lodoss Island. *We'll deliberately avoid discussing what continental-style buildings actually look like here.*

### The Town of Roid（罗德）

Being the capital of Valis, a country with strong religious influences, the cityscape must surely reflect that character. *What kind of cityscape exactly? Best not to ask.* Despite being the royal capital, it's apparently not a very large city, and there are few tall buildings. This is likely due to the sandy ground beneath.

### The Town of Blade（布雷德）

Given that the nation of Flaim has an Arabian image, the buildings surely share a similar aesthetic. The city is filled with stone structures, and drought-resistant plants are planted here and there throughout the streets. *We won't go into specifics about their exact appearance here.*

### Village of Tarba（塔尔巴村）

A village in the northernmost part of Alania, built in a basin overlooking the White Dragon Mountains. This is said to be the place where Marfa fell, which is why the head temple of Marfa is located here. In winter it is closed off by snow, and given that the northern dwarf tribe lives here, it is imagined that the annual alcohol consumption is quite substantial.

## Notable Buildings 著名建筑

### Academy of Sages（贤者学院）

A university for mages in the city of Alan. Because it is a prestigious institution, graduates have no trouble finding employment, making it very popular. All the buildings are constructed of **black marble**. *Surprisingly, the founder may have had nouveau riche tastes.*

### Tower of Wort（沃特之塔）

The residence of the great sage Wort in Moss. A stone tower, rumored to be built atop the **"Deepest Labyrinth."** Wort himself lives at the top of the tower, *which is very convenient since there is a direct escalator to get there.*

### Roid Liberation Army Headquarters（罗德解放军总部）

They used a nobleman's mansion in Rood as their headquarters. It had quite a convenient structure, with **underground passages leading to the town** and such. Naturally, it was built of stone. It seems that noble mansions are constructed with the assumption that they will be attacked.

### Karla's Mansion（卡拉的宅邸）

She built an ancient kingdom-style mansion on a small island floating in **Lake Lenoana** and used it as a hideout. Buildings of the ancient kingdom are often described as having geometric construction, so it's probably a building like the **Yumedono** of Hōryūji Temple. Or perhaps it's a building designed by an avant-garde artist.

### Temple of Fire（火之神殿）

A stone temple built by the desert savages who lived in this land during the ancient kingdom era, shaped as a square pyramid. To put it bluntly, **a pyramid**. It is said to represent the shape of burning flames.

### Tower of Dust Storms（沙暴之塔）

This is a stone tower also built by the desert savages to enshrine **Jinn, the King of Wind**. A corridor runs spirally along the tower's outer wall, and its appearance is said to evoke a tornado. Since it's protected by sandstorms, *perhaps a boy in a school uniform lives there, guarded by three servants.*

> **Translator's Note**: This is referencing the classic anime/manga *Babel II*, in which a schoolboy lives in a desert tower protected by sandstorms alongside three supernatural guardians.

## Vessels 船舰

### Sea Hawk（海鹰号）

While most ships on Lodoss Island are galleys, this is a less common **three-masted sailing ship**. Whether King Kashue imported it from the continent, plundered it, or built it himself is uncertain. It serves as a **state-run warship** (or perhaps a privateer) of the Flaim Kingdom.
