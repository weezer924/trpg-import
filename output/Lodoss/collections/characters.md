# Character Collection 角色集

> 来源：Record of Lodoss War Companion I（1989, Group SNE）PDF p.66–72
> 译本：英文同人翻译版

The Record of Lodoss War has accumulated a rich history through Replays I, II, III, Novel Volume 1, and Volume 2, giving birth to many heroes and heroines. You surely have your favorite characters among those who have passed through the story (though some energetic ones continue to appear even now). Here, we've gathered these beloved characters and brought them all back to life in the world of Lodoss RPG. Please use them as NPCs when you have the opportunity. Note that when migrating from a different game system, some statistics may have been changed while preserving only their essential characteristics.

> **Ages note**: The ages listed are from the time of the original serialization. Five years pass between Replay I and II, and twelve years between II and III, so please adjust accordingly to fit the era when using them.

## I — The Grey Witch（灰色魔女）

The first-generation Lodoss party — heroes of Replay I and Novel Volume 1.

### Parn（帕恩）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Knight（骑士）/ Human / Male / 18
- **Stats**：ST 13 · EN 10 · AG 10 · IN 9 · LU 16 · PB 13
- **Weapon**：Bastard Sword（杂种剑）
- **Armor**：Plate Mail, Medium Shield

The first-generation leader of the Lodoss party. He was a young man who dreamed of becoming a splendid knight like his late father. Since Lodoss I, he has been constantly pursuing the whereabouts of the Wood Karla together with Deedlit.

### Etoh（艾特）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Priest of Pharis（法利斯神官）/ Human / Male / 18
- **Stats**：ST 11 · EN 6 · AG 13 · IN 14 · LU 13 · PB 17
- **Weapon**：Mace
- **Armor**：Plate Mail

He was a novice priest of Pharis, but before anyone knew it, he had become the king of the great nation of Valis. He would probably insist it wasn't thanks to marrying Princess Fianna, but others call this *marrying up*.

### Deedlit（蒂德利特）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Shaman（精灵使）/ Elf / Female / 160
- **Stats**：ST 12 · EN 9 · AG 15 · IN 12 · LU 13 · PB 13
- **Weapon**：Short Sword
- **Armor**：Hard Leather (AV same as Scale)

A proud High Elf maiden from the Forest of No Return. Through a twist of fate, she ended up adventuring with humans, but she could never have imagined it would become such a long association. Of course, for an elf, it might be but a fleeting moment.

### Ghim（吉姆）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Warrior（战士）/ Dwarf / Male / 60
- **Stats**：ST 19 · EN 12 · AG 11 · IN 10 · LU 12 · PB 10
- **Weapon**：Battle Axe
- **Armor**：Plate Mail

Set out on adventure after being asked by the great Neese to bring back her kidnapped daughter Leylia. He distinguished himself as a warrior trusted by his companions, but met a tragic end in the confrontation with Karla.

### Slayn（斯雷因）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Sorcerer（魔术师）/ Human / Male / 25
- **Stats**：ST 7 · EN 10 · AG 12 · IN 16 · LU 14 · PB 14
- **Weapon**：Dagger
- **Armor**：Robe

Studied sorcerer magic at the Academy of Sages in Alan. He joined the adventure out of concern for Parn, but he might actually be the most hot-blooded of them all. After the confrontation with Karla, he won Leylia's heart — lucky guy.

### Woodchuck（伍德查克）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Scout（斥候）/ Human / Male / 40
- **Stats**：ST 7 · EN 8 · AG 17 · IN 12 · LU 13 · PB 6
- **Weapon**：Short Sword
- **Armor**：Leather

Was imprisoned in Alania but released under amnesty. Thinking there might be some profitable opportunity, he set out on the adventure with Parn and the others. He managed to obtain Karla's circlet, but his misfortune is that he ended up being controlled by it.

### Karla（卡拉，灰色魔女）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Sorcerer（魔术师）/ Human / Female / —
- **Stats**：ST — · EN — · AG — · IN 20 · LU — · PB —
- **Weapon**：—
- **Armor**：—

A sorcerer from the ancient kingdom era. She specializes in enchantment magic. She continues to exist by sealing her spirit within a circlet and controlling its wearer. Currently controlling Woodchuck and is wanted.

### Wort（沃特）

A sorcerer from Kanon. *(Stats not given.)*

## II — The Return of the Hero King（英雄王归来）

Heroes of Replay II — set five years after Replay I.

### Orson（奥森）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Warrior / Human / Male / 20
- **Stats**：ST 14 · EN 11 · AG 15 · IN 7 · LU 12 · PB 14
- **Weapon**：Long Sword
- **Armor**：Plate Mail, Large Shield

A berserker from Kanon. Ah, that's it.

### Cecil（塞西尔）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Sorcerer / Human / Male / 21
- **Stats**：ST 7 · EN 18 · AG 9 · IN 15 · LU 10 · PB 11
- **Weapon**：Dagger
- **Armor**：Robe

Despite being a mage, he has a habit of engaging in melee combat. It's troublesome that this type of mage became popular after him. He used to get furious when mistaken for a woman based on his appearance. *Well then, cut your hair.*

### Maar（玛尔）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Scout / Grassrunner (©Sword World) / Male / 25
- **Stats**：ST 10 · EN 12 · AG 17 · IN 8 · LU 15 · PB 15
- **Weapon**：Short Sword
- **Armor**：Leather

The party's idol who sings cheerful songs (or so he claims?). His repertoire includes the *Pee!ling Song* and the *Ballad of Surrender Recommendation*.

### Fourth / Pseudo（弗斯 / 普修多）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Scout / Human / Male / 25
- **Stats**：ST 11 · EN 11 · AG 18 · IN 10 · LU 14 · PB 16
- **Weapon**：Estoc (Damage is D10+2)
- **Armor**：Leather

A scout from Raiden. The title "scout" was also given to him after his success. Also known as Pseudo. He is also famous for being handsome.

### Shiris（席丽斯）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Warrior / Human / Female / 18
- **Stats**：ST 15 · EN 9 · AG 16 · IN 10 · LU 14 · PB 15
- **Weapon**：Long Sword
- **Armor**：Plate Mail

Claims to be the daughter of nobility from Kanon. Has an aggressive personality. Currently married to Highland's Dragon Lord Redrick. There are rumors that she rules the country by force.

### Shari（夏丽）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Priest of Myrii（玛利神官）/ Human / Female / 25
- **Stats**：ST 10 · EN 14 · AG 6 · IN 16 · LU 13 · PB 16
- **Weapon**：Warhammer
- **Armor**：Chain (often wears a robe over it)

Joined the party as an NPC. Didn't have any particularly noteworthy achievements, but was extremely popular nonetheless — probably because she was a quiet girl with long hair.

### Ashram（阿修拉姆）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Knight（暗黑骑士）/ Human / Male / 31
- **Stats**：ST 17 · EN 14 · AG 13 · IN 10 · LU 19 · PB 10
- **Weapon**：Soul Crusher (Greatsword)（断魂剑）
- **Armor**：Plate Mail

Former captain of Marmo's royal guard. Deeply devoted to Beld and wishes to carry on his legacy. Compared to the simply twisted Wagnard, he could be considered a not-entirely-hateable character. A classic villain who survives even after throwing himself into a crater.

### Kashue（卡修）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Warrior / Human / Male / 33
- **Stats**：ST 16 · EN 12 · AG 11 · IN 14 · LU 15 · PB 14
- **Weapon**：Bastard Sword
- **Armor**：Plate Mail (often wears Hard Leather as well)

The Mercenary King of Flaim. Rose to kingship at a young age through his swordsmanship. There are various theories — that he's actually the missing third prince of Kanon, or a swordsman who drifted over from the continent — but readers probably know his true identity better than anyone.

### Lucia（露西亚）

The songstress of the Sea Dragon Inn. The adventurers' solace... *(Stats not given.)*

## III — Soul Crystal Ball（灵魂水晶球）

Heroes of Replay III — set twelve years after Replay II.

### Spark（斯帕克）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Knight / Human / Male / 21
- **Stats**：ST 14 · EN 12 · AG 14 · IN 12 · LU 11 · PB 15
- **Weapon**：Greatsword
- **Armor**：Plate Mail

A knight serving King Kashue. He sets out on an adventure on a mission. His personality is serious and hot-blooded (that's how it was at first, I swear), but perhaps because of his player, he has terrible luck. He insists that he rolls average dice, but there's a theory that the problem is that he pales in comparison to the surrounding players.

### Garrack（加拉克）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Warrior / Human / Male / 30
- **Stats**：ST 15 · EN 10 · AG 11 · IN 9 · LU 13 · PB 9
- **Weapon**：Battle Axe
- **Armor**：Plate Mail, Small Shield

A warrior who has known Spark since childhood. He was traveling around Lodoss as a mercenary and had just returned to his hometown, but was talked into going on another journey by Spark. Well, he must truly love battle from the bottom of his heart.

### Aldonova（阿尔多诺瓦）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Sorcerer / Human / Male / 35
- **Stats**：ST 11 · EN 15 · AG 10 · IN 14 · LU 16 · PB 9
- **Weapon**：Staff
- **Armor**：Robe

Why did he become a sorcerer when he was born with such a blessed physique? Oh, I got it! He must have thought he'd learn magic to acquire material for showing off his erudition.

### Greevus（格里夫斯）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Priest of Myrii / Dwarf / Male / 110
- **Stats**：ST 13 · EN 13 · AG 11 · IN 15 · LU 11 · PB 9
- **Weapon**：Halberd
- **Armor**：Chain Mail

The traditional image of dwarves was that of silent but reliable allies, but this guy has magnificently shattered that image. Well, it's true that he's reliable in combat, but I wish he'd stop with the stupid jokes.

### Leaf（莉芙）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Shaman / Half-Elf / Female / 15
- **Stats**：ST 7 · EN 9 · AG 14 · IN 16 · LU 10 · PB 16
- **Weapon**：Flail
- **Armor**：Leather

She's traveling in search of her human mother. Among the players, she was predicted to win first place in a popularity contest, but she was defeated by the GM's conspiracy (the appearance of Neese as a rival).

### Ryna（莉娜）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Scout / Human / Female / 25
- **Stats**：ST 10 · EN 11 · AG 17 · IN 13 · LU 15 · PB 13
- **Weapon**：Whip
- **Armor**：Leather

A spirited female thief. Perhaps because she carries a whip, she somehow ended up becoming the torturer. She herself is into it so it's fine, but it's actually pretty dangerous.

### Mika Yahawe（米卡·雅哈维）

A girl suffering from an incurable disease. *(Stats not given.)*

### Neese (Granddaughter)（小妮丝）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Priest of Marfa（玛法神官）/ Human / Female / 15
- **Stats**：ST 9 · EN 10 · AG 14 · IN 16 · LU 18 · PB 15
- **Weapon**：Short Sword
- **Armor**：Leather

Little Neese, who is the granddaughter of Neese, Leylia's mother. In other words, her father is Slayn. She seems to come from a family genetically prone to being kidnapped, and she ends up being forcibly abducted.

### Ishua（伊舒亚）

Mika's female warrior bodyguard. *(Stats not given.)*

### Wagnard（瓦古纳德）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Sorcerer（黑暗魔术师）/ Human / Male / 54
- **Stats**：ST 5 · EN 6 · AG 11 · IN 17 · LU 10 · PB 8
- **Weapon**：Staff
- **Armor**：Robe (black robe dyed with dark elf blood)

A magician from the Academy of Sages like Slayn, but drawn to the power of darkness, he serves Beld. Due to a curse from Larcus, the headmaster of the Academy of Sages, he experiences excruciating pain throughout his body every time he casts a spell.

### Medicia（梅迪希亚）

Mika's super-strong female warrior bodyguard. *(Stats not given.)*

## Novel Volume 2 — The Fire Demon（火焰魔神）

### Nardia（娜尔迪亚）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Warrior / Human / Female / 22
- **Stats**：ST 12 · EN 13 · AG 15 · IN 13 · LU 10 · PB 15
- **Weapon**：Scimitar (damage same as Short Sword)
- **Armor**：None (she is wearing clothes, just to be clear)

The female chieftain of the Fire Tribe. She genuinely cares about her tribe, but ironically, it is precisely this that leads her to take her own life.

### Marsh（马什）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Warrior / Human / Male / 28
- **Stats**：ST 18 · EN 15 · AG 11 · IN 11 · LU 13 · PB 8
- **Weapon**：Battle Axe
- **Armor**：Leather

A mercenary from Flaim. Also called the axe-wielder. Looking at his relationship with Parn, he seems to be the type who fights first and becomes friends later.

## Other NPCs（其他 NPC）

### Azumo（阿祖莫）

A priest of the Fire Tribe. He was incited by Karla to release the Efreet. He tried to fulfill his ambitions using that power, but was stripped of his power through Deedlit's efforts, and in the end was finished off by Leylia. *All while thinking she was Karla.* *(Stats not given.)*

### Pseudo（普修多）

- **Class / Race / Sex / Age**：Shaman / Human / Male / 34
- **Stats**：ST 10 · EN 13 · AG 14 · IN 15 · LU 8 · PB 12
- **Weapon**：Dagger
- **Armor**：Leather

The same person as Fourth. *Ah, easy, easy.*

> **Note**: PDF lists Pseudo here as Shaman with these stats. In Replay II he is "Fourth," a Scout from Raiden — see entry above. The two stat blocks reflect different appearances or are an artifact of the source. Use whichever fits your scenario.

### Deni（德尼）

- **Stats**：ST 10 · EN 12 · AG 15 · IN 11 · LU 11 · PB 7
- **Weapon**：Short Sword
- **Armor**：Leather

Pseudo's partner, who uses short swords with both hands.
