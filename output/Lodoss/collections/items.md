## Melee Weapons 近战武器

### Dagger 匕首

A one-handed blade with a cutting edge of about 10–20 centimeters. Even though we simply call them daggers, there are various types, including single-edged, double-edged, curved, and straight blades. In short, they're combat knives. Speaking of knives, along with clubs, they're the most fundamental of weapons, and even today knife combat is a required basic subject for special forces in countries around the world.

Originally, daggers were heavily utilized as self-defense or assassination weapons due to their small size. If you aim for an opponent's vital point with a dagger hidden in your pocket, you can very well finish them off in one strike. Moreover, even in situations where you're grappled by an enemy and can't use normal weapons, you can still use a short dagger. This makes the dagger quite a powerful weapon indeed. **Warriors, just because it does less damage, don't ever make the mistake of underestimating the dagger.**

### Short Sword 短剑

While lacking in striking power, this handy weapon is the short sword — light, easy to use, and with decent reach. This, too, is a name that encompasses similar forms, with various variations in existence. Basically, it's a sword with a cutting edge of 30 centimeters or more, one-handed, double-edged, and straight, but that's just a guideline. It's roughly a bit longer than the distance from your fingertips to your elbow.

The short sword is a weapon whose main tactics are thrusting and sweeping. In this respect, it's similar to the dagger, but it's heavier, longer, larger, and more powerful. It has been used as an infantry weapon since ancient times. Also, tactically speaking, it pairs well with a shield.

Furthermore, this sword is often worn during formal occasions, and there are many with elaborate designs. For those who care about their appearance, by all means, get yourself one.

### Broadsword 阔剑

A blade length of 50 centimeters to just under 1 meter, a straight sword with a wide blade wielded in one hand — that's a broadsword. Swords larger than this generally correspond to this description. Therefore, there's no need to think too complicatedly about its form.

The broadsword falls into the most popular category of weapons appearing in games. It's one of the hero's standard weapons. Hold a small or medium shield in your left hand, and swing it around with your right arm (in other words, your dominant hand) to fight. It has power and a long reach — it's a good weapon. That's probably why it becomes a status symbol for warriors.

This is a digression, but there's a popular saying that *no warrior who poorly maintains their sword is skilled.* If you aim to be a first-class swordsman, don't neglect the care of your sword.

### Bastard Sword 杂种剑

Its shape is almost the same as a broadsword. However, there's just one difference: the sword's hilt is longer, allowing it to be held with both hands as well. It can be used in various ways — sometimes nimbly with one hand, sometimes powerfully with both hands.

### Greatsword 大剑

A massive two-handed straight sword that, if you're not careful, can have a blade length exceeding 1 meter. It boasts the strongest destructive power among swords, capable of lopping off a human head with a single blow. Of course, being so large makes it difficult to handle, and considerable Strength is required to wield it freely. *How much Strength, you ask? Well, about Schwarzenegger-level, perhaps?*

### Spear (Short & Long) 长枪 / 短枪

This is what we call a spear. Among these, **short spears** refer to the type you wield in one hand for close combat — those with a length of 1 meter to just under 2 meters. **Long spears** are over 2 meters in total length and were mainly used by heavy infantry.

The characteristics of spears are that they allow you to maintain a longer reach than other weapons, and they have penetrating power, making them effective against armored opponents. Short spears can also be thrown at enemies.

If you form a dense formation with long spears and create a wall of spears, most opponents won't be able to get close. Originally a weapon for group battles, it's not really something an adventurer would carry around, but it will still prove useful in adventurer-specific situations, such as when you want to attack a giant monster from as far away as possible.

Finally, as a minor note of caution, **long spears cannot be thrown.** Well, I don't think anyone would try to throw a spear longer than their own height when it's not even a modern javelin, but just to be safe.

### Hand Axe 手斧

This is, so to speak, a hatchet — a camping essential. While it's more of a tool than a weapon, it's still quite powerful in its own right.

There's a variation of the hand axe called the **tomahawk**. It's a weapon favored by Native Americans. Being short, it's easy to swing around, and with its good balance, when thrown, it spins through the air and flies quite far. It's also perfect for mounted combat. While its power as a weapon isn't much to write home about, the hand axe is worth having — you can throw it, split firewood, cut rope, and more.

### Battle Axe 战斧

If the hand axe is a hatchet, then the battle axe is like a lumberjack's axe, fashioned into a weapon. It has considerable striking power, and depending on the model, can even surpass a sword. It's also famous as the **weapon of choice for dwarves**.

This too can be used in various ways beyond combat. You can fell trees to build rafts or bridges, and you can smash through a wooden door in less than five minutes. Of course, since you can only swing it and not thrust, it's harder to handle than a sword or spear.

### Great Axe 巨斧

Extend the handle of a battle axe and attach an even heavier head, and you get a great axe. This weapon can't even be lifted by someone of average strength, but its destructive power is unmatched. It's highly recommended for those confident in their strength.

### Mace (Light & Heavy) 钉头锤（轻 / 重）

It's said that the first weapon humans ever wielded was the club, and the mace is the evolved and refined version of that club. It consists of a metal head of considerable weight fitted onto a handle of manageable length — the ideal weapon for bludgeoning an opponent.

Broadly speaking, maces are divided into the **light mace**, about the length of a short sword and used by cavalry, and the **heavy mace**, with a longer handle, used by infantry. Since the impact translates directly into damage, it's effective against heavily armored opponents. *That's why soldiers fighting knights always carry a mace as a backup weapon.*

### Maul 双手锤

A giant two-handed mace, or hammer. A long handle over one meter in length with a massively heavy head at the end — one swing and it'll easily crush a skull. That's the maul. **Getting hit by this thing means death!**

In fact, in English, "maul" means not just the name of the weapon but also serves as a verb meaning *to batter or pulverize*. It's no exaggeration to say this weapon's destructive power lives up to its name, ranking alongside the great axe as one of the most brutal weapons.

### Staff 法杖

When you think of a staff, you think of a wizard's companion — inseparable from ancient magic. Originally just a walking stick for traveling, it proved useful enough as a weapon for self-defense that wizards adopted it en masse. Many staves can cast magic and can also be used as weapons for protection. *Talk about killing two birds with one stone.*

The staff's distinguishing features are that it's **light and long**. Because it's light, even physically weak wizards can use it, and because it's long, it packs more punch than its weight would suggest. It's far more effective than a mediocre club. Against human opponents, it's more than adequate. Of course, it might not be very useful against tough monsters. *Not for a mage, at any rate, though a trained martial artist would be a different story.*

### Flail 连枷

This is a strange weapon with a short club attached by a chain to the end of a long handle. The club portion rotates widely as you swing it, and through that centrifugal force, it delivers much more damage to your opponent — a very mechanical principle for a weapon. Originally, it was a farming implement used to thresh wheat by beating it. Then it was brought to the battlefield to beat the enemy soldiers instead.

There are many variations of this, too: ones with short handles, ones with long chains and weights instead of clubs, ones with multiple chains, and so on. The type where the weight is a spiked ball is specifically called a **morning star**. It's quite a powerful weapon, but unfortunately, this flail is difficult to handle. **If you're not careful, you might end up smacking yourself with it, so watch out.**

## Ranged Weapons 远程武器

### Bow 弓

When it comes to bows, they're the quintessential ranged weapon in fantasy worlds. In Lodoss, as well, they're without exception an important weapon. In volume 1 of the novel, heroes failed to finish off a goblin, and in the replay, heroes couldn't take down a pirate lookout — nothing to write home about — but originally bows might be said to be more powerful weapons than swords or spears.

The greatest advantage of a bow is, after all, being able to **attack one-sidedly from a distance**. Conversely, if the enemy gets too close, you won't have time to nock an arrow, and this time you'll be the one getting beaten one-sidedly. When you delve into dungeons, the distance when you encounter enemies inevitably becomes short, and things quickly turn into melee combat, so bows can't be used very effectively.

### Crossbow 弩

Among ranged weapons in fantasy worlds, the crossbow belongs to the most powerful category. It's a powerful weapon that uses a mechanical device to forcibly pull back a bow so strong that normally human strength couldn't draw it, and fires thick, heavy bolts that are twice as large as normal arrows. There are various types, from small lightweight ones for hunting to heavy combat models that can't be used without a cranking mechanism, but they all feature a stock for aiming that makes them much easier to handle than bows.

Their striking power is considerable, too — enough to punch straight through a knight's armor in one hit. If you take a direct hit from one of these, you can hardly escape serious injury.

The crossbow is so convenient, but actually, it has a critical flaw serious enough to cancel out all the advantages mentioned so far. **The crossbow must have its string drawn before firing**, and the time this takes is not negligible. For ambushes and preemptive strikes, and other uses suited to the situation, there's no weapon more reliable than this.

### Sling 投石索

You attach a patch of fabric to the middle of a long cord, fold it in half, and firmly hold a stone in place. You wrap one end around your wrist and grip the other end with the same hand. You swing it around and around, and when it's built up enough speed, you suddenly release your grip and the stone flies away at tremendous speed. That's the principle of the sling.

Simple construction with decent power — that's what the sling's advantages boil down to. Though its range is inferior to the bow, you can pick up stones from the roadside to use as ammunition, and you can make one with just cord and cloth even when you have no weapons at all. It's a completely handy and useful weapon.

Additionally, it can be used in various ways, such as passing a stolen jewel to a companion on the other side of a river. This one, too, could be said to have quite a lot of utility in non-combat situations.

## Armor 护甲

### Cloth Armor 布甲

Now, when you write it in katakana, it sounds cool, but it's basically just sturdy clothing, nothing more. Well, it doesn't have the defensive power you'd call armor, but since it's light and easy to move in, let's include it as part of the armor family.

### Leather Armor 皮甲

Made from tanned animal hides crafted into armor, it loses to other armors in terms of defense, but it's **cheap, light, and silent**. It's the go-to armor for scouts who are poor (is that a prejudice?), weak, and specialize in stealth. Shamans who dislike metal also favor it.

While armor is primarily about defense, and combat-focused characters wear the strongest armor they can within their limits, even for such people, it can be quite useful depending on the time and situation. For example, if you're fighting on a ship and fall into the sea, it's obvious that wearing heavy plate mail will send you to a watery grave, but if you're wearing light leather, you can swim. Also, in hot places like the deserts of Flaim, leather allows for longer marches than heavy metal armor. **Even warriors cannot afford to look down on leather armor.**

### Ring Mail Armor 环甲

An enhanced version of leather armor with metal rings sewn onto it. Much lighter and quieter than other metal armors. And tougher than leather. The reason it's so quiet is that the rings are spaced apart and don't rub against each other, though this makes it somewhat **weak against heavy weapons**. Be very careful.

### Chain Mail Armor 锁子甲

A medieval all-purpose defense made by weaving small chains together. It's fine against cuts and thrusts, but **weak against blunt impacts**, so a quilted cushion is worn underneath. Though heavy, it's flexible and well-ventilated, making it easier to wear than plate mail. However, it's noisy and clanking, so it's absolutely unsuitable for stealth.

### Scale Mail Armor 鳞甲

Scale-shaped metal plates are layered multiple times and attached to leather armor, covering it completely without gaps. Its distinguishing feature is that it's harder than chain mail and more flexible than plate mail. It's said to have been surprisingly weak against pointed weapons, but its defense is excellent and it's cheaper than chain mail.

### Plate Mail Armor 板甲

Plate mail is the symbol of a strong warrior. In Lodoss, too, knights and regular soldiers of each country all wear this. Each part is made from a single sheet of metal, catching every blow. It deflects most weapons, and its defensive power lives up to the name of the strongest armor. Moreover, it looks beautiful. **Not just Parn, but many warriors place their dreams in a knight's armor.**

Well, if I were to point out its flaws, it's heavy, difficult to move in, and hard to take off. They say knights of old couldn't mount a horse by themselves, and *if you fell into the sea wearing something like this, it would be a disaster.*

### Shield 盾

A defensive tool held in the hand, but there are various variations depending on size and shape. However, the purpose of all of them is to dodge, catch, and deflect enemy attacks. While armor is **passive** protective equipment that assumes the opponent's attack will hit, a shield can be said to be **active** protective equipment designed to prevent the opponent's attack from hitting.

## Magic Items 魔法物品

### Spider Sword 蛛剑

A magical sword said to have been presented to the king by the Sorcerers Guild **with irony** when Alania was founded, in response to their choice of a spider as the national emblem. Since it's originally a short sword, its power isn't all that impressive, but it can cast the **Spider Web** spell. It could be said to be a weapon suitable for shamans and scouts. In the computer version, you could receive it as a gift from the King of Alania if you informed him of an assassination plot. Due to its ability, it was also called the *"casting net"* sword.

### Lawful Blade 律剑

A magical sword possessed by King Fahn of Valis. It's quite a powerful item, equivalent to a **long sword +4**. It's a sword that Fahn obtained when he was young and still just a knight, but it was named this way because when he became king of a nation, he swore an oath to this sword to maintain order. After King Fahn's death, it is said to be kept in the castle of Roid, or to have passed into the hands of some adventurer, and its whereabouts are uncertain.

### Light Reflector 光之反射盾

One of the magical items owned by King Fahn. It looks like a large shield, but when you hold it, the difference becomes clear. **You feel absolutely no weight at all.** Moreover, in battle, it protects you from enemy weapons as if it had a will of its own.

### Rune Staff 符文法杖

A magical staff crafted by a mage of the ancient kingdom. If you use this staff, you can wield the spell **Meteor Strike**, the ultimate secret of sorcerer magic. Its existence has now become legend, but it is said that one of the dragons living on Lodoss Island has hoarded it along with other treasures.

### Protection Ring 守护戒指（+1～+3）

A ring that, when worn, can reduce damage taken through an invisible force field. In the era of the ancient kingdom, it seems they could be created quite easily, and those that have been excavated are currently in circulation. Because the effect varies depending on the quality, they are classified from **+1 to +3**. If it's around +1, you might be able to obtain one in a large city if you pile up enough gold, but when it comes to +3, you'll have no choice but to find it on an adventure or excavate ruins.

### Resist Ring 抗性戒指

This ring has the power to repel hostile magic. When worn, it **increases RE**. This item, like the Protection Ring, can also trace its origins to the ancient kingdom.
