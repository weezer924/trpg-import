# Spell Collection 法术集（评注版）

> 来源：Record of Lodoss War Companion I（1989, Group SNE）PDF p.73–76
> 译本：英文同人翻译版

Magic is one of the appeals of fantasy RPGs. With advanced magic, things that would be impossible through ordinary means can be resolved with ease. In the world of Lodoss, there are monsters that absolutely cannot be defeated with a sword, making magic indispensable for adventuring.

Magic can be classified by the source of its power. Which magic you choose to master will greatly influence the rest of your life. Not just your own life, but your companions' lives as well.

> **本章性质**：每条法术的完整规则数据（MP 消耗、距离、效果、抗性等）见 `rules/04-magic.md`。这里只收录每条法术的「编辑评注」——风味文字与战术心得，作为 GM/玩家的实用参考。

## Priest Magic（神官魔法）

> Priest magic would be more accurately described as **miracles** rather than magic. A priest's faith guides the power of the gods into this world.

| 法术                            | 评注                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Healing 治愈**                | Heals wounds with the power of the gods. This is the spell that most directly demonstrates the majesty of the gods. Healing the wounded and weary is a priest's duty and must never be neglected. |
| **Transfer Mental Power 精神力转移** | A spell to encourage those whose souls have been crushed by sharing the priest's spiritual power with them. To put it concretely, it's magic that transfers the caster's MP to another person, but it has various uses. It can restore consciousness to a warrior whose MP has been stolen by Demon Scream magic, or transfer MP to an exhausted mage for them to use for attack magic — it can be quite effective depending on how you use it. |
| **Turn Undead 退散不死**         | A spell to send foul undead back to the earth and drive them away with the great power of the gods. When you encounter undead, shout in a loud voice: "Perish, immortal ones!" After all, the effectiveness of this spell depends on the priest's spiritual strength. |
| **Wounds 伤创**                  | The power to heal is also the power to harm. So this spell is meant to deliver divine punishment to those who have sold their souls to Demons and scoundrels who do not fear the gods. Therefore, as a priest, you must not use this spell indiscriminately. Use it only when fighting evil. |
| **Bless 祝福**                   | May the blessings of the gods be upon you — this is a spell for bestowing divine blessing. With the gods on your side, no enemy is frightening. Your attack power increases, and courage wells up within you. You can't help but thank the gods. This is probably how followers multiply. |
| **Holy Weapon 圣武器**           | If you purify a weapon with holy power, foul monsters will immediately flee. Behold the power of the gods. So this spell imbues a weapon with a holy aura. Think of it as an Enchant Weapon that's especially effective against undead. The sight of facing evil with a sword shining with sacred light must be really cool. |
| **Cure Injury 重伤治愈**         | A more powerful healing spell. Indeed, only when a priest can use magic of this level can they be considered a full-fledged professional. Since it can heal even quite serious injuries, now you can go on even more dangerous adventures. Right? |
| **Sunshine 阳光**                | O holy light of the sun, drive away these evil ones! In other words, this spell scatters rays of light similar to sunlight all around, possessing the power to weaken the undead. With this spell cast, you no longer need to fear lower-level undead like zombies and skeletons, and you'll be able to fight on equal or better terms even against moderately strong foes. |
| **Feign Death 假死**             | When an ally has sustained critical injuries that put them on the brink of death, and healing can't keep up, cast this spell to put them in a state of suspended animation. Once it's safe, heal them, and they'll be fine. It's a spell that minimizes combat casualties. However, I can't say whether the revived character will declare, "There IS an afterlife!" |
| **Neutralize Poison 解毒**       | A spell that heals a body afflicted by poison. If left untreated, poison can be fatal. Make sure to treat it early. Until you learn this spell, don't forget to carry antidote potions. |
| **Cure Paralysis 解麻痹**        | A spell that restores a paralyzed body to normal. If you can't move your body, even the strongest character won't stand a chance against a kobold. Quickly use this spell to recover. |
| **Cure Disease 解病**            | A spell that drives disease from the body and restores health. If there's a disease this spell can't cure, it must surely be one involving an evil deity.                          |

## Shaman Magic（精灵魔法）

> Fire burns, water flows, wind blows — all through the power of spirits. Shaman Magic, which harnesses this power, can be considered the most powerful magic.

| 法术                                | 评注                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Firebolt 火矢**                   | The most basic of the basics in Shaman Magic, which manipulates the power of the elements. It becomes ineffective if resisted, so it's not suited for slightly stronger opponents, but it's more than enough for small fry. |
| **Snare 捕缚**                      | Mobility is also part of combat ability. Use this spell to stop fast opponents in their tracks. Just be careful — it won't work on flying enemies.               |
| **Silence 沉默**                    | Spirit of the wind, steal that one's words! In other words, it's magic that prevents sound from traveling, interfering with spellcasting. The only drawback is that you perhaps won't be able to hear the dramatic final lines at the climax of a scenario. |
| **Will-o'-Wisp 鬼火**               | Saying that shamans can fight a little is only in comparison to sorcerers and wizards. When you're short on warriors, it's good to have the spirit of light, Will-o'-Wisp, fight as a substitute. |
| **Shade 暗影**                      | An even more reliable ally than Will-o'-Wisp. Of course, its offensive power is not to be underestimated either.                                                  |
| **Invisibility 隐形**               | If you completely disappear from sight, you can get behind enemies or even escape. However, it won't work on monsters that don't rely on sight.                  |
| **Push 推飞**                       | Basically, like telekinesis, it is a psychic power. You could push a strong enemy off a cliff. Pushing forward with brute force often proves fatal.              |
| **Shadow Body 影身**                | If you increase your DE with this spell and make it harder for enemy attacks to hit, you won't be easily defeated.                                                |
| **Flame Body 焰身**                 | A spell that makes it look to enemies like flames are approaching. Of course, if they come next to you, they'll take fire damage. If you have this spell cast on you, go berserk and charge right into the middle of the enemy. |
| **Air Armor 风甲**                  | A doubly beneficial spell that not only makes enemy attacks less likely to hit, but also reduces damage even if they do hit.                                      |
| **Sleep 睡眠**                      | Sleeping enemies become harmless. Once you cast this spell, the question becomes whether to attack enemies that are still awake or launch a preemptive strike on sleeping enemies. |
| **Valkyrie's Javelin 女武神之枪**   | Even though damage is reduced by the enemy's AV, Valkyrie's Javelin has tremendous power. Use this spell to defeat enemies one by one.                            |
| **Fanaticism 狂信**                 | A spell that increases attack power but decreases defense. It's safer to use this only against enemies with very high DE.                                         |
| **Confusion 混乱**                  | When surrounded by many enemies, use this spell to create a traitor among them. You might even be able to escape in the confusion.                                |

## Sorcerer Magic（魔术）

> Sorcerer Magic is how people use words to control matter. By learning this magic, which has been studied since ancient times, humanity draws closer to the divine.

| 法术                                | 评注                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Energy Bolt 能量箭**              | This spell is effective for dealing reliable damage to an opponent. Even if resisted, it still has some effect. Well, either way, it's still a low-level spell. |
| **Shield 护盾**                     | Though the principle differs, this spell has the same effect as the Shaman's Shadow Body. It's quite unfortunate that both cannot be cast simultaneously.        |
| **Read Language 解读文字**          | The reason wizards emerge exhausted from their studies after being holed up in their laboratories is likely because they've been casting this spell all night long. |
| **Sleep Cloud 睡眠之云**            | A more powerful spell than the Shaman's Sleep, as it can put multiple enemies to sleep. However, you must be careful not to put your allies to sleep as well.   |
| **Fire Weapon 燃焰武器**            | While it doesn't increase hit rate, it significantly increases damage. Moreover, it becomes effective against enemies with magic defense. It's effective against enemies with high AV but no DE. |
| **Vision 远视**                     | Veteran adventurers avoid danger as much as possible (otherwise, they would have died before becoming veterans). It's best to scout suspicious caves and such with this spell before entering. |
| **Levitation 浮空**                 | While it can be used when you can't reach something on a high shelf, it's useful in other situations too. For example, when attacked by wolves on a wide grassland. *What, how do you escape after that? That's for you to figure out.* |
| **Spider Web 蛛网**                 | Spider silk is quite strong, and magical spider silk is even more so — getting entangled in it is terrible. The only way to escape from this web is to have Dispel Magic cast on you. |
| **Dragon Tooth Warrior 龙牙战士**   | Dragon tooth warriors are among the strongest monsters you can summon and control. Since enemies use them frequently too, summon your own to make them fight each other. Even if the enemy's warriors remain, they shouldn't have much LP left. |
| **Slow 减速**                       | Do you cast it to lower the enemy's hit rate, or to run away? When the situation allows, running away is important. But if you only have one sorcerer, it's obviously more convenient to cast Quick next and flee. |
| **Quick 加速**                      | The importance of this spell lies not so much in the increased movement but in the increased TS. Even just 10 points makes quite a difference.                  |
| **Lightning Bolt 闪电**             | When fighting in the pyramid's inner chamber in the computer version, I was really happy when enemies lined up in a row. Because Lightning Bolt could be used effectively, but it was disappointing when the enemy moved first and avoided it. |
| **Fireball 火球**                   | This spell is also difficult to use in a different way than Lightning Bolt. It's fine when you're far enough away, but once a melee breaks out, it's a bit hard to cast. But there are wizards who don't care about that, and they'll cast it where enemies and allies are mixed together, with the troublesome result that allies always take more damage (damage is rolled separately, of course). |
| **Teleportation 传送**              | In the back of the Sorcerer's Guild, there is a heavy door. Where that door leads depends on the wizard casting this spell. Whenever you visit a major city, you should always go to the Guild. The registration fee varies by location, but it's usually around 1000 gold. |

## Common Magic（共通魔法）

> Common Magic can be positioned as a new school within the traditional Sorcerer Magic. It's an easy-to-use, popularized version of magic.

| 法术                                | 评注                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Counter Magic 反魔法**            | A subtle spell, but its effect is tremendous. When enemies who cast spells, like Dark Elves, appear, you should cast this spell first. Its effectiveness is guaranteed. |
| **Body Protection 身体守护**        | A spell that reliably reduces damage. Especially useful when you can't wear armor properly, such as on the sea.                                                    |
| **Light 光明**                      | A ray of light in a dark world. After all, you can't see in the darkness, so this spell becomes necessary. It might seem more convenient than a torch since it doesn't require preparation, but be careful because it will disappear if dispelled (not that you can do anything about it). |
| **Detection 探查**                  | It reveals traps and hidden doors, though if a hidden door has a trap on it, you can't tell the difference. Still, anyone who sets a trap on a hidden door is a bit strange. |
| **Unlock 开锁**                     | Along with Detection, this is a hateful spell that diminishes the value of scouts. Even though it opens any door, it won't open dimensional doors. That would be scary if it did, right? |
| **Enchant Weapon 附魔武器**         | If you miss an attack after having this spell cast on you, everyone should give you a hard time. If you miss with an FS boost of 20, you don't deserve to be a warrior. |
| **Dispel Magic 解除魔法**           | In a sense, it is the most powerful spell. After all, it can erase other spells. But since spells don't have intelligence, the drawback, if you can call it that, is that it also erases helpful magic. |
