> 来源：Record of Lodoss War Companion I（1989, Group SNE）PDF p.93–97
> 译本：英文同人翻译版

> *This is a grand encyclopedia collecting every conceivable matter related to Lodoss Island. If you've forgotten something about Lodoss, pull this out and look it up. Something should be written there for sure. **However, the authorities take no responsibility whatsoever for whether the contents are actually useful, so keep that in mind.***

| **Valis** | 瓦利斯 | A powerful nation on Lodoss Island. *Perhaps because kings have been chosen haphazardly for generations, turmoil occurs frequently.* |
