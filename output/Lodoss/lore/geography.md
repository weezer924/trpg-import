## 1. Northeast Region 东北区域

> **主要势力**：Alania（千年王国阿拉尼亚）

### White Dragon Mountains 白龙山脉

The famous **glacial mountain range** in the northeast.

- Named after **Bramd（布拉姆德），the ice dragon** who has dwelt in this land **since the fall of the ancient kingdom**
- Several places in these mountains have **ice spirits gathering** — rumors say careless travelers will be **frozen solid**
- Feared by people as a **cursed land rivaling the "Forest of No Return"**

### Northern Dwarves 北方矮人

Settlements of dwarves who:

- **Mine the abundant mineral resources** of the White Dragon Mountains
- **Display their full prowess as craftsmen and blacksmiths**

### Forest of No Return 不归之森

Forms the boundary between the **northeast and southeast regions**.

- **Largest forest on Lodoss Island**
- True to its name, **no one who has entered has ever come out again**
- Rumored cause:
  - Either **sealed by the magic of the High Elves** before Kastuul came
  - Or **the whispers of tree branches recite death spells whenever the wind blows**
- The truth remains uncertain.

## 2. Southeast Region 东南区域

> **主要势力**：Kanon（卡农王国，目前被 Marmo 占领）+ 黑岛 Marmo

South of the Forest of No Return lies the **Kingdom of Kanon**.

### Kanon Region

- **Gently rolling hills, fertile soil**
- **Prospers through animal husbandry and agriculture**
- **Renowned for producing fine quality liquor**

### Marmo — The Dark Island 玛尔莫黑岛

To the **south of Kanon** lies the island of **Marmo**, called the **"Dark Island"**.

- Feared as a **land of chaos** inhabited by **evil Youma（妖魔）**
- Cursed by the **evil god Kardis** and constantly emanates an **evil "aura"**
- The trees in the **"Forest of Darkness"** have trunks **twisted into bizarre shapes**, making it impossible to even distinguish the species

> **设定关键**：Marmo 是反派阵营 Beld 皇帝的根据地。Kardis 的尸骸腐朽于此，使整个岛永久被邪气笼罩。

## 3. South-Central Region 中南区域

> **主要势力**：Holy Kingdom of Valis（瓦利斯神圣王国）

Crossing the **Nazaar Mountains（纳扎尔山脉）**, also known as the **"Roof of Lodoss Island"**, from the southeast region.

### Geography

- Most of this region: **alluvial plains** formed by the **"Holy River" Fargo（圣河法尔戈）**
- At the river mouth lies a **famous large delta**
- **Western coast**: a **massive sand dune** stretches along it, forcing the road southwest to **make a deep detour inland**

### Royal Capital Roid 罗德首都罗伊德

To the **west of the royal capital Roid**:

- **"Lake Stillness" Lenoana（莱诺亚那湖，「寂静之湖」）**
- Adjoining: **"Great Marsh of Silence" Narse（纳尔斯，「寂静大沼泽」）**

> **重大遗迹**：Lake Lenoana is the **former site of Kuudo**（卡斯图尔时代的湖上都市）—— 它的废墟据说仍沉睡在湖底，**伴随着大量宝藏**。

## 4. Southwest Region 西南区域

> **主要势力**：分散的城镇与村庄（无统一王国）

A **plateau area** enclosed by the **"Green and Blue Mountains" Albora（阿尔波拉「翠蓝山脉」）**.

- Towns and villages **scattered in basins** here and there

### Lake Above the Clouds — Horus 云上之湖霍鲁斯

The famous **"Lake Above the Clouds" Horus** lies in these mountains.

- Formed when a **volcano of the same name erupted massively** during the ancient kingdom era
- The eruption **completely blew away the area near the crater**, water accumulated there
- **Warm water even in winter** (high geothermal heat at the lake bottom)
- **Many hot springs nearby**

## 5. Northwest Region 西北区域

> **主要势力**：Free City of Raiden（自由都市雷登）

The northwestern part of Lodoss Island, where **Raiden** is located, is dominated by the **"Brown Mountains" Lulauza（鲁劳萨「褐色山脉」）**. Habitable areas are extremely limited.

### Fire Dragon Mountain 火龙山

The **only active volcano** on Lodoss Island.

- Home to the **"Fire Dragon" Shooting Star（流星）**
- **Ferocious and hostile toward humans**

### Fire Dragon's Hunting Ground 火龙的狩猎场

The plains stretching east of the Lulauza Mountains.

- Became **uninhabitable** because they became this dragon's **feeding ground**
- Alternative name: **"Fire Dragon's Hunting Ground"**

### Great Straight Cliffs 大直崖

In the **northwestern part** of the Lulauza Mountains.

- **Cliffs that look as if they were cut with a blade**
- Stretch in a **straight line for hundreds of kilometers** from northeast to southeast
- How this terrain was formed remains a **mystery**
- **Legend**: it is the trace of where **Lodoss Island was separated from the continent by the magic of the goddess Marfa**

### Mirror Forest 镜之森

In the small plain between the Lulauza Mountains and the southern Albora Mountains.

- Said to have **existed since ancient times**
- **Ancient trees thousands of years old at its center**
- **Famous as the dwelling place of fairy folk** such as fairies and elves

### Blue Dragon Island 蓝龙岛

In the sea **north of Raiden**.

- Home to the **"Water Dragon" Abram（艾布拉姆）**
- Many **reefs around this island** — feared as a **graveyard of ships**

## 6. North-Central Region 中北区域

> **主要势力**：Kingdom of Flaim（弗雷姆王国）

A **desert land**. Mostly occupied by the scorching **"Wind and Fire Desert"**, with towns and villages thriving only along the **Sand River** and at **oases**, making habitable areas extremely limited.

### Sand River 沙之河

> Gets its name because **during the dry season, the water completely dries up in the downstream areas, miserably exposing the riverbed**.

## 五大龙王速查 Five Dragon Lords Reference

> 罗德岛上有 5 条**古龙**，皆是神战遗存的传奇生物：

| 龙           | 名      | 居所                | 区域       |
| ------------ | ------- | ------------------- | ---------- |
| Ice Dragon   | Bramd 布拉姆德 | White Dragon Mountains | 东北       |
| Fire Dragon  | Shooting Star 流星 | Fire Dragon Mountain | 西北       |
| Water Dragon | Abram 艾布拉姆 | Blue Dragon Island | 西北外海   |
| Earth Dragon | *未在 Companion I 命名* | — | — |
| Golden Dragon King | Mycen 米森 | Highland 公国 | Moss 区域 |

> **注意**：Mycen 是 Highland 开国王 Mycen I 的座骑（战后这条金龙继承了王名）。其余三条野生古龙（Bramd / Shooting Star / Abram）是潜在 BOSS 级敌人。
