## Age of the Ancient Kingdom: Kastuul 古王国时代

### 5000 Years Before the New Royal Calendar

After the final war in which all the gods perished, **a dark age continued for thousands of years**, but from within that devastation, a race gradually began to grow in power.

These were **humans**, said to have been **created by the gods in their own image**. These ancient humans were a **superior race**, far surpassing modern humans in **intelligence and spiritual power**.

### The Kingdom of Kastuul

The kingdom founded primarily by these ancient humans was **Kastuul（卡斯图尔）**, the **"wizard-king nation"** now known as **the Ancient Kingdom**.

- Arose from the **eastern regions of the Alecrast continent**, far north of Lodoss Island
- Eventually dominated the entire world, **including Lodoss Island**
- The wise wizards of the early Kastuul **avoided conflict with other races** — fairies, giants
- Built **cities connected by magic gates**, allowing instant travel between them
- The kingdom's dominion was **limited to small areas around the cities**, with other lands left as wilderness

### 2000 Years Before — Corruption Sets In

Prosperity eventually breeds corruption. Kastuul came to desire **trampling the other races living in the wilderness with magical power and bringing them under its control**.

In that wilderness lived not only fairies and giants, but also **humans — savages who were our ancestors**.

- They were **inferior in intelligence and spiritual power** to Kastuul humans
- But possessed **robust physical bodies more than enough to compensate**

The humans of Kastuul **despised these savages** (and feared them). The wizards **mercilessly hunted down these kinsmen and enslaved them**.

> **The Wizards' Hidden Fear Was Justified**: As Kastuul entered its later period, those with **inferior magical abilities gradually began to increase**. The wizards became divided into classes — **nobles** retaining traditional power, and **citizens** whose power was far inferior. The number of nobles **decreased every year**.

## Kastuul Reaches Lodoss 卡斯图尔到达罗德岛

### 1000 Years Before the New Royal Calendar

Lodoss Island was in a remote land far across the sea from the northern continent, and thus remained **free from Kastuul's rule for a long time**. However, the day finally came.

### Governor Le Frey — The Peaceful First Governor

The first governor sent to Lodoss was **Le Frey（勒弗雷）**, who excelled in the **power of enchantment**.

- Mild-mannered among the nobles of Kastuul
- Built a beautiful city named **"Kuudo"** on the **"Stillness Lake" Lenoana（莱诺亚那湖）**
- Sought to establish **peaceful relations** with the indigenous people of Lodoss

The indigenous people **rejected this** and challenged him to battle. Le Frey still attempted to settle matters peacefully and held a meeting with the chieftains of the savages — but at that gathering he was **killed at the hands of the savages**.

### Governor Aganaar — Rule by Force

After Le Frey's death, the one who succeeded him was **Aganaar（阿加纳尔）**, versed in the magic of **"spirit arts"**. From this point onward, members of his lineage would inherit the position of governor.

- In complete contrast to Le Frey, **Aganaar ruled through force**
- Employed all schools of Kastuul's magical power to **forcibly subjugate the resisting savages**

### The Last Resisters: The Flaim Tribe

The tribe that continued to resist until the very end was the one that lived in the land of **present-day Flaim**. This tribe was defeated during the reign of the governor after Aganaar. The tribespeople who despised submission **entered into a long life of wandering**.

> Thus, all of Lodoss Island came under Kastuul's rule. **Though the dark island of Marmo alone was an exception.**

For several hundred years, Lodoss Island would be governed by Kastuul with the lakeside city of **Kuudo as its capital**.

## The Fall of Kastuul 卡斯图尔的衰亡

This system continued until **Kastuul lost its power due to the rampage of magical forces**.

### New Royal Calendar Year 1–100

These new people drove out the forces of Kastuul and **established new kingdoms throughout Lodoss Island**. Wars were waged between these new kingdoms — the strong defeated the weak — and gradually large kingdoms formed.

This was the **early age of warring states**, lasting approximately 100 years.

### Tribal Society

The kingdoms of this era had a **strong regressive character**, harking back to pre-slavery times:

- **Tribal chiefs as leaders**, little division of labor
- **Social classes were not clearly defined**
- Even kings tilled the fields in peacetime
- When war came, **all adult males took up arms and fought**
- Defeated foes faced **slavery or death** with no mercy

### Transition to Modern Kingdoms

As nations grew larger, the blood of different tribes mixed, and the tribal state system began to collapse. Several **slave uprisings** occurred and the slave class was liberated. Kingdoms gradually took on forms similar to those of today.

> **Of all the early kingdoms, only one has survived to the present day: Alania, the great nation in the northeastern part of Lodoss Island.**

## The Six Kingdoms of Lodoss 罗德岛六大势力

### 1. Alania 阿拉尼亚 — "The Millennium Kingdom"

- **Founded:** ~400 years ago
- **Region:** Northeastern Lodoss
- **Status:** Continuing to prosper, but at the **end of the recent War of Heroes**, **Duke Raster, the king's brother, rebelled**. The nation is currently in **civil war over the throne**, facing potential division or collapse.

### 2. Kanon 卡农

- **Founded:** ~200 years ago
- **Region:** Southeastern Lodoss
- **Founding King:** **Ezor I（艾佐尔一世）**
- **Status:** **Destroyed by Emperor Beld in the early stages of the War of Heroes** — currently under **Marmo's control**.

> **Origin Story**: 200 years ago, **Vartis, chieftain of the dark elves**, invaded from Marmo. The land was engulfed in warfare for nearly 10 years and reduced to scorched earth. Ezor I unified the fragmented small kingdoms of Kanon, confronted Marmo's forces, achieved victory, and after the war established this venerable kingdom.

### 3. Raiden 雷登 — Free City

- **Region:** Northwestern Lodoss (port region)
- **Status:** **No kingdom** — the great merchants have formed a **council** to govern themselves.

> **History**: Not blessed with land suitable for agriculture, Raiden's people became **skilled in navigation and trade with the continent**, growing prosperous. **150 years ago**, the first kingdom was born here — but **20 years later** it was easily destroyed by **mercenaries hired from the continent by wealthy merchants who disliked paying taxes**. Since then, no kingdom has ever risen.

### 4. Holy Kingdom of Valis 瓦利斯神圣王国

- **Founded:** ~100 years ago
- **Founding King:** **Asnan（阿斯南）** — most influential noble of the previous era
- **Successor (current king):** **King Fahn（法恩王）** — known as the Hero King

> **Origin Story**: A great nation called **Elvec** had ruled this region with harsh oppression. The **Temple of Pharis led a rebellion**. The tide turned when **Knight Asnan**, moved by the teachings of Pharis, joined the rebellion. His subordinates and supporters all raised banners against the king, and **Elvec fell**. Asnan agreed to become king on the condition that he would **not become a hereditary monarch**, and began governance under the **law of the Temple of Pharis**.

### 5. Highland (Moss Region) 高地公国

- **Region:** Mountainous Moss region (south of Valis)
- **Founder:** **Mycen I（米森一世）— "the first Dragon Knight"**, who rode the **Golden Dragon King**
  - The dragon would inherit the name **Mycen** after the death of the founding king
- **Current Leader:** **Redrick I（瑞德里克一世）**, descendant of Mycen I
- **Status:** Likely to **reunify the Moss region** at the current pace.

> **Origin Story**: In the Moss region, small nations had been vying with each other since the fall of the ancient kingdom. The **warring states period continued until ~50 years ago** — until **the king of one small nation released Demons that had been sealed in "the deepest labyrinth"**. The battle against these Demons embroiled all of Lodoss, fiercely fought for **6 years** until the **Demon King was defeated by the Six Heroes**, including **King Fahn** and **the great sage Wort of Moss**. Mycen I took advantage of the chaos to unify the region.

### 6. Flaim 弗雷姆

- **Founded:** ~20 years ago — **the newest kingdom**
- **Founding King:** **Kashue（卡修），"the Mercenary King"** — still alive and well today
- **Region:** Central-northern Lodoss (desert)

> **Origin Story**: After the fall of Kastuul, two desert peoples — **the Wind Tribe** and **the Fire Tribe** — continued to fight, and no unified kingdom arose. **Kashue, originally hired as a mercenary by the Wind Tribe**, put an end to the conflict between the two tribes and established Flaim.

## Closing Note 结语

Flaim is the newest kingdom so far, but currently **warfare continues in various parts of Lodoss Island**, and from this warfare, **new kingdoms may yet emerge**.
