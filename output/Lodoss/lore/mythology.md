## The Separation of Lodoss 罗德岛的分离

In ancient times, **Lodoss Island is said to have been connected to the northern continent**. Our temple has passed down the following account of why it was separated from the continent and became an island.

---

## The War of the Gods 神战

As you all know, at the **end of the Age of the Gods**, there was a war between the **gods of light** and the **gods of darkness**. Of course, we can only imagine what their battle was like, but it was undoubtedly a fierce one.

In this battle:

- **The gods lost their physical forms** and became unable to guide us directly.
- Most of the **ancient races** that were powerful at the time perished.

---

## The Dragon Lords 龙王

The most powerful beings in this great war were the **ancient Dragon Lords（古龙王）**, said to be the most physically resilient race.

- These primordial dragons were **larger than mountains**; when they spread their wings, they covered the entire sky.
- The **flames they breathed melted everything**.
- Their **scales, harder than mithril silver**, along with their even **stronger minds**, repelled any attack.
- According to priests who believe dragons to be gods, **dragons were creatures born from the very beginning to kill gods**.

It is almost certain that these Dragon Lords destroyed the physical bodies of the immortal gods.

The Dragon Lords on both sides:

1. **Burned all the gods** belonging to the opposing side
2. Then **slaughtered each other** until only one remained
3. Even that **last one** is said to have been **gravely wounded and fallen into an eternal slumber**

---

## Marfa vs Kardis 玛法对卡迪斯

Our great **Marfa（玛法）** also participated in this war of the gods. The goddess Marfa is a **goddess who loves peace**. However, battles for survival and self-defense are natural, so as long as there were those who wished to defeat Marfa, fighting them was unavoidable.

### Marfa's Two Major Enemies

Among the gods on the side of darkness, **few regarded Marfa as an enemy**. However, those gods were the two **most powerful** ones:

| 黑暗神      | 中译     | 司掌                                           |
| ----------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Phalaris    | 法拉利斯 | The dark god himself                           |
| Kardis      | 卡迪斯   | The mad goddess of destruction (玛法的对偶)    |

> **Kardis was a counterpart to Marfa** — Marfa being a god of creation, Kardis a god of destruction. Her hatred was especially intense. When the battle began, **Kardis paid no attention to the other gods and targeted only Marfa**.

### Their Powers

- **Marfa**: promised abundance, possessed great magical power
- **Kardis**: an abominable power that would **rot everything she touched**, both heaven and earth

---

## The Fall of the Two Goddesses 两位女神之陨落

Marfa was **protected by three Dragon Lords** and possessed great magical power herself, so even with Kardis's terrible power of destruction, she could not be defeated. **Kardis herself was wounded** before the three Dragon Lords protecting Marfa.

So **Kardis borrowed five Ancient Dragon Lords from Phalaris** and sent them against Marfa.

### The Final Battle

1. The five (Phalaris's) and three (Marfa's) Dragon Lords fought fiercely and **mutually destroyed each other**.
2. **Kardis was finally able to engage Marfa herself in battle.**
3. While the two fought fiercely, they somehow came to **the land of Lodoss**, which at that time was connected to the northern continent.
4. **It was on this land that their fierce battle unfolded.**

The opposing powers of the two wore each other down, and finally **both perished together**:

- **Marfa fell in the land of Alania**
- **Kardis rotted away on the dark island of Marmo**

---

### Why Marmo is the Dark Island

The reason the dark island became an island where monsters run rampant is probably due to the **evil "aura" of Kardis** that remains even after her physical body perished.

### How Lodoss Was Separated

**Marfa**, fearing that this evil "aura" would spread to other lands, used her last magical power to **separate the land of Lodoss from the continent**.

The trace of that magical power is the **Great Straight Cliff（大直崖）** that extends west of Raiden.

> **Lodoss Island = where Marfa fell**, so abundance is promised. **But also where Kardis perished** — we must always be mindful of the threat of destruction and decay.

---

## Hope of Resurrection 复活的希望

Furthermore, the legend tells us:

- **Gods killed by the Dragon Lords will never be resurrected.** They were burned by their magical flames — not even a fragment remains.
- **Gods who fell in battle against other gods may still have the possibility of resurrection**, because the destruction of their bodies was not complete.

### The Sorcerers' Attempt

In the age of the **ancient kingdom**, there was a faction of sorcerers who attempted to resurrect the gods. That attempt seems to have been halted out of fear that it might trigger another war of the gods.

> *I have my doubts about whether that was a wise decision. After all, if they had resurrected only Marfa and the other gods of light, even the realization of an ideal world would have been possible.*

### Prayer Gives Power to Marfa

> *However, prayer gives power to Marfa. Our prayers may someday bring about Marfa's return. Everyone, let us pray with hope. May Marfa's blessing come upon us.*
>
> (What follows is a prayer in the holy language)

## 设定备注

- **Pharis**（法利斯，光之至高神）vs **Phalaris**（法拉利斯，暗黑至高神）— 两者拼写相近但是相对的神。本系统神官可信仰 Pharis（光），不可信仰 Phalaris（黑暗）。
- 本章是 Lodoss 世界观的根基，也是后续 Replay/小说中 **Kardis 复活计划** 与 **Marmo 黑暗势力** 的设定来源。
