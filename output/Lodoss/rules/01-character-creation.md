# Chapter 1: Character Creation 角色创建

> 来源：Record of Lodoss War Companion I（1989, Group SNE）PDF p.24–30
> 译本：英文同人翻译版

This chapter covers the creation of your character who will serve as your avatar in the game. By following the instructions and filling out the character sheet, you'll create an adventurer living on Lodoss Island. You can decide your character's personality and background yourself. However, we recommend that everyone consult together before creating characters to ensure the party is well-balanced. After all, you can't fight monsters if everyone is a wizard.

## Race Selection 种族选择

While many intelligent beings exist on Lodoss Island, there are only four types that can become player characters (PCs) in the game. These are humans, elves, half-elves, and dwarves, each with their own strengths and weaknesses.

### Human 人类

In other words, you (sorry if I'm wrong). Their strong points are being fortunate and their "flexibility" in being able to become any class.

- **Available classes:** All 7 classes

### Elf 精灵

When you say elf, you think of Deedlit, and when you say Deedlit, you think of elf — they're beautiful and wise. Their special abilities include longevity and having no beard. They can also produce half-elves.

- **Available classes:** Warrior, Shaman
- **Note:** Deedlit（蒂德利特）is a Shaman.

### Half-Elf 半精灵

Not to be like Spark, but half-elves are absolutely unfortunate. They're raised in either human society or elf society, but they get bullied in both — they're like a bat-like race. But thanks to that, they've become tough.

- **Available classes:** Warrior, Sorcerer, Shaman

### Dwarf 矮人

To all 3,005 dwarf fans across the nation, thanks for waiting. You don't need to be beautiful as long as you've got strength, just like Ghim, who stubbornly glared at the stars.

- **Available classes:** Warrior, Priest, Scout
- **Special ability:** Darkvision — see in the dark as clearly as in daylight

## Ability Scores 属性

A PC's abilities are represented by **seven stats**: Strength (ST), Endurance (EN), Agility (AG), Intelligence (IN), Luck (LU), Physical Beauty (PB), and Concentration (CN). Each takes a value of 5 or higher, and the higher the value, the greater the ability.

| 属性 (英) | 缩写 | 中文     | 说明                                                                                                                |
| --------- | ---- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Strength      | ST   | 力量     | 想要力量、力量、再力量的话就以这项为先。除魔法外，影响战斗的诸多面向。                                              |
| Endurance     | EN   | 耐力     | 一再被打也能爬起来的人这项数值高。注意僵尸不一定耐力高。                                                            |
| Agility       | AG   | 敏捷     | 行动速度的代表值。希望比别人先行动者必备。和早起无关——魔法睡眠下能否醒来用 EN 检定。                                |
| Intelligence  | IN   | 智力     | 通常代表智慧并影响魔法能力，本系统中也代表洞察力。                                                                  |
| Luck          | LU   | 幸运     | 是否走运。也影响对陷阱与魔法的抗性。Spark 的不幸不是这项数值低，而是玩家本人运气差。                                |
| Physical Beauty | PB | 外貌     | 美形之人无论古今都有优势——但也有 Fourth 这种例外。对人际关系（也许）有显著影响。                                  |
| Concentration | CN   | 精神力 / 气魄 | 通称「胆魄」。关键时刻这项值高有用。具体怎么有用，后面会揭晓。                                                       |

## Class Selection 职业选择

There are many professions in the world, but not many are suited for adventurers. PCs choose just one from the following seven specialized professions. **You cannot change careers later, so choose carefully.**

### Warrior (W) 战士

Fighter, soldier, whatever you call them — they're people who fight for those who are happy just punching things, regardless of principles or beliefs.

### Priest (P) 神官

A cleric who serves a god. Of course, there are denominations, and different ones are like completely different classes. Choose just one god to believe in from the following.

| 神      | 中译     | 神格 / 司掌                            | 备注                                                                                  |
| ------- | -------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pharis  | 法利斯   | 罗德岛神话中的至高神（光之最高神）     | 至高神在神话里通常都不是好相处的家伙。                                                |
| Marfa   | 玛法     | 地母女神，司掌治愈                     | 由 Neese 等侍奉。Slayn 之妻 Leylia 也是其神官。                                       |
| Myrii   | 玛利     | 战神                                   | 最简单直接的神。看 Greevus 的行动就懂。                                               |
| Rahda   | 拉达     | 知识神 / 学问之神                      | 如果古代有入学考试，这位神大概会赚翻。                                                |
| Cha Za  | 夏扎     | 财神                                   | 「赚钱了吗？」是其问候语，被商人广泛信仰。                                            |

### Scout (T) 斥候

A subtle class for connoisseurs. They're thieves, but saying that takes all the romance out of it. Think of Scouts as the Robin Hood types among thieves.

### Knight (K) 骑士

Warriors with principles and beliefs. Ultimately, they're folks who want to serve something — whether it's a king or some ideology. Because of their faith, they can use **Common Magic**, though their combat abilities are slightly inferior to those of Warriors.

- **Note:** Parn（帕恩）is a Knight.

### Shaman (H) 精灵使

A spellcaster who uses **Shaman Magic** by borrowing the power of spirits. They have pretty good combat abilities — an energetic soul who can sing and dance.

### Sorcerer (S) 魔术师

The quintessential magic-user; when people say "wizard," they generally mean Sorcerer. They learn highly offensive **Sorcerer Magic**.

### Wizard (Z) 贤者

The ultimate spellcaster who can wield both **Sorcerer and Shaman** spell lines. However, they learn spells slowly and might be dead weight for a while. **Secret:** at high levels, they can even learn Priest Magic. (This is the same in the computer version.)

## LP and MP 生命点与精神点

### LP (Life Points)

When this value reaches **zero or goes negative, you die.** It decreases (temporarily) when you're hit or have magic cast on you.

Reduced LP can be restored with Priest Magic spells like **Healing**, **Cure Injury**, and **Refresh**.

### MP (Mental Points)

When this value reaches **zero or goes negative, you lose consciousness.** It decreases when you use magic (also temporarily).

With rest, you recover **10% of your maximum value per hour** (fractions rounded down). Example: a character with a maximum of 55 would recover 5 points per hour.

## Skills 技能

In Lodoss RPG, actions like combat are resolved using skills. Detailed usage of skills is covered in **Chapters 3 and 5**.

| 技能 (英)        | 缩写 | 中译         | 说明                                                                          |
| ---------------- | ---- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fighting Skill   | FS   | 战斗技能     | 战斗中至关重要。代表攻击命中率。                                              |
| Defense Skill    | DE   | 防御技能     | 对物理攻击的防御值，越高越难被命中。携带盾牌可提升此值。                      |
| Thief Skill      | TS   | 盗贼技能     | 代表手部灵巧与肢体动作的技能。                                                |

A bonus is added to weapon damage when making physical attacks. **DB is not used for ranged weapons.**

DB is determined from ST — see [Table 1.3](#table-13-damage-bonus).

## Character Creation Procedure 创建流程

Even when race and class are decided, this alone is not enough to begin the game. Fill in the character sheet according to the following procedure.

### ① Determining Race, etc. 决定种族等

Decide on **race, class, age, gender, level, and name**, and enter them in the designated fields.

For beginners, it is appropriate to start at **level 1**, but the others are free choices.

You begin with **GP (gold)** equal to **10 × (IN + LU + PB)**.

### ② Rolling and Entering Ability Scores 掷骰决定属性

Determine the values from ST to CN by rolling dice.

- **ST through PB（六项基本属性）**：3D6 + race modifier（见 [Table 1.1](#table-11-race-modifiers)）
- **Concentration (CN)**：3D6 + 30

> **注意**：矮人 AG 可能变为 0，此时视为「不幸的流产」，从头来过。

### ③ Determining LP and MP

LP and MP are determined as follows:

- **LP** = EN + dice roll (varies by class; see [Table 1.2](#table-12-lpmp-table))
- **MP** = IN + dice roll (varies by class; see [Table 1.2](#table-12-lpmp-table))

### ④ Determining Damage Bonus

The damage bonus added varies depending on the ST value (see [Table 1.3](#table-13-damage-bonus)).

### ⑤ Determining Skills

Each skill value is determined as follows. Class modifiers come from [Table 1.4](#table-14-skill-modifiers-by-class).

| 技能 | 公式                                        |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------- |
| FS   | ST + AG + class modifier                    |
| DE   | (EN + AG) ÷ 2 (round down) + class modifier |
| TS   | AG × 2 + class modifier                     |
| MS   | IN + class modifier                         |
| RE   | LU × 2 + class modifier                     |

## Example: Greevus the Dwarf Priest 范例 — 矮人神官 Greevus

> 来源：PDF p.28（Chapter 1 末尾示例）

Today is the memorable birthday of Greevus. The plan is to have a character who can fight and also use some magic, so a **Dwarf Priest** would be optimal. The sect is, of course, the war god **Myrii**.

**1. Roll 3D6 + race modifier for each ability score:**

- ST 13, EN 13, AG 11, IN 15, LU 11, PB 9

> Hmm, how average. Just like a human. Oh well, "One does not fight with ability scores alone."

**2. LP and MP** (Priest uses D8 LP / D10 MP, plus EN / IN):

- LP = D8 + EN → max **21**
- MP = D10 + IN → comes to **23**

**3. Skills** (Priest class modifiers):

| Skill                     | Formula           | Value |
| ------------------------- | ----------------- | ----: |
| Fighting Skill (FS)       | ST + AG + 20      |    44 |
| Defense Skill (DE)        | (EN + AG)/2 + 10  |    22 |
| Thief Skill (TS)          | AG × 2 + 5        |    27 |
| Magic Skill (MS)          | IN + 20           |    35 |
| Resistance (RE)           | LU × 2 + 10       |    32 |
| Damage Bonus (DB)         | from ST 13        |     2 |

Greevus is successfully born.

## Note on Party Composition (PDF p.30)

Several characters gather to form a **party**. RPGs have something in common with team sports — just as in baseball, you can't win if everyone's a pitcher, a party of all wizards won't survive.

For a party of **6 or 7 people**, a typical formation:

- **2–3 combat personnel** (Warriors and Knights)
- **1 Priest, 1 Scout**
- **1–2 Sorcerers or Shamans**

Group SNE warns against the **"Eternal Champion"** type — a player fixated on the same kind of muscular warrior in every game (different from Moorcock's Elric/Corum; just means "Eternal Warrior"). They restrict their companions' class choices and miss out on RPG variety.
