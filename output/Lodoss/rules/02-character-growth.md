## Experience Points 经验值

In the Lodoss Island RPG, characters gain experience points through adventures. Experience points are accumulated each time, and when they reach a certain value, the character's level increases. At that time, the character's skills, LP, and MP increase and the character grows.

**The required experience point values are common to all classes.**

### Table 2.1 Experience Point Table

| Level | Experience Points (Total) |
| ----: | ------------------------: |
|     1 |                         0 |
|     2 |                     1,000 |
|     3 |                     2,000 |
|     4 |                     4,000 |
|     5 |                     8,000 |
|     6 |                    16,000 |
|     7 |                    32,000 |

> **注意**：本系统支持的角色等级范围为 1–7 级。

## Level Advancement 升级

In the computer version of Lodoss, the experience points needed to reach the next level were calculated using formulas like *"the value needed to reach the previous level × 1.7"*. But for us humans, managing such awkward numbers is troublesome, so we use **nice round values** (see Table 2.1).

The GM should manage experience points and award them to each character at appropriate times. **Refer to Chapter 8 for how to award them.**

## Increasing Stats 数值成长

Characters who reach a new level grow according to the following procedure.

### ① Increasing LP and MP

According to **Table 1.2** (LP/MP Table), roll the dice and add to the current values. Note that the dice differ by class.

| Class    |  LP |  MP |
| -------- | --: | --: |
| Warrior  | D10 |  D4 |
| Priest   |  D8 | D10 |
| Scout    |  D6 |  D6 |
| Knight   |  D8 |  D8 |
| Shaman   |  D6 | D10 |
| Sorcerer |  D4 | D10 |
| Wizard   |  D4 | D10 |

### ② Increasing Skills

Each skill increases by **1/5 of the skill modifier for that class** (see Table 1.4).

Example skill increases per level:

| Class    |  FS |  DE |  TS |  MS |  RE |
| -------- | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| Warrior  |   6 |   2 |   4 |   1 |   2 |
| Priest   |   4 |   2 |   1 |   4 |   2 |
| Scout    |   4 |   1 |   6 |   1 |   2 |
| Knight   |   5 |   2 |   1 |   4 |   3 |
| Shaman   |   3 |   1 |   2 |   6 |   2 |
| Sorcerer |   1 |   1 |   4 |   6 |   2 |
| Wizard   |   1 |   1 |   2 |   8 |   2 |

### ③ Increasing Spell Level

**Shamans, Priests, Sorcerers, and Wizards** learn new spells at **odd-numbered levels**. Refer to the tables below.

## Example: Greevus reaches Level 2 范例 — Greevus 升至 2 级

After several adventures, Greevus finally reached **level 2**.

**1. Increase LP and MP** (using class-based dice from Table 1.2 — Priest = D8 LP / D10 MP):

- Rolled D8 → +3 LP → **24 LP** (was 21)
- Rolled D10 → +5 MP → **28 MP** (was 23)

**2. Raise skills** (1/5 of class modifier, Priest):

- Fighting Skill: 20/5 = **+4**
- Magic Skill: 20/5 = **+4**
- Thief Skill: 5/5 = **+1**
- Resistance: 10/5 = **+2**
- Defense Skill: 10/5 = **+2**

**3. Magic** — Priest learns no new spells at level 2; will learn level-2 Priest Magic upon reaching **level 3**.
