## The Concept of Magic 五大法术系统

Magic in Lodoss RPG has **five schools**, each with its own distinct history and source of power.

| 系统           | 中译       | 来源 / 性质                                                                   | 学习者                  |
| -------------- | ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Shaman Magic   | 精灵魔法   | 与精灵结盟、借用其力量；通过妖精传入人类                                      | Shaman, Wizard          |
| Sorcerer Magic | 魔术       | 古王国遗产，研究物质变换的学问之术；阿拉尼亚的贤者学院仍在研究                | Sorcerer, Wizard        |
| Common Magic   | 共通魔法   | Randar 创制，可用罗德岛通用语操控；亦在贤者学院研究                           | Sorcerer, Knight, Wizard |
| Priest Magic   | 神官魔法   | 神官引发的神迹，依信仰之神而异                                                | Priest, 高级 Wizard     |
| Demon Scream   | 魔神咏唱   | 黑暗精灵、魔神、暗黑魔术师等黑暗阵营所用，本质成谜                            | NPC 专属 / 黑暗阵营     |

> **About mage equipment**: Mages are restricted in armor and weapons because they interfere with the precise movements needed to cast magic. **However, if they're not casting, they may wear them.**

## How to Use Magic 施法规则

Magic of any school can be used **without failure** if one has the correct words and gestures, plus sufficient mental power (MP).

### Resistance Check 抗性判定

Some spells have effects that differ depending on whether the target succeeds at resisting:

- When using a spell whose **Resist** column is **not "—"**, the target must immediately make a resist check.
- Roll **D100**: if the result is **≤ target's RE**, the resist succeeds.
- The Resist column lists what happens on success: **Effect negated** / **Effect halved** / etc.

### Stacking Rules 同名/同效叠加

- Spells that alter the **same value** (such as DE or FS) **do not stack**. The previously cast spell disappears, the new one takes effect.
- Casting the same spell repeatedly has no change in effect.
- **Exception:** The effects of **Fanaticism, Bless, and Battle Song** are **cumulative** across each other (though each spell still does not stack with itself).

### Casting Time 施法时间

- Approximately **10 seconds**, depending on spell length.
- During combat: **once per round only** (mental concentration required).

### MP Cost 精神消耗

- Once magic takes effect, MP decreases by the spell's required amount.
- If you don't have enough MP, **you cannot cast** that spell.
- If casting would reduce MP to **exactly zero**, the caster casts the spell **and then immediately falls unconscious** — won't wake without rest or magical recovery.

## Spell List Format 法术列表格式

Each spell entry uses the following format.

| Field            | 中译         | 说明                                                                       |
| ---------------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Name             | 名称         | The spell's name translated into the common tongue.                        |
| Range / Area     | 范围         | Single target / X-meter diameter / Summon / etc. **Limited to caster's visible range.** Allies in the area are also affected. |
| MP Cost          | MP 消耗      | Mental Points consumed by the caster.                                      |
| Resist           | 抗性         | Effect on target's successful D100 resist check; "—" if not applicable.   |
| Duration         | 持续时间     | From instantaneous to permanent. LP/MP changes remain after duration ends. |

### Summon Common Rules 召唤法术通则

For spells with Range = "Summon":

1. The data for summoned monsters is as listed in the **monster list**.
2. While controlling them, **all of the monsters' abilities can be used, including magic**.
3. While controlling a summoned monster, **the caster cannot perform any other actions**. If the caster is injured (loses LP/MP, or falls asleep due to magic), concentration breaks and the monster is **immediately returned to its original world**.
4. The monster acts on the **caster's turn**.

## Example: Greevus turns undead 范例 — Greevus 面对不死生物

> 来源：PDF p.39（Chapter 4 中段示例）

One day, after reaching **Level 3**, the party was ambushed by **zombies and ghouls** while raiding an evil cult's temple.

**1. Turn Undead** — Three enemies within 3 meters. One fails its resistance check and collapses.

> "As expected, the undead have high resistance values."

**2. Holy Weapon** — Greevus spends one round each on Garrack's and Spark's weapons to imbue them with holy power. Beside them, Aldonova fires off **Energy Bolts**.

**3. Result** — All enemies crumbled away before Greevus could even strike directly.

> "What the — I didn't even get a chance to hit anything!"
