# Chapter 5: Action Resolution 行动判定

> 来源：Record of Lodoss War Companion I（1989, Group SNE）PDF p.49–51
> 译本：英文同人翻译版

This chapter deals with how to determine the success or failure of player actions. Most actions in the game (such as walking or picking up objects) don't require any resolution, but **special actions** like walking without making a sound or picking up a 100-kilogram rock do require a success check.

## Skill Checks 技能检定

In Lodoss RPG, **skill values are primarily used to determine the success or failure of character actions**. The procedure:

1. Roll **D100**
2. If the result is **≤ relevant skill value (after modifiers)**, you succeed
3. This is called a **skill check**

### Who Rolls?

- **In most cases, the player rolls.**
- **Exceptions** (GM rolls): trap detection, secret door discovery, etc.
  - Reason: if the player knows the check succeeded and the GM still says there's no secret door, the player can be **absolutely certain that no secret door exists there** — which spoils the game.

## Success Rate Modification 成功率修正

The GM should assess the difficulty level and modify the skill value **before the check**.

### Success Rate Modification Table

| 难度描述                                    |   修正 |
| ------------------------------------------- | -----: |
| Only a god could succeed at this!           |   −100 |
| Only a master could succeed at this!        |    −50 |
| A bit difficult for an amateur, isn't it?   |    −20 |
| Pretty average, I'd say                     |      0 |
| Even an amateur could manage this, right?   |    +20 |
| Usually succeeds                            |    +50 |
| Even a baby could do this                   |   +100 |

## Per-Skill Usage 各技能用法

### Fighting Skill (FS) 战斗技能

Represents **strength and combat ability**. Primarily used for hit rolls during combat, but also used for **strength checks** such as lifting heavy rocks.

### Defense Skill (DE) 防御技能

Represents the ability to **protect oneself from physical attacks**. Specifically, reduces the enemy's hit probability during combat. **Temporarily increases** when carrying a shield or using defensive magic.

### Thief Skill (TS) 盗贼技能

Represents **agility and manual dexterity**. Used for:

- Balance checks in places with poor footing
- Climbing walls
- Success checks when handling **locks and traps**
- Finding hidden objects
- Leaving footprints (or covering them)

### Magic Skill (MS) 魔法技能

In this world, magic is synonymous with knowledge — this skill primarily represents **depth of knowledge**.

- Used to check whether a character has knowledge about a particular matter
- Also serves as a **sixth sense / extrasensory perception** — used to check whether a character senses danger

### Resistance (RE) 抗性

Represents **resistance against magic**. For hostile spells, in most cases a Resistance check is made with D100, and if successful, the magical effect is **nullified or reduced**. See Chapter 4 for details.

## Concentration (Guts) Check 精神力（胆魄）检定

Also called **"guts"** — represents the potential to manifest superhuman strength in dire situations.

### Rules

- A character can attempt this check **up to a number of times per day equal to their level**.
- When making any skill check, you can declare a **CN check**: roll D100, and if **≤ CN value**, the action **succeeds spectacularly**.
- If successful during an attack roll, it is treated as a **critical hit**.
- The CN check can also be attempted **after a normal skill check has failed**.
- If you attempt the CN check first and fail, you can **still proceed to make the normal skill check**.

> **重要**：CN（精神力 / 胆魄）**不会随等级提升**。"Guts are determined from the moment you are born."

> **使用顺序**：
> 1. 直接 CN 检定 → 成功＝壮举（攻击则致命一击）
> 2. CN 失败 → 仍可做普通技能检定
> 3. 普通技能失败 → 还可补一次 CN 检定（每日总次数受等级限制）

## Example: Greevus escapes 范例 — Greevus 越狱

> 来源：PDF p.51（Chapter 5 末尾示例）

The party was carelessly captured. Tied up and thrown into separate cells.

**1. Tear the ropes** (Fighting Skill, GM rules −20 modifier):

- Greevus rolls FS check at −20 → success. **Tore right through them.**
- All other party members **failed**.

**2. The cell lock** (Thief Skill, simple lock but no tools = no modifier):

> "BUT, however, but still — priests don't steal. My Thief Skill is practically nonexistent."

- Greevus rolls D100 → **fail**.

**3. Spark calls out: "Greevus, guts! Use your guts!"**

- Greevus's CN value is **38**. Rolls D100 → gets **15**. **Success.**
- The lock clicked open.

> "Time to escape. Wait for me, you villains — I'll finish you off soon enough."
