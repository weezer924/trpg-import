## Currency 货币

The currency of Lodoss Island is gold coins, classified into two types by size:

| 货币          | 缩写  | 价值          | 备注                                      |
| ------------- | ----- | ------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Gold          | GP    | 1 GP          | 各国按通用规格铸造，基本货币单位          |
| Raiden        | RP    | 100 GP        | 由 **Raiden（雷登）** 铸造的更大金币       |

> **1 RP = 100 GP**

## Item List Reading 物品表读法

Classes that **cannot use** an item generally don't wear or carry it because it would interfere with the class's abilities.

> **Rule:** If you wear or carry **forbidden equipment**, all your abilities are halved and you cannot use magic.

The item lists **don't include weight** — this is intentional. Play according to common sense rather than worrying about detailed numbers. *Carrying 10 swords or 3 spare sets of armor is ridiculous to imagine — in such cases, **buy a horse**.*

### Notes

- **☆ Two-handed weapon**
- **Lance** can only be used **on horseback**.
- **Bastard Sword** can be used with one or two hands. The first damage value is one-handed (`D6+4`); the second is two-handed (`D10+3`).
- **Whip:** On a critical hit, **entangles the enemy** and restricts movement. To break free, must roll **TS or below on D100** (for monsters, hit rate or below).
- **\* Ranged weapons** have a range of **100 meters** — except **Sling: 50 meters**.
- **Damage Bonus (DB) does NOT apply to ranged weapon damage.**

## Magical Weapons and Armor 魔法装备

Some weapons and armor have been enchanted when made, classified into ranks **+1 to +3**.

### Pricing Multiplier

| Rank | Price Multiplier |
| ---: | ---------------: |
|   +1 |             ×10  |
|   +2 |            ×100  |
|   +3 |          ×1,000  |

### Effects

| 装备类型        | +1 效果         | +2 效果          | +3 效果          |
| --------------- | --------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- |
| Weapon          | FS +10, Dmg +1  | FS +20, Dmg +2   | FS +30, Dmg +3   |
| Armor           | AV +1           | AV +2            | AV +3            |
| Shield          | DE bonus +10    | DE bonus +20     | DE bonus +30     |

| 戒指                | 效果                                              |

## Famous Magical Items 名物

> Items so valuable they have **no fixed price** — valued at market price. Possessed by heroes and royalty.

### Rune Staff 符文之杖

A relic from an ancient kingdom, equivalent to a **Staff +3**. Additionally, can cast **Meteor Strike** twice per day.

### Spider Sword 蛛剑

A magical sword passed down in the **royal family of Alania**. Equivalent to a **Short Sword +2**. Can cast **Spider Web** up to **5 times per day**.

### Lawful Blade 正义之刃

A sword owned by **King Fahn of Valis（瓦利斯王法恩）**. Equivalent to a **Longsword +4**.

### Light Reflector 光辉反射者

Also owned by **King Fahn**. Equivalent to a **Large Shield +1**, but separately has a **20% chance each round to completely block an enemy's attack**.

> **设定备注**：这些是出现在罗德岛战记小说与 Replay 中的著名魔法物品。Companion I 中只列出 4 件代表，Companion II 与后续作品有更多扩展。
