## Awarding Experience Points 经验值发放

### Combat XP 战斗经验

The total experience points from defeated monsters (you don't have to **kill** them — just **defeat** them) go to **each character**.

> **重要**：These points are **NOT divided by the number of players** — every PC who participated gets the full XP, regardless of party size.

However, **combat XP is merely supplementary**. The largest source of experience is **mission points**.

### Mission Points 任务经验

Mission points are awarded when the **scenario's objective is achieved**.

| Outcome | Mission Point Guideline                                    |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Success | Enough that characters level up after **2–3 missions**     |
| Failure | About **half** of the success amount                       |

### Why This Two-Track XP System? 为何这样设计？

The system has **two purposes**:

1. **Prevents the game from becoming a slaughter-fest.** Since combat XP is negligible compared to mission XP, players can focus on achieving objectives **while avoiding combat as much as possible**.

2. **Makes it easier to reward or penalize characters.** While mission points are **basically awarded equally**, you can give bonuses or penalties to characters who had an extreme impact on mission completion.

> *TTRPG is a game where everyone works together to create fun. If you penalize characters who diminish the fun and reward those who do the opposite, each player will learn to make the game enjoyable for everyone. Isn't this kind of guidance what determines a master's skill?*

## The GM Speaks: "Being God is Hard" 成为神并不容易

> 来源：PDF p.65（Group SNE 自述专栏）

In RPGs, the **Game Master (GM)** is such an important figure that they're often compared to a **god**. When you think of a god, you might imagine someone with absolute power, but it's actually quite a tough job.

- Preparation alone takes quite a bit of time.
- The players chirp like hungry chicks saying "Let's play~"
- During the game, if you slip up even a little, they immediately call you out on it (though maybe that's fine).

> *The only thing that binds a god isn't the rules, but the spirit of having fun together. Because the greatest joy for a GM is when the players are having fun.*
