## Premise

The mice of **Oaksgrove** (hex 1) are worried about their friends and cousins in **Stumpsville** (hex 15) after the village's usually reliable delivery of cheese failed to arrive on market day.

**Secret** (unknown to the players): the mice of Stumpsville have been **kidnapped by the Dedratz** (a rat gang, hex 9) as a gift for the cat lord **Balthazar** (hex 8). The rats are holding the villagers in their **secret hideout at hex 9 (Woodshed)** until the trade can be made.

Will the players solve the mystery and find where the villagers have been taken? Can they rescue the mice in time?

Let's play to find out.

## How to start

Drop the PCs at **hex 15: Stumpsville** (the assumed entry — "The players start here" per the source map). The village appears abandoned but still contains a handful of clues:

- A surviving snake in its cage (still being tended, so the kidnapping was recent)
- Two villagers (Roquefort and Paneer) hiding, trapped in spider webs — they saw the rats
- "Dedratz Live" graffiti on the walls
- The cheese factory left mid-production, with **Jack the cheesemaker** hiding behind a vat

Jack and the rescued villagers can point players toward hex 9 (Woodshed) or hex 8 (Balthazar's Gate). Players may also want to report back to Oaksgrove (hex 1), petition Lord Larkspur at Menhir Mot (hex 16), or bypass the hexcrawl entirely and try to infiltrate Balthazar's barony past hex 8.

See **`stumpsville.md`** for the full Stumpsville site, **`hexes.md`** for the wider region.

## Factions

Format per `mausritter-hexcrawl.md` — resources + progress-tracked goals.

### Balthazar (Cat lord)

Corresponds to the generic Cat lord template (`mausritter-hexcrawl.md` § Example factions 1).

**Resources**
- Terrifying presence
- Hired mercenaries and bandits
- Exorbitant wealth

**Goals**
- ○○○ Raise bribes from settlement
- ○○○ Kidnap mouse servants
- ○○○○○ Subjugate a settlement

### Dedratz (Rat gang)

Corresponds to the generic Rat bandits template, but tied upward to Balthazar (appeasing him is goal #1, not the generic "dominate a trade route").

**Resources**
- Ruthless gang
- Secret woodshed hideout (hex 9)

**Goals**
- ○○○ Appease a cat lord *(this is the active goal — deliver kidnapped Stumpsville mice to Balthazar)*
- ○○○ Establish protection racket
- ○○○○ Capture a fortress

### Lord Larkspur (Noblemouse)

Corresponds to the generic Noblemouse template.

**Resources**
- Indolent knights
- Menhir Mot (hex 16, his seat)

**Goals**
- ○○ Raise tax revenue
- ○○○ Acquire new land rights
- ○○○○○ Supersede the Earl of Ek
