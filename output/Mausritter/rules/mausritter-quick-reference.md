## Best practices

- **Ask lots of questions.** Make notes. Draw maps.
- **Work together.** Devise schemes. Recruit allies.
- **Dice are dangerous.** Clever plans don't need to roll.
- **Play to win.** Delight in losing.
- **Fight dirty.** Run. Die. Roll a new mouse.

## Saves

Roll **d20 equal or under** an attribute:

- **STR** — tests of physical strength and resilience.
- **DEX** — tests of speed and agility.
- **WIL** — tests of strength of will and charisma.

- **Opposed saves:** both Save, **lowest successful value** wins.
- **Advantage:** save from a strong position → roll **2d20, take lowest**.
- **Disadvantage:** save from a weakened position → roll **2d20, take highest**.
- **Attribute damage:** when an attribute is reduced by damage, roll d20 equal or under the **reduced value** (not the original).

## Magic

1. Invest **1–3 Power** (up to number of **unused dots**).
2. Roll **d6 for each Power** invested.
3. For each die of value **4-6**, mark one **usage**.
4. The spell effect happens, using **[SUM]** and **[DICE]**.

**Mishaps:** for every **6** rolled, take **d6 WIL damage**. Then make a **WIL save**; fail → gain **Drained** Condition.

## Combat

On your turn, **move** and take an **action**.

**Initiative (Surprise):**
- Enemy surprised → you go before them.
- Enemy not surprised → make a **DEX Save** to act first.

**Attacks:** roll your weapon's die and deal that much damage to an enemy, **minus armour**.
- **Impaired** → roll **d4** instead (regardless of weapon).
- **Enhanced** → roll **d12** instead.

**Damage flow:**
1. Dealt to **HP** first.
2. Once HP is depleted → damage spills into **STR**.
3. After taking STR damage → **STR Save**.
4. Fail → **Injured** Condition + **incapacitated**.

**Death:** if STR is reduced to **zero**, OR you are incapacitated for **6 Turns**, you die.

**Usage:** after combat, roll **d6** for each weapon, ammunition, and armour **used**. On **4-6**, mark usage.

## Rest

| Type      | Time   | Effect                                                                                       |
| --------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Short** | 1 Turn | Restore **d6 + 1 HP**.                                                                       |
| **Long**  | 1 Watch | Restore **all HP**. If HP is full, restore **d6 to an attribute score**.                   |
| **Full**  | 1 week | **Fully restore** HP and attribute scores. (Typically 20p for food and board in a settlement.) |
