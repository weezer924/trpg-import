## General Features

- **Lighting:** Oil lamps, torches, and hearths throughout; most rooms are lit.
- **Sound carry:** The **guards in Area 4** hear any noise made in Areas **2, 3, 5, and 8**. This is the single biggest tactical constraint in the north half of the Den.
- **Doors:** Wooden, creaking, stuck, heavy, iron, or secret — each is noted per room. Iron doors generally block sound; wooden and creaking ones do not.
- **Violin music** (faint, melancholy) drifts through most of the Den from the two skeleton violinists atop the pillar in Area 9 — a constant ambient cue.

## The Rotblack Sludge (General Rules)

*(Applies wherever PCs enter the central sludge cavern — Areas 9, 12, 15.)*

Oily, black, reeks of sulfur. **Scorching hot.**

- **Submersion heat:** Test Toughness **dr8** or take **d4 damage every round** submerged.
- **Crossing time:** Swimming from one sludge-adjacent room to another takes **4 rounds**.
- **Gutworm attack:** Every swim, **1–2 on a d6** the Gutworm bites for **d10 damage**. When bitten, test Agility **dr6** or be **devoured and die instantly**.
- **Gutworm reach:** The Gutworm can easily reach Areas **9, 12, and 15**.

> Full Gutworm stat block appears in Area 9 and in `encounters.md`.

## 1. Entrance

*(PDF p.5)*

_Stale smell._

- **Lit oil lantern** hangs from the ceiling.
- **Small stream of water** crosses the room from holes in the walls. **Black-violet butterflies** flap haplessly over the water.
- **Eat the butterflies:** heal **d6 hP**.

**Exits:**
- North: into the Den proper (toward Area 2).

## 2. Dining Hall

*(PDF p.6)*

_Warm and bright. Rotten smell. Faint, sad violin music from the north._

- **4 giant oil lamps.**
- **Large wooden table** with 20 chairs. Set with mugs, plates, and spoons, but no food or drink.
- **A bearded man** sits at the far end of the table. Skin ashen grey, eyes dark, dressed in a dusty old cloak. It is impossible to communicate or contact him. The PCs can poke, hit, or attack him **without response**.

**Exits:**
- **East: creaking door** to the Guard room (Area 4). The guards audibly complain and move about.
- **South: wooden door** to the Entrance (Area 1).

**Interaction — If everyone is seated at the table:** The bearded man comes to his senses and erupts with rumbling laughter. He tells stories of old for a few minutes, then slowly and irrevocably regresses into his own world again.

> ⚠ The guards in Area 4 hear any noise made here.

## 3. Library / Bedroom

*(PDF p.6)*

_Cool, slightly smoky air._

- **A pair of torches** are dimly glowing.
- **2 beds:** three skeletons (likely human) lie slumbering on them.
- **Gigantic bookshelf:** obscure literature, written with a frenzied hand in an unknown language.
- **Bedside table:** withered flowers in a clay vase.

**Exits:**
- **North: heavy door** to the Guard room (Area 4). An ear pressed against it will hear voices.
- **South: wooden door** to the Entrance (Area 1).

**Interaction — Steal the flowers or a book:** If anyone carries the vase or a book out of this room, **the skeletons awaken and attack**.

**Interaction — Study the books more closely (d4):**

| d4 | Outcome |
|---:|---------|
| 1 | Booklet. The text can be interpreted and appears to be a **random unclean scroll** (see Mörk Borg core, p.35). The skeletons do **not** awaken if taken. |
| 2 | The reader gets light-headed and for the next half hour gains **dr+2 on Presence and Agility tests**. |
| 3 | The text is completely incomprehensible. |
| 4 | The text makes the reader yell: _"You dead, arise!"_ after which a **Tired crystal demon** appears. The skeletons do **not** awaken. |

**Monsters:**

- **3 slumbering skeletons** — thoughtless, without goal.
  `hP 5  Morale 7  No armor  Bony fists d4`
  One skeleton instead wields a **Jagged scimitar d4**.

- **Tired crystal demon** (appears on book-study result 4) — bound to this room, longing for a way to escape.
  `hP —  Morale —  Cannot be harmed`
  The demon will **drain the text** from one scroll presented to it, then sends out a **Mental shockwave** before vanishing into thin air.

> ⚠ The guards in Area 4 hear any noise made here.

## 4. Guard Room

*(PDF p.7)*

_Rotten stench. Hot. Sad violin music from the staircase._

- **Crooked guards (d4)** pass time here. **One carries prison-cell keys** (for Area 5).
- **Fireplace** is blazing hot.
- **Debris and wooden table** with chairs.

**Exits:**
- **West: iron door** to the Cells (Area 5) — scratching noises are heard through it.
- **West: creaking door** to the Dining hall (Area 2).
- **South: heavy door** to the Library / Bedroom (Area 3).

**Searching the debris (d6):**

| d6 | Find |
|---:|------|
| 1 | Bony remains of a dog. There is still some meat (enough to sustain one person for one day). |
| 2 | Necklace with a black stone. |
| 3 | 3d10 silver. |
| 4 | Urn with a fine powder (poison: test dr14 or lose **−d8 hP**). |
| 5 | 1–3: sacred scroll; 4–6: box containing a small beetle (harmless but bites). |
| 6 | Small crossbow (d6) with **Presence +6 bolts**. |

**Monsters:**

- **d4 Crooked guards** — follow Fletcher's every word. He cursed their memories; they do not recall why they serve him. They don't care about Lesdy in the Greenhouse. If pressed they tell the PCs about the lost son but do **not** know how to enter his room.
  `hP 8  Morale 7  Leather −d2  Sword d6  Femur d4`

## 5. Cells

*(PDF p.8)*

_Repulsive stench of death and gore, bodies everywhere._

- **Corpses.** Some whole, some torn apart.
- **Prisoners.** Emaciated and pleading but mad.

**Exits:**
- **East: iron door** to the Guard room (Area 4) — audible voices behind.
- **West: stuck door** to the Corridor (Area 6) — dead silent.

**Monsters:**

- **10 Mad prisoners** — they only know the guards. Every now and then a prisoner is taken from the cells, never to be seen again. Weak and insane, they beg for help but strangle anyone who gets too close.
  `hP 2  Morale 4  No armor  Strangling hands d4 / round`
  - **To avoid being grappled:** test Agility **dr12**.
  - **To break free once grappled:** test Strength **dr12**.

> ⚠ The guards in Area 4 hear any noise made here.

## 6. Corridor

*(PDF p.8)*

_Dark and cold, light at the end of the corridor. Violin music._

- **Paintings on the walls:** landscapes, bland.
- **Large hidden pit trap** in front of the paintings. Covers the entire width of the corridor.

**Exits:**
- **East: stuck door** to the Cells (Area 5) — scratching noises.
- **South: quiet door** to the Dining hall (Area 2).

**Discover the pit trap:** With enough light and a successful **Presence dr14** test (by the PC with the highest Presence), angular lines are visible in the floor.

**Take or adjust the paintings:** If anyone removes a painting, the pit trap drops the PCs into **Area 10 (the Tunnel)**. The fall deals **d6 damage**.

## 9. Gem Room

*(PDF p.12)*

_Illuminated with spectacular colors. Stuffy air reeks of sulfur. Rotblack Sludge and melancholy violin music._

- **50-foot-tall pillar** in the middle of the sludge. Atop it, **two skeletons play the violin** (the music heard throughout the Den).
- **Cave wall with gems:** beautiful green-pink-violet gems fixed into the wall.
- The room is mostly **Rotblack Sludge** — see the general rules at the top of this file.

**Exits:**
- **East: archway** to the Chain room (Area 8).
- **West (through sludge):** Statue room (Area 12) and Forge/slaughterhouse (Area 15), both 4 rounds' swim away.
- **Ceiling tunnel:** connects down from the pit trap in the Corridor (Area 6) via Area 10.

**Entry from the Tunnel:** If anyone fell through the Corridor pit trap and arrives here via Area 10, **the Gutworm attacks immediately**.

**Monsters:**

- **Gutworm** — unfathomably long, thick as an oak. Hides in the Rotblack Sludge. Can easily reach Areas 9, 12, and 15. Will **not defend Fletcher**; if Fletcher dies, the Gutworm sinks, dead but dreaming.
  `hP 50  Morale —  Thick hide −d6  Razor-sharp teeth d10`
  When bitten, test Agility **dr6** or be **devoured, dying instantly**.

## 10. Tunnel

*(PDF p.13)*

_Voices emanating from the ceiling sing in a minor key._

- **Passage tight west of the pit trap.** PCs must squeeze through. **Large and awkward things won't fit** (heavy armor included).
- **Ceiling tunnel** up to the pit trap in the Corridor (Area 6).

**Exits:**
- **West: opening** to the Greenhouse (Area 11).
- **East: opening** to the Gem room (Area 9) — entering triggers an immediate Gutworm attack (see Area 9).

## 11. Greenhouse

*(PDF p.13)*

_Hot, stuffy but pleasant. Smells sweet._

- **Oil lamps** hang from chains in the ceiling.
- **Glass-walled room with plants:** palms, cacti, and flowers grow rampantly. Behind the glass, dim **moonlight** is visible.
- **Small camp:** **Lesdy** sits by a campfire, reading. She offers PCs food and rest. If they accept, she introduces her **three young companions** as her _hosts_. Initially outgoing and kind.

**Exits:**
- **East: tight crawlway** to the Tunnel (Area 10). Fletcher cannot fit through it — this is why he can't reach Lesdy.

**NPCs:** See `npcs.md` for Lesdy's full profile and agenda. Quick stats:

- **Lesdy** — long dark hair, dressed in a gunny sack with armholes. Nice but manipulative. Trying to turn the Gutworm against Fletcher; seeks the tunnels and caves deep beneath the Sludge; believes consuming the sludge grants power to control creatures, but the Gutworm attacks her whenever she tries.
  `hP 5  Morale 4  No armor  Unarmed attack d4`

- **3 Hosts** — young, dressed in rags. Zealous.
  `hP 7  Morale —  No armor  Long knives d6`

## 12. Statue Room

*(PDF p.13)*

_Stinks of sulfur. Rotblack Sludge and violin music._

- **Black statue** of the one-eyed **King Lenard II**. Obvious cavity where the eye would be. Investigation reveals **bloodstains in the socket**.
- **Cobbled floor** — unlike the rest of the Den.

**Exits:**
- **West: Rotblack Sludge.** The western wall collapsed into a large cavern 60 feet deep. Sulfuric haze obscures the view, but one can almost see the Forge/slaughterhouse (Area 15) and the Gem room (Area 9) through the fumes.
- **North: wooden door** to the Debris room (Area 14). **Locked** — Fletcher has the **oddly-shaped key**.
- **South: iron door** to the Chain room (Area 8). Can be opened from **this** side (not the other).
- **East: secret, heavy stone door** to the Son's room (Area 13).

**After a few minutes in this room:** the floor **tilts to the west**. Test Agility **dr16** or fall into the sludge.

**Place an eye in the King's socket:** the secret door to the Son's room opens with a loud crack.

> The Gutworm can reach any PC in the sludge here — see general rules.

## 15. Forge / Slaughterhouse

*(PDF p.15)*

_Sooty and very hot, sulfuric haze from the Rotblack Sludge._

- **Fletcher, the cannibal warlock,** dwells here. He carries the **oddly-shaped key** to the door between the Debris room and the Statue room.
- **Lit by two large hearths,** used as smelters and ovens for preparing meals.
- **Chunks of meat** hang on ceiling hooks — largely human flesh, adults and children.

**Exits:**
- **South: Rotblack Sludge.** The southern wall collapsed into a large cavern 60 feet deep. Sulfuric haze obscures the view, but one can almost make out the Gem room (Area 9) and the Statue room (Area 12) through the fumes.
- **East: wooden door** to the Debris room (Area 14).
- **West: stepladder** to a hatch in the Pump room (Area 7).

**Monsters:**

- **Fletcher, the cannibal warlock** — 7 feet tall, built like a grizzly. Sooty, bald, and covered in tattoos. Rules the Den. Hates Lesdy in the Greenhouse but **can't fit through the Tunnel** to reach her.
  `hP 20  Morale —  Hardened skin −d4  Red-hot flail d8 + severe burn`
  **Severe burn:** victim suffers **Agility tests at dr+2** for a day.
  **Powers:** every third round Fletcher uses a Power (automatically succeeds). Roll d4:

  | d4 | Power |
  |---:|-------|
  | 1–2 | **Nine Violet Signs Unknot the Storm:** d2 bolts of lightning, **d6 damage** each. |
  | 3 | **Daemon of Capillaries:** one creature chokes for **d6 rounds**, **d4 hP damage per round**. |
  | 4 | **Ich-bin-luft** (unique Power): Fletcher is **invisible for the next two rounds**. He can still attack. When Fletcher takes damage, big chunks of human flesh rain down — the PC that hit him must test Agility **dr8** or take **d4 damage**. |

  **If Fletcher dies:** the Gutworm (which does not defend or help him) **sinks, dead but dreaming**.

**Fletcher's story:** As a child Fletcher was led out into Sarkash and left to die. Desperate necromancers found the feral boy chewing rabbit carcasses in a gloomy glade. They took him in, but no force or threat could control him, and he slowly grew more powerful. Eventually they too abandoned him to die, hurling him into the _Accursed Den_. This became his domain, ruled purely by his will. He wants to use the heir **Aldon** to pressure the Shadow King into getting rid of **Lesdy** for good.
