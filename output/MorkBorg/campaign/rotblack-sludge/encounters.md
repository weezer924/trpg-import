## Random Encounter Tables

**When to roll:** Areas **7 (Pump room)**, **8 (Chain room)**, and **14 (Debris room)** explicitly call for a random encounter on entry. The Referee may also roll Table a when the party lingers or makes persistent noise.

### Table a — Repeatable

Roll **d4** each time a random encounter is triggered. This table may repeat throughout the scenario.

| d4 | Encounter |
|---:|-----------|
| 1 | **d4 Dusk gnoums** |
| 2 | **d2 Mongrels** |
| 3 | **d4 Guards with sharpened teeth** |
| 4 | **Nesting Death** |

### Table b — One-Shot (only once during the scenario)

If a random encounter would otherwise repeat and the Referee wants novelty, they may substitute one roll on Table b. Each entry fires **at most once** in the whole adventure. Roll **d4**.

| d4 | Encounter |
|---:|-----------|
| 1 | **Distraught spirit** |
| 2 | **Bazaar from a distorted dimension** — Sagsobuth manifests |
| 3 | **A terrible silence** — all sound in this room is inexplicably muted |
| 4 | **A sarcophagus** in the middle of the room — opening the lid sucks all PCs in and teleports them to the camp in Area 11 (Greenhouse) |

## Monster Stat Blocks

All stat blocks follow Mörk Borg bare-bones format:
`hP X  Morale X  Armor −dY (or No armor)  Attack d?`

### Random-table creatures

**d4 Dusk gnoums** — crawl from holes and climb the walls. Small, quick, but frail. **dr14 to hit them.**
`hP 4  Morale 7  No armor  Knife d4`

**d2 Mongrels** — massive black dogs with shaggy, oily fur.
`hP 8  Morale 9  No armor  Bite d4 +1`
**Infection:** on a hit, test **Toughness dr12** or become infected.

**d4 Guards with sharpened teeth** — weak and gaunt; lick their lips.
`hP 6  Morale 7  Leather −d2  Shortsword d4  Bite d4`

**Nesting Death** — hound-sized spider that built a nest of bones in the ceiling. **Wins initiative on 1–4.**
`hP 12  Morale —  Thick carapace −d2  Bite d4`
**Freezing bite:** on a hit, test **Toughness dr12** or freeze up (tests are **dr+2** for one hour).

**Distraught spirit** (Table b, #1) — agitated by the depravities and cannibalism plaguing the Den; pleads with the PCs to end the horror. **Can tell the PCs about the Gutworm.** No stat block: cannot be attacked or compelled; treat as a narrative encounter.

**Sagsobuth** (Table b, #2) — shapeless and ethereal; her face a vortex of light. Interdimensional trader.
`hP —  Morale —  Cannot be harmed`
**Will not attack unless provoked.**
**Explosion (if provoked):** **2d10 damage distributed among the group.**

Inventory (all prices in **silver**):
- **Violet poison** — 30s. **dr14** to resist; **2d10 damage**; **d4+1 doses**.
- **Beechwood Tube** containing a **foul protean scroll** — 30s. Each dawn the scroll has a new unclean Power. The Power can be used once. **Misinterpreted on 1–3 on a d20** (instead of the usual 1).

### Fixed dungeon inhabitants

*(Full narrative profiles in `npcs.md`; stat-block summary here for quick reference.)*

**Bearded man** (Area 2) — impossible to communicate with; unresponsive to being poked, hit, or attacked. No stat block; cannot be harmed in the usual sense. Set the table with everyone seated and he briefly laughs and tells old stories.

**3 slumbering skeletons** (Area 3) — thoughtless, without goal. Awaken if the vase or a book is carried out of the room (unless a book-study result 1 or 4 was obtained).
`hP 5  Morale 7  No armor  Bony fists d4`
One skeleton wields a **Jagged scimitar d4** instead.

**Tired crystal demon** (Area 3, book-study result 4) — bound to this room, longing to escape.
`hP —  Morale —  Cannot be harmed`
Drains the text from one scroll presented to it, then releases a **Mental shockwave** before vanishing.

**d4 Crooked guards** (Area 4) — follow Fletcher's every word; he cursed their memories, so they do not recall why they serve him. One carries the **prison-cell keys**. They don't care about Lesdy. If pressed they reveal the lost son but do **not** know how to enter his room.
`hP 8  Morale 7  Leather −d2  Sword d6  Femur d4`

**10 Mad prisoners** (Area 5) — emaciated, insane. Beg for help but strangle anyone who gets too close.
`hP 2  Morale 4  No armor  Strangling hands d4 / round`
- **To avoid being grappled:** test **Agility dr12**.
- **To break free once grappled:** test **Strength dr12**.

**Lesdy** (Area 11) — manipulative cult leader in the Greenhouse. Trying to turn the Gutworm against Fletcher.
`hP 5  Morale 4  No armor  Unarmed attack d4`

**3 Hosts** (Area 11) — young, dressed in rags; zealous followers of Lesdy.
`hP 7  Morale —  No armor  Long knives d6`

**Aldon, the lost heir** (Area 13) — pudgy, arrogant; gnaws human bones. No combat stat block given in the module — treat as a non-combatant NPC (see `npcs.md`).

### The Gutworm

*(Area 9; can reach Areas 9, 12, and 15 via the Rotblack Sludge.)*

Unfathomably long, thick as an oak. Hides in the Rotblack Sludge.

`hP 50  Morale —  Thick hide −d6  Razor-sharp teeth d10`

- **Instant-death bite:** when bitten, test **Agility dr6** or be **devoured, dying instantly**.
- **Swim encounter:** every swim between sludge-adjacent rooms (4 rounds to cross), **1–2 on d6** the Gutworm bites.
- **Submersion heat:** test **Toughness dr8** or take **d4 damage every round** submerged (sludge general rule — see `dungeon.md`).
- **Immediate attack:** if anyone enters the Gem room (Area 9) via the Tunnel (Area 10, from the Corridor pit trap), the Gutworm attacks at once.
- **Fletcher's death:** the Gutworm will **not** defend or help Fletcher. If Fletcher dies, the Gutworm **sinks, dead but dreaming**.

### Fletcher, the Cannibal Warlock

*(Area 15; carries the **oddly-shaped key** to the Debris–Statue door.)*

7 feet tall, built like a grizzly. Sooty, bald, covered in tattoos. Rules the Den. Hates Lesdy but can't fit through the Tunnel to reach her.

`hP 20  Morale —  Hardened skin −d4  Red-hot flail d8 + severe burn`

- **Severe burn:** victim's **Agility tests are dr+2 for one day**.
- **Powers:** every **third round** Fletcher uses a Power (automatically succeeds). Roll **d4**:

| d4 | Power |
|---:|-------|
| 1–2 | **Nine Violet Signs Unknot the Storm** — d2 bolts of lightning, **d6 damage** each. |
| 3 | **Daemon of Capillaries** — one creature chokes for **d6 rounds**, **d4 hP damage per round**. |
| 4 | **Ich-bin-luft** (unique Power) — Fletcher is **invisible for the next two rounds**. He can still attack. When Fletcher takes damage, big chunks of human flesh rain down: the PC that hit him must test **Agility dr8** or take **d4 damage**. |

**If Fletcher dies:** the Gutworm sinks, dead but dreaming.
