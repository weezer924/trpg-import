## Factions at a Glance

Three powers share the Accursed Den. None trust each other.

```
          Fletcher (Area 15)  ◄───── hates ─────►  Lesdy (Area 11)
           │  rules the Den                         │  schemes in the Greenhouse
           │  keeps Aldon as leverage               │  seeks to turn the Gutworm
           │  cursed the guards' memories           │    against Fletcher
           │                                        │  cannot be reached by
           │                                        │    Fletcher — Tunnel too
           │                                        │    tight for his bulk
           ▼                                        ▼
                 ┌──────────────────────────┐
                 │  The Gutworm (Sludge)    │
                 │  linked to Fletcher:     │
                 │  attacks Lesdy on sight, │
                 │  but won't defend        │
                 │  Fletcher — dies with    │
                 │  him, "dead but          │
                 │  dreaming."              │
                 └──────────────────────────┘
```

- **Fletcher ↔ Lesdy:** Open hatred. Fletcher wants the Shadow King to dispose of Lesdy; Lesdy wants to kill Fletcher via the Gutworm. Neither can reach the other directly — the Tunnel between them is too tight for Fletcher.
- **Fletcher ↔ Gutworm:** Bound in some occult way. The Gutworm **will not defend or help** Fletcher, yet if Fletcher dies the Gutworm sinks, **dead but dreaming**.
- **Lesdy ↔ Gutworm:** Lesdy is trying to bend the Gutworm to her will by consuming the Rotblack Sludge — every attempt so far provokes the Gutworm to attack her.
- **Aldon ↔ Fletcher:** Fletcher keeps Aldon alive as leverage over the Shadow King.
- **Crooked Guards ↔ everyone:** Memory-cursed by Fletcher; indifferent to Lesdy, uncertain why they serve.

## Fletcher, the Cannibal Warlock

*(PDF p.15)* — location: dungeon.md Area 15 (Forge / Slaughterhouse)

**Appearance / personality:** 7 feet tall, built like a grizzly. Sooty, bald, and covered in tattoos. Rules the Den by sheer will. Cruel, implacable, accustomed to unquestioned authority.

**Goal / attitude toward PCs:** Rules the Accursed Den as his personal domain. Wants to use the heir **Aldon** as leverage to pressure the Shadow King into getting rid of **Lesdy** for good. Treats intruders as meat — hostile on sight.

**Key info they know:**
- Carries the **oddly-shaped key** that unlocks the door between the Debris room (14) and the Statue room (12).
- Knows Aldon's location (Area 13) and the secret of the Statue room's eye socket (placing an eye opens the Son's room).
- Knows Lesdy is in the Greenhouse but cannot fit through the Tunnel to reach her.
- Can use unclean Powers at will.

**Background:** As a child Fletcher was led out into Sarkash and left to die. Desperate necromancers found the feral boy chewing rabbit carcasses in a gloomy glade. They took him in, but no force or threat could control him, and he slowly grew more powerful. Eventually they too abandoned him to die, hurling him into the _Accursed Den_. The Den became his domain, ruled purely by his will.

**Stat block:** `hP 20  Morale —  Hardened skin −d4  Red-hot flail d8 + severe burn`. Powers (d4, every third round) and "Ich-bin-luft" reflexive damage — **see `encounters.md`**. If Fletcher dies, the Gutworm sinks, dead but dreaming.

## Lesdy

*(PDF p.12 sidebar; p.13 Area 11)* — location: dungeon.md Area 11 (Greenhouse)

**Appearance / personality:** Long dark hair, dressed in a gunny sack with armholes. **Nice but manipulative.** Initially warm and hospitable — offers food, rest, and introduction to her "hosts." Zealous beneath the charm.

**Goal / attitude toward PCs:** Seeks the tunnels and caves **deep beneath the Rotblack Sludge**. Believes that consuming the sludge grants power to control creatures — but every attempt provokes the Gutworm. Currently trying to **turn the Gutworm against Fletcher** so she can finally pass through the sludge. Sees the PCs as potential tools, converts, or, if things sour, sacrifices. Will recruit or betray as circumstances demand.

**Key info they know:**
- The Gutworm's existence and its link to Fletcher.
- Fletcher is the true master of the Den and her enemy.
- The Tunnel (Area 10) is the only connection to her Greenhouse — and Fletcher can't fit through it.
- Nothing specific about Aldon or the Son's room mechanism.

**Stat block:** `hP 5  Morale 4  No armor  Unarmed attack d4`. See `encounters.md` for reference.

## The Three Hosts

*(PDF p.12 sidebar)* — location: dungeon.md Area 11 (Greenhouse), at Lesdy's camp

**Appearance / personality:** Young, dressed in rags. Zealous. Initially outgoing and kind when the PCs accept Lesdy's hospitality; their veneer cracks if Lesdy is threatened or if she signals.

**Goal / attitude toward PCs:** Devoted to Lesdy. Will defend her or attack on her word. Potentially recruitable if separated from her, but no such hook is given in the module.

**Key info they know:** Whatever Lesdy has told them about the Den — treat as a subset of Lesdy's knowledge; GM's discretion.

**Stat block:** `hP 7  Morale —  No armor  Long knives d6`. See `encounters.md`.

## Aldon, the Lost Heir

*(PDF p.14)* — location: dungeon.md Area 13 (Son's Room)

**Appearance / personality:** Pudgy and **arrogant**. Sits on a bench gnawing human bones. The Shadow King's one true son — kept here by Fletcher as political leverage.

**Goal / attitude toward PCs:** As the Seer warns (Table of prophecies #7, _true_): **"Aldon may be unwilling to leave this place."** He has adapted to, or embraced, his captivity. Extracting him is the mission; persuading or coercing him is a real obstacle. Hostile, dismissive, or imperious depending on how he is approached.

**Key info they know:**
- The Shadow King is his father.
- Fletcher's control over the Den; tolerates or enjoys it.
- Contents of his room: iron hook, mirror, crossbow with four bolts, and the rune-bullwhip **d4 + necroplasmic shock (d4 damage)** on the wall.

**Stat block:** None given in the module — treat as a **non-combatant NPC**. If reduced to violence, the Referee should improvise something frail.

## The Crooked Guards

*(PDF p.7)* — location: dungeon.md Area 4 (Guard Room); d4 of them

**Appearance / personality:** Crooked, dispirited men. One carries the **prison-cell keys** (for Area 5).

**Goal / attitude toward PCs:** Follow **Fletcher's every word**. Attack intruders, but are not fanatics — normal morale (7). Hear any noise in Areas 2, 3, 5, and 8.

**Key info they know:**
- They do **not** recall why they serve Fletcher — he **cursed their memories**.
- They **don't care** about Lesdy in the Greenhouse.
- **If pressed:** they will tell the PCs about the **lost son** (Aldon), but **they do not know how to enter his room** (i.e., they don't know the eye-socket trick).
- One of them has the cell keys.

**Stat block:** `hP 8  Morale 7  Leather −d2  Sword d6  Femur d4`. See `encounters.md`.

## The Bearded Man

*(PDF p.6)* — location: dungeon.md Area 2 (Dining Hall)

**Appearance / personality:** Skin ashen grey, eyes dark, dressed in a dusty old cloak. Sits at the far end of a 20-chair banquet table. **Unnamed.** It is impossible to communicate or contact him. PCs may poke, hit, or attack him **without response**.

**Goal / attitude toward PCs:** None — catatonic. Harmless unless the room's trigger is met.

**Key info they know:**
- **Trigger:** if **every chair at the table is filled**, he comes to his senses and erupts with rumbling laughter, telling **stories of old** for a few minutes before slowly and irrevocably regressing.
- The module leaves the content of those stories to the Referee — treat as a hook opportunity: rumors of the Shadow King's court, old Sarkash, or the Den's origin.

**Stat block:** None. Apparently cannot be meaningfully harmed.

## Tired Crystal Demon

*(PDF p.6)* — location: dungeon.md Area 3 (Library / Bedroom), conditional

**Appearance / personality:** A bound crystalline thing, weary. **Bound to this room, longing for a way to escape.**

**Goal / attitude toward PCs:** Only appears if a PC studies the books and rolls **4 on the d4 book-study table**, yelling _"You dead, arise!"_ (The three slumbering skeletons do **not** awaken on this result.) The demon is not aggressive — it wants a scroll.

**Key info they know:**
- Nothing it will share; acts, not speaks.
- **Behavior:** drains the text from **one scroll** presented to it, then releases a **Mental shockwave** and vanishes into thin air.

**Stat block:** `hP —  Morale —  Cannot be harmed`. See `encounters.md`.

## Mad Prisoners

*(PDF p.8)* — location: dungeon.md Area 5 (Cells); 10 of them

**Appearance / personality:** Emaciated, pleading, and insane. They plead for help but strangle anyone who comes close.

**Goal / attitude toward PCs:** They **only know the guards**. Every so often a prisoner is taken from the cells, never to return. Will beg — then attack if a PC closes into reach.

**Key info they know:** Nothing useful beyond rumor: prisoners disappear, the guards come and go, violin music drifts down. They cannot identify Fletcher, Aldon, or Lesdy.

**Stat block:** `hP 2  Morale 4  No armor  Strangling hands d4 / round` (Agility dr12 to avoid grapple; Strength dr12 to break free). See `encounters.md`.

## Sagsobuth

*(PDF p.4)* — location: manifests via **Random Encounter Table b, result 2** (one-shot, anywhere)

**Appearance / personality:** Shapeless and ethereal. Her face is a vortex of light. An **interdimensional trader** from a distorted dimension — a "bazaar" that manifests.

**Goal / attitude toward PCs:** Strictly transactional. **Will not attack unless provoked.** If provoked: **Explosion, 2d10 damage distributed among the group.**

**Key info they know:** Nothing given — she is a merchant, not a lore source. Her wares:
- **Violet poison** — 30s. **dr14** to resist, **2d10 damage**, **d4+1 doses**.
- **Beechwood Tube** — 30s. Contains a **foul protean scroll**: each dawn it has a new unclean Power; usable once; **misinterpreted on 1–3 on d20** (instead of the usual 1).

**Stat block:** `hP —  Morale —  Cannot be harmed`. See `encounters.md`.

## Distraught Spirit

*(PDF p.4)* — location: manifests via **Random Encounter Table b, result 1** (one-shot, anywhere)

**Appearance / personality:** A restless spirit, **agitated by the depravities and cannibalism** plaguing the Den.

**Goal / attitude toward PCs:** Pleads with the PCs to **end the horror**. Non-hostile; a pure information / moral-push encounter.

**Key info they know:**
- **The Gutworm exists** — can tell the PCs about it.
- Implied awareness of Fletcher's cannibal rites (reason for its agitation).

**Stat block:** None — narrative encounter; cannot be harmed or compelled in normal terms.

## The Gutworm

*(PDF p.12)* — location: dungeon.md Area 9 (Gem Room); reaches Areas 9, 12, and 15 through the Rotblack Sludge

**Role:** The dungeon's apex threat. Unfathomably long, thick as an oak. Hides in the sludge. Bound to Fletcher in some occult way — **will not defend** him, but **dies with him** ("sinks, dead but dreaming"). Attacks Lesdy whenever she tries to consume the sludge.

**Stat block and swim/bite rules:** see `encounters.md` → _The Gutworm_. Sludge submersion rules are in `dungeon.md` → _The Rotblack Sludge (General Rules)_.
