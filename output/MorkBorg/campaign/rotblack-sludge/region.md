## Adventure Background

*(PDF p.1–2)*

The PCs face execution for heretical theft. A masked **Seer**, a Courtier of the **Shadow King**, offers them a chance at life. The King's one true heir, his son **Aldon**, is missing. Without an heir, the Shadow King will eventually be forced to hand his crown to his imbecile brother.

Get Aldon back **discreetly** and wealth, life and freedom will be theirs.

It is believed Aldon is imprisoned in an infamous underground locale — a place no free man would willingly go — **The Accursed Den（被诅咒的巢穴）**.

---

## The Hook

The masked Seer — somewhat odd and delirious — offers to scry the Accursed Den and tell the PCs **two truths** (roll d8 twice on the table below, or pick).

The scenario assumes the PCs take the deal. Refusal means execution.

## The Seer's Visions (d8)

*(PDF p.2)*

| d8 | Vision | Truth |
|---:|--------|:-----:|
| 1 | A mysterious **house of glass**, within it lairs the leader of a cult. | true |
| 2 | **Gasses leak from holes and cracks,** driving anyone who breathes them mad. | false |
| 3 | **Cannibals!** They feast upon their own kind! | true |
| 4 | The lamps burn red, the lamp-oil **touched with human blood!** | uncertain, largely untrue |
| 5 | An ancient man lives within a ruined room. **Fear and avoid him.** | false |
| 6 | Something hungers in the depths, **an enormous creature** like a vast intestine! | true |
| 7 | I fear Aldon may be **unwilling to leave this place.** | true |
| 8 | **I fear none have ever willingly escaped the Accursed Den!** | uncertain |

**Notes for the Referee:**
- Entry 1 points to the **Greenhouse** (glass-walled room, Area 11) and **Lesdy**.
- Entry 3 refers to **Fletcher's** forge/slaughterhouse (Area 15) and the **mad prisoners** in the Cells (Area 5).
- Entry 6 is the **Gutworm**, lurking in the Rotblack Sludge.
- Entry 7 is literal: Aldon has gone feral and does not wish to return.

## Factions in the Den

*(PDF p.4, 11–15)*

| Faction | Area | Description |
|---------|------|-------------|
| **Fletcher, the cannibal warlock** | 15 | Seven-foot bald tattooed giant, feral since childhood. Rules the Den by raw will. Hates Lesdy but cannot fit through the Tunnel to reach her. |
| **Lesdy & her 3 hosts** | 11 | Manipulative cult leader in the glass greenhouse. Wants to turn the Gutworm against Fletcher; seeks tunnels beneath the sludge. Zealous young hosts serve her. |
| **The Gutworm** | 9, 12, 15 (sludge) | Unfathomably long creature hiding in the Rotblack Sludge. Neutral to all factions; devours anything that swims. |
| **Fletcher's crooked guards** | 4 | d4 guards, memories cursed by Fletcher — do not know why they serve. Have prison-cell keys. |
| **Mad prisoners** | 5 | 10 emaciated wretches. Not a true faction — too broken to organise. |
| **Aldon, the lost heir** | 13 | The scenario objective. Gnaws human bones, arrogant and unwilling to leave. |

---

## Faction Relationships

- **Fletcher → Lesdy:** Hates her. Wants Aldon used as leverage on the Shadow King to rid the Den of her for good. Cannot physically reach her (Tunnel is too tight).
- **Lesdy → Fletcher:** Actively plotting. Tries to turn the Gutworm against him by feeding it sludge — the Gutworm attacks her instead.
- **Guards → Fletcher:** Unquestioning obedience (memory-cursed). Indifferent to Lesdy. Will tell the PCs about the lost son if pressed but do not know how to enter his room.
- **Gutworm → All:** Devours anything swimming in the sludge. Does **not** defend Fletcher.
- **Aldon → World:** Wants nothing to do with rescue. Has embraced the Den.
- **The bearded man (Area 2):** Unresponsive ghost-like presence; not a faction, but party dynamics shift briefly if **everyone sits at the table** (he comes to his senses and tells old stories).

## The Rotblack Sludge

*(PDF p.9–15)*

An oily, scorching black pool of sulfurous gunk filling a 60-foot-deep cavern beneath the Den. It can be seen and entered from **Areas 9, 12, and 15**; the **Gutworm** lairs within and can reach any of these rooms.

General rules for the sludge (full details in `dungeon.md`):

- **Heat:** test Toughness **dr8** or take d4 damage every round submerged.
- **Swimming:** four rounds to cross from one room to the next.
- **Gutworm attack:** each swim has a **1–2 on d6** chance the Gutworm bites (d10 damage). When bitten, test Agility **dr6** or be devoured and die instantly.

## Adventure Flow

The Accursed Den has no fixed main-line; it is a small sandbox with two hostile factions converging on the central Rotblack Sludge cavern.

Typical paths to Aldon:

1. **Front approach (north half):** Entrance (1) → Dining hall (2) → Guard room (4) → Cells (5) or Library (3) → Corridor (6). From the Corridor, either the pit trap drops the party into the Tunnel/Gem-room loop, or they bypass the trap and swim.
2. **South approach:** After the pit trap or via the sarcophagus teleport, the party emerges near the Greenhouse (11), where **Lesdy** may offer hospitality (and recruit or betray).
3. **Reaching Aldon:** Statue room (12) → place an eye (Aldon's, Fletcher's, or a sacrificed companion's) in King Lenard's socket → secret door to Son's room (13).
4. **Fletcher's domain:** Reached via Pump room (7) hatch → ladder down to Forge (15), **or** via Debris room (14) (locked — Fletcher's oddly-shaped key needed).

**Complicating factors:**
- The **guards in Area 4 hear any noise** made in Areas 2, 3, 5, and 8.
- Aldon is **unwilling to leave**. Getting him out discreetly — as the Seer demands — may require subduing, deceiving, or killing him.
- If **Fletcher dies**, the **Gutworm** sinks, "dead but dreaming" — the Sludge becomes passable without bite-checks.
- **Lesdy** is plainly a second boss-in-waiting; the scenario leaves her fate to the Referee.
