## Named Squad Leaders & Attachments (d8)

Roll **d8** when a generic grvnt detachment needs a named "main character". These can also serve as Squad Attachments acting separately from their parent squad.

### 1. "Runner" — Sadistic Soldier

**HP** 8 · **Morale** 7 · **Ammo** 2
**Malevolent Rifle** d10 · **Punch Dagger** d4 · **SLAGG-quenched Iron** −d6
**SHOCK 1 — Terminal cough**

- **+2 damage in melee** (Sadistic Soldier trait).
- **Collects Teeth** — for every tooth collected, the **squad** gains −d2 Armor.

### 2. "Lazy" — Lovesick Rifleman

**HP** 6 · **Morale** 8 · **Ammo** 3
**Beheader Rifle** d8 / bayonet d8 · **Scream Grenades** (3) · **Battered Gambison** −d2
**SHOCK 1 — Permanent 1,000 yd stare**

- **Lovesick** — casts a random War Scroll once per combat.

### 3. "Che" — Troll-Blooded Veteran

**HP** 10 · **Morale** 9 · **Ammo** 2
**Cvlt .45** d4 · **Zweihander** d10 · **Chewed Mail** −d4
**SHOCK 2 — Battle tinnitus**

- **Troll-Blooded** — heals d4 HP per turn.

### 4. "Lucky" — Falchon-Winged Mercenary

**HP** 7 · **Morale** 9
**Razor Talons** d10 · **Short Bow** d6 · **Dark Plume** −d2
**SHOCK 1 — Bite mark from… something**

- **DR14 to hit** while flying.

### 5. "Roach" — Foresight Sapper

**HP** 5 · **Morale** 7 · **Ammo** 3
**Schleswig Sprayer** d6 · **SLAGG Bangs** (3) · **Tattered Clothes** —
**SHOCK 2 — Weak knees and hunched posture**

- **Shinrippers (2)** — may be detonated anywhere on the battlefield.

### 6. "Petulant Dehorns the Beginning" — GROWTH-Embedded One

**HP** 10 · **Morale** — · **Blacktrunk Club** d8
**Creeping Vines** −d2, Partial cover

- **GROW or SHRINK** d3 grvnts once daily (Embedded One ETHER ability).

### 7. "Tergol's Cousin"

**HP** 6 · **Morale** 5
**666 Shooter** d4 · **Punch Dagger** d4 · **Clean Robes** —
**SHOCK 0 — Greasy mustache**

- **If taken alive**, can be ransomed for **d100 silver**.

### 8. "Lobster Man"

**HP** 12 · **Morale** —

## Enemy Squads (d8)

Roll **d8** for a generic faction-agnostic squad. Stats are for the squad as a whole (see `farewell-rules.md` § Squad Combat Rules for Squad Deaths / Squad Morale).

### 1. "Stuntmen"

**Grvnts** 3 · **Morale** 7 · **Armor** —
**Dual Cvlt .45s · Barbed Wire Club · Detonation Pikes**

Each Detonation Pike lets the squad lose one member to make a single attack with a **Boom Lance**.

### 2. "Untouchables"

**Grvnts** 4 · **Morale** 10 · **Armor** −d4
**Malevolent Rifle · Punch Dagger · Shielded**

**Shielded** — −1 damage received from all sources.

### 3. "Long Shots"

**Grvnts** 4 · **Morale** 8 · **Armor** —
**666 Shooter · Shorter Swords · Life Eternal**

**Life Eternal** — each felled squad member gets one free attack with a **Scream Grenade** as they die.

### 4. "Silent Flames"

**Grvnts** 5 · **Morale** 6 · **Armor** —
**Casts Nay-Palm · Short Bow · Sharpshooter**

**Sharpshooter** — expend a squad member to make one **d10 attack, IMPACT 2**.

### 5. "Blunderbusses"

**Grvnts** 5 · **Morale** 10 · **Armor** −d2
**Schleswig Sprayers · SLAGG Bangs · Cover Huggers**

**Cover Huggers** — base **DR13** to hit them.

### 6. "Fire and Forget"

**Grvnts** 5 · **Morale** 6 · **Armor** −d6 + helmets
**Boom Lance · Tergol's Secret Cocktail · SLAGG Transport**

**SLAGG Transport** — squad carries **2 SLAGG** when downed/captured.

### 7. "Rangers"

**Grvnts** 4 · **Morale** 7 · **Armor** −d4 + helmets
**Vermin Maimer · Fire Spear · Well Equipped**

**Well Equipped** — attacks with a **Richters MG** instead of Firearm (2d6 to d3 targets, IMPACT 1; d2 turns to set up).

### 8. "Waymakers"

**Grvnts** 3 · **Morale** 9 · **Armor** −d4 + helmets
**Slug Launcher · Zweihander · Temporally Displaced**

**Temporally Displaced** — the remaining squad size **doubles after 2d2 turns**.

## Heavy Machinery

War machines. Usually accompany Companies and Regiments; rarely joins a Squad.

### Gaslobber

**HP** 12 · **Morale** —
**Gas Canister shell** — releases GAS within 20 ft of target
**Blast Screen** −d4

- Usually operates **far from the front**.

### Landcrusher

**HP** 20 · **Morale** —
**Mounted Cannon** d12 once per d4 rounds, **IMPACT 4**, **SHOCK**
**Forged SLAGG Plating** −d10

- Tries to **run over grvnts** between Cannon shots. **Destroys all cover** it drives through.

### Skycrawler

**HP** * · **Morale** 9
**Strafe** 3d6 spread, **IMPACT 1**, **DR14** to avoid
**Sheet Metal** −d4

- When Skycrawler **would take damage**, test **Morale** (it flees rather than dies).

### Seasplitter

**HP** 30 · **Morale** 10
**Heavy Guns** d12 to all within 30 ft of target
**Iron Lung** −d12

- **Seacrawler cannot dodge**. All attacks hit automatically.

## Faction Specialist Units

Elite units signature to each faction. Roll or choose a sub-variant per encounter.

### The Singed — Beggars of Death

> *Baptized in the river of fire, they burn forever in service of their King.*

**HP** d10 + 4 · **Morale** 10
Roll **d10** for Firearms and **d6** for Armor.

- **Attacks cause BURN 3**.
- **Immune to BURN**.

| d3 | Variant |
|---:|---------|
| 1 | **Smelting Skin** — 1-in-2 chance to melt projectile attacks and melee weapons. |
| 2 | **Magma Heart** — taking damage increases BURN by 1. |
| 3 | **Reality Refraction** — +2 DR to hit. |

### Endajagare — Faithspire Horde

> *A single-manned, mobile weapons platform.*

**HP** d8 + 4 · **Morale** 9
Roll **d6** for Firearms, start with **−d6 Armor**.

- **Immune to HEAVY**.

| d3 | Variant |
|---:|---------|
| 1 | **Firespitter Six-Wheeler** — d8, **BURN 2**, extremely mobile. |
| 2 | **Richters MG Biped** — 2d6 to d2 targets, **IMPACT 1**, boost jump. |
| 3 | **Relv Mortar Full Track** — 2d6, **BLAST 2**, destroys cover on contact. |

### Low Roar — White Crown Special Forces

> *Equipped with SLAGG-powered rocket packs.*

**HP** d6 + 4 · **Morale** 10
Roll **d10** for Firearms and **d4+1** for Armor.

- **+2 DR to hit** (while airborne).
- **Cannot fly at Tier 0 Armor**.

| d3 | Variant |
|---:|---------|
| 1 | **Raider** — immune to Specialities and War Scrolls. |
| 2 | **White Baron** — 1-in-6 chance to deal max damage, **IMPACT 3**. |
| 3 | **Rain of Death** — drops small mortars (2d4, **BLAST 2**, **SHOCK**). |

### Coffin Corps — Mad Mists

> *Mad Mists who have turned to GAS and now exist in pressurized Lobster Armor.*

**HP** — · **Morale** 8
Roll **d6** for Firearms, start with **−d8 Lobster Armor**.

- When suffering damage, **decrease Armor by one Tier**. At Tier 0, **GAS escapes** in a large area and you are free.

| d3 | Variant |
|---:|---------|
| 1 | **Vaporial Armor** — lose one Tier of Armor to reduce all nearby enemy Armor by 1 Tier. |
| 2 | **Miasma** — taking melee damage releases GAS at your attacker. |
| 3 | **Fume Walker** — start with one additional Tier of Armor. |
