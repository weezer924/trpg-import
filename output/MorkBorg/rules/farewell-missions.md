## Mission Anatomy

Every Farewell mission has the same skeleton:

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Mission Briefing** | One long paragraph framing the battle, key locations, and landmarks. |
| **Objectives** | 3 possible wins. Players may accomplish some or all. |
| **Twists** | 6 optional surprises. Twist **#5** triggers automatically on **failed Diplomacy**. Twist **#6** triggers automatically on **successful Diplomacy**. Use none, one, or a few. |
| **Advance** (Act 1) | First-act encounter. Three options; one usually suffices. Builds tension. |
| **Action** (Act 2) | Second-act encounter. Three options. Attempts the objective(s). |
| **Aftermath** (Act 3) | Third-act encounter. Three options. Enemy's last gasp or the squad's escape. |
| **Rewards** | SLAGG gained, Requisitions unlocked, Promotions, etc. |

## They Shall Not Pass

> *Exhausted grvnts are force-marched to the crossing of Rivercrest to disrupt Galgenbeck's supply of SLAGG.*

### Mission Briefing

A well-equipped Horde infantry detachment is guarding a highly valuable SLAGGVARA deposit among some ruins along Rivercrest. Extraction equipment rises from the fields beyond, guarded by Galgenbeck forces. SLAGG is being transported north by a heavily-guarded wagon convoy. Reconnaissance has not revealed just how heavy it might be. A faint blue light seeps from the mine, casting odd shadows on the ancient walls of the ruins. Thin, dead trees poke up like fingers from the fields, offering little cover for the operation.

### Objectives

1. **Destroy** the armored detachment transporting SLAGG.
2. **Paint designated occult symbols** on extraction equipment for artillery strikes.
3. **Blow the bridge** to Galgenbeck and fight your way back to friendly territory.

### Twists

1. The enemy is **distracted by brass** visiting them for inspection.
2. A **one-armed Lich** is chained in one of the ruins. They want revenge.
3. A band of **Pale Ones** are peacefully blocking the bridge.
4. The **mine is gushing SLAGG**, covering the area.
5. **(Diplomacy Fail)** A Beggars of Death **Seasplitter** is bombarding the riverfront indiscriminately.
6. **(Diplomacy Win)** A Beggars of Death Seasplitter is bombarding the **Galgenbeck forces** defending the bridge.

### Advance — First light, empty town, surrounded by the sound of gunfire

1. **Early Reinforcement** — through the grey morning mist, a Horde Landcrusher crosses the bridge. Opens fire on the target with the greatest firepower, charges everyone else.
2. **Dogs of War** — d3 Hyena-Throated Faithspire Altered Mercenaries are loosed, laughing at their ensuing carnage.
3. **All Fun and Games** — the Galgenbeck troops stayed up all night playing *Too Many Fingers*. Half of them can't shoot anymore and are armed with melee weapons.

### Action — Misty, gray dawn, not all of the enemy has awakened

1. **Death From Above** — a group of Falchon-Winged Altered Mercenaries spot you from above. One flies off to alert their fellow grvnts.
2. **Glint of a Scope** — a Recluse Marksman sits in a tall ruin overseeing the entirety of the operation. Blow your cover and he'll blow your brains out.
3. **Watching their Flank** — a small patrol has broken off from the convoy and is headed in your direction. One wears vestments of the Church.

### Aftermath — A lull in the fighting. A deep rumble of impending doom

1. **Gruff** — your explosive escapades awaken a troll from a long nap. His bridge is occupied/destroyed. He's not happy… and you look like a billy-goat.
2. **Tides Have Turned** — the natural world heals its own, as water claws its way to the mines. DR14 Strength test or be drowned in the rivers of rage. If wearing Tier 4 armor, you automatically fail.
3. **Munition Megiddo** — seeing that all hope is lost, the Horde Warlord unleashes an artillery barrage in an attempt to level the ruins. 1-in-6 chance grvnts are struck. 1-in-4 if next to an enemy with a flare. Test **SHOCK** every turn it misses.

### Rewards

- **d3 SLAGG** (additional d4 if the mine is kept intact).

## Battle at the Gates

> *With the Beggars' capital Seasplitter, the "Prometheus", away at the battle of Gleymtford, Icefell and the White Crowns of Anthelia have seen it as a key moment to strike the heart of Grift.*

### Mission Briefing

It has been a lifetime since the grvnts first set off on their journey. At the start of the war, the Beggars were the scourge of the Endless Sea, pillaging the weary, firing their guns on the unsuspecting. But now they've come home to see that they too have fallen victim to the cruelty of organized murder.

The sodden heart of Grift, the capital of Múrland, is under siege. Skycrawlers bombard constantly, as White Crown infantry proceed to secure the city block by block. Boot heels echo among the burning stone edifices. Faithspire Landcrushers storm the beachhead, as Josilfa's holy horde begins to reinforce the "children" of Anthelia. **This is the final hour.**

With the "Prometheus" away assisting the Mad Mists along the Rivercrest, the Beggars find themselves vulnerable... but they must hold out. *"By my own hand!"* is the oath every Beggar takes. Death by any other source is deemed impermanent.

### Objectives

1. **Secure the Bell Tower.**
2. **Hold off the White Crown Squadron.**
3. **Defend the Beachhead.**

### Twists

1. In the initial bombardment, the **aqueduct routing the flaming river** to the sea spews water-fire into the streets.
2. The **Bell Tower is rigged to blow**.
3. The enemy has **entrenched the beachhead**.
4. The "Prometheus" is **swarmed by Skycrawlers** and Faithspire frigates.
5. **(Diplomacy Fail)** The **Low Roar have stolen Firespitters**.
6. **(Diplomacy Win)** The **Singed are set loose** in the city, fires rage everywhere.

### Advance — A large bell tower oversees the entire city

1. **Death From Above** — a squad of Low Roar bombard the position without mercy.
2. **Ace in the Hole** — one of the Skycrawlers is an Ace, pilots "Downpour" (+2 attack, +2 Morale).
3. **Get it Working** — a non-functioning Richters MG at the top of the tower. Can you fix it in time?

### Action — Open courtyard, ranged death in the open, close quarters inside

1. **Scars of the Earth** — bomb craters litter the courtyard. Perhaps you can hide in one and avoid death? Provides Partial cover.
2. **Dog of War** — a squad of Carbine Riflemen led by a Hyena-Throated Faithspire Mercenary seeks revenge for their fallen comrades of Gleymtford.
3. **Major Arcana IV** — Sigfúm himself leads a parade of death-obsessed civilians through the streets, creating a distraction of carnage.

### Aftermath — The "Prometheus" is in sight. Hope, danger, a brave death

1. **Faithspire Armor** — Faithspire Reinforcements land in a last ditch effort to seize the beachhead. They brought the fvcking **"Third Head of HE" Landcrusher**.
2. **Cannon Fodder Rise Up!** — the people of Grift, unarmed, rush willingly into the front lines. Charge!
3. **Debunked** — White Crown New-World Engineers fire on the grvnts once per turn from hastily constructed bunkers.

### Rewards

- **Promote the entire squad**.
- **+1 SLAGG** per Low Roar downed.
- If the **"Third Head of HE"** is captured intact, gain access to it as a Requisition (**8 RP**).

## Do Not Go Gentle

> *A botched trench raid leads to the Faithspire grvnts being surrounded without aid. They must either fight, flee, or die with their bootstraps on.*

### Mission Briefing

Three trenches on the western front sit deadlocked across No Man's Land. Recent reports indicate that White Crown repositioning has weakened their lines of defense, presenting an opportunity. The Faithspire orders a suicide charge as soon as dawn breaks.

What the grvnts don't know is that this is all a ruse from Alliáns. As the enemy trench is taken, a White Crown counter offensive captures their trench behind them, leaving the Faithspire grvnts stranded as enemies assault every day. **It's a fight for survival.**

### Objectives

1. **Defend your positions!** Live long enough for reinforcements (end of Day 3).
2. **Fight your way back** to friendly territory (Trench Raid again).
3. **Take as many of them with you as you can** — d6 enemies per grvnt before dying.

### Twists

1. **Faithspire artillery** barrages the area constantly, making it impossible to sleep.
2. The trenches are **filled with rats**, devouring food rations while grvnts aren't looking.
3. **Captured comrades** from the Faithspire counteroffensive are forcibly used as cannon fodder.
4. The **anger of the earth manifests**, filling craters with stalagmite punji traps.
5. **(Diplomacy Fail)** Nearby denizens of the **Hunger Wound** stalk No Man's Land from dusk 'til dawn.
6. **(Diplomacy Win)** The walls of Galgenbeck are laden with **Requisitions firing down** upon the White Crowns position.

### Advance — Dark night gives way to a gray day, poor weather, another merciless attack

1. **Attacks from Both Sides** — both front and rear flanks assaulted at the same time, reducing trench cover from Full to Partial.
2. **Storm Rolls In** — violent storms from the south join the fray, as if the earth itself wants you dead.
3. **Intercepted Communications** — messenger birds fly over the trench, delivering attack information from main White Crown forces to the west and the trench trapping the grvnts to the east.

### Action — In the enemy trench, dealing with SHOCK, surrounded throughout the first day

1. **Eye for an Eye** — the ravens of White Crown Marksmen serve as spotters, allowing them a surprise round on the grvnts from the trench they just came from.
2. **As Above, So Below** — White Crown Skycrawlers bomb the trench. Test Agility DR14 or take d10 damage. Test **SHOCK**.
3. **In the Belly of the Beast** — an oversized Troll, captured in the depths of Treeswell and brought to the front lines, is loosed from its chains to wreak bloody bedlam.

### Aftermath — Day 3. On their last leg, yet another day in their hellhole

1. **Tired and Fatigued** — all grvnts with SHOCK add +1. Grvnts without SHOCK suffer d4 SHOCK.
2. **White Flag Against the Black Sky** — the White Crown army offers peace. A representative from each side must meet halfway in No Man's Land to negotiate. **But it's a trap.**
3. **No Man's Land** — angered by the constant fighting, trenches on both sides begin to swallow the militants. Test Strength DR12 or be swallowed beneath the earth.

### Rewards

- **+1 SLAGG** for each downed Skycrawler.
- **Promote one grvnt.**

## Fog of War

> *The stench of gas hovers over the mountain peaks. As you fall slowly towards your objective, the sounds of industry and death greet you.*

### Mission Briefing

A sore battle between the White Crowns and Mad Mists is being staged just outside the factory of **Kistfabrik**. Icefell has bled the lives of countless of their soldiers to get to this point. Your squad has paradropped behind the lines, tasked with quietly infiltrating the Kistfabrik factories, retrieving any information you can on the **Coffin Corps** project, and sabotaging the Mad Mists' ability to produce these monsters.

Drop in from the sky, stealth your way through the horrors, and collect intel on the project. Avoid detection and newly-awakened Coffin Corps at all costs. Then **burn the place** on your way out.

### Objectives

1. **Discover and retrieve** knowledge on how the Coffin Corps are created.
2. **Avoid detection.**
3. **Sabotage the factories at Kistfabrik**, preventing further fabrication.

### Twists

1. This mission is **on-site procurement only**. You may bring nothing but what is on your person.
2. Kistfabrik is **more heavily defended** than previously thought.
3. Your **landing was rough** — test **SHOCK** upon touching ground.
4. **Fathmu X is on site.** So is his Royal Company.
5. **(Diplomacy Fail)** One of you is a **double agent**. Only the GM knows who.
6. **(Diplomacy Win)** Your contact in Schleswig has procured **Gas Masks** for the entire squad.

### Advance — A constant hiss. Deep, mournful wailing echoes through metal halls

1. **Awakened Slumber** — passing a small hall, empty Coffin Corps armor lines the walls… until one awakens prematurely and attacks.
2. **Meet the Contact** — a mole undercover signals to meet you, but is locked in the brig. They have important information.
3. **Alert!** — sirens set guards on high alert. They know you are here.

### Action — Rent fissures in the ground feed glass tubes. Scarred workers answer to a boss barking impossible orders

1. **Daily Trudge** — poorly fed workers push enormous metal vats of green plasma-Gas. An accident waiting to happen.
2. **Mad Dog Barking** — a high-ranking Mad Mists official pushes a worker into a roiling vat of Gas. Chaos ensues.
3. **Stench of Death** — pulpy red paste overflows in open barrels, causing slick floors throughout the interior.

### Aftermath — Alarms blare. Pressures build. Get out or become Gas

1. **Blow The Load** — perfect target, explosives sit just right. The echoing boom awakens every Coffin Corps in manufacturing. Too many. **RUN.**
2. **Loud Pipes** — the master lever pumping ghost breath SLAGG into the factory. Spin all the wheels, build the pressure. You don't want to be around when this blows.
3. **SLAGGohodd** — you escape, but given chase by an experimental airtight Landcrusher firing Gas shells. Lose it, destroy it, just fvcking survive.

### Rewards

- **+3 SLAGG**.
- If you defeat a Coffin Corps, gain a **single Gas Mask that never breaks**.

### Enemy Faction & Twists (d4)

## GM Tips

- **Generation order:** Order×Objective → Area → Time Limit → Enemy Faction → Defense/Buildings/Time/Weather → Squad Leader & Size → Twists.
- **The Twist #5/#6 convention** (from the official missions) rewards Diplomacy. Keep slots 1–4 as flavor; reserve 5 as the fail-state complication and 6 as the win-state bonus.
- **3-act pacing:** every mission should have Advance / Action / Aftermath beats even when improvised. Pick one encounter option per act.
- **SLAGG as pacing:** after each mission, roll **d20 − total SLAGG expended**. If 1 or below, **VEX appears** (see `farewell-rules.md` § VEX). This nudges the campaign toward its apocalyptic endgame — big SLAGG expenditures risk the world itself.
