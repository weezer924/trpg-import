## Core Concepts

**SLAGGVARA (SLAGG)** — a newly-discovered natural resource that ignited the war. Exists as oily *earth blood*, vaporous *ghost breath*, and crystalline *stone heart*. Addictive, combustible, magical, and coveted by every power. Silver (s) is now secondary to SLAGG.

**Grvnts** — player characters. Short-lived conscripts, criminals, and zealots dragged into the war. The word replaces "Scvm" from Mörk Borg.

**The Dying Lands at War** — Mörk Borg's setting, now consumed by industrialized trench warfare. Landscape actively hostile: mountains crawl, rivers change course, GAS drowns soldiers on dry land.

**Four factions** control most of the fighting (see `farewell-setting.md` when it exists): Faithspire Horde (Josilfa), Beggars of Death (King Sigfúm), Mad Mists (Boy King Fathmu X), White Crowns (Countess Anthelia). Plus subfactions SHE, Shadow King, Hunger Wound, Tergol, and the omnipotent demon VEX.

## The Three Phases of Play

Farewell to Arms REDUX runs three distinct, fully roleplayed phases:

1. **Diplomacy Phase** — players act as Diplomats, swaying a Rival faction to aid the upcoming mission. Success/failure changes which battlefield Twist is applied.
2. **Warlord Phase** — players act as Warlords planning the next mission. Each has a once-per-mission Warlord Order. Squad requisitions Squad Equipment using SLAGG as Requisition Points (RP).
3. **Missions** — players act as grvnts in a squad, fighting tactical battles for SLAGG and territory.

A Scvm who survives two Promotions becomes a **Warlord** (playable in Warlord Phase). A Warlord Promoted again becomes a **Diplomat** (playable in Diplomacy Phase).

## Stats & Tests

Roll **3d6** for each of four stats. Cross-reference with the table below. The 3d6 result itself is not used later — only the resulting modifier.

| 3d6 | Stat Modifier |
|----:|--------------:|
| 1–4 | −3 |
| 5–6 | −2 |
| 7–8 | −1 |
| 9–12 | 0 |
| 13–14 | +1 |
| 15–16 | +2 |
| 17–20 | +3 |

Through Promotion or special effects, a stat can never exceed **+6** or drop below **−3**.

### The Four Stats

| Stat | Used For |
|------|----------|
| **Toughness** | Resist SHOCK / GAS / COLD / HEAT |
| **Agility** | Defend, sneak, avoid explosions |
| **Presence** | Shoot, perceive, use War Scrolls |
| **Strength** | Melee, throw, grapple, carry Squad Equipment |

### Tests & Difficulty Ratings (DR)

Roll **d20 ± relevant stat**. Meet or beat the DR to succeed. NPCs don't use stats; they roll d20 against a DR.

| DR | Description |
|---:|-------------|
| 6 | Simple grvnt work |
| 8 | Routine |
| 10 | Pretty basic |
| 12 | Challenging (default) |
| 14 | Difficult |
| 16 | Really tough |
| 18 | Should not be possible |
| 20 | Totally fvcked |

**Examples:** Fire your rifle (Presence DR12) · Avoid mortar shells (Agility DR12) · Resist SHOCK (Toughness DR14) · Bayonet an enemy behind Partial cover (Strength DR14).

## Omens

All grvnts gain Omens per Class. On a **gray, new dawn**, only after all Omens have been spent, regain all spent Omens.

Spend **1 Omen** to:

- Deal maximum damage with an attack.
- Reroll a dice roll (yours or someone else's).
- Lower damage dealt to you by d6.
- Neutralize a Crit or Fumble.
- Lower one test's DR by −4.

## Weapon Keywords & Rules

### Keywords (order of operation)

1. **SCOPED** — automatic Aim. Cannot be used in melee.
2. **BLAST (X)** — deal rolled damage to X targets within blast.
3. **IMPACT (X)** — ignore X Tiers of Armor.
4. **HEAVY** — degrade cover by one level (hit or miss). Destroy one Tier of Armor on hit.
5. **SHOCK** — force Toughness DR14 or roll d4 on SHOCK table.
6. **BURN (X)** — deal d4 damage each round for the next X rounds.

### Firearm Rules

- **In Melee:** Firearms cannot be reloaded. +2 DR to fire.
- **Two-Handed:** Spend an action to switch to Firearm / Side Arm / Shield.
- **Aiming:** Spend a round to Aim. Gain either −2 DR to hit OR +2 damage.
- **On Watch:** Players may go On Watch. Gain one free ranged attack before Initiative, but **cannot Rest**.

### Getting Ammo

Ammo is rolled at a new gray dawn, but **only if entirely spent**. A grvnt without Ammo is just a meatshield.

## Combat

> *Players make all rolls when attacking and defending. Enemies never roll.*

### Initiative

Roll **d6** at the start of each combat:

- **1–3:** Enemies act first.
- **4–6:** Players act first.

If the order of individual actions becomes important, test **Agility + d6**. Grvnts acting out of turn test **Agility + d6**.

### Engaging the Enemy

| Type | Test |
|------|------|
| **Melee** | Strength DR12 (± target Cover) |
| **Ranged** | Presence DR12 (± Aim and target Cover) |
| **Defense** | Agility DR12 (± your Armor and Cover) |

### Crits (natural 20)

- **Attacking:** Roll damage twice **AND** reduce enemy Armor or Cover by 1 Tier.
- **Defending:** Make an immediate attack.

### Fumbles (natural 1)

**Attacking (d4):**

| d4 | Result |
|---:|--------|
| 1 | Lose all Ammo |
| 2 | Weapon jams / unusable until an action is taken to fix it |
| 3 | Weapon misfires / breaks — only repaired after battle |
| 4 | Weapon explodes, dealing its damage to you |

**Defending:** Take double damage AND Armor reduced by 1 Tier.

## SHOCK

> *The sight of wartime horror snaps even the toughest grvnts. SHOCK is the measure of that breaking.*

**Start with d2 SHOCK.**

When **receiving** SHOCK, test **Toughness DR14**. On failure: add d4 to your current SHOCK, and immediately resolve the new effect.

When **losing** SHOCK, ignore the new effect.

Only remedied by days of rest and lots of booze — or some more expedient method.

## Promotions

The GM decides when to issue Promotions — typically for perilous deeds of great personal bravery or self-sacrifice. When earned:

### More HP

Roll **6d10**. If the result is ≥ the grvnt's current max HP, increase max HP by **d6**.

### Stat Changes

Roll **d6 against each Stat**:

- Result **≥ Stat** → Stat increases by 1 (max +6).
- Result **< Stat** → Stat decreases by 1 (min −3).
- Stats of **−3 to +1** are always increased by 1 **unless** the d6 is a 1, in which case it decreases.

On your **second Promotion**, you are unable to return to active duty. You become a **Warlord** — pick any Warlord Order. Your PC can now be chosen in the Warlord Phase. Roll a new grvnt.

## Diplomacy Phase

Players act as **Diplomats** enjoining a Rival faction to aid in the next mission. Players may create their own Diplomat with a **MOOD**, **INTEREST**, and **FEAR**. The Rival is generated by **3d6**; their traits are kept secret.

### Asking Questions

Each player asks questions of the Rival. The GM decides whether the question resonates; the Rival answers accordingly. Answers secretly build points:

| Answer | Points |
|--------|-------:|
| Truth | 3 |
| Half-Truth | 1 |
| Non-Answer | 0 |
| Lie | −2 |

### Outcome

Assign a **Diplomat Rating** appropriate to squad size. After all questions:

- **Swayed:** Next mission **always** includes Twist #6 (a bonus).
- **Foiled:** Next mission **always** includes Twist #5 (a curse).

Only enter Diplomacy Phase when something important is on the line. Warlords Promoted to Diplomat gain insight into Rivals — GM reveals one aspect per Promoted Diplomat. Special relationship: +3 starting points.

Players can never have more than one Diplomat.

## The Trenches & No Man's Land

### In the Trenches

Walls tight, fighting close.

- **Melee attacks** are DR10.
- **Agility tests** are **+2 DR**.

### Crossing No Man's Land — d20 Rule

At the start of each PC's turn, roll d20 and respond. At end of each turn, **1-in-4** chance the grvnt makes it to the enemy trench (**2-in-4** if horseback, vehicle, or flying). Players still take an action each turn as normal.

| d20 | Event |
|----:|-------|
| 1 | Muddy, clawed fingers — test Strength (fail automatically in Tier 4 Armor) or sink. |
| 2 | Razor wire snares your flesh. Trapped until you cut free. |
| 3 | Monstrous anger of the guns. Recruits are Downed. Test SHOCK. |
| 4 | Shrill choirs of shells. Test SHOCK. |
| 5 | Heeding Bannerman's cries, you reach the enemy trench and leap into bayonets (d6). |
| 6 | Faint click of a Shinripper. Your last step. |
| 7 | Hot lead from a Richters MG levels No Man's Land. |
| 8 | Ecstasy of fumbling — lose an item to the mud. |
| 9 | Scared scampering turns into stampede. Test SHOCK. |
| 10 | GAS. If braved, enough cover to reach the trench. |
| 11 | Deserters flee, turning bayonets on comrades. Test SHOCK. |
| 12 | Enemy priests rise and cast Nay-Palm. Dodge or become cinder. |
| 13 | Missed mortars opened a crater. Something lurks in the gangrenous water. |
| 14 | Enemy prisoners loosed — cannot pass until dealt with. |
| 15 | Gunned down, holding a dying comrade. Save them, or leave them to enter the trench. |
| 16 | GRENADE! Your pitiful life flashes before your eyes. |

| 18 | Distant enemy colors demoralize you. Test SHOCK. |
| 19 | A brave comrade sacrifices themselves to save you. Lose an Omen. |
| 20 | Otherworldly rage possesses you. You hurl yourself into the enemy trench. |

### Creating Enemy Squads

Where **(X)** is number of units in the squad:

- **Squad** — 3–5 enemy grvnts with the same weapons, acting as one. Accompanied by a separately-acting Squad Leader. Rarely joined by a Heavy Machinery unit. May have a Squad Attachment.
- **Company** — 10 grvnts (squad rules apply). Usually joined (6-in-10) by two Squad Attachments and a single Heavy Machinery unit, all acting separately.
- **Regiment** — 20 grvnts. Usually joined by three Squad Attachments and at least two Heavy Machinery units, all acting separately.

**Squad Leader** — a grvnt, rolled ignoring Omens, Powers, Reason for Enlistment, Vices, and SHIT.

**Squad Attachment** — a second rolled grvnt, attached to a squad but acting separately.

### Squad Combat Rules

- **Squad Attack:** weapon damage to (X) PCs.
- **Squad Deaths:** kill one enemy grvnt per 4 damage taken in a round (damage does not carry over). Adjust (X) accordingly round to round.
- **Squad Morale:** test Morale after killing more than one enemy grvnt in a round.

### Random Squad Generation

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| **Members (d3)** | 1: 3 · 2: 4 · 3: 5 |
| **Morale** | base 8, ±d2 |
| **Armor** | d6 roll |

Four archetype tables (**Squad A/B/C/D**) generate weapons and a special trait. See source PDF for full generator; highlights below.

- **Squad A** — riflemen flavor (Vermin Maimer / Schleswig Sprayer / Malevolent Rifle + SLAGG Bangs / Fire Spear / Barbed Wire Club). Specials: Cover Huggers (DR13 to hit) · Ferocious (no SHOCK) · Medic · Well Equipped (Richters MG).
- **Squad B** — brawler flavor. Specials: Life Eternal (each felled member lobs Scream Grenade) · Cold Iron (Zweihander bearer dies on any damage) · Munitions Expert · Detonation Pike (Boom Lance).
- **Squad C** — scroll flavor (Napalm / 666 Shooter / Malevolent Rifle). Specials: Shielded (−1 damage) · Sorrowful Sonnet (PCs test SHOCK each round) · Sharpshooter · Banners Unfurled (blocks Omens).
- **Squad D** — heavy weapons (Bell Tolls / Boom Lance / Slug Launcher). Specials: SLAGG Transport (2 SLAGG) · Temporally Displaced (squad doubles after 2d2 turns) · Destructive (removes cover instead of attacking) · Flanking.

## VEX — The Deathbringer

An omnipotent demon born of the trauma caused to the world by this war. Fueled by rage and revenge, VEX bides their time in the depths of the land. Drawn to places of destruction, chaos, and ecological disaster.

### Summoning VEX — End of Mission

At the end of a mission, roll **d20 minus the total SLAGG expended this mission**. On a result of **1 or below**, VEX appears.

**This is the last thing your squad will ever see (d12).** Twelve lavish final visions from the source — sky turning electric blue, missing grvnts falling from the sky retching GAS, rivers becoming VEX's maw, mountains exploding to reveal a horned colossus, etc.
