## Opening: The Shape of War

> *"Never think that war, no matter how necessary, nor how justified, is not a crime."* — Ernest Hemingway

The Dying Lands have never been peaceful. For as long as any can remember, the threat has always existed of being robbed on the road, gutted in the street over a petty squabble, or being mauled by black eyes in the dark forests only to be forgotten. As children, we were raised to believe that Tergol would snatch you from your bed while still in a dream, or that the Inquisition would discover your sins and lock you in abyssal, light-starved dungeons.

Now, few would call that old world "violent". **NOT LIKE THIS.**

### Hellfire Rains from the Skies

Decent folk turn wicked and malevolent, as if the part of their skull removed by the blast contained their final shred of decency. A sea of green floats over ravaged and desolate land, turning trenches into graves as comrades die drowning without water. Covered in mud, we wake every morning skewering whoever we fear — friend or foe — because it is better to be safe than sorry. And those who endure become hollow, as if they have lost the battle in their own minds.

New recruits reeking of piss and shit see more life than ever before — but down the length of a barrel, before extinguishing it. These mirages have taught us all the thousand-league stare. Ardent for a faraway place where we do not use Death's cloak as a pillow, another bastard stares into oblivion, seeing but not seeing, waiting for the end to stop these visions — these horrors. A morbid jealousy, and all for slagg.

## The War (I–IV)

**I.** In the beginning the land was as it had always been: bleak, desolate, and hopeless. That was before the **First Bloom**, before the natural resource called **SLAGGVARA** was discovered. In the barren wastes between Lake Onda and the river Múr, in the place that is now called **Newfield**, the flowers blossomed: innumerable poppies, as far as the eye could see. What was viewed as a miracle foreshadowed violence to come, and warned of the blood that would be spilled throughout the Dying Lands.

**II.** It started with Galgenbeck, the city of Josilfa's Faithspire. In their immediacy, they were able to quickly mobilize and gather SLAGG. Earth was tilled, scraped, picked at like an old, reinfected pustule. Others quickly came soon after: **Anthelia**, the Death Beggar **Sigfúm**, and even the late **King Fathmu IX**. All laid wicked claim to the resources in Newfield.

**III.** Then grew the onslaught of terrible machines, built by the downtrodden hands of the many to ravage the earth at the behest of faraway lieges. The discovery of different veins of SLAGGVARA in their respective domains mattered little. Those in power desired more than the others, no matter the cost — for their greed was endless; their avarice, insatiable; their thirst for power, unquenchable. Like starving hounds at the smell of mutton, they bore fangs and struck.

**IV.** No one living remembers who shot first. In a short time, avarice ravaged the Dying Lands. Never-ending trenches gouged the flesh of the earth like gashes, inhabited by conscripts, murderers, and meat-shields. Metallic crawlers, siege weapons, and SLAGG-powered weaponry replaced the beasts that lurked in the dark places, as the dark places themselves were consumed by an inhospitable **No Man's Land**.

SLAGG — the violent greed for which had started the war — now fuels the carnage. The dying world is changing. Death is not the final state. It does not excuse you from suffering; you've just changed sides. Death itself is just a battleground, a place where fear and weapons meet. Until that last day, when all shall fade to dust and the lands made whole. **Until the Farewell to Arms.**

## SLAGGVARA

Originally discovered amongst the poppies in **Newfield**. Now it bleeds in the lonely places of the world that suddenly see too much foul company. Merchants from **Grift** were the first to taste the Dying Lands' lifeblood; but like scavengers to the slaughter, any nobility with even a fraction of power vied immediately for dominance over this new resource.

There are many accounts of who shot first; none matter. All engage in mindless killing. Silver has become secondary to SLAGGVARA in all its forms:

- **Oily "earth blood"** — the liquid form, fuels engines and burns eternally.
- **Vaporous "ghost breath"** — the gaseous form; drowns soldiers on dry land.
- **Crystalline "stone heart"** — the solid form; cut, shipped, and worshipped.

What might have elevated culture and birthed enlightenment in the gloomy twilight of time instead fuels machinations of war and mass murder. All are drawn to it, both the dead and the living. Its pull draws both cold metal and forlorn spirits into conflict. The world heaves and convulses as its soul is plundered.

## The World As It Is Now

The land itself is reacting to this war of all wars: shifting and changing unlike ever before. The Dying Lands, writhing in pain, command their own weapons of war:

- **Mountains crawl**, the rivers tear new courses, and the very trees move and shift, traveling to a home not yet found.
- Impervious, angry **machines feed ravenously off the earth's blue blood**.
- **Crystallized flora** cry truths that were once whispered by the quiet bosques.
- **Noxious GAS** drowns soldiers on dry land. The sky buries all under frigid snow.
- The **dead litter the land** between the scars dug into it, trenches filled with those who are too angry or stubborn to die.

But at least there is "progress".

## Rivercrest & the Faithspire Horde

### Josilfa Migol and her Faithspire Horde

Now a city choked by smoke and clamoring metal, **Galgenbeck** has become the center of the world once more with their alchemy of SLAGG. Terrible factories bellow their hate into the sky, casting a permanence of twilight on the great city.

The monolithic **Faithspire**, a towerlike cathedral erected as an offering to HE, cuts through the smog. At its peak sits **Josilfa**, commanding her hordes of zealots on a crusade against the heretical Dying Lands. Those that are captured are sent to the torture chambers of the Pit to be rebuked and redeemed back into the ranks of the Faith. And they are many.

For Josilfa, the war has only one end: every knee shall bow, and every tongue confess. Her Horde of grvnts and numerous Landcrawlers trample everything they set their sights on. Terrible **Endajagare** are christened as Her Holiness' inquisitional arm over the battlefield.

> *For they are many, and their faith is justified by HE.*

### Rivercrest

From the northern Endless Sea, past the bridges of Múr, lands that were once plowed into oblivion were again given new life. As the enormous river slithered into its new home across the land, the Rivercrest rose up around it. Swamplands drown the south of Galgenbeck, its great stone walls sinking ever deeper into the muck. Extreme danger in all its forms lurks these waters.

### Faithspire

A large ivory tower that sees far over the Dying Lands. A beacon to many who believe in the word of **HE**, the Faithspire stands also as a light that casts the shadow of dogmatic oppression. It is the home of Her Holy of Holies, Josilfa Migol, who does not leave her sacristy, save for mass liturgy and war revelations from HE.

### The Pit

Far below the splendor of the Faithspire lie countless dungeons and cruel devices of torture. Prisoners and heretics are taken here, down deep into the wretched parts of the undercity, to have their minds "adjusted". Relentless SLAGG magicks terrorize the enemies of HE, forcing them to relive their worst memories, fears, and waking nightmares until their minds shatter or go blank. Only then — after ego death and psycho-butchery — can they be inculcated into obedient soldiers.

The Pit's very existence is obscured. After all, those who enter leave with no memory of such gruesome procedures, though... rumors on the front lines say that Galgen combatants have been known to… **snap**.

### The Southern Swamps

Between Galgenbeck and the Rivercrest lies the putrid muck of the Southern Swamps, home to the Krok-Toothed and other unsavory heretics that flee the inquisition's reach. A mass graveyard of floating bone and bloated corpses, the air is rank with disease and plague. Vengeful souls trapped beneath the murky water seek to drown those who dare wade into toxic runoff from the great city.

### Northern Fires

As the wood of Sarkash crept closer, a silent war waged between the farmers outside Galgenbeck's northern walls and the Shadow King. Eventually, a persistent fire was set, stretching in a wide swath across the continent, daring the forest to move another foot further.

## Múrland & the Beggars of Death

### King Sigfúm and the Beggars of Death

> *"By my own hand!"*

As was promised, Sigfúm marched his Beggars of Death to the sea — not in Death, but **IN CONQUEST**.

With acolytes ready to follow their liege to the death, Sigfúm the Kind steered their zealotry to his gain. With the use of SLAGGVARA, enthusiastic martyrs constructed abominable **Seacrawlers** and tamed the flames of **The Pyre**. Grift, once the home to great libraries, now harnesses fire and water in fiendish union. Canals burn. Great waterfalls of scorch and blaze pour over **The Singed** in final baptism. Seacrawlers from Múrland patrol the expanse of the Endless Sea and range into the Rivercrest, bombarding those who would reap the river's bounty, thirsting for battle.

King Sigfúm seeks to prolong the war as long as possible to postpone his people's "gentle" destruction in the last days. War weary and longing for reprieve, the Beggars of Death throw themselves into harm's way, desperately seeking the original promise their King has withheld from them. The same mantra drives each Beggar, all too zealous for a taste of the next life.

### Múrland

With the First Bloom rose the waters of the Rivercrest. The great bridges of Múr were all washed away — save one — submerging the island in knee-deep water. Mother of Rivercrest, the sunken home of respite for the scvm of the Dying Lands, has gone the way of all the earth: change ushered in sink and desperation. A great heart of fire burns at her core: eternally fueled by her deposits of liquid SLAGG, never ending, and reaching to the heavens. Its seemingly endless shores of sand have rapidly dissolved into torrents of seawater, slick with SLAGG oil. Thus far, none have claimed her shores.

### Shipsbreath

A large shipyard and refinery, rusted from the perpetual wash of brine and seafoam. Cold coastal winds wail past the stone craigs and treacherous sea stacks. Every **12 minutes**, a new vessel springs forth, buoyant and filled with Beggars of Death. Every **28 minutes** one of these vessels sinks to the bottom of the river Múr to haunt at low tide.

### World's End

Sunken into the northern coast of Múrland, outside the walls of Grift, is the world traveler's lone bastion. Divine seafood, well-heeled fishermen and sailors, craftsmen and tradesmen of every variety: all have eventually settled here as the world burns. Neglect holds the door locks fast; there, the sea-hermits wait, holed up until either the war or the world comes to an end.

### The Pyre

Flames lick the sky above a massive, perpetually-burning heap of waste and dross. Beneath the pyre sits a massive repository of SLAGG, seeping from the soil below. Its gasses are legendary, giving birth to the Beggar's fire-based weaponry. Unburnt SLAGG floats atop the rivers, carrying the burning with it until it reaches the Endless Sea. It's in these rivers that **The Singed** are baptized to receive their new mantle.

### The Depth

King Sigfúm has deceived his people. Their toil, struggle, and longing for death has built him and his progeny a vast palace, stowed in secret beneath the waves. As Grift collapses into ruin, he hastens the plans to finish this desperate hideaway. Resources are covertly siphoned from the war to fill his larder; no one has noticed… yet.

## Mountfont & the Mad Mists

### King Fathmu X and the Mad Mists

**King Fathmu IX is dead.** Rumors allege that his untimely death at the start of the war was brought about from abusive consumption of SLAGG, but darker murmurings claim it was a coup brought about by his son — **FATHMU X, THE BOY KING** — who currently reigns in his father's stead. All he knows is cruelty.

With Mountfont encroaching from the West, and the Rivercrest washing land away from the North, the Wästland has seen even greater famine and desperate starvation. Whispers of revolt among the peasantry float on the Gas-tainted winds. Even the Boy King's own nobles conspire against him. Only with an iron fist and the terror of **vaporized SLAGG** does he maintain control.

### Mountfont

Corruptly colonized by the warring White Crowns and Mad Mists. Munitions explode in the skies overhead, while toxic gasses settle in the valleys. Bodies riddle the plateaus, buffets for **Veirwinged Falchons** and mountaineering cannibals who seek the release of SHE. The highest peaks bring revelation, while despots hide in crags.

Eyes stay locked on the skies, horrified by the never-coming attack — forgetting the long-forgotten tripwires laid ages ago. Like a forced liberation, spires of stone sift out the enlightened from the squabble. However, the victors never return, not without an experience of the other side of the veil… or what could be.

### Sylt

The once-great fishing and farming community of Sylt was swallowed by the Sea of Onda. Only rooftops and chimneys now break its placid surface, creating incredible fisheries — but few remember what they caught and when. The rest lies at the murky bottom, too deep for any to dare explore: the long-forgotten remnants of a time before the war.

### La Fosse

A mass graveyard where bodies of all types are hauled and unsanctimoniously "buried", burned, or pulverized by great and terrible apparatuses. Great grinding machines turn human refuse into soulless, newly-formed inhumans. Where these unholy engines came from or who built them remains a mystery; some blame Tergol, or SHE herself. None know who these ardorless pledge fealty to.

### Kistfabrik

The "re-embodiment" factory where Mad Mists, vaporized by their own Gas, are forced into human-shaped "containers" to fight in a special unit dubbed the **Coffin Corps**. Screams of immense pain echo throughout the Mountfont as phantasms are ripped from their afterlife and forced to serve in anguish.

### Noax

Amidst the vortex that swallows all ships sits Noax, the isle of immaculate abundance. Envy of the war-corrupted and violence-minded, who seek to subdue her beauty, twisting this SLAGG-rich paradise into a foul concubine of destruction.

## Icefell & the White Crowns

### Countess Anthelia and the White Crowns

Saddened by the dulling grey of the dying lands, Anthelia's ageless eyes view color for the first time. After the untimely death of so many suitors, she finds love in the everglow of SLAGGVARA.

Desperate to please their Queen, the White Crowns take to the firmament piloting **Skycrawlers** that soar high above the frozen Icefell peaks. Her purest subjects are bestowed the mantle of **Low Roar** — grvnts capable of flight via a volatile SLAGG propulsion system.

Wise beyond her unnaturally youthful appearance, Anthelia strikes hard and fast from her defensive position in the northern crystalline city of **Alliáns**. She values the lives of her White Crowns dearly — even referring to them, at times, as her own children. Above all, though, is SLAGG. She must have more! Even the cold demeanor of her countenance grows impassioned at the thought of it (a fondness shared by her armies). But there remains the fear that even SLAGGVARA will ultimately prove unworthy of the Cold Queen's affection.

> **What will happen when the Dying Lands are bled dry?**

### Icefell

A land where cold chills the bones, and fear freezes the veins. Sparsely populated villages and steads haunt the snow banks, populated with visages of the reclusive and spectral. They speak with none; why would they? In a land where ice and the power of Alliáns roars high above their heads, they trust no one — least of all, the unceasing snows.

Beneath their feet is a crawling monster, hardened by the freezing cold and the raging war. They give no quarter. This land is numb, as though frostbite has crept into its very soul. Every breath is an admittance towards death.

### Angers

Set atop an ice-capped plateau in the south of Icefell, the people of Angers attempt to ignore the war. From manors hewn upwards into the mountainside, they watch the devastation of the world from baroque balconies and through lavish fireplaces backed by their renowned translucent glass. An unassuming wrought-iron and glass gate safeguards the lone entry to the village.

### Thneed

Child spirits of snow haunt the town, playing harmless jokes as a reminder of what once could have been. A SLAGG processing plant consumes every aspect of life for this forlorn locale. Snow here falls black as the souls lost to forced servitude. Their pathetic citizens cling to the fool's dream of escaping this smoggy hell through hard enough labor, often perishing unnoticed and alone in the pursuit.

### Neumort

Frozen tears slick the streets as children of the impoverished are conscripted into armed conflict. Thralls and nobility intermingle, but not without clear rank and file. Secret wars waged for social supremacy are fought by the poor for their commanding house. The ruling family contributes less to the cause and infighting is fierce. But, even blood hardens to ice.

### Skycrown

A fell peak higher than any devil can reach… unless they incarnate flesh. Horrible **Skycrawlers** are pushed from her mountaintop, plummeting like baby chicks to soaring mayhem or gravitational death.

## Treeswell

### Bright Crossing

On the northern river between Icefell and Treeswell lies a trapped glacier bridging the gap betwixt the shadow and blinding white horizon. This tundra-hardened behemoth serves as the safest passage over the icy waters that flow North into the Endless Sea. A mournful snowfall laments those who have fallen into the deathly cold waters below but never speaks their names.

### Goor Wood

Named for the cannibals living there. The **Goor** do not abide outsiders. Victims are flayed and pinned to the trees for communal feasting, before their bones are grafted into limb and bough. Finger-like branches reach out to trap the unfortunate passerby. Perhaps they reach for salvation?

### Víst

Travel to and from the village of Víst is almost impossible, given the shifting nature of the bosques that surround it or the movement of the hamlet itself. An abbey — half constructed and abandoned — perches atop a hill overlooking the thorp. It is the only place in all the Dying Lands that can clearly see the **Palace of the Shadow King**.

### Singebark

Here at the northern edge of the Fires, the angry, old forest has stalked from the deepest depths of Treeswell to wage a silent and imperceptible battle with devouring flames. As their limbs are burned, their courage grows. Constantly bathed in red, the crack of timber in the arid air echoes among the indignant pines.

## Newfield

Where the war began. A mercantile no-man's-land now: home to greedy city-states, fields of poppies, and the last farms in the Dying Lands still producing food.

### Sea of Onda

Usurping farms, livestock, homes, graveyards, and living souls, the Sea grows ever larger. What once was a lake has swollen fatter than the late Fathmu IX: never satiated, overfed and overfilled by the Rivercrest. Trade routes and coastal merchants plague its waters like flies on a corpse, fueled by SLAGG.

### Gleymtford

Abandoned, for good reason. The bridge to the north groans under the incessant movement of grvnts and SLAGG. Trouble seems inevitable at this strategic position to the Mad Mists, Faithspire Horde, and the Beggars of Death.

### Birth

The southernmost tip of the known lands has seen unprecedented growth due to changes in climate. Small mountains have risen tall. Winter storms bring needed snowfall, but the sun shows itself in regular intervals. Farming and fishing are abundant. Deep in the mines, they have uncovered something extraordinary…

### Newfield Landing

A large city-state — bearing no allegiances — who defends itself with a pathetic army. Yet, none dare invade, for there is more to be lost in destruction than to be gained in plunder. This mercantile city may soon surpass Galgenbeck in population, as every corner crawls with the crafty, the cunning, the destitute, and the desperate. Constant, heavy traffic brings SLAGG to every corner of the Dying Lands — for an eye-gouging price.

## VEX — The Deathbringer

> **VEX: RECOMPENSE.**
> An unseen power thunders beneath the land. Its back is the highest mountain, its blood the river waters. The trees speak to it; the ice blankets it. All laying eyes on it have vanished from the world.
>
> **And it is angry.**

As the land cries out in pain, dying more rapidly with every passing dawn, VEX amasses an army to exact vengeance. Lost souls from every corner of the Dying Lands have been collected to fight the last battle the world will ever see.

**HAS COME FOR VENGEANCE.**

Born of the trauma caused to the world by this war for power and SLAGG, VEX is fueled by rage and revenge. An omnipotent demon, VEX bides their time in the depths of the land. Where destruction, chaos, and ecological disaster are most rampant, VEX is drawn. THEY eradicate the infestation of war — and the soldiers who fight to save their own wretched lives — without hesitation or mercy. The maw of VEX devours armies whole, swallowing them into the depths to add to THEIR growing hordes.

Each morning, VEX gains even more strength; each night, THEY writhe and bellow at the day's devastation. This cannot continue, cannot be allowed, cannot be tolerated. **It will be ceased.**

### Summoning VEX

*Mechanical rule — see `farewell-rules.md` § VEX for the d12 arrival table.*

At the end of a mission, roll **d20 − total SLAGG expended this mission**. On a result of **1 or below**, VEX appears. This is the last thing your squad will ever see.

## Others Who Call This War Home

### SHE, Shadowking

SHE has known it would come to this for quite some time; SHE welcomes it. To HER, all reality is a lie, hence why she cast out **VERHU** from the highest peak of her Chrypt. SHE and her followers move to amass SLAGG for one superweapon, a technology that only SHE knows: **the Magik of Ontological Fission**. SHE will End the world: erase the lie of existence from every memory, burn every soul to ash, and unravel the very concept of time into fragments of stardust amidst the unborn cosmos.

### The Shadow King

Obscured by the shifting forest, the Palace of the Shadow King wavers in the darkness, his intentions ensconced beneath its shroud. Gloom births deceitful apparitions of fortresses shining in the moonlight. Does he seek war or merely prepare for its inevitability? None have witnessed him since before the war began. Many prisoners claim to be his heir, but he answers them not.

### The Hunger Wound

The pilgrimages have all but ceased. Nearly none visit the Hunger Wound without firearm in hand. The **Black Salt Wind** violently pours out of the abyss, defending the ruins of the massive crevasse. Lords of the crypt and saviors from the grave crave their claim to veneration. Grvnts are trapped as forever thralls to their worship, caught somewhere between life and unlife, but never in undeath.

### Tergol

War is an amazing proposition for an alchemist of flesh. He maintains allegiance only to himself and his work. He deals in guile with fixations of his interest: weapons of war, impossible steeds and machinery, living weapons, scrolls and forbidden knowledge, as well as the true nature of SLAGGVARA. His home is chaos, and he is a vagabond.
