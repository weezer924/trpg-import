# The Big Random Event Example: Weird Times At Wutwo Labs

> Source: *Mythic Game Master Emulator 2nd Edition*, p.55-59.
> Demonstrates Random Events: Event Focus rolls, Meaning Table interpretations, and Current Context as a Focus alternative.

## Setup

PC: **Dyson McGrew**, a security guard at Wutwo Labs. An errant experiment on Level 10 has released radiation and a noxious gas that is mutating creatures and warping reality. Dyson is fighting his way upward to Level 1 to trigger Protocol WWZero — a containment failsafe.

Companion: **Dr. Sandra Copenhagen**, a scientist letting Dyson use her clearance cards in exchange for protection. Equipment: security baton + cola-vending-machine door used as a shield.

Scene: Dyson has reached the stairwell that leads from Level 10 up to Level 9. Three Random Events fire across this Scene's progression up Levels 9 and 8.

## Play

### Event 1 — Spider brawl on Level 9 hall (p.56)

Dyson peers around a corridor corner. Fluorescents are out; dim red emergency lights are on. Stairwell door visible at hall's end.

**Question:** "Is there anything in the hall?"
**Answer (Mythic):** No, **but a Random Event triggers**.
**Event Focus:** Context suggests no specific Focus → roll on Event Focus Table → **NPC Negative**.
**Subject:** Roll on Characters List → **Giant spiders** (a known threat from Level 10).
**Meaning roll (Actions table):** "Betray" + "Weapon"

**Interpretation:** "Betray" → spiders fighting amongst themselves. "Weapon" reinforces — they were part of a secret weaponizing project. Synthesis: through the conference-room plate-glass window down the hall, Dyson watches one giant spider pounce on another; a violent intra-spider brawl erupts. The hall is empty. Dyson and Sandra make a shielded dash for the stairwell while the spiders are distracted, with one spilling out into the hall to be defended against on the way through.

### Event 2 — Moss-man's behavior on Level 9 (p.56)

Mid-Scene continuation: They reach Level 9, overrun with mutated writhing vines. They become trapped in a lab with a former scientist transformed into a contagious moss-person (Sandra warns: contact may infect them).

**Question:** "Is there a way out of here?"
**Answer (Mythic):** Yes, **plus a Random Event**.
**Event Focus:** Player elects **Current Context** instead of rolling — uses the Event to determine what the moss-man is *doing* right now (his behavior had been undefined).
**Meaning roll (Characters Elements):** "Frantic" + "Ambush"

**Interpretation:** Aware of their presence, the moss-man frantically waves his arms in slow, shambling lurches — for a slow-witted moss-man this is his best attempt at an ambush.

> **Sidebar (p.57):** "Frantic" + "Ambush" interpreted as slow-motion frantic ambush is a stretch, but that's fine — it was what came to mind and it fits closely enough with the generated results.

Sandra warns Dyson not to let it touch him. Dyson rushes in and bashes the creature with his shield, clearing enough space for them to reach the door and seal it behind them.

### Event 3 — Vampiric water coolers in Level 8 cafeteria (p.57)

Level 8 is overrun with sentient water coolers — fanged faucets that stab people to drink blood. Dyson and Sandra cobble together a makeshift bomb to clear the cafeteria where vampiric coolers gather. Dyson sneaks in and crawls across the floor, planning to plant the bomb under a table.

**Question:** Player asks a Fate Question (text doesn't specify the wording) that **triggers a Random Event**.
**Event Focus:** Roll on Event Focus Table → **PC Negative**.
**Meaning roll (Actions table):** "Fight" + "Outside"

**Interpretation:** Halfway to the bomb's target table, a commotion erupts in an adjoining hall — yelling and gunfire (other survivors also trying to reach the stairwell). The coolers go berserk, sliding to attack the *outside* threat. Dyson is crouched mid-cafeteria surrounded by agitated blood-sucking water coolers — bad timing.

## Outcome

Three Random Events demonstrate three interpretive postures:

1. **Standard Focus roll** (Event 1) — straight Mythic loop.
2. **Current Context as Focus** (Event 2) — use the Event to resolve a previously undefined NPC behavior, instead of injecting something new.
3. **Pure-prompt fit** (Event 3) — Focus + Meaning words snapped together so cleanly that no interpretation gymnastics were needed.

## Lessons (for engine/07-interpretation-principles)

- Use **Current Context** as the Focus when the Random Event lands while something is already undefined and waiting to be resolved (an NPC's behavior, an unseen room, a sound from elsewhere).
- A "stretch" interpretation is acceptable as long as the Meaning words are still recognizable in the result; don't grind for the perfect fit.
- The **"I Dunno" Rule** (p.55 sidebar): if the Random Event genuinely won't interpret, drop it and continue. Use sparingly — distinguish "stuck" from "haven't thought yet."
- Meaning words are seeds, not constraints — "Betray + Weapon" became spiders fighting each other (a betrayal of pack-loyalty) plus a callback to the weapons program.
- When an Event Focus says **NPC Negative** + the Meaning roll is short and punchy, the bad thing usually happens *to* the NPC subject, not to the PC — but the consequences ripple back.
