# The Big Example: Rapid Red's Grand Adventure

> Source: *Mythic Game Master Emulator 2nd Edition*, p.178-186.
> Full end-to-end demonstration: First Scene generation from a Random Event, Interrupt Scene, Expected Scene chain, with progressive list/CF management.

## Setup

PC: **Rapid Red** (alter ego **Rick Burgundy**, reporter at *The Blurb*), a speedster hero who can move supersonically fast. Game system: *SuperBreakers!* Adventure starts blank — no prior history; blank Adventure Journal and blank Lists.

**Starting Chaos Factor:** 5 (default)

> **Sidebar (p.178) — Choices, Choices:** Throughout this example the Player chooses moment-to-moment whether to consult Mythic or trust their expectations. There is no rule about when you "must" ask. Personal preference and gut feeling are valid guides; the goal is the end result you enjoy.

## Scene 1: A rift in reality causes chaos

> First Scene — generated from scratch via Random Event.

**First-Scene seed (Random Event Focus roll):** "**New NPC**".
**Meaning roll (Actions):** "Triumph" + "Cooperation" → a team of superheroes tackling a problem.

**Q (to find the problem):** Player goes straight to a Meaning roll instead of a Fate Question.
**Meaning roll (Actions):** "Strange" + "Outside" → a strange disturbance downtown — a rift in space, energies from outside our world wreaking havoc.

Setting: Rick is at his downtown reporting job at *The Blurb* when news of disturbances comes in.

**Q:** "Can he see what's going on?" → Odds **Likely** → **Yes**. (Player skips a Meaning roll for the visual — runs with expectation: blue energy strands warping reality, sirens, screaming.)

Rick excuses himself, changes into Rapid Red on the way down, races to the center.

**Q:** "Does Rapid recognize the team helping people?" → Odds **Likely** → **Exceptional Yes** → not just recognize: famous superhero team.
**Meaning roll (Names):** "Fah" + "Ow", reroll → "N" + "R" → interpreted as "Founder" → **The Founders**.

Rapid joins the rescue.
**Meaning roll (Actions, for emergency to handle):** "Release" + "Vehicle" → a car teetering on a warped overpass, driver trapped.
**Q:** "Does the car start to fall before Rapid gets there?" → Odds **Nearly Impossible** (his speed) → **No**. He pulls the driver clear.

**Q:** "Does the rift close?" → Odds **Very Likely** → roll **22** at CF 5 → **Yes** + Random Event (double digits, single digit ≤ CF).
**Event Focus roll (91):** "**Current Context**".
**Meaning roll (Actions):** "Imprison" + "Representative" → the rift closes; The Founders captured someone associated with it.

**Q:** "Do I find out what's going on?" → Odds **Nearly Certain** → **Yes**.
**Meaning roll (Character Appearance, for the captive):** "Strange" + "Colorful" → a strange flying-eye creature with bright red leathery skin.

Detailing two Founder members:
- **Leader's power (Powers Elements):** "Others" + "Emission" → eye beam blast.
  **Name (Names Elements):** "R" + "Wah" → **Ra** (Egyptian sun god motif).
- **Member 2's power (Powers Elements):** "Damage" + "Emotion" → mental power, control others' emotions.
  **Name (Names Elements):** "Fi" + "Ar", continue → "Oh" + "Ah" → **Fear Aura**.

**End of Scene 1:**
- CF transition: PC mostly in control → **CF 5 → 4**
- Threads: add "Why the rift?"
- Characters: add "The Founders", "Ra", "Fear Aura", "Captured Alien", "City", "The Blurb"

State after Scene 1:
```
Threads:    Why the rift?
Characters: The Founders, Ra, Fear Aura, Captured Alien, City, The Blurb
```

## Scene 2: Learn about an impending invasion from another world

**Expected Scene plan:** The Founders take the prisoner back to HQ for questioning; Rapid accompanies them.
**Test:** d10 → **2** at CF 4 → **Interrupt Scene**.

**Interrupt seed — Event Focus:** "**NPC Action**".
**NPC selection:** Characters List has 6 entries (just into a second section). d4 → 3 → second section; d10 → 6 → blank line.

> **Sidebar (p.181):** A blank-line roll lets you **choose** an element instead, or reroll. Use it to nudge the narrative when a logical choice is obvious.

Player chooses the Captured Alien — most plausibly the one who'd act unexpectedly here.
**Meaning roll (Characters Elements):** "Friend" + "Unexpected" → reconsider the alien's nature.

In the containment field, the alien wakes and **telepathically** announces it has come as a friend to help.

**Meaning roll (Character Conversations Elements):** "Inform" + "Threatening" → tested with a Fate Question:
**Q:** "Does it warn us of an impending invasion from its home dimension?" → Odds **Likely** → **Yes** (rolled 26 at CF 4, 50% chance).

**Naming the alien (Names Elements):** "Ahg" + "Animal" → **Ogberd** (sounds appropriately strange).

Ogberd apologizes for the messy arrival; opening portals between worlds is dangerous business but it was the only way.

Player decides the in-fiction question of belief is best handled in the chosen RPG, not Mythic.
> *SuperBreakers!* **Persuasion Task Roll** by Rapid → succeeds. Player drops the planned "Do The Founders believe him?" Fate Question.

**Q:** "Do The Founders have some tech to help verify Ogberd's claim?" → Odds **Very Likely** → **Yes** (rolled 24 at CF 4, 65% chance). A Founder scientist opens a comm channel; resistance allies in Ogberd's dimension confirm the warning.

**Q:** "Is this a warlord of some kind?" (the invader) → Odds **Nearly Certain** → roll **12** at CF 4 → **Exceptional Yes** (≤15). Interpretation: the villain rules her entire dimension — a cosmic force.

**Meaning roll (Characters Elements, what she's like):** "Combative" + "Anxious" → conqueror, also paranoid and power-hungry.
**Meaning roll (Powers Elements, her abilities):** "Skill" + "Magic" → powerful sorcerer with godlike abilities.
**Naming (Names):** "Or" + "Tal", continue → "Lee" + "Et" → **Orta Leet**.

**End of Scene 2:**
- CF transition: invasion threat introduced, no answer yet → less in control → **CF 4 → 5**
- Threads: cross out "Why the rift?" (resolved). Add "Stop the invasion".
- Characters: rename "Captured Alien" → "Ogberd". Add another instance of "The Founders" and "Ogberd". Add "Orta Leet" and "Resistance fighters".

State after Scene 2:
```
Threads:    Stop the invasion
Characters: The Founders (×2), Ra, Fear Aura, Ogberd (×2), City, The Blurb,
            Orta Leet, Resistance fighters
```

## Scene 3: There is a magic spell that can stop Orta. Hopefully.

**Expected Scene plan:** Ogberd has a solution — what is it?
**Test:** d10 → **7** at CF 5 → **Expected Scene happens**.

**Q:** "Is there something here, on Earth, that will help us fight Orta?" → Odds **50/50** (no confidence) → **Yes** (CF 5, 50%).
**Meaning roll (Actions):** "Praise" + "Hope" — Player stretches: "Praise" feels magical (Orta is mystical); "Hope" in context = protection / banishment.

Interpretation: Ogberd traveled here both to warn and to find a **lost book of magic** containing a spell to banish Orta from both dimensions. The book was brought to Earth long ago by Orta's evil acolytes to hide it.

**Q:** "Does Ogberd know where the book is?" → Odds **Likely** → **Yes** (CF 5, 65%).
**Meaning roll (Locations Elements, where):** "Confusing" + "Unpleasant" → ensconced in a **graveyard in France** centuries ago; he doesn't know which graveyard or where within it.

**Q:** "Do any of The Founders accompany Rapid?" → Odds **Nearly Certain** (85%) → roll **92** → **No**.

Interpretation: Ra and the team will stay to prepare global defenses — searching for an uncertain spell isn't a use of their resources. Rapid and Ogberd will go alone.

**End of Scene 3:**
- CF transition: PC was central in forming the plan → in control → **CF 5 → 4**
- Threads: add another "Stop the invasion". Add "Find the book".
- Characters: add another "Ogberd" (Ogberd now ×3, **maxed**). Decline another "The Founders" — they didn't play a big role this Scene.

State after Scene 3:
```
Threads:    Stop the invasion (×2), Find the book
Characters: The Founders (×2), Ra, Fear Aura, Ogberd (×3, capped), City, The Blurb,
            Orta Leet, Resistance fighters
```

## Lessons (for engine/07-interpretation-principles)

- **A First Scene can be generated entirely from prompts** — no setup is needed if you start with a Random Event roll for context.
- Going **directly to a Meaning Table** instead of asking a Fate Question is a valid shortcut when you have no opinion on a detail.
- Every detail you record (Rick works downtown, he's experienced at slipping out, etc.) **becomes Context** that constrains and inspires later expectations — the layered, consistent adventure emerges from these.
- Multiple Names rolls combined into one name is normal — keep rolling until you have enough phonemes to compose something.
- A **Random Event mid-Fate-Question** (double-digit roll with single digit ≤ CF) is layered onto the Yes/No, not separate from it — both apply.
- A **blank-line roll** on a populated section lets you *choose* the element or reroll — use it to nudge the narrative toward what fits, not to invent.
- **Defer to the chosen RPG** for moments that are properly RPG mechanics (Persuasion roll, combat, skill check) instead of asking a Fate Question — a successful task roll resolves the question.
- **Decline to reroll** when the next layer would slow play; commit to the answer you have. (E.g., declining to detail Founders' tech further.)
- A Thread can be **crossed out** (not just "completed") when its question is answered — replace with the next-tier Thread.
- An Exceptional result with high Odds (Nearly Certain → ≤15 = Exceptional) **intensifies further** — "warlord" became "cosmic ruler of an entire dimension."
- The **Lists cap at 3 instances** for any single element; once capped, that element's selection probability stops growing relative to others.
