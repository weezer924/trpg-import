# Keyed Scenes Record Sheet

> Source: *Mythic Game Master Emulator 2nd Edition*, p.150-154 (rules) + p.224 (template).
> Keyed Scenes let the Player pre-plan future scenes that MUST happen at specified Chaos Factor or Thread states. See `../rules/05-variations.md` §Keyed Scenes for full rules.

## Keyed Scenes

Up to 5 pre-keyed future scenes. Each scene has a trigger condition and a brief setup. When the trigger fires (checked at end-of-Scene Bookkeeping), the next Scene is forced to be the keyed Scene — overriding the normal Expected/Altered/Interrupt test.
