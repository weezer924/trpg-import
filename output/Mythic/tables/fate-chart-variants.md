# Fate Chart & Fate Check — Chaos Variants

> Source: *Mythic GME 2e* p.147-149, p.222-223 (collected sheets).
> When: To dampen the Chaos Factor's influence on Fate Question answers.
> Input: Odds (row) + current Chaos Factor (mapped to a banded column).
> Output: Same `ExcYes ≤ / Yes ≤ / ExcNo ≥` format as the regular Fate Chart.

Pick one of these three variant pairs (chart + modifier table) to replace the regular Fate Chart and Fate Check Modifiers throughout your adventure (or just for specific Question types — see `../rules/05-variations.md` §Choose Your Chaos Flavor).

`—` = result not possible at this cell.
