# Meaning Tables — Descriptions

> When: interpreting a Random Event or generating random narrative content needing tone/quality flavor.
> Input: two d100 rolls.
> Output: a compound description seed (Descriptor 1 adverb + Descriptor 2 adjective).
> Source: PDF p.49 (primary); duplicated at p.201.

## How To Read

Roll d100 twice. The first roll selects from Descriptor 1 (adverb); the second from Descriptor 2 (adjective). Treat the pair as a single compound seed — force synthesis rather than interpreting them independently. See `../engine/07-interpretation-principles.md` for guidance.
