# NPC Behavior Table

> Source: *Mythic GME 2e* p.107-110, p.218 (collected sheets).
> When: You need to know what an NPC does next, the action is important to the adventure, AND you have an idea of what to expect. Frame it as the Fate Question **"Does the NPC do X?"** and resolve as normal.
> Input: Fate Question result (Yes / Exceptional Yes / No / Exceptional No / Random Event).
> Output: How to interpret the NPC's behavior.

(If you have *no* expectation, roll a word pair on a Meaning Table for inspiration. If you have a clear expectation and the action *isn't* crucial, just go with your expectation. See `../rules/04-scenes.md` §Generating NPC Behavior.)

## NPC Behavior Table

| Fate Question Result | "Does the NPC do X?" |
|---|---|
| **Yes** | The NPC does what you expect, or continues with their ongoing action. |
| **No** | The NPC does the next most expected behavior. If you're unsure what that is, roll on a Meaning Table for inspiration. |
| **Exceptional Yes** | The NPC does the expected action (or continues their ongoing action) with **greater intensity**. |
| **Exceptional No** | The NPC does the **opposite** of what you expected — or does the next most expected behavior with **greater intensity**. If you're unsure, roll on a Meaning Table and intensify that action. |
| **Random Event** | Roll on a Meaning Table for an **additional action** from the NPC. Treat the Random Event with Event Focus = Current Context. Combine with the original action if it makes sense; otherwise treat as two separate actions. |
