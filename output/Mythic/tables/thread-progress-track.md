# Thread Progress Track

> Source: *Mythic GME 2e* p.132-138 (rules), p.219-220 (collected sheets).
> When: You want to focus an adventure on completing a particular Thread.
> Input: Choose a 10/15/20-point Track, mark Progress and Flashpoint events, make Discovery Checks when stalled.
> Output: A scaffold of Progress, Flashpoint events, and a final Conclusion.

Full rules in `../rules/05-variations.md` §The Thread Progress Track. Tables and tracks below.

## Track Sheets

Each Track has a Focus Thread label. Mark off boxes as Progress Points are earned. The Track is divided into **5-point phases**; at the end of each phase, if no Flashpoint has happened, one is forced (`FLASHPOINT+2`). After the final phase, the Track ends in `CONCLUSION`.

Each phase has a `DID A FLASHPOINT HAPPEN? ☐` checkbox to track whether you've already had a Flashpoint in that phase.

### 10-Point Track

```
FOCUS THREAD: ___________________________

  1   2   3   4   5    FLASHPOINT+2
  6   7   8   9   10   CONCLUSION

  DID A FLASHPOINT HAPPEN? ☐
```

## Discovery Fate Question

> Triggered when you make a Discovery Check (Fate Question "Is something discovered?", Odds no lower than 50/50).

| Fate Question Result | "Is something discovered?" |
|---|---|
| **Yes** | Roll on the Thread Discovery Check Table. |
| **No** | Nothing useful is found. No roll on the Thread Discovery Check Table. |
| **Exceptional Yes** | Roll **twice** on the Thread Discovery Check Table, combining results. |
| **Exceptional No** | Nothing useful is discovered AND you cannot make another Discovery Check for the rest of this Scene — the PC has hit a dead end on Discovery and must continue such searches in another Scene. |

## Thread Discovery Check Table

> Roll **1d10 + current Progress Points** and consult below. Then roll on a Meaning Table for inspiration to interpret the Discovery as you would a Random Event.

| 1d10 + Progress | Result |
|---:|---|
| **1-9**   | Progress +2 |
| **10**    | Flashpoint +2 |
| **11-14** | Track +1 |
| **15-17** | Progress +3 |
| **18**    | Flashpoint +3 |
| **19**    | Track +2 |
| **20-24** | Strengthen Progress +1 |
| **25+**   | Strengthen Progress +2 |

### Result interpretations

- **Progress +2 / +3**: Discover something that moves the PC closer to the Focus Thread. Award the Progress Points.
- **Flashpoint +2 / +3**: Discover something dramatic and important involving the Focus Thread. Award Points; mark the phase's "Did A Flashpoint Happen?" box.
- **Track +1 / +2**: Nothing useful discovered, but the act of trying moves you 1 or 2 points along.
- **Strengthen Progress +1 / +2**: Reinforces previous Progress. Award Points; tie this discovery into a previous one (e.g., the mage who learned about Fay Jars now finds the components needed to *build* one).
