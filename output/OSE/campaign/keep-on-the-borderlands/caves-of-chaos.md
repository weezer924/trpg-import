## Ravine Description

*(PDF p.16 — read aloud)*

The forest has been getting denser, gloomier, twisted. Suddenly the growth ends — a ravine-like area. Walls rise steeply ~100' on either side, dark streaked rock mingled with earth. Clumps of trees grow on the floor and slopes. The opening is ~200' wide; the ravine runs ~440' west where it rises in a steep slope. At varying heights on all sides, black cave mouths gape in the rock walls. Dim sunlight, dank air, an oppressive feeling of lurking evil. Dead trees, a perching vulture, croaking ravens. Bones and skulls of men, animals, and other things litter the ground.

## General Features

*(PDF p.16)*

- **Woods:** Thick, tangled growths. Movement in single file. May contain stirges, humanoids from nearby caves.
- **Underground:** Caves on different levels. Passages slope between contours (even without marked stairs). Areas roofed by ≥5' solid rock.
- **Interiors:** Natural or cut from living rock. Rough surfaces — easy for thieves to climb. Small ledges, cracks, holes.
- **Ransoming Prisoners:** See `region.md`.
- **Tribal Alliances:** See `region.md` Factions.
- **Monsters Learning:** See `region.md` DM Notes.
- **Emptied Areas:** Deserted 1–4 weeks, then reoccupied by survivors or new monsters.

## Cave A — Kobold Lair (Areas 1–6)

*(PDF p.16, 21)*

### Entrance

2-in-6 chance that 8 kobolds emerge from trees above to ambush the entering party.

- **Ambush Kobolds** ×8: AC 7 [12], HD ½ (3hp each), Att 1 × weapon (1d4), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 6, AL C. Each carries d8 sp.

### Pit Trap

30' inside the entrance. 3-in-6 chance each front rank person falls in (probing ahead avoids). 1-in-6 chance for second rank (only if close and first rank person fell). Pit is 10' deep, 1d6 falling damage. Lid closes — trapped persons cannot escape without outside aid. Noise attracts Areas 1 and 2. Planks stored at Area 1.

### 1. Guard Room

- **Kobold Guards** ×6: AC 7 [12], HD ½ (3hp each), Att 1 × spear (1d4), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 6, AL C. Throw spears first round if they have initiative. Each carries d6 sp. One will run to warn Areas 4 and 6. Alerted by loud noises or lights.

---

### 2. Giant Rats

Amidst garbage and waste.

- **Giant Rats** ×18: AC 7 [12], HD ½ (2hp each), Att 1 × bite (1d3 + disease), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL N. Each bite: 1-in-20 chance of disease (save vs. Poison to avoid). If save fails: 25% chance of death in 1–6 days; otherwise too sick to adventure for 1 game month. Pets of the kobolds. Rush to sound of pit trap or battle. No treasure.

- **Rat Leader (#18):** AC 5 [14] (speed and cunning), HD 1−1 (4hp), Att 2 × bite (2d2/2d2), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8. At back of pack. Wears thin silver chain set with 5 small gems (jewelry value 400 gp; chain 50 gp; each gem 50 gp). Rats' weight does not trigger pit trap.

---

### 3. Food Storage Room

Door locked. Dried/salted meat, grain, vegetables in sacks, boxes, barrels. Bits of past human victims. Thin, vinegary wine in a large cask. Nothing of value.

### 4. Guard Room

- **Large Kobold Guards** ×3: AC 5 [14] (chain mail), HD 1+1 (5hp each), Att 1 × bow (1d6) or hand axe (1d6), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 120' (40'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 6, AL C. Hide behind corner for cover (−2 to hit them with missiles). Each carries 2d6 gp in purse.

## Cave E — Ogre Cave (Area 22)

*(PDF p.23)*

Strong sour odor. Appears to be a huge bear asleep in the SW — actually a bearskin bed on piled leaves. The ogre sits in the eastern portion.

- **Ogre:** AC 4 [15] (thick hide + bearskin), HD 4+1 (25hp), Att 1 × club (3–12, 1d10+2, high Str), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F4), ML 10, AL C.

Mercenary — usually serves goblins. Has been bought off by orcs and gnolls too. Rushes to aid goblins when they toss him 250 gp (see Area 18). If offered a greater visible fee: 90% likely to just take it (and the goblins' money too) and return to lair.

### 22. Treasure

Sits on a great leather bag containing 7 large sacks:
- **#1:** 287 sp
- **#2:** A hard cheese
- **#3:** 182 cp, 91 ep
- **#4:** 289 gp
- **#5:** Keg of brandy (80 gp value)
- **#6:** 303 cp
- **#7:** 241 gold-washed lead coins (each worth only 1 cp!)

**Bribe:** If offered ≥20 gp, 90% chance ogre lets them leave. But if he catches them again, attacks regardless.

**Hidden treasure (under bone heap, south):** 6 **arrows +1**, **potion of invisibility**, **magic scroll** with 2 cleric spells: *cure light wounds*, *hold person*.
