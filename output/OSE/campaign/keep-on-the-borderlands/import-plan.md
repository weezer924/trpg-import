## Module Structure Overview

### The Keep (Areas 1–27)
- **Outer Bailey:** Main Gate (1), Flanking Towers (2), Entry Yard (3), Stable (4), Warehouse (5), Bailiff's Tower (6), Private Apartments (7a–b), Smithy (8), Provisioner (9), Trader (10), Loan Bank (11), Watch Tower (12), Fountain Square (13), Travelers Inn (14), Tavern (15), Guild House (16), Chapel (17)
- **Inner Bailey:** Inner Gatehouse (18), Small Tower (19), Guard Tower (20), Inner Bailey (21), Cavalry Stables (22), Great Tower (23), Keep Fortress (24–25–26), Castellan's Chamber (27)

### Wilderness (Area Map Encounters)
1. Mound of the Lizard Men
2. Spiders' Lair
3. Raider Camp
4. The Mad Hermit

### Caves of Chaos (Caves A–K)
- **A.** Kobold Lair (Areas 1–6)
- **B.** Orc Lair (Areas 7–12, g)
- **C.** Orc Lair (Areas 13–16, g)
- **D.** Goblin Lair (Areas 17–21)
- **E.** Ogre Cave (Area 22)
- **F.** Hobgoblin Lair (Areas 23–31)
- **G.** Shunned Cavern (Areas 32–34) — Gray Ooze, Owl Bear
- **H.** Bugbear Lair (Areas 35–41)
- **I.** Caves of the Minotaur (Areas 42–45)
- **J.** Gnoll Lair (Areas 46–50)
- **K.** Shrine of Evil Chaos (Areas 51–64)

### Key Factions / Tribal Alliances
- Goblins + Hobgoblins (allied, share secret door between D/F)
- Orcs B + Orcs C (rival tribes, secret meeting room 13)
- Gnolls + Orcs (loose alliance)
- Kobolds (try to stay out of everyone's way)
- Bugbears (pick off stragglers from all sides)
- Ogre (mercenary, usually hired by goblins)
- Minotaur (independent, may ally with bugbears)
- Shrine of Evil Chaos (K) — evil clerics commanding undead
