## Provisioner (Area 9)

Sells all standard dungeon adventuring equipment per rulebook. Buys at 50% of listed price.

**Sells:** All equipment from the standard equipment list. Spears, daggers, arrows, bolts. 7 shields.

**Does NOT sell:** Weapons (other than above), armor, mounts.

**Directs to:** Trader (Area 10) for armor, weapons, and bulk goods.

---

## Trader (Area 10)

Deals in all armor, weapons, and bulk goods (salt, spices, cloth, rare woods).

**Buys:** At 50% of listed price. **Exception:** Furs bought at full stated value if seller demands.

**Sells:** All armor and weapons per rulebook at standard prices.

## Mercenaries for Hire

Available at the Tavern (Area 15). 50% chance of 2–5 (d4+1) being present.

- **Men-at-Arms:** AC 6 [13] (leather + shield), HD 1 (5hp), Att 1 × sword (1d6), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 7, AL N.

**Equipment:** Sword and dagger only. Employer must purchase all other gear (missile weapons, dungeon equipment, etc.).

**Wages:**

| Condition | Rate |
|-----------|-----:|
| With gear purchased by employer | 1 sp/day |
| Without gear (or only minor items like a spear) | 1 gp/day |

**Note:** Must buy them a drink before discussing terms of employment.

**Also available (10% chance each):**
- **Wanderer:** 2nd or 3rd level fighter, dwarf, elf, or halfling with complete adventuring equipment. Will join for 25% of treasure. 1-in-6 chance of chaotic alignment.
