## DM Notes About the Keep

*(PDF p.8–9)*

**Security:** The Keep is well-organized for defense. Sentries are alert. Guards patrol walls irregularly; a commander checks every half hour to hour. Very unlikely anyone can enter or leave unseen without magic.

**Law and Order:** Townspeople are law-abiding and honest. Boorishness frowned upon. Criminal acts trigger instant alarm. Citizens try to prevent escape; guard arrives in 1–2 turns. Offenders locked in dungeons under the Keep.

**Siege Weapons:**
- **Ballista (+):** Range 480', hits as F1–3, damage 2d6+2 (4–14), 12 missiles each, fires 1/4 rounds (3 rounds to load, 1 to fire). Manned by 2.
- **Light Catapult (⊕):** Range 241'–480', fires 1/8 rounds. 2 crew, hits as Normal Man, hits 1–6 targets in close group for 1d6 each (or 6d6 vs 1 large target). 6 shots per machine.

**Access to Inner Bailey:** See `region.md` Adventure Flow.

## Outer Bailey (Areas 1–17)

### 1. Main Gate

*(PDF p.10)*

Two towers 30' high with battlements flank a gatehouse 20' high. Arrow slits throughout. Deep crevice spanned by a drawbridge (usually up). Portcullis at entry, large gates at far end. Passage ~10' wide and high; murder holes in ceiling, arrow slits in walls. Constructed of hard granite.

- **Guards:** 2 men-at-arms approach when drawbridge is lowered.
  - **Men-at-Arms** ×2: AC 3 [16], HD 1 (5hp), Att 1 × pole arm (1d10), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 10, AL L.
  - Clad in plate mail, carry pole arms. Require visitors to put weapons away, escort them to Area 3.

---

### 2. Flanking Towers

*(PDF p.10)*

Atop each tower: 4 crossbowmen with crossbows cocked and ready.

- **Tower-top Crossbowmen** ×4 per tower: AC 5 [14] (chain mail; AC 4 [15] with shield), HD 1 (4hp), Att 1 × sword (1d6) or 1 × crossbow (1d6, 1/2 rate), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 10, AL L. Each carries d6 cp and d4 sp.

Inside each tower: 12 more men-at-arms. 4 on-duty (armed as above). 8 resting (takes 1 full turn to ready); these carry longbows instead of crossbows. Stats identical.

Tower contents: supplies of bolts, arrows, spears, rocks, barrels of oil (for hurling on attackers), pallets, clothing, small tables, stools, benches.

### 7. Private Apartments

*(PDF p.10–11)*

Five small apartments along the south wall — occupied by families of Outer Bailey residents. Two large apartments (7a, 7b) house special guests.

#### 7a. Jewel Merchant

Eastern portion of building. The merchant (normal man) and his wife on upper floor. Guarded by:

- **Guards** ×2: AC 4 [15] (chain mail + shield), HD 2 (17hp, 12hp), Att 1 × sword (1d6), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 8, AL N. Each commands a huge war dog.

**Trade:** Buys gems at 60%–90% (d4×10 + 50%) of value. Sells at 110%–140% (d4×10 + 100%) of value. Jewellery also available for sale. See `shops.md`.

#### 7b. The Evil Priest (Spy)

*(PDF p.11)*

Western portion. A jovial priest taking advantage of his stopover to discuss theology and convert others. Everyone speaks well of him. His two acolytes never speak ("vows of silence").

**The Truth:** All three are **chaotic and evil**, in the Keep to spy and defeat adventurers seeking the Caves of Chaos. Once in the caves, the priest will betray the party during a crucial encounter.

- **Evil Priest:** AC -1 [20] (plate mail +1, shield +1, Dex 15), HD 3* (18hp), Att 1 × mace +1 (2–7), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 60' (20'), SV D11 W12 P14 B16 S15 (C3), ML 10, AL C. Spells: *cause light wounds* (2–7 damage on touch, "to hit" required), *light* (can blind). Scroll: *hold person*, *silence 15' radius*. Gold chain worth 100 gp with bloodstone gem worth 500 gp.

- **Acolytes** ×2: AC 4 [15] (chain mail + shield), HD 1 (7hp each), Att 1 × mace (1d6), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 60' (20'), SV D11 W12 P14 B16 S15 (C1), ML 7, AL C. Gold chain worth 100 gp each.

Each cleric carries 4d6 sp. Hidden in the priest's chair: small sack with 30 each of pp, gp, ep, sp, cp, plus jeweled clasp worth 300 gp (for bribes or escape).

### 17. Chapel

*(PDF p.12–13)*

Peaked roof, two stories tall. Interior is one large room. Altar at east end with colored glass window (worth 350 gp intact), 20' tall × 8' wide. Offering box on heavy pedestal (southeast corner): 1–100 cp and 1–100 sp at any time, emptied each evening by the Curate.

*(PDF p.13)*

Most influential person in the Keep except the Castellan. 5th level cleric (+1 to hit for high level).
