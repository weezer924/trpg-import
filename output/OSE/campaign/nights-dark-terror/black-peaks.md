## Through the Valley

*(PDF p.40)*

North of Threshold, the route follows the Foamfire river valley along the Hutaakans' ancient road into the Black Peak mountains. A few miles from Threshold, the party enters **Death's Head gnoll** territory. Upstream, gnoll territory ends at a majestic waterfall where the Foamfire crashes 320 feet down from a mountain pass. The pass leads to the gates of Hutaaka.

No fixed-location encounters in this section. Events represent the gnolls' reactions to the intrusion and should occur in order. The party should keep moving -- the gnolls cannot catch them before the gorge. Fighting gnolls en masse is suicidal; discourage it.

## VE2. Dead Humans

*(PDF p.40)*

Smoke ahead. A flock of crows takes flight. The party finds the remains of a fire, cooking pots, torn bedrolls, and a broken spear-shaft. Three hunters killed by Death's Head gnolls. Blackened bones in the fire. Heads missing. A trail leads north.

## VE3. Sacred Ground (Map VE3)

*(PDF p.40–41)*

10 minutes after leaving the hunters' camp: thick mist rolls down the valley. The land grows quiet. This is sacred ground where gnolls dispose of their dead -- impaled on stakes, left to decay. The site is roughly circular: chiefs and bodyguards in a central ring, lesser gnolls in concentric rings outward.

North of the circle: the hut of **Krasgat**, Death's Head gnoll shaman. She is wailing in prayer, unaware of the party. The wailing unsettles animals and grates on nerves.

### Ghouls

Mist clears to 30' visibility. Impaled gnoll corpses become visible. Two fresh corpses (killed by the hunters). Before the party can explore further:

- 2 ghouls burst from a hidden tunnel (surprise 1–3 unless footprints found)
- 2 more ghouls sneak behind the party

**Ghouls** x4: AC 6 [13], HD 2* (hp 10, 7, 12, 9), Att 2 x claw / 1 x bite (1d3/1d3/1d3 + paralysis), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 9, AL C, XP 25 each. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

### The Tunnels

Ghouls retreat to the central chamber if turned or morale fails. Tunnels are home to 20 giant rats (1d6 attack each turn the party is in the tunnels).

**Giant Rats** x20: AC 7 [12], HD 1/2 (2hp), Att 1 x bite (1d3 + disease), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 8, AL N, XP 6 each. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

- **Entrances:** Concealed (as secret doors), only 3' high -- daggers and hand-axes only
- **Central chamber:** 8' high ceiling
- **Treasure:** Shelf with two shrunken skull drinking vessels, each containing a **potion of undead control**

### Krasgat the Gnoll Shaman

Becomes aware of the party during the ghoul fight. Sends a helper for aid -- a war-party (1 gnoll leader + 10 ordinary gnolls) arrives 4 turns later.

**Appearance:** Female, old, bald. She and 4 helpers smear their faces and arms white with ash. Wears a filthy cloak of enemy skins (humans, dwarves, goblins, orcs). Necklace of finger bones.

**Krasgat:** AC 6 [13], HD 3* (14hp), Att 1 x spiked club (1d4+2), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 90' (30'), SV D8 W9 P10 B13 S12 (C6), ML 9/12, AL C, XP 65.

**Spells:**
- **1st level:** *cause fear*, *darkness*
- **2nd level:** *blight*, *hold person*
- **3rd level:** *curse* (halves Dex)

**Gear:** Spiked club (goblin skull with iron spikes), bone rattle (elf skull), elf-skin pouch with 4 divination stones, stone sacrificial knife, ball of string, two stoppered kobold skulls (one with 2 *potions of healing*, one with *potion of speed*).

**Tactics:** Watches from the mist. Attacks if the party approaches her hut or is about to leave. Casts *blight* -> *hold person* -> *cause fear*. Helpers rush in. If losing: casts *darkness* and flees.

**Helpers** x4: Use ordinary gnoll stats (see [Death's Head Gnolls](#deaths-head-gnolls-full-tribe-stats)).

### Krasgat's Hut

Humanoid and animal skulls, mangy fur bed. Hidden backpack contains: dried herbs, pouch with **5 amber gems** (100gp each), **clerical scroll** (*cure light wounds*, *hold person*, *striking*, *neutralise poison*). Lean-to outside: 4 fur beds, one hides **23ep**.

*(PDF p.42)*

3 hours after leaving the burial ground: mist clears, plumes of dark smoke rise behind the party. Gnoll signalling fires. More smoke appears. A large group of humanoids (1 gnoll leader + 1 ogre + 30 gnolls) is moving up the valley 2–3 miles behind. By day: smoke visible. At night: gnoll drums echo through the valley.

*(PDF p.42)*

A gnoll hunting party has set an ambush ahead: 1 gnoll leader + 12 ordinary gnolls. They throw spears first, then attack with swords. Normal surprise chance. If no surprise: the party spots the ambushers 100 yards away.

## Passage of the Ancients

*(PDF p.42–43)*

### V1. The Gorge

The Foamfire river falls 320 feet over a majestic waterfall. The ancient road enters a tunnel that spirals up to the pass above. By now, the whole gnoll tribe is pursuing.

**Pursuit distance:**
- On foot or warhorses: gnoll advance group (40 gnolls + 2 leaders + 3 ogres) is only 200 yards behind
- On riding horses: the party has greatly outdistanced them

Gnolls follow into the gorge, blocking escape. They intend to drive the adventurers into the tunnel, confident the steel statues in the tower will kill them. Gnolls will not enter the tunnel (afraid of the statues -- they have no magical weapons).

**Tunnel:** 20' wide x 20' high, spirals up to a bridge over the falls. Undisturbed mud on the floor.

### Tower on the Bridge

At the top of the tunnel, directly above the raging torrent. A wide stone bridge (20' wide, 80' long) spans the river, straddled by a squat crumbling stone tower. Three gnoll skeletons lie face-down near the tower (killed by the statues -- every piece of iron and steel from their gear is gone).

**Tower:** 20' square, bronze door on each side (currently closed). The nearest door has an engraved pattern of entwined needles and threads (covered in verdigris). Single bare windowless room. Only the ground floor is intact.

**Steel Living Statues** x2: AC 1 [18], HD 5** (22hp), Att 2 x fists (1d8/1d8), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 30' (10'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 12, AL N, XP 425 each. Non-magical iron/steel weapons do no damage but stick to the statue and are absorbed in 1 round (statue gains 1d4+1 hp, max 40hp). Magical weapons do normal damage. See New Monsters.

- **Bypass:** If shown a magical silver needle, the statues stand aside and bow
- **Treasure on floor:** *Wand of polymorphing* (3 charges), scroll with *lightning bolt*, *haste*, *confusion*, *magic missile* (3 missiles)

## The Mountain Pass

*(PDF p.43)*

The ancient road climbs through a narrow mountain pass toward the gates of Hutaaka.

### Horses and Mules

In places the road is unsafe. There is a base chance of an animal falling, modified as follows:

| Condition | Modifier |
|-----------|:--------:|
| Mule | +2 |
| Horse | 0 |
| Carrying pack | -2 |
| Being ridden | -2 |
| No pack / unridden | 0 |
| Being led | +2 |

- **Ridden animal falls:** Rider may save vs. Wands to leap to safety. Fail = fall 1d3 x 10 feet to a ledge, taking 1d6 damage per 10'. Mount dies.
- **Led animal falls:** Leaders roll combined Str on 3d10 to save the animal; otherwise it dies.

Occasional streams cross the road. Some are bridged; others are eroded channels.

### VE7. Broken Road (Map VE7)

Sections of road have fallen away. Rough rocky sloping area above a 900+ foot drop. Characters cross safely. Animals fall on a modified roll of 1–4 on 1d20.

---

### VE8. Rockfall (Map VE8)

Large rocks and boulders on the road; small stones tumbling from the cliff above. Safe if dismounted and in single file. Otherwise: dangerous rockfall -- save vs. Wands or take 2d6 damage. Animals fall on a modified roll of 1–4 on 1d20.

---

### VE9. Narrow Road (Map VE9)

Road has collapsed to a 1–2' wide ledge. War horses can be led across. Other horses and mules require combined Str 20+ from leaders. Animals fall on a modified roll of 1–7 on 1d20.

## V2. The High Bridge

*(PDF p.43)*

A 20' wide arching bridge spans a 200' gorge. Crumbling, with pieces dropping away (but actually safe to cross). Halfway across: 2 griffons swoop from a side-gorge (200 yards away, 2 rounds to reach the bridge).

**Animal panic:** Griffon screeching causes riding horses and mules to panic (war horses unaffected).
- **Leading characters:** Roll Str on 3d10 or the beasts break free
- **Mounted characters:** Save vs. Wands or thrown from mount (1d6 damage)
- **Loose animals:** Rush for the far side

Griffons ignore adventurers and try to kill and carry off animals.

**Griffons** x2: AC 5 [14], HD 7 (hp 38, 23), Att 2 x claw / 1 x bite (1d4/1d4/2d8), THAC0 13 [+6], MV 120' (40') / 360' (120') flying, SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F4), ML 8, AL N, XP 450 each. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

## V3. Fallen Bridge (Map V3)

*(PDF p.43)*

A 200' wide gorge; the bridge has collapsed. Both sides have narrow ledges. Walls are rough (+10% thief climb bonus; non-thieves 10% standard chance). Roll per climb between ledges. Falls: 1d6 per 10' onto the ledge below. Roped from above: no fall damage.

### Giant Tuatara Lizard

At the bottom, in a cave. Retreats to its cave on failed morale; fights to the death there.

**Tuatara Lizard** x1: AC 4 [15], HD 6 (26hp), Att 2 x claw / 1 x bite (1d4/1d4/2d6), THAC0 14 [+5], MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F3), ML 6, AL N, XP 275. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

**Cave:** Noxious, filled with decayed animal carcasses. Searching requires save vs. Poison or contract disease (-1 Str and Con per week for 1d3 weeks).

### Mountain Rattler

Halfway up the far side. Hear: thief hear noise or 1 on 1d6. Avoid: thief move silently or 10% for others. If the avoidance roll fails, the snake attacks. Characters attacking must make a Dex check each round or fall.

**Rock Rattler** x1: AC 7 [12], HD 1* (3hp), Att 1 x bite (1 + poison: 1d4+1 extra damage), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 7, AL N, XP 13. See New Monsters.

## V4. Gateway to Hutaaka (Map V4)

*(PDF p.43)*

The only entrance to the Lost Valley. Mountains are impassable elsewhere. The road ends at a sheer drop above a 200' deep, 200' wide gorge. On the far side: a rock wall with a pair of large stone gates. Bridge pillars remain but spans have collapsed. Ledges on both faces make climbing easier (as V3).

### The Gates

15' high, 20' wide. Covered in geometric designs. Closed with heavy iron bars on the far side; they open outward. A 2' wide ledge at the foot of a 40' high wall.

Two 12' tall **rock living statues** with jackal heads stand inside. They attack anyone opening the gates or approaching within 20' unless shown a magical silver needle.

**Rock Living Statues** x2: AC 4 [15], HD 5** (hp 28, 36), Att 2 x magma squirts (2d6/2d6), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 60' (20'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 12, AL N, XP 300 each. See New Monsters.

### The Wall

Can be climbed by thieves (normal chance). Flat top, 10' wide. 12 ravens nest there -- they caw and swoop at climbers but do not attack. Flee if one is killed. The statues are visible from the top and easily avoided when descending.

**Ravens** x12: AC 7 [12], HD 1/2 (1–2hp), Att 1 x beak (1), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 12' (4') / 360' (120') flying, SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 5, AL N, XP 5 each.
