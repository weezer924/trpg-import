## Overview

*(PDF p.44)*

The Lost Valley of Hutaaka is the climax of the adventure. The valley is divided into two halves:

- **Eastern half:** Desolate, abandoned buildings, wild animals by day, undead by night.
- **Western half:** Two warring factions — the **Hutaakans** and the **Traldar** — locked in bitter conflict.

Both factions seek to recruit the adventurers to their cause. **Kartoeba**, the ancient temple guardian, stalks the valley at night.

### Warring Factions

The Hutaakans were driven from the **Temple of Pflarr** five months ago by the Traldar. However, the Traldar do not know the rites needed to restrain the temple's denizens. As a result, undead now roam the valley at night, and Kartoeba (the temple's guardian creature) is also loose.

Both groups speak a dialect of the common tongue. Each faction attempts to recruit the party through persuasion, bargaining, flattery, deceit, or bribery.

## Eastern Hutaaka — First Impressions

*(PDF p.44–45)*

A broad valley enclosed by sheer mountains. A faded ancient road runs through it, flanked by decaying stone buildings. No birds or insects can be heard. An unnatural silence pervades.

## Meeting the Inhabitants

*(PDF p.45–46)*

The party discovers **3 dead Traldar warriors** near a building. **14 Hutaakans** killed them and now hide behind nearby rocks.

Shortly after, **8 Traldar warriors + 1 vocal** arrive on foot-pad lizard mounts. They believe the party killed their kin and are hostile, but willing to talk first.

**Evidence of innocence:**
- PCs' weapons are unbloodied
- A Hutaakan spear protrudes from one body
- Claw wounds visible on 2 corpses
- Clawed footprints around the scene

**Outcomes:**
- If the party and Traldar fight: Hutaakans watch, then aid the newcomers.
- If the party and Traldar follow the tracks: Hutaakans ambush from above.
- Whichever faction befriends the party takes them to their village (H1 Byxata or H2 Traldar compound).

## Inhabitants

### Hutaakans

*(PDF p.46–47; Maps H, H1)*

**Population:** 247 total — 45 minor priests, 76 warriors, 126 others.
**Leader:** Priestess **Kforedz** (see `npcs.md`).
**Description:** Jackal-headed humanoids. Infravision 60'. Move silently as a thief of equivalent level.

**Fortified village Byxata (H1):** Stone wall with 25 guards on ramparts. ~20 buildings. Kforedz's dwelling (c) contains 5,000gp in jewellery and art objects.

**Attitudes:** Haughty, callous, dominated by priests of Pflarr. See themselves as civilised pacifists suffering from barbarian aggression. Call the Traldar "Wreckers." Will not mention their previous enslavement of the Traldar. Prefer ambush and missile attacks to melee.

### Traldar

*(PDF p.47–48; Map H2)*

**Population:** 254 total — 108 warriors, 18 vocals, 128 others.
**Leader:** Chief **Guri-ben-Kaal** (see `npcs.md`).
**Description:** Short, muscular humans, hairless except on the backs of hands. 53 giant foot-pad lizard mounts (war mounts and pack animals).

**Fortified compound (H2):** Near the Temple of Pflarr. 20 buildings surrounded by 15' stone wall and 10' ditch. Guri's 3-storey building (a) has 2 rooftop lookouts. Contains 7,500gp in looted Hutaakan artefacts.

**Attitudes:** Cruel, embittered. Present themselves as freedom-fighters. Call the Hutaakans "Mutts." Will not mention that the Hutaakans were once benevolent mentors. Aggressive and bloodthirsty. Fear the dark — barricade indoors at night.

## Western Hutaaka Events

### HE1. Hutaakan Ceremony

*(PDF p.48)*

14 minor priests + Kforedz attempt an all-night ceremony at a ruined shrine. No useful effect. Just after midnight, undead attack:

- **Skeletons** x12 + **Zombies** x6 (controlled pawns — turn as wraiths)

At dawn, Traldar raiders attack:

- **Traldar warriors** x8 + **vocal** x1 + **foot-pad lizard mounts** x9

### HE2. Old Enemy

A scout reports that "an outsider" (Golthar or Karllag, if alive) has contacted the opposing faction. Iron Ring associates may be with him. DM can develop encounters: lead an enemy war-party, enter the Temple, enter Kartoeba's lair. Possible dramatic death by Kartoeba.

### HE3. Enemy Prisoner

A raiding party returns with a captive from the opposing faction. The prisoner is locked in a hut. The party can sneak close to hear the other side's perspective. Eventually: cruel interrogation, mangled body dumped outside.

### HE4. Restless Dead

Nightly undead attacks on the settlements:

- **Traldar compound:** Attacked 0–3 times per night (1d4-1)
- **Hutaakan village:** Priests deter most attacks; 0–2 times per night (1d3-1)

**Attack types:**

| Type | Composition |
|------|-------------|
| Uncoordinated mob | 2d6+6 skeletons + 1d6+3 zombies |
| Controlled squad (pawns; turn as wraiths) | 1d8+4 skeletons + 1d4+2 zombies |
| Wights | 1d3 wights |

### HE5. Kartoeba Strikes

At night. Despite its size, Kartoeba moves silently, climbs sheer surfaces, and dissolves through wooden doors. The party may hear screams but the creature is gone by the time they arrive. Only a slime trail remains. Should convince the party to track it down.

## The Final Solution

*(PDF p.49)*

Both factions want to stop the temple creatures. Both methods are only effective against undead (not Kartoeba).

### Hutaakan Method

The Hutaakans need the **Knowledge of the Elders** (an ancient book) from the **Vault of the Elders (H3)**. They possess an inscribed rod (equivalent to a *knock* + *continual light* scroll) but cannot read the inscription.

### Traldar Method

The Traldar believe filling the temple altar bowl with water from the **Singing Pool (H4)** will work. The round trip takes a full day with no shelter en route. The Traldar are too afraid of the dark to make the journey.

---

### H3. Vault of the Elders

*(PDF p.49)*

Behind the altar: a magically locked stone trapdoor (*knock* spell opens it). Steps 5' wide, 30' long. Hutaakan priests flee if they see the guardian.

**Guardian:** A special ghoul wrapped in bandages (appears as a mummy).

- **Special Ghoul** x1: **AC** 5 [14], **HD** 5** (27hp), **Att** 2 x claw / 1 x bite (1d4/1d4/1d6 + paralysis), **THAC0** 15 [+4], **MV** 90' (30'), **SV** D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), **ML** 11, **AL** C, **XP** 425
  - **Fear effect:** Roll under Wis on 1d20 or -2 to hit / -1 damage.
  - Turn as wraith.

**Contents:**
- The book (*Knowledge of the Elders*) on a shelf — describes an hour-long ceremony at the main altar of the temple
- Ring of weakness
- Gold necklace (400gp)

---

### H4. The Singing Pool

*(PDF p.49–50)*

At the head of a narrow valley. A wide pool surrounded by natural stone pillars. Mirror-like surface. Wind through the pillars sounds like distant singing. Water is easily collected (a small amount fills the altar bowl).

**First night after leaving the pool:** Attacked by 2 rhagodessae.

- **Rhagodessa** x2: **AC** 5 [14], **HD** 4+2 (14hp), **Att** 1 x leg suckers / 1 x bite (auto-bite next round / 2d8), **THAC0** 15 [+4], **MV** 150' (50'), **SV** D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), **ML** 9, **AL** N, **XP** 125 each

**Nearby lair treasure:** 3 gold earrings (150gp each).

---

### HE6. Laying the Dead to Rest

*(PDF p.50)*

The allied faction wants access to the temple and asks the party to escort them.

**Hutaakan escort:** Kforedz + 2 minor priests. Know the temple layout (not secret doors). Will not carry weapons or fight inside.

**Traldar escort:** Guri-ben-Kaal + 6 warriors. Fully armed but no knowledge of the temple. Morale drops to 6 inside.

If the ceremony is successful: all undead in the valley collapse. Kartoeba and Kforedz's animated dead are unaffected. **500 XP each.**

---

### HE7. Trail of Kartoeba

*(PDF p.50)*

Kartoeba attacks increase in frequency. After one attack, a clear slime trail leads to a hidden tunnel entrance. Allies point out the opportunity to track the creature.

**Volunteers:** 5 Traldar warriors or 3 Hutaakan priests + 2 warriors.

---

### HE8. Peace in the Valley?

*(PDF p.50)*

After Kartoeba and the undead are dealt with, both factions turn on the party. Both covet the party's looted treasure. Mutual hatred prevents the factions from cooperating — each tries to deal with the party alone. Kforedz may use *animate dead*.

The action should be exciting but survivable. Foot-pad lizards are vital for transporting treasure out of the valley.

## Temple of Pflarr

*(PDF p.50–52; Map H5)*

### H5. Above Ground

Large areas are in ruins (earthquake ~50 years ago). The structure looks about to collapse. Rubble strewn outside. A hole in the roof leads to area H5b. Rotten wooden doors are barred from inside (open doors check to smash).

The **golden altar bowl** must be replaced for ceremonies — it was removed by the fleeing High Priestess (whose corpse is in area H5d).

All skeletons and zombies are initially **controlled pawns** of the special ghouls. If the ghouls are destroyed, the undead revert to mindless behaviour (still attack intruders).

---

### H5a. Vestibule

- **Skeletons** x12 + **Zombies** x6 guard the entrance
- Elaborate defaced frescoes on walls
- Empty ceremonial washstand platform

---

### H5b. Main Temple

50' high centre, 20' balcony along the south wall. A 30' tall jackal-headed statue stands on the north platform. **Black marble altar** with a shallow recess for the bowl.

**Secret door** behind the statue — only opened with the magical key from Kartoeba (a *knock* spell will not work).

**Pit and cage:** A man-sized metal cage hangs on a chain over a 180' deep pit. The pit is surrounded by a 10' wall of **blue flame** (2d6 damage to pass through). Cage lowered by a winch on the balcony. The pit opens into Kartoeba's lair (H6b). 3 *continual darkness* spells fill the pit. Sides are extremely slippery (-60% thief climb).

**Grey Oozes** x4 on pit walls — 1 emerges if the cage is lowered or anything dropped into the pit; others follow at 1d4 round intervals.

**Jade Living Statues** x4 (life-sized Hutaakan figures) attack if the altar or large statue is touched.

**Reinforcements:** 2 turns after the party enters, 8 skeletons + 3 zombies are sent by the ghouls from H5c.

---

### H5c. Robing Rooms

Vestments, capes, and cowls. The **western room** contains 3 special ghouls (temple variant: HD 4**, 18hp each; see Haunters stat block) who control the temple's undead. They only attack in self-defence or if the ceremony begins.

**Secret door** to H5e from the western room — magically trapped: unless the proper words are spoken, an electrical discharge deals 2d4 damage to all within 10' (Save vs. Wands for half).

---

### H5d. Secret Passage

The **High Priestess's corpse** lies in the doorway, clutching the **golden bowl**. She rises as a wraith when approached.

- **Wraith** x1: **AC** 3 [16], **HD** 4** (16hp), **Att** 1 x touch (1d6 + energy drain), **THAC0** 16 [+3], **MV** 120' (40'), **SV** D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F4), **ML** 11, **AL** C, **XP** 175

**Corpse loot:**
- *Plate mail armour +2*
- Gem-studded platinum Star of Pflarr (2,000gp)
- *Potion of levitation*
- Scroll: *cure serious wounds*, *raise dead*, *remove curse*

---

### H5e. Priests' Quarters

Mostly buried under rubble. Mundane furniture remains. The passage between the western rooms has a sagging ceiling — crawlway only. **50% chance per character** of 1d4 falling rock damage.

---

### H5f. Antechambers (x2)

Unlit. Each contains:

- **Zombies** x5 + **Skeletons** x8
- Silver ceremonial washstand (150gp each)
- Elaborate intact frescoes of religious ceremonies and Hutaakan history — **reveals the true history of the Hutaakan-Traldar relationship**

---

### H5g. Room of Records

Double doors from H5f — locked and trapped. Unless opened by Kforedz: all within 15' must Save vs. Spells or be **blinded for 2d6 turns**.

Contains complete records and history of the Hutaakan empire on shelves. *Read languages* required. 1d4+2 hours of study reveals the truth about the Hutaakans and Traldar.

---

### H5h. The Crypts

50' descent. Guarded by:

- **Skeletons** x8 + **Zombies** x4 + **Wights** x2

A large ornate sarcophagus stands in front of a secret door. If disturbed, a mummy attacks.

- **Mummy** x1: **AC** 3 [16], **HD** 5+1** (21hp), **Att** 1 x touch (1d12 + disease), **THAC0** 14 [+5], **MV** 60' (20'), **SV** D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), **ML** 12, **AL** C, **XP** 575

**Behind the secret door:** Passage leads to the Catacombs of Kartoeba.

**Inside the sarcophagus:**
- Jewelled orb (3,500gp)
- Pearl-encrusted sceptre (6,500gp)
- Silver needle with golden thread

## Catacombs of Kartoeba

*(PDF p.51–52; Map H6)*

Meandering dark slimy passages throughout the valley. Numerous concealed entrances. Only the tunnels near the temple are shown on Plan H6.

Without following Kartoeba's slime trail: the party becomes auto-lost, wandering 1d4+1 hours before emerging elsewhere.

**Passage description:** Rank, dark, stagnant oily water dripping from the ceiling. Strange echoes.

*Detect evil:* Results grow stronger near Kartoeba's lair.

### Random Encounters (1-in-4 per half-mile)

| Roll | Encounter |
|------|-----------|
| X | **Green Slime** on walls/ceiling; 1-in-6 to notice on ceiling |
| Y | **Ochre Jelly** oozes from a jackal-headed statue's mouth in a wall niche |
| Z | **Carrion Crawler** in a cavern |

---

### H6. Under the Temple

When the party first enters, Kartoeba is lurking at the point shown on the plan. It attempts to catch and devour the party, surprising from behind if possible.

During the fight, one adventurer notices an **engraved silver bar** embedded in Kartoeba near its maw — this is the **magical key** to H5i.

---

### H6a. Dark Pit

*Continual darkness* on a 20' deep pit, 20' across, with a 2' ledge on the east side. Falls deal 2d6 damage. Kartoeba's trail continues beyond.

---

### H6b. Kartoeba's Lair

Ankle-deep in slime. Vile odour. The pit from H5b is visible as a dark hole in the ceiling. Northern secret door held open by rubble.

---

### H6c. Observers' Gallery

Balcony 20' above the floor, protected by double-thickness iron bars. A passage leads to the crypts (H5h).

---

### H6d. Empty Cavern

Trapdoor with ladder leading up to H5i.
