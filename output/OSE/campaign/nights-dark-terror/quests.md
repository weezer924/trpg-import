## Main Quest Chain

### 1. Horse Escort

- **Quest-giver:** Stephan (in Kelven)
- **Objective:** Escort white horses from Sukiskyn to Rifllian for sale to the Callarii elves
- **Reward:** 100–160gp per character (negotiable)
- **Status:** Overtaken by events — siege interrupts, but horses are eventually sold at Rifllian
- **File:** `sukiskyn.md`, `journey-to-threshold.md`

### 2. Survive the Siege

- **Quest-giver:** (Circumstantial — party arrives during attack)
- **Objective:** Defend Sukiskyn through the night against Red-blade and Wolfskull goblin tribes
- **Reward:** Shelter, supplies, Pyotr's gratitude
- **File:** `sukiskyn.md`

### 3. Recover the Horses

- **Quest-giver:** Taras / Pyotr
- **Objective:** Track the Viper goblins who stole 41 horses; recover from bandit camp (W3)
- **Reward:** 20gp per horse recovered; free mounts from bandit stock
- **File:** `sukiskyn.md` (W2, W3)

### 4. Rescue Stephan

- **Quest-giver:** Pyotr (at the Muted Feast)
- **Objective:** Find and free Stephan from the Wolfskull goblins
- **Reward:** Half-share of white horse sale proceeds; Sukiskyn as base; equipment loan
- **Leads to:** Explore wilderness (W4–W17) → Wolfskull Lair (W16) → Babushka's Tale → Loshad's Bargain → Xitaqa
- **File:** `south-of-the-river.md`, `ruins-of-xitaqa.md`

### 5. Loshad's Bargain

- **Quest-giver:** Loshad (chevall, on the moor)
- **Objective:** Kill werewolves Bailakask and Kalkask (W17) within 4 nights
- **Reward:** Directions to the Ruins of Xitaqa
- **Conditions:** Party must also free their horses within 25 days
- **File:** `south-of-the-river.md` (W17)

### 6. The Tapestry's Secret

- **Quest-giver:** Stephan (after rescue from Xitaqa)
- **Objective:** Use the magical silver needle and golden thread on the Hutaakan tapestry at Sukiskyn
- **Reward:** Map to the Lost Valley of Hutaaka revealed
- **File:** `journey-to-threshold.md`

### 7. Journey to the Lost Valley

- **Quest-giver:** Stephan (self-motivated, party companion)
- **Objective:** Travel via Rifllian → Threshold → Black Peak Mountains → Lost Valley
- **Obstacles:** Iron Ring pursuit, Scange brigands, gnoll territory, mountain hazards
- **File:** `journey-to-threshold.md`, `threshold.md`, `black-peaks.md`

### 8. The Lost Valley

- **Quest-giver:** Hutaakans (Kforedz) or Traldar (Guri-ben-Kaal)
- **Objective:** Choose a faction, help them lay the temple undead to rest, track down Kartoeba
- **Reward:** Faction support, treasure from Temple of Pflarr (H5i)
- **Warning:** Both factions turn hostile once threats are eliminated
- **File:** `lost-valley.md`

## Side Quests

### Rescue the Slavers' Captives (WE7)

- **Quest-giver:** Loshad
- **Objective:** Free 34 slaves (19 humans + 15 Red-blade goblin slaves) and 11 horses from Iron Ring slaver camp
- **Reward:** Loshad's goodwill; freed prisoners can be escorted to Sukiskyn
- **File:** `journey-to-threshold.md`

### Retrieve the Elven Statue (W11)

- **Quest-giver:** Goriidel (Vyalia elf, Lake of Lost Dreams)
- **Objective:** Recover silvered-bronze statue stolen by Ilya pixies from the island
- **Reward:** 600gp in gems; 3 potions of healing (if mirror destroyed)
- **File:** `south-of-the-river.md`

### Kill the Shroud Spider (W12)

- **Quest-giver:** Vissaryon (gnome mine owner)
- **Objective:** Kill the shroud spider terrorizing the gold mine; rescue missing miners
- **Reward:** 800gp (half the mined gold); necklace worth 3000gp (if Tekaryon + dwarves rescued alive)
- **File:** `south-of-the-river.md`

### Golthar's Defeat

- **Quest-giver:** (Self-motivated — Golthar pursues the party)
- **Objective:** Eliminate Golthar and the Iron Ring threat throughout the adventure
- **Locations:** Xitaqa (X10–X12), Threshold (TE1–TE8), Lost Valley (HE2)
- **File:** `ruins-of-xitaqa.md`, `threshold.md`, `lost-valley.md`

### Vault of the Elders (H3)

- **Quest-giver:** Kforedz (Hutaakan priestess)
- **Objective:** Retrieve the Knowledge of the Elders from the sealed vault
- **Reward:** Hutaakan alliance; ceremony to lay undead to rest
- **File:** `lost-valley.md`

### The Singing Pool (H4)

- **Quest-giver:** Guri-ben-Kaal (Traldar chief)
- **Objective:** Collect water from the Singing Pool to fill the temple altar bowl
- **Reward:** Traldar alliance; alternative ceremony to lay undead to rest
- **File:** `lost-valley.md`

## Further Adventures

*(PDF p.53–54)*

- **Death's Head Gnolls:** Return journey through gnoll territory; possible pursuit
- **Sukiskyn Calls:** Iron Ring has burnt Sukiskyn and abducted the clan; clue left behind
- **Threshold Base:** Elaborate town as ongoing base; explore ruins north of town
- **The House:** Merchant offers cheap house (3000gp) — infested, possibly an Iron Ring trap
- **Revenge:** Relatives of dead hunters (VE2) ask party to avenge them in gnoll lands
- **The Hutaakans:** Other Hutaakan ruins/settlements in Karameikos or beyond
- **To the Aid of the Gnomes:** Wufwolde Hills gnomes offer gold for help against goblins
- **Iron Ring Revenge:** The Ring doesn't forget — surviving members pursue the party; may lead to Black Eagle Barony
