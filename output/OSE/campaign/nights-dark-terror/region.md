## Adventure Background

*(PDF p.4–6)*

The adventure unfolds in the wild lands of the **Grand Duchy of Karameikos** (Expert rulebook Map #1). A river journey takes the adventurers to the besieged steading of **Sukiskyn**, where goblin hordes assail the walls throughout the long night. But this is only the beginning: soon the adventurers find themselves in a life-or-death struggle against the evil of the nefarious **Iron Ring**, a struggle which leads them ultimately to confront the ancient perils of the **Lost Valley of Hutaaka**.

### Getting Started

The module begins in **Kelven**, a thriving frontier town where the rivers Windrush, Shutturga, and Volaga meet. Much timber passes through Kelven on its way to Specularum. **Stephan** (Pyotr's brother) approaches the party with an offer of work: escort a herd of white horses from Sukiskyn to Rifllian, where the Callarii elves will pay a high price (the white horse is their tribal symbol).

- **Pay:** 100gp per character (can be bargained up to 160gp)
- **Transport:** Riverboat of trader **Kalanos**, departing Moldain the 7th of Thaumont
- **Destination:** Misha's Ferry, within a day's march of Sukiskyn

## Ancient History

*(PDF p.4–5)*

The roots of this adventure stretch back many millennia to the days when the **Hutaakan Empire** extended over the lands now covered by northern and eastern Karameikos.

The **Hutaakans**, a race of jackal-headed humanoids, took their name from Hutaaka, the valley in the Black Peak mountains where they originated. They were a civilised race, obsessed with cultural and religious pursuits, but also well versed in crafts and trade. Their conquest of the inhabitants was achieved through economic and cultural superiority rather than military force. Thus they achieved dominance over a primitive human tribe, the **Traldar**, who were content to trade their labour for the benefits of Hutaakan culture and craft.

The valley remained the site of their most important temple to the deity **Pflarr**. As power grew, the valley of Hutaaka developed into a religious centre inhabited only by clerics of Pflarr and their servants.

When the lowlands were invaded by **gnoll hordes**, the Hutaakans were ill-prepared. The remnants withdrew into the sanctuary of Hutaaka. Hidden within the Lost Valley, they came to terms with an increasingly spartan lifestyle, growing ever more introverted.

Isolation had a different effect on the Traldar who accompanied them. The harsh conditions meant the Traldar were forced to work ever harder while receiving fewer benefits. Eventually, the Traldar rose up against their masters in a bloody revolt. Years of bitter inter-racial warfare followed. Today sporadic fighting still occurs amongst the ruins as the Traldar and Hutaakans continue their struggle.

## The Hutaakan Tapestries

*(PDF p.5)*

When the edict was passed restricting access to Hutaaka, the High Priestess created **magical tapestries** which both recorded and concealed the location of the valley. By themselves, the tapestries showed only intricate but meaningless patterns. The "keys" were **magical silver needles**, each with a length of fine golden thread attached. Touching a needle to a tapestry caused it to weave through the cloth, producing a clear map to Hutaaka. Touching the needle again would pull the thread out, leaving no trace.

The tapestries were entrusted to high clerics in the lowlands, and over the years, each was buried with the tapestry as her symbol of office.

## The Iron Ring

*(PDF p.5–6)*

The **Iron Ring** is a secret organisation controlling the slave trade in Karameikos and beyond. Its interests extend to any scheme that makes money and increases the Ring's power. From **Black Eagle Barony**, the Ring's Masters watch over their secret empire.

**Hierarchy:**
- **Masters:** The ruling elite, avoiding danger personally
- **Reavers:** Underlings responsible for day-to-day operations
- **Hounds:** Former slaves, minds twisted by torture and brainwashing, fanatically loyal warriors

**Identification:** All members bear a brand on the left forearm — two manacles linked by a chain. Hounds also bear manacle scars on wrists and ankles. Members recognise each other by codes and passwords.

### Golthar's Quest

An ancient manuscript arrived at Black Eagle Barony, taken from a captured elf. **Golthar**, one of the Ring's lesser Masters (a magic-user), discovered it told of the ancient Hutaakan civilization and its vast wealth.

Golthar found **Xitaqa** (the ancient Hutaakan settlement) and took up residence with his hobgoblin aides. In a crypt, he found a sacred tapestry with silver needle and gold thread. But before the map could fully form, gelatinous cubes attacked. Golthar grabbed the tapestry and fled — the ancient fibres crumbled in his hands. Only the magical silver needle and golden thread remained.

A captured traveller revealed that another golden tapestry was hung in one of the settlements east of Kelven — but which one? Golthar sent his hobgoblin lieutenants to rally the goblin tribes of the Dymrak forest and lead them against all settlements. No homestead was to be spared. Those not killed were to be captured as slaves for Black Eagle Barony.

### Hutaakans & Traldar (Lost Valley)

- **Hutaakans:** Jackal-headed humanoids, remnants of an ancient civilisation, led by Priestess **Kforedz**. Aloof, manipulative, will animate dead against the Traldar.
- **Traldar:** Degenerate human tribe descended from Hutaakan servants, led by Chief **Guri-ben-Kaal**. Barbaric but shrewd, hostile to Hutaakans.
- Both factions seek to recruit the adventurers against the other.

## Travel Rules

*(PDF p.3–4)*

### Movement

- **Wilderness scale:** Movement rates read as yards, not feet (e.g. MV 120' = 120 yards/turn outdoors)
- **Missile & spell ranges:** Read as yards in wilderness; spell areas of effect remain in feet
- **Daily travel:** ~18 miles per day on forest tracks (Misha's Ferry to Sukiskyn = 1 full day)
- **Terrain modifiers:** Forest trails meander; mountain travel is slower

### Weather Effects

*(Weather table on Pull-out Sheet VII — day-by-day from Thaumont 7 onward)*

| Condition | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Rain | Visibility max 400 yards; 2 consecutive days = downpour for movement |
| Downpour | Visibility 100 yards; off-road movement -25%; 2+ days = -50% |
| Rain/Downpour | Unprotected bow/crossbow strings become damp, cannot be used until dried |
| Wind | All missile ranges increased by one band (short→medium, medium→long, no long range) |
| Gale | Missiles limited to short range only, fired at -1 to hit |

### Recovering Hit Points

Characters may rest to regain lost hit points: 1 hp per full day of rest with no energetic activity. Must be in a relatively safe location (Sukiskyn after siege, Rifllian, Threshold).

### Awarding Experience Points

Only award XP when characters are in a relatively safe place. No XP for killing NPCs/monsters with no listed xp value.

### Bargaining

*(Pull-out Sheet VII)*

Common in Karameikos. Buyer/seller start with outrageous prices, then negotiate. NPC's initial offer = 1/3 base (if buying from PCs) or 2× base (if selling). Roll 2d6 + Charisma modifier:

| Roll | Max Offer (buying from PCs) | Min Price (selling to PCs) |
|------|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| 2 | 40% | 160% |
| 3–5 | 60% | 140% |
| 6–7 | 80% | 120% |
| 8–10 | 100% | 100% |
| 11–12 | 120% | 80% |
| 13+ | 140% | 60% |

## Adventure Flow

The adventure follows a roughly linear progression, with increasing player freedom:

1. **Kelven** — Party hired by Stephan, travels upriver on Kalanos' boat
2. **River Ambush** — Iron Ring squad attacks the boat (chain across river)
3. **Misha's Ferry (W1)** — First stop; Misha is dead (killed by Vlack's vampire bats); wounded cave bear attacks
4. **Siege at Sukiskyn** — Party arrives at dusk to find homestead under attack by Red-blade and Wolfskull goblins; Viper tribe has stolen the horses. Survive the night siege → See `sukiskyn.md`
5. **After the Siege** — Refugees arrive from Ilyakana lumber camp; Stephan has been captured by goblins; Pyotr asks party to rescue him
6. **South of the River** — Explore goblin lairs to find Stephan; encounter wilderness locations; recover horses; deal with Wolfskull tribe → See `south-of-the-river.md`
7. **Ruins of Xitaqa** — Trail leads to ancient Hutaakan ruins where Golthar has his base; rescue Stephan; find the tapestry → See `ruins-of-xitaqa.md`
8. **Journey to Threshold** — Travel west with the tapestry; Iron Ring pursuit; stop at Rifllian (elf village) and encounter various events → See `journey-to-threshold.md`
9. **Threshold** — Major town; Golthar's agents try to steal tapestry/kill party; Fogor Isle infiltration → See `threshold.md`
10. **Towards the Black Peaks** — Mountain travel through gnoll territory; Foamfire Valley → See `black-peaks.md`
11. **The Lost Valley of Hutaaka** — Discover the ancient valley; navigate between Hutaakan and Traldar factions; explore Temple of Pflarr; face Kartoeba → See `lost-valley.md`
12. **Further Adventures** — Return journey through gnoll territory; Sukiskyn destroyed by Iron Ring; hooks for future campaigns
