## General Features

*(PDF p.25–26)*

Xitaqa (pronounced "zit-AH-ka") is an ancient Hutaakan settlement built in a knot of winding, steep-sided gullies eroded into the escarpment above the Volaga river. Apart from the dominating tower, ruins are single-storey, windowless dressed stone blocks cut into bedrock.

**Inhabitants:**
- **Yellow-fang (Faz-plak) goblin clan** — headquarters in X1–3, others scattered in minor buildings (G)
- **Rock baboons** — several wild troops; some trained as goblin slaves
- **Golthar** (Iron Ring Master) + Bloodhead hobgoblins + Iron Ring warriors — in the tower (X5–X12)

Goblins will not enter the tower under any circumstances (Golthar's orders).

## River Crossing

*(PDF p.25)*

Upstream from Sielo (W5), the Volaga can be crossed at a ford, by swimming, or on horseback. Between the river and Xitaqa's escarpment is a level plain.

An Iron Ring patrol sees the party ~half a mile away and gallops to attack, firing one arrow volley before closing to melee.

- **Reaver** ×1: AC 4 [15] (chain mail & shield), F3, hp 17, Att 1 × sword (1d8+2, inc. S bonus), THAC0 17 [+2] (inc. S bonus), MV 90' (30') / 240' (80') mounted, SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F3), ML 10, AL N, XP 50. **Treasure:** 50gp.
- **Hounds** ×7: AC 7 [12] (leather), HD 1+1 (6hp), Att 1 × short sword or 1 × arrow (1d6), THAC0 16 [+3] (inc. ferocity bonus), MV 120' (40') / 240' (80') mounted, SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 12, AL N, XP 19 each. R 50/100/150. No treasure; no information if captured.
- **Riding Horses** ×8: AC 7 [12], HD 2 (9hp), MV 240' (80'), Att nil when ridden, SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 7, AL N.

## Outer Ruins

*(PDF p.26)*

### The Streets of Xitaqa (gully route)

Along the gully floor, overgrown cobbles. Party encounters a patrol:

- **Yellow-fang goblins** ×6 + **slave rock baboons** ×5

If PCs hide at once: ambush (surprise 1–4). Otherwise: mutual detection and attack.

### The High Ground (escarpment route)

Attacked by 5 giant bats as they approach the tower.

- **Giant Bats** ×5: AC 6 [13], HD 2 (7hp), Att 1 × bite (1d4), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 180' (60') flying, SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL N, XP 20 each.

### Denizens of the Outer Ruins (Stats)

- **Plak** (goblin king): AC 6 [13], HD 3 (15hp), Att 1 × sword +2 (1d8+2; casts *cure light wounds* 1/day, command word "silmar" engraved on blade), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F3), ML 10, AL C, XP 35. **Treasure:** 50gp, gold armbands ×2 (120gp each).
- **Yellow-fang bodyguards:** AC 6 [13], HD 2 (9hp), Att 1 × sword or 1 × arrow (short bow) (1d8 or 1d6), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 10, AL C, XP 20 each. R 50/100/150. **Treasure:** 35gp each.
- **Yellow-fang goblins:** AC 6 [13], HD 1-1 (4hp), Att 1 × short sword or 1 × arrow (short bow) (1d6), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 90' (30'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 8 (10 with leader), AL C, XP 5 each. R 50/100/150. **Treasure:** 15gp each. All wear iron collars and arm-bands.
- **Rock baboons** (slave): AC 6 [13], HD 2 (8hp), Att 1 × club / 1 × bite (1d6/1d3), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 8, AL N, XP 20 each. Wear iron collars.

### Minor Buildings

Unless PCs enter a building or make presence known, occupants won't notice them.

- **B — Wild Baboon Lairs:** 70% chance of 2d4 wild rock baboons. No treasure.
- **G — Goblin Lair:** 80% chance of 1d4+1 Yellow-fang goblins, 50% chance of 1d4 slave baboons. Bedding, hearths, cooking pots, food.
- **S — Stables:** Horses for the Reaver and Hounds who serve Golthar.
- **E — Empty.**

## Yellow-fang Headquarters (X1–X3)

*(PDF p.26–27)*

Larger than most ruins. Yellow-fang ruined tower motif scratched on doors. Original Hutaakan decorations defaced. Smoky from floor-level hearths.

### X1 & X3. Front and Rear Lobbies

8 ordinary goblin guards lounging in each. Combat noise from either lobby summons goblins from the hall (X2) after 5 rounds, and from the other lobby 5 rounds after that.

### X2. Hall

Wooden platform for goblin king **Plak**, who watches 5 ordinary goblins + 4 bodyguards bet on cockroach races. 200gp in coins on the table.

**Clan trophies** hanging below Plak's platform: 10 human skulls in rusty helmets, tattered flag (worthless), decorated hunting-horn (60gp), jewelled spurs (250gp each), beautiful goblet (350gp).

## Golthar's Tower

*(PDF p.27–28)*

Characters climbing the tower exterior are attacked by 3 giant bats. No entry from the top.

### X4. Plaza

Overgrown plaza before the tower building.

### X5. Entrance Hall

Impressive bronze doors (20' high each), central door open. 5 Bloodhead hobgoblin guards notice and attack with crossbows.

- **Bloodhead Hobgoblins** ×5: AC 6 [13], HD 1+1 (6hp), Att 1 × sword or 1 × crossbow (1d8 or 1d6), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL C, XP 15 each. R 60/120/180. **Treasure:** 25gp each.

Splendid mosaic floor, painted plaster walls and ceiling, rough wooden furniture.

### X6. Crypt

Appears empty — alcoves cleaned out. Two **gelatinous cubes** hide in alcoves marked g, waiting to attack from both sides.

- **Gelatinous Cubes** ×2: AC 8 [11], HD 4 (18hp), Att 1 × touch (2d4 + paralysis 2d4 turns, Save vs. Paralysis negates), THAC0 16 [+3], MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 12, AL N, XP 125 each. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`. **Treasure:** 3 gems (330gp total) inside the cubes.

### X7. Old Library

Mouldering scrolls and books coated in thick webs. Two giant spiders — one attacks on entry, the other hides with brood in nest (marked s), attacks only if approached.

- **Giant Black Widow Spiders** ×2: AC 6 [13], HD 3* (14hp), Att 1 × bite (2d6 + poison: Save vs. Poison or suffer 4d4 extra damage), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 60' (20') / 120' (40') in web, SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 8, AL N, XP 50 each. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

**Treasure:** Ebony writing set inlaid with ivory (370gp) in spider's nest. Books/scrolls in Hutaakan (read languages spell needed): innumerable births, marriages, deaths.

### X8. Vlack's Quarters

Marble walls, tiled floor, dusty jackal-headed statues, dried-up fountain filled with rubbish. Curtained alcoves.

One **ice wolf** hides in a curtained alcove, ready to attack. If Vlack escaped from WE5, he and his ice wolf are in the other alcove.

- **Ice Wolf** ×1 (or ×2 if Vlack escaped): AC 4 [15], HD 4+1** (23hp), Att 1 × bite or 1 × frost breath (1d10 or 4d4), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 150' (50'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 10, AL C, XP 275. See New Monsters.
- **Vlack** (if present): See `south-of-the-river.md` WE5.

**Treasure:** 10 small gold ingots worth 150gp each hidden in fountain litter.

### X9. Dormitory

Empty dormitory for Iron Ring Reaver and Hounds (encountered at river crossing). 8 beds with polished iron manacles hanging above each (symbols of loyalty, not for wearing).

**Reaver's locker** (locked, not trapped): 150gp, silver belt (75gp), leather armour, sword, short bow, 15 arrows — all taken from **Stephan** (found in X10).

### X10. "Empty Room"

*(PDF p.27–28)*

Everything in this room (including internal glass walls) is **invisible**. Creatures/objects become invisible on entering, visible again on leaving. Unlike *invisibility* spell, this doesn't end when attacking or casting.

**Golthar** is interrogating **Stephan** (bound) in the central cubicle (a), using an invisible **minotaur** to threaten him. Golthar cast *detect invisible* 2 turns earlier.

When party enters: Golthar flees through the far door (gains surprise via invisibility), briefly becoming visible in the doorway. Stephan warns the party as the minotaur attacks.

**Note:** Adventurers are invisible to each other while inside.

- **Minotaur** ×1: AC 0 [19] (4 [15] if visible, leather armour), HD 6 (26hp), Att 1 × sword +1 (1d8+3), THAC0 14 [+5], MV 120' (40'), SV D8 W9 P10 B11 S12 (F6), ML 12, AL C, XP 275. Sword +1 grants *detect invisible* 1/day (60' range). See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Stephan** (rescued): AC 8 [11], F5, hp 13 (26 normal), Att unarmed (+1 with any melee weapon), THAC0 16 [+3], MV 120' (40'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 10. Torn blue robe, bruised and haggard, bound. Full stats: See `npcs.md`.

### X11. Art Gallery

*(PDF p.28)*

Door from below **locked** by Golthar. Open by thief, *knock* spell, or force (AC 9, 35hp damage).

Flaking portraits, two pink jackal-headed sculptures. **Paintings are trapped:** Hacking through canvas: 1d8 electrical damage (Save vs. Spells for half) + stunned 1d3 rounds (no save).

Portraits a–d have small peepholes allowing anyone in the outer corridor to see and cast spells into the gallery. **Golthar** hides behind portrait d, waits for an opportunity, then attacks with an offensive spell and activates the sculptures (**jade living statues**).

While statues attack, Golthar moves between peepholes casting spells. If he exhausts offensive spells, the party defeats the statues, breaks through a portrait, or seems likely to find the secret door — Golthar runs upstairs to X12.

- **Jade Living Statues** ×2: AC 4 [15], HD 3+1** (16hp), Att 2 × hands (1d6/1d6), THAC0 16 [+3], MV 60' (20'), SV D4 W5 P6 B7 S8 (F10), ML 12, AL N, XP 100 each. Immune to sleep, charm. Magic resistant (save as F10). Magical weapons have no bonuses to hit or damage. Crumble to worthless powder when destroyed. See New Monsters.
- **Golthar** (Iron Ring Master): AC 4 [15] (ring of protection +2), M6, hp 17, THAC0 14 [+5], MV 120' (40'). **Spells:** 1st — *magic missile* (3 missiles), *shield*, *sleep*; 2nd — *detect invisible*, *web*; 3rd — *fly*, *hold person*. Full stats: See `npcs.md`.

### X12. Golthar's Room

*(PDF p.28)*

Golthar casts *fly*, triggers an explosive device blasting a hole in the wall, grabs his spellbook, flies out, and casts *mirror image* (4 yellow-robed figures flying away).

**Furnishings:** Dragon-jaw bed (badly damaged by explosion), green-stained oak triangular table, two orange humanoid-shaped chairs. Wall map showing positions of ruined homesteads.

**Treasure** (iron-bound chest, locked, untrapped):
- 2 large sacks: 5555gp each (11,110gp total)
- Potion of delusion
- Amulet vs. crystal balls & ESP
- Jewelled silver casket (1400gp) with 15 gems (1×5000gp, 4×1000gp, 10×500gp)

**Ebony box:** The **magical silver needle** and **golden thread** — used to activate the Hutaakan tapestry at Sukiskyn.

**Carved ivory scroll case** (not trapped): Ancient Hutaakan scroll (fragile). Read by *read languages* or thief level 4+ (80% chance):

> *"I, Baistqet, high priestess, do command your return to Hutaaka. The stars have changed and our power wanes. Strange creatures harry us from the mountains and our people grow restless. We are stretched too thin. Bring the treasures of the temples and the people of the lowlands to feed the one. The sacred tapestries and your silver needles show the path."*

## Stephan's Tale

*(PDF p.28)*

Once free, Stephan first asks about his relatives at Sukiskyn. Then:

- Captured during the goblin attack on Ilyakana; taken to Wolfskull lair, then to Xitaqa
- **Golthar** interrogated prisoners, demanding to know where the "golden tapestries" were hidden
- Stephan realised the tapestry in Sukiskyn's hall (S4) was what Golthar sought, but would not divulge this
- Other prisoners didn't know; they were taken elsewhere (Stephan doesn't know where)
- From Golthar's questions, Stephan worked out the secret of the tapestry and the riches it might lead to
- **Now Stephan is obsessed** with finding the needle/thread and unlocking the tapestry's secret

**Next steps:** Return to Sukiskyn, use the needle and thread on the tapestry to reveal the map to the Lost Valley. Then journey to Threshold (the nearest town with supplies for an expedition) before heading into the Black Peak Mountains. See `journey-to-threshold.md`.
