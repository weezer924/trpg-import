## General Features

*(PDF p.14–15)*

This section covers wilderness exploration east of Sukiskyn. The party seeks Pyotr's brother Stephan and his Wolfskull captors. Four elements shape the adventure:

1. **Fixed Encounters (W4–W17)** — no set order; DM controls discovery via clues and information
2. **Wilderness Events (WE1–WE5)** — added in any order at DM's discretion
3. **Optional Encounters (Pull-out Sheet VIII)** — random or as desired
4. **Weather (Pull-out Sheet VII)** — realistic backdrop

**Key constraint:** The Wolfskull lair location (W16) should not be discovered until other exploration possibilities are exhausted. Finding it opens the path to Xitaqa and later sections.

## Wilderness Events

*(PDF p.14–15)*

### WE1. Lucky Find

The party finds a dead human trapper and a dead Red-blade goblin — killed by each other's swords. A pack mule grazes nearby.

- **Trapper's gear:** Leather armour, short sword, longbow, arrows, 38gp
- **Goblin:** Short sword, 23gp
- **Mule** (unharmed): hp 10. Pack contains: pelts (bear, beaver, etc. — total 350gp), one-man tent, bed-roll, dried food, cloak, 40 arrows, 50' rope, potion of healing.

### WE2. Lost Item

A character notices a valuable item (DM's choice) has been lost from pack or saddle-bag. For each hour backtracking (up to 6 hours, half speed), 20% chance of finding it. Do not lose vital items.

### WE3. Lame Horse

A horse catches its foot in a rabbit hole, losing a shoe and injuring its leg. Healed by 2–4 days rest or *cure light wounds*. Until healed: ¼ normal MV. Even if healed, ½ normal MV until reshod by a blacksmith (e.g. Yuri at Sukiskyn).

### WE4. Horses' Friend

*(Not in forest)*

A superb black stallion and three horses graze a few hundred yards away. The stallion is **Loshad**, a chevall in horse form, checking on the PCs' horses' welfare. If chased or attacked, Loshad and horses flee.

If the PCs' horses tell Loshad they've been maltreated: Loshad follows the party until nightfall, then in centaur form (invisible) attempts to free the mistreated horses. If forced to flee, takes stallion form. Freed or unfree, he commands maltreated horses not to cooperate — they become too wild to ride. Only **Alfana** (at Sukiskyn) can re-tame them (1d4+1 weeks).

- **Loshad** (centaur form): AC 5 [14] (1 [18] if invisible), HD 7* (46hp), Att 2 × hooves / 1 × arrow (1d6/1d6/1d6), THAC0 13 [+6], MV 180' (60'), SV D8 W9 P10 B13 S12 (F7), ML 9, AL N, XP 850. R 50/100/150. Gear: ring of invisibility (earring), short bow.
- **Loshad** (stallion form): AC 2 [17], MV 270' (90'), Att 2 × hooves / 1 × bite (1d6/1d6/1d4), ML 11.
- **Wild horse bodyguards** ×3: AC 7 [12], HD 3 (14hp), Att 2 × hooves (1d6/1d6), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 240' (80'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 9, AL N, XP 35 each.

### WE5. Vlack's Retinue

Vlack, fearing another Sukiskyn attack, heads to report to Golthar. His path crosses the party's. Giant vampire bats (scouts) attack first; Vlack arrives 4 rounds later on his ice wolf.

- **Giant Vampire Bats** ×5: AC 6 [13], HD 2* (7hp), Att 1 × bite (1d4 + paralysis), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 180' (60') flying, SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL N, XP 25 each. Bitten: Save vs. Paralysis or unconscious 1d10 rounds.
- **Vlack** (hobgoblin king): AC 3 [16] (chain mail +2), HD 5 (22hp), Att 1 × two-handed sword +1 (1d10+3), THAC0 14 [+5], MV 90' (30') / 150' (50') mounted, SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 10, AL C, XP 175. **Treasure:** 35gp, pouch with 3 gems (75gp each), garnet in sword hilt (500gp).
- **Ice Wolf** ×1: AC 4 [15], HD 4+1** (23hp), Att 1 × bite or 1 × frost breath (1d10 or 4d4), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 150' (50'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 10, AL C, XP 275. Breath: 15' range, auto-hit, 1d4 per full HD (4d4), Save vs. Dragon Breath for half. Immune to normal cold, half damage from magical cold. Fire: +1 per die of damage. See New Monsters.
- **Bloodhead hobgoblins** ×4: AC 6 [13], HD 1+1 (6hp), Att 1 × sword (1d8), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 10 (8 without Vlack), AL C, XP 15 each. **Treasure:** 2d10gp each.

If reduced to ≤7hp, Vlack flees on ice wolf. If he escapes, encountered again at Xitaqa (X7).

## Scenes of Destruction

*(PDF p.16)*

### W4. Ilyakana (No Map)

Lumber camp attacked on the night of 7th Thaumont. Most killed, Kalanos' riverboat sunk. Gregor and three others escaped to Sukiskyn. **Kalanos** hid in a tree and witnessed the massacre — his mind is affected; he goes berserk at the sight of any armed creature.

When PCs arrive: Kalanos wanders among corpses, armed. When he sees them, he flees into the woods screaming. He shoots arrows at pursuers, fights maniacally if cornered. If ignored, follows and fires arrows. If captured alive, madness can be cured by rest.

- **Kalanos** (berserk): AC 7 [12], F5, hp 22, Att 1 × battle axe or 1 × arrow (longbow) (1d8 or 1d6), THAC0 15 [+4] (inc. berserk +2), MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 12, AL N. R 70/140/210. Bonuses: +4 vs. mind-affecting spells (insanity), +2 to hit (berserk). Leather armour, 2 gold arm bands (60gp each).

### W5. Sielo (No Map)

Lumber camp on Volaga river bank. Attacked and destroyed by Wolfskull and Viper goblins on 6th Thaumont.

### W6–W8. Ruined Homesteads (No Map)

- **Cherkass (W6):** Attacked on 4th Thaumont
- **Hokol (W7):** Attacked on 5th Thaumont
- **Segenyev (W8):** Overrun by Wolfskulls the night after the Sukiskyn attack. Smoke visible up to 9 miles (3 hexes) on 10th Thaumont.

## Goblin Lairs

### W9. Red-blade Lair (Map W9)

*(PDF p.15–16)*

With Gnhass killed during the Sukiskyn siege, the disorganised Red-blade remnants abandoned their lair. Tunnels and chambers are 10' high. Entrance at the foot of a wooded slope.

**Rats!** At DM's discretion while in the lair:

- **Giant Rats** ×12: AC 7 [12], HD ½ (2hp), Att 1 × bite (1d3 + disease: roll 1 on 1d20 = ill for 1 week), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 8, AL N, XP 5 each. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

#### W9a. Guardroom

Large wooden door daubed with a bloodied sword (Red-blade symbol), slightly ajar. A flock of stirges has moved in; they attack only if the party enters.

- **Stirges** ×6: AC 7 [12], HD 1* (5hp), Att 1 × proboscis (1d3, then automatic 1d3/round), THAC0 17 [+2] (first attack, then 19 [+0]), MV 30' (10') / 180' (60') flying, SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 9, AL N, XP 13 each. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

#### W9b. Strongroom

Iron-bound oak door. Locked (not trapped). Thieves have +10% to pick. Takes 3 turns to hack through. Inside: Gnhass' "guard-dogs."

- **Giant Shrews** ×6: AC 4 [15], HD 1* (4hp), Att 2 × bite (1d6/1d6), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 180' (60'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 12, AL N, XP 13 each. If warned, surprise on 1–4. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

**Treasure:**
- Ornate tapestry of red dragon (120gp)
- 5 tall terracotta jars of sour red wine
- Large "ruby" on cushion (apparent value 1000gp — actually glass explosive)
- Yew wood chest bound with copper (locked, trapped +10% detect). **Trap:** If triggered/moved/forced, the glass "ruby" explodes: 2d6 damage within 10', Save vs. Dragon Breath at +2 for half (no save if holding gem). Chest contains: 550gp, leather pouch with 3 gems (200gp + 2×75gp), golden amulet (300gp), potion of diminution.

### W10. Viper Lair (Map W10)

*(PDF p.17)*

After defeat at W2, the Viper survivors fled here under a bodyguard leader. Barricaded inside with escape route. Tunnels 10' high.

#### W10a. Entrance

Wooden door closed and barred from inside. Brightly-painted wooden snake (clan symbol) lies behind the door.

#### W10b. Barricade

Tunnel blocked by a man-high barricade of rocks and rubbish. 7 Viper goblins man it (-3 AC bonus vs. missiles). Retreat to area c if 4 killed or morale fails.

- **Viper Goblins** ×7: AC 6 [13] (3 [16] behind barricade), HD 1-1 (3hp), Att 1 × war hammer or 1 × thrown dagger (1d6 or 1d4), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 90' (30'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 6, AL C, XP 5 each. **Treasure:** 2 daggers, war hammer, 1d4gp each.

#### W10c. "Last Stand"

Goblin leader + 9 goblins + survivors from barricade wait here with +2 initiative bonus (first round). Chamber lit by two smoking braziers; appears full of snakes (mostly stuffed/carved). The leader throws live snakes!

- **Viper Goblin Leader** ×1: AC 6 [13], HD 2 (11hp), Att 1 × war hammer or 1 × thrown dagger (1d6 or 1d4), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 6, AL C, XP 20. **Treasure:** 55gp, ornate snakeskin belt inlaid with gold and gems (500gp).
- **Viper Goblins** ×9: AC 6 [13], HD 1-1 (3hp), Att 1 × war hammer or 1 × thrown dagger (1d6 or 1d4), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 90' (30'), SV D14 W15 P16 B17 S18 (NM), ML 6, AL C, XP 5 each. **Treasure:** 1d4gp each.
- **Small Rock Pythons** ×3: AC 6 [13], HD 3* (11hp), Att 1 × bite / 1 × squeeze (1d2/1d8), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 8, AL N, XP 50 each. Thrown as weapon (range 20/40/60). If hit: automatically coils and constricts 1d8/round until killed.

**Hidden treasure** (inside a large carved snake): 3 gems (100gp each), small pearl on gold chain (275gp). **Casket** (locked, not trapped): 50pp, potion of strength, potion of animal control, potion of clairvoyance.

Retreat at half killed or failed morale → flee via escape tunnel (d).

#### W10d. Escape Tunnel

Halfway down: crude pit trap (50% notice, 70% for dwarves; -30% if hurrying). Falling: 1d8 damage.

## W11. Lake of Lost Dreams

*(PDF p.16–18)*

Mist-shrouded lake with a barely-visible island. Five **Vyalia elves** (led by Goriidel, E3) gaze at the island.

**Elves' story:**
- Members of the Vyalia clan from the SE forest
- The island is taboo — believed haunted
- Ilya pixies moved in months ago, began harassing elves
- Pixies stole a silvered-bronze statue the elves got from gnomish/dwarven miners
- Goriidel offers 600gp in gems to retrieve the statue

### The Black Mirror

The island radiates magic turning lawful/neutral creatures chaotic (no save). Elves become chaotic 1 turn after arrival. Other races: days equal to half Wisdom (rounded down). During transition: disturbing dreams, distorted self-images. The effect radiates from a **black mirror** in area f. If the mirror is smashed, all affected creatures revert to former alignment.

### Crossing the Lake

Raft can be built in 2 days from trees around the lake. During crossing, invisible pixies attack (auto-surprise, -4 to hit penalty).

- **Ilya Pixies** ×6 (lake): AC -1 [20] (3 [16] if visible), HD 1*** (4hp), Att 1 × dagger (1d4), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 90' (30') / 180' (60') flying, SV D8 W9 P10 B13 S12 (E1), ML 7, AL C, XP 19 each. Platinum neck-bands (50gp each). See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

### W11a. Pixie Village

14 pixies total on the island (plus lake survivors). Live in hidden chambers (secret doors) in large tree trunks. Attack at night or if adventurers try to enter chambers/remove statue. Will not enter the building. Retreat if half killed.

### W11b. Ladder Down

Centre of the building: ladder 30' down a shaft.

### W11c. Skeleton Guardians

- **Skeletons** ×39: AC 7 [12], HD 1 (4hp), Att 1 × bone club (1d4), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 12, AL C, XP 10 each. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

### W11d. Ladder Trap

20-foot ladder is trapped. First character touching it: electric shock 1d8 damage (Save vs. Spells for half), then roll under Dex on 1d20 or fall for 1d8 more damage.

### W11e. Dart Trap

Door to room f is trapped. Two darts (one per wall) fire at THAC0 12 [+7]. 1 damage each, poisoned (Save vs. Poison or unconscious 2d20 hours).

### W11f. The Mirror

Black room — walls, floor, ceiling absorb light. Two "statues" by far wall are gargoyles. Black obsidian mirror hangs on iron chains between two pillars, facing away from door.

- **Gargoyles** ×2: AC 5 [14], HD 4** (19hp), Att 2 × claw / 1 × bite / 1 × horn (1d3/1d3/1d6/1d4), THAC0 16 [+3], MV 90' (30'), SV D6 W7 P8 B10 S10 (F8), ML 11, AL C, XP 125 each. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

**The Mirror:** Polished obsidian in jewelled frame. Looking in: see hideously distorted reflections + twisted images of those perverted. **Shatters** 2 turns after gargoyles destroyed, or by any single blow of 5+ damage. Frame worth **4500gp**.

**Aftermath:** Surviving pixies become friendly, give 3 potions of healing, return statue to Vyalia elves, restore relations.

The silvered-bronze statue is nearly 3' tall, hollow, weighs 150cn.

## W12. Gold Mine

*(PDF p.17–20)*

Two gnome brothers (Vissaryon and Tekaryon) with a dozen dwarven miners began gold mining. They broke into ancient tunnels, found a statue chamber, and a large cavern divided by stalagmite/stalactite "bars." They shattered the bars to reach a jewelled necklace — releasing a **shroud spider** imprisoned behind them.

The spider was worshipped by the **Dark Web (Nyy-akk) orcs** whose lair is on the far side of the mountain spur. Five miners have gone missing (4 dwarves + Tekaryon).

### W12a. Outside the Mine

Dam, wooden water chutes for gold extraction. Steps to miners' quarters.

### W12b. Miners' Quarters

Frightened miners welcome the party. Offer 800gp (half their mined gold) to kill the "black creature." If dwarves volunteer (up to 1d6), reward reduced by 30gp per dwarf, each expects full treasure share. Miners won't mention the necklace.

- **Vissaryon** (gnome mine owner): AC 5 [14], HD 3 (12hp), Att 1 × war hammer or 1 × crossbow (1d6), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 60' (20'), SV D8 W9 P10 B13 S12 (D3), ML 10. R 60/120/180. Green cloak over chain mail. AL N; honest but overfond of gold.
- **Dwarf Miners** ×8: AC 4 [15], HD 1 (5hp), Att 1 × pick axe or 1 × crossbow (1d6), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 60' (20'), SV D8 W9 P10 B13 S12 (D1), ML 8, AL N. R 60/120/180.

### W12c. Old Tunnels

Just over 4' high. Characters taller must stoop (-1 to hit). Orcs are used to them (no penalty). Max missile range: 50'. Dwarves have double chance of noticing hazards.

**Hazards:**
- **v:** Paralysed orc in fresh shroud spider webs (touching = paralysis, see New Monsters)
- **w:** Old workfaces (dead end)
- **x:** Roof collapse on 1d3 characters: 1d10 damage (Save vs. Dragon Breath for half). Tunnel not blocked.
- **y:** Yellow Mould: AC auto-hit, HD 2* (12hp), 50% chance of spore cloud if touched (10'×10'×10'), 1d6 damage + death (Save vs. Death Ray). XP 25.
- **z:** Unsafe floor: falls into shallow crevasse, 1d10 damage. Easy rescue.

### W12d. Statue Chamber

10' high. Empty except for plinth that held the statue (now with elves).

### W12e. Ancient Guardians

Dank, once-fine chamber. Dripping water, smeared wall-paintings, flaked plaster, humanoid bones. On stone benches: a dozen silver statuettes of robed men with jackal-like heads. They are **silver living statues** — all attack if any are touched.

- **Silver Living Statues** ×12: AC 4 [15], HD 1+1* (6hp), Att 1 × bite (1d4), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 12, AL N, XP 19 each. Immune to non-metal weapons and non-magical fire. Half damage from edged weapons. Silver content worth 50gp each when killed. See New Monsters.

Orc skeletons have 9gp each in belt pouches.

### W12f. Large Cavern

50' high centre. Stalagmites, stalactites, slender pillars. Broken "cage-like screen" where miners smashed through. Miners' furniture: table, chairs, beds, locked chest with gold (hidden necklace). Shroud spider's lair entrance: dark tunnel 30' above floor.

### W12g. Slurp!

Damp side-tunnel. Ochre jelly attacks.

- **Ochre Jelly** ×1: AC 8 [11] (only harmed by cold or fire), HD 5* (17hp), Att 1 (2d6), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 30' (10'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F3), ML 12, AL N, XP 300. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

### W12h. Shroud Spider's Lair

Floor littered with gnawed bones and clothing fragments. Human skeleton wearing silver and gold belt (450gp).

**Shroud Spider** (encounter anywhere in the mine at DM's discretion — build tension first):

- **Shroud Spider** ×1: AC 4 [15], HD 5*** (28hp), Att 1 × bite or 1 × web squirt (1d10 + paralysing poison or paralysing web), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 120' (40'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 9, AL C, XP 675. Only hit by magical or silver weapons. Bite: Save vs. Poison or paralysed 2d4 turns. Web: fresh web (<24h) — Save vs. Paralysis at +2 or paralysed (while touching + 2 rounds after). Can shoot web strand at 30' — Save vs. Paralysis at +1. See New Monsters.

### W12i. Food Store

Five web-shrouded bodies hang from ceiling: 2 orcs, 2 dwarf miners, gnome **Tekaryon**. All alive but paralysed; revive after 2 rounds if webs removed.

**Reward:** If Tekaryon and dwarves returned alive, miners give the necklace (3000gp) plus the agreed 800gp.

### W12j–k. Smaller Caverns

30' high. Narrow connecting tunnel (found automatically if searched). 2–5 orc guards in area k.

### W12l. Hidden Entrance

Two walls of debris conceal hidden entrances (secret doors) to orc lair. 2–5 orc guards between the doors.

### W12m–p. Orcs' Lair

Squalid, cramped, 7' ceilings. Black web-like wall paintings.

- **Dark Web Orcs** ×22 total: AC 6 [13], HD 1 (5hp, leader 8hp), Att 1 × sword or 1 × throwing axe (1d8+1 leader / 1d8 or 1d6), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 6 (8 with leader), AL C, XP 10 each. **Treasure:** 2d8gp personal.

**Area m (leader's room):** Threadbare stuffed lion. Treasure inside (reach through mouth). **Trap:** Mouth snaps closed (1d8 damage), locks shut (usual chance to pick). Contains: 2000gp, ornate silver anklet (350gp), **one elven boot** (other half of pair — see S9), scroll with *cure light wounds*, *hold person*, *resist cold*. If lion hacked open: 50% chance boot and/or scroll ruined.

## W13–W15. Tombs on the Ridge

*(PDF p.20–21)*

Three ancient burial mounds on a prominent ridge, visible up to 6 miles. Each surrounded by scorched, barren soil. Sleeping inside any barren area: awful dreams, spellcasters cannot re-learn spells next day.

### W13. Circle of Night

Entering the barren area: world becomes dark as night. Glowing entrance pillars cast weak green light. Attacked by 5 shadows (surprise 1–5).

- **Shadows** ×5: AC 7 [12], HD 2+2** (10hp), Att 1 × touch (1d4 + drain 1 Str for 8 turns), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 12, AL C, XP 35 each. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

#### W13a. Entrance

Evil presence sensed inside. All protective spells auto-dispelled. Magical defensive items temporarily cease (rings of protection, magic armour → ordinary).

#### W13b. Passage of Corpses

Tunnel slopes down. 10 frescoes of jackal-head priests — actually painted plaster screens over alcoves. 9 contain mouldering skeletons (no treasure). 1 (marked X) holds a **special ghoul**. Breaks out and attacks if party tries to leave with the mace from area c.

- **Special Ghoul** ×1: AC 5 [14], HD 5** (27hp), Att 2 × claw / 1 × bite (1d4/1d4/1d6 + paralysis), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 11, AL C, XP 425. So horrible: roll under Wis on 1d20 or fight at -2 to hit and -1 damage (fear). Clerics turn as wraith.

#### W13c. Crystal Coffin

Crystal coffin on black stone plinth. Recumbent figure in blue robes clutches a jewel-encrusted mace.

- **Zombie** ×1: AC 8 [11], HD 2 (12hp), Att 1 × claw (1d8, always loses initiative), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 12, AL C, XP 20. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

**Treasure:** Mace +1, +2 vs. undead.

### W14. Tomb of Warriors

Entering barren circle: stench of death, area littered with mangled corpses of orcs, goblins, men (illusion). **Warning:** Looting the 210 corpses yields 1d8gp each, but treasure vanishes when leaving the circle. Looters are cursed to carry the weight for a year and a day. Remove curse requires cleric level 8+. Threshold (T2) is recommended.

This tomb was partially plundered by Golthar (seeking another tapestry), who fled when the wyrds were released.

#### W14a. Pillaged Chamber

Stone door hanging open. Broken bones, rusted weapons, twisted armour. Smashed plaster doorway. Two **wyrds** in room beyond attack if approached.

- **Wyrds** ×2: AC 4 [15], HD 4* (21hp), Att 2 × glowing spheres (1d6/1d6, or 1d6+3/1d6+3 vs. elves), THAC0 16 [+3], MV 120' (40'), SV D8 W9 P10 B13 S12 (E4), ML 12, AL C, XP 175 each. R 30/60/90. Only hit by magical or silver weapons. Immune to sleep, charm, hold. Clerics turn as wraith. Each wears chain mail, helm with gem (150gp), belt with jewelled sword (250gp). See New Monsters.

#### W14b. Crypt

Two smashed coffins (held the wyrds). Hundreds of broken weapons, mangled armour, smashed shields.

### W15. Fool's Gold

False tomb, trap for grave robbers. Secret exit (from area c) cannot be found from outside.

Entering barren circle: bird-song (no birds), spring flowers sprout, lichen door becomes burnished bronze, stone becomes white marble.

#### W15a. Trap

Entering chamber: bronze door clangs shut and locks (even if spiked). Only opened by procedure in area b.

13 skeletons (6 humans, 2 elves, 1 halfling, 3 orcs, 1 bugbear) — all previous victims. **Usable gear:** 3 swords, 2 daggers, 1 longbow, 3 shields, 2 chain mail (halfling and elf sizes), 1 plate mail (human). **Thief's pack:** Silver holy symbol (150gp), silver incense burner (100gp), 110gp coins, 3 gems (25gp each), short sword +1.

#### W15b. The Mocking Face

3 more orc skeletons. Wall carvings: patterns + procession of 16 stylised humanoid figures + 1 per trapped character (recent). **Solution:** Erase/deface the fresh carvings → bronze door opens.

Secret exit found automatically if searched.

#### W15c. Touch of Death

Wet grey stone. A grey shape forms into a man-figure — a **living rock/ooze statue**.

- **Living Rock/Ooze Statue** ×1: AC 4 [15], HD 5** (29hp), Att 2 × grey ooze squirts, THAC0 15 [+4], MV 60' (20'), SV D8 W9 P10 B13 S12 (D5), ML 11, AL N, XP 425. Immune to sleep, charm, hold. Each ooze blob: AC 8 [11], 4hp, 2d4/round automatic + dissolves metal (1 round normal, 1 turn magical). See New Monsters.

## Reckoning with the Wolfskull

*(PDF p.20–23)*

The search for Stephan leads to the Wolfskull lair. Stephan has since been taken to Xitaqa.

### W16. Wolfskull Lair (Map W16)

#### The Petrified Forest

A dismal valley smitten by magical blight — every living thing turned to stone. Dense, opaque stone canopy; shadowy twilight. Only living things: pale moss, lichen, web-like creepers. Visibility: 100'. Use dungeon movement rates/ranges. Goblins don't suffer daylight -1 penalty here.

Petrified undergrowth: 8' high, 40hp per 10'×10' area to smash through.

2 turns after entering, attacked by giant bats:

### Loshad's Bargain

Nobody at Sukiskyn knows where Xitaqa is. Kuzma or Stellios suggests "asking the horse-man on the moor" (Loshad). They say he can be summoned "by whistling at midnight on the high moor for three nights running while standing on one leg."

**Meeting on the Moor:** If the party whistles on the moors at night (exact time/leg unimportant), Loshad comes on the 2nd or 3rd night in centaur form with 3 warhorse bodyguards.

**Loshad's conditions for revealing Xitaqa's location:**
1. Within 4 nights, bring the heads of werewolves **Bailakask** and **Kalkask** (blood enemies of horses) from their den (W17). Advises: attack at night when most of the pack is hunting.

## W17. Wolves' Den

*(PDF p.23–24)*

Werewolves Bailakask (mother) and Kalkask (son) + wolf pack. Den of caves in rocky outcrop by Volaga river (Map W17). Entrance: narrow natural tunnel (a), found only with Loshad's directions.

**By day:** All werewolves and wolves present — very dangerous assault.
**By night (recommended):** Bailakask, Kalkask, and most wolves hunting. Only 5 wolves guard the den. After defeating them, party can ambush the returning pack (Bailakask + Kalkask + 6 wolves) in 2d4 hours. Horses left outside will be attacked by the returning pack.

In melee/travel: werewolves use wolf form. Revert to human form while resting or for ranged attacks.

### The Den

Rocky cliffs: not difficult to climb (thieves: auto; others: same chance as thief of same level). Within 100 yards, horses shy away (wolf scent).

#### W17a. Entrance Tunnel

6' high. Horse skulls hang on wooden pegs on both walls.

#### W17b. Open Area

Horses are very frightened here; riding: roll under Dex on 1d20 each round or be thrown (1d6 damage).

Dusty barren hollow, littered with rocks and chewed bones. Statue of jackal-headed human on a pinnacle (natural platform, winding path). Statue worthless but **gems in eyes worth 750gp each**.

#### W17c–g. Caves

Dirty, dusty, chewed bones. Horses fight if forced inside.

**Cave g (werewolves' cave):** Slightly cleaner. Battered chest (unlocked, untrapped): tattered clothing, 500gp, ornate jewelled necklace (2500gp), pouch with 10 gems (5×100gp, 3×200gp, 2×500gp).

**Cave d:** Chewed remains of fine saddle (useless). Decorative gilt mounts and silver stirrups worth 350gp total.

### Fulfilling the Bargain

Return to the meeting-place with proof of werewolves' death → Loshad gives clear directions to Xitaqa, describes the shattered tower and surrounding ruins.

**Breaking the promise (horses):** Loshad seeks out the party, attempts to free horses (WE4). If successful: 1d6 hours later, stampede of 30 horses (1d20 damage per character, no hit roll), then Loshad + bodyguards attack.
