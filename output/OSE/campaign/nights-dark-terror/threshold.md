## General Notes

*(PDF p.34–36)*

Threshold is a thriving frontier town (~500 humans, ~50 demi-humans) on the shores of a small lake. Built primarily of wood. Ruled by **Patriarch Sherlane** (C14) from Tarnskeep castle.

### Customs and Law

- **Allowed weapons:** Daggers, swords (not two-handed), staffs only
- **Prohibited weapons:** Confiscated at gate, stored at town hall (T1), returned on departure (escorted to gate by a C4 cleric)
- **Magic-user spells:** Casting forbidden in town. Offenders tried by the Clerical Court
- **Armour:** Legal but frowned upon. Characters wearing armour openly are questioned by guards and escorted to their lodgings

### The Clerical Court

Five clerics appointed by Sherlane, meets at town hall (T1).

**Spell-casting penalties:**
| Offence | Punishment | Cure |
|---------|-----------|------|
| 1st | Loss of hair / green skin / pimples | Any *remove curse* |
| 2nd | Insomnia (no spell recovery) | *Remove curse* from C9+ |
| 3rd | Struck dumb | *Remove curse* from C14+ |
| 4th+ | Indefinite imprisonment below Tarnskeep | — |

Conventional lawbreakers: fines to imprisonment to execution.

### The Town Guard

Quartered at town hall, with contingents at both gates and (daytime) Fogor Isle wharves. Night: guard posts near bridges, patrols through main town (no Fogor Isle patrols).

**Patrol frequency:** Main streets every ~4 hours; side streets every ~6 hours. Arrive at disturbances in 1d8+4 rounds (varies by location/time).

- **Guard Sergeant:** AC 4 [15] (chain & shield), F3, hp 13, Att 1 × sword (1d8+1 inc. S bonus), THAC0 18 [+1] (inc. S bonus), MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F3), ML 10, AL L. White surcoat with golden chalice.
- **Guards** ×6 per patrol: AC 5 [14] (chain), F1, hp 5, Att 1 × pole arm (1d10+1 inc. S bonus), THAC0 18 [+1] (inc. S bonus), MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL L. White surcoats with golden chalice.

### Townsfolk

50% normal humans, 50% F1. Defend with daggers or improvised weapons.

- **Townsfolk:** AC 9 [10], NM or F1, hp 1–8, Att 1 (variable), THAC0 20 [-1] or 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV NM or F1, ML 6, AL L or N.

## Arrival

*(PDF p.36)*

Party is hailed by **Sergeant Arthol** (see `npcs.md`) at the gate or on a patrol boat. A scribe records each adventurer's name. Arthol reads the weapon rules, confiscates prohibited weapons. Recommends the **Hook and Hatchet** tavern — he'll be there after his shift.

## Important Locations

*(PDF p.36–37)*

### Ostlers

Horses, mules, tack and harness at 200% of standard prices.

### Inns

Very common. 1d4×10 patrons each. Rooms: 1d10+10sp/night. Stabling: 1d4sp/horse/night. Food quality: roll 1d6 (1=terrible, 3–4=average, 6=excellent).

**The Hook and Hatchet:** Guard favourite. Next to old town wall. Quality 5 (above average). Rooms 15sp/night, stabling 2sp/night.

### Hiring Retainers

10% chance per inn of 1–2 F1 fighters looking for work. Advertisements in market square also possible — but if PCs advertise, Golthar sends a thug to apply as a spy.

### T1. The Town Hall

Stone building. Houses Clerical Court. Always 1d4+1 guard patrols stationed here. Valuables stored at 10% of value.

### T2. The Temple

Impressive stone building near town hall. 20 clerics. Spellcasting services: **100gp per spell level** (up to 4th level). Higher spells only by Sherlane at Tarnskeep.

### T3. Cardia's Carpet Service

Outside the town hall. Cardia is currently flying one of the Patriarch's aides to Specularum — gone at least a week.

### Fogor Isle

*(PDF p.36)*

Connected by two stone bridges. Main port and warehousing. Thieves' Guild exercises considerable influence. Day: thieves keep low profile. Night: fully under Guild control, no guard patrols.

Dark, dingy streets. Numerous inns and dens of vice. Small groups (1–3) walking at night: 50% chance of accosting by thugs. Large groups (6+): members likely to have pockets picked by a level 6–11 (1d6+5) thief.

- **Thugs** ×5–8: AC 7 [12] (leather), F1, hp 5, Att 1 × short sword or 4 × throwing daggers (1d6 or 1d4), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL N, XP 10 each. R 10/20/30.

## Information

*(PDF p.35–36)*

### Specific Locations

40% chance any inhabitant knows any establishment's location. Modified for fame/obscurity. Crossed Swords: half normal (20%).

### Local Customs

Patriarch Sherlane is respected. Guards well-liked (except by thieves).

### Fogor Isle

Tavern patrons: "shunned by law-abiding citizens, under thieves' guild control."

### Black Peak Mountains

Locals look worried. Warn against going. Stories of hunting expeditions that never returned — gnoll attacks.

### The Iron Ring

Most inhabitants have never heard of it. Evidence of activities would interest Sgt. Arthol, who would report to "appropriate authorities." If PCs are blatant in enquiries, word reaches Golthar.

### Golthar/Jolenta's Arrival

Gate guards are observant but need drinks to share info. Off-duty guards at Hook and Hatchet confirm: a blue-eyed lady entered hours before the party, with two silent surly men, met by a "rat-faced man" who led them toward Fogor Isle. Arthol is suspicious of the rat-faced man.

## Golthar's Plan

*(PDF p.37–39)*

Golthar (or Karllag if Golthar died at Xitaqa) arrived via flying carpet with bodyguard **Sligh**. Contacted local Iron Ring cell (4 wererats + 10 humans) on Fogor Isle. When Jolenta arrived, met her at the gate. All staying at the wererats' hideout on Fogor Isle.

**Plan:** Lure adventurers at night to the deserted **Crossed Swords Inn** on Fogor Isle's wharfside. The inn houses a **troll**. Golthar will ambush outside after.

### TE1. Patriarchal Parade

*(Morning of day 2)*

Procession from Tarnskeep to temple: white-robed clerics, musicians, guards in finest surcoats, Patriarch Sherlane in white-and-gold palanquin.

During parade, adventurers glimpse **Golthar and Jolenta** across the street. Golthar glares and disappears into crowd. Procession blocks pursuit; they're gone when PCs get across.

### TE2. Phoney Rescue

**Mafka** (T5 Iron Ring Reaver, disguised as aged fortune teller) stumbles into a runaway cart's path in view of the PCs. If rescued (Dexterity check; failure = 2d4 damage to rescuer), Mafka "rewards" with a fortune:

> *"I see a tall man in yellow robes... He is here, in Threshold and he searches for something.... I see him quite clearly: he stands outside a building near the river... a sign bearing two crossed swords swings over his head... he — but the picture dims..."*

If not rescued: Mafka saves herself, deliberately drops a **purse** containing a key tagged with crossed swords, and a message from Golthar (Iron Ring mark): *"Meet me tonight at the Inn of the Crossed Swords. The trouble makers are in town and need your assistance. Golthar."*

### TE3. The Iron Ring Sighted

*(First evening)*

PCs notice **Vokos** (wererat) at the bar of any inn. Distinctive "rat face." As he drains his mug, his sleeve reveals an **Iron Ring brand**. He puts down the tankard and heads for the door. If called, he glances back but doesn't stop. Exits just before 4 town guards enter (blocking the door). If PCs try to physically restrain him, guards arrest *them*.

### TE4. Outside the Crossed Swords

Neglected inn. Painted sign of two crossed broadswords, hanging crookedly. Door: stout, solidly padlocked (Mafka's key doesn't fit). Windows boarded up (50hp to smash through — very noisy, attracts crowd). Chimney bricked up.

**Day:** 12 stevedores off-loading a boat nearby (Golthar arranged this). They warn off anyone approaching the inn. Attacking them summons 1d6×10 more stevedores; guard patrol arrives after 10 minutes to "escort" party off the isle.

- **Stevedores** ×12: AC 8 [11], F1, hp 4, Att 1 × boathook (1d4), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL N, XP 10 each.

**Night:** Stevedores leave at dusk. Wharf deserted except **Vokos** (wererat) watching from shadows for the party. When they arrive, he slips away to report to Golthar. PCs automatically notice his shadowy figure leaving — can follow.

### TE5. Through the Alleyways

Vokos hurries through Fogor Isle back-alleys to the wererat hideout (Map F). After his report, Golthar + Sligh + Jolenta + 2 Hounds + 4 wererats (inc. Vokos) + 5 hired thugs set out for the inn. 6 thugs remain to guard the hideout.

If PCs follow Vokos: DM determines if he notices. Even if he does, he still goes to the hideout but alerts occupants. If captured before reaching hideout: tells PCs "the yellow wizard" is in the Crossed Swords cellar with 20 fighters and a troll. Won't reveal hideout.

### TE6. Ambush

Golthar's party follows Vokos' alleyway route. They wait outside the inn for PCs to emerge, then ambush.

If PCs don't emerge within 1 hour: Golthar examines the inn. If unvisited, they return to hideout. If Vokos reported being followed, the ambushers are very wary.

### TE7. The Crossed Swords Tavern

Dark, dusty, cobweb-covered, nauseating stench. Downstairs: broken furniture. Upstairs: smashed packing crates.

**The Troll:** Lives in dank cellars. Entered via secret tunnel from Threshold's sewers. If it hears movement on ground floor, climbs through the trapdoor and attacks.

- **Troll** ×1: AC 4 [15], HD 6+3* (39hp), Att 2 × claw / 1 × bite (1d6/1d6/1d10), THAC0 13 [+6], MV 120' (40'), SV D8 W9 P10 B11 S12 (F6), ML 10 (8 if attacked by fire/acid), AL C, XP 650. Regenerates 3hp/round starting 3 rounds after injury. Cannot regenerate fire or acid damage. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

### TE8. The Wererat Hideout (Map F)

Surprisingly clean (except room c). Layout:

- **a.** Entrance
- **b.** Guard room — always 5 thugs (asleep unless alerted)
- **c.** Wererat sleeping area — filthy straw beds
- **d.** Kitchen / eating area
- **e.** Stairs — very worn, every step creaks (impossible to move silently)
- **f.** Golthar's room
- **g.** Jolenta's room
- **h.** Sligh's room

**Golthar's room (f):** Secret compartment behind fireplace contains his spell books and a locked silver casket (not trapped, value 1350gp):
- Carved ivory comb (500gp), matching brush (750gp), vanity mirror with platinum frame (1000gp)
- Golden seal bearing Iron Ring stamp (2500gp)
- Pouch: 5 gems (1×1000gp, 4×500gp)
- Leather purse: 50pp, 200gp

### Hideout Inhabitants

- **Golthar:** See `npcs.md`. AC 4 [15], M6, hp 17.
- **Jolenta:** See `npcs.md`. AC 4 [15] (chain & shield), C4, hp 16.
- **Sligh** (Reaver, fighter): AC 3 [16] (chain & shield +1), F4, hp 26, Att 1 × sword (1d8+2 inc. S bonus), THAC0 15 [+4] (inc. S bonus), MV 90' (30'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F4), ML 10, AL C, XP 75. **Treasure:** Silver belt buckle (75gp), platinum-inlaid scabbard (500gp), 35gp.
- **Wererats** ×4 (inc. Vokos): AC 7 [12] (9 [10] human), HD 3* (12hp), Att 1 × bite or 1 × short sword (1d4 or 1d6), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 120' (40'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F3), ML 8, AL C, XP 50 each. Attack in rat form. Bite can cause lycanthropy. **Treasure:** 2d10gp each. Vokos has pouch with 4 gems (75gp each), engraved silver bracelet (150gp).
- **Hounds** ×2: AC 7 [12] (leather), HD 1+1 (6hp), Att 1 × short sword (1d6), THAC0 16 [+3] (ferocity), MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 12, AL C, XP 19 each. **Treasure:** Gold earring (75gp each).
- **Thugs** ×10: AC 7 [12] (leather & shield), F1, hp 4, Att 1 × short sword (1d6), THAC0 19 [+0], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL C, XP 10 each. **Treasure:** 2d6gp each.

## Optional Events

*(PDF p.39–40)*

Roll on Table 5 or select as desired. Events marked * can occur more than once.

### Dogged!

Crazed former lumberjack **Chester** approaches with his dog "Jackie" on a string. Fears (incorrectly) he's dying. Wants PCs to adopt the dog.

- **War Dog** ×1: AC 7 [12], HD 2+2 (15hp), Att 1 × bite (2d4), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 9, AL N.

### The Thief

PCs are followed by a grimy T3. Watches where they go. If PCs deposit wealth at town hall: loses interest. If PCs keep wealth at an inn: reports to thieves' guild. Two T8 thieves (28hp each) attempt robbery when PCs are out.

### Beggar* (Repeatable)

**1st:** Pitiable urchin with empty bowl. If given money: surrounded by 2d10 more children demanding more. Follow PCs until paid or chased off.

**2nd:** Same start, but 3 T1 thieves (hp 2, Pick Pockets 20% +20% bonus from jostling) among the children. Pickpocket as many PCs as possible.

### Shower* (Repeatable)

Old woman empties chamber pot from upper window. Dexterity check or smell badly until next bath (-2 to all reaction rolls).

### Wagons* (Repeatable)

Heavy cart plows through muddy patch. Unless PCs took evasive action: 1–2 sprayed with acrid mud.

### Bear

Dancing bear escaped from handler (drunk in nearby tavern). Wrecking a baker's shop. Fond of currant buns. If captured and returned: owner rewards with gold/garnet ring (200gp). If killed: owner demands 500gp compensation.

- **Black Bear** ×1: AC 6 [13], HD 4 (24hp), Att 2 × claw / 1 × bite (1d3/1d3/1d6), THAC0 16 [+3], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 7, AL N, XP 75. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.

### ID Crisis* (Repeatable)

Drunk bumps into a PC, then accuses them of stealing his money. Calls for the guard unless pacified quickly.

### Accident* (Repeatable)

Wagon/street entertainer collision causes a brawl. 1–2 PCs hit by bottles (1d2 damage). Four guard patrols (led by Arthol) arrive in 1d10+6 rounds, arrest combatants.

## Leaving Threshold

*(PDF p.39)*

PCs collect weapons from town hall without difficulty. Follow trail NW toward Black Peak Mountains along the Foamfire river (not navigable). After passing outlying farms, trail peters out — real wilderness.

If Golthar is still alive, he doggedly pursues with whatever forces remain. If not dealt with on the trail, the party encounters him inside the Lost Valley. See `black-peaks.md`.
