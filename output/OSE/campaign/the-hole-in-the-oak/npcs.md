## Sheep-Headed Fauns (Areas 5–9)

### Ramius
- **Species/Identity:** Sheep-headed faun, male, leader of the flock
- **Location:** Area 8 (Fauns' Kitchen)
- **Attitude:** Welcoming, twee, secretly murderous
- **Key Info:** Chunky spiralling horns. Tweed jacket and short trousers. Refers to females as his "flock". Offers drugged tea (save vs spells or sleep 2d6 turns). Has key to chest in Area 9. Will call enchanted sheep skulls from Area 9 if in trouble. Has access to a potion of invisibility.
- **Stats:** See `the-hole-in-the-oak.md` Area 8

### Ewely
- **Species/Identity:** Sheep-headed faun, female
- **Location:** Area 8 (Fauns' Kitchen)
- **Attitude:** Docile
- **Key Info:** Nub horns. Polka-dot dress. Bow on top of head.

### Shorny
- **Species/Identity:** Sheep-headed faun, female
- **Location:** Area 8 (Fauns' Kitchen)
- **Attitude:** Suspicious
- **Key Info:** Nub horns. Black lace dress.

## The Mutagenic Ogre (Area 25)

### Mutagenic Ogre
- **Species/Identity:** Ogre, mutant, male
- **Location:** Area 25 (Den)
- **Attitude:** Enraged at burglary; otherwise indifferent to adventurers. Loves to eat halflings and gnomes (will pay 100gp for a live one).
- **Key Info:** Deep asleep (snoring). Diseased flesh, weeping pustules. Keys around neck (room door + cages). Has mutagenic breath (10' area, permanent mutation on failed save vs breath). Keeps 4 caged mutants as pets. If in trouble, releases mutants. Treasure: 724gp in pouches, human skull of Huugal-Barathk.
- **Stats:** AC 5 [14], HD 6+1* (32hp), XP 650. See `the-hole-in-the-oak.md` Area 25.

## Heretic Gnomes (Areas 51–60)

**General:** 20 forest gnomes, 3' tall, cast out from their community for dabbling in evil magic. Worship the evil tree stump in Area 60. May act friendly but will try to capture PCs for sacrifice. Each carries 2d10cp, 1d10sp, and a random item under their hat.

### Grimm (Leader)
- **Species/Identity:** Gnome, adult male, leader
- **Location:** Area 58 (or 2-in-6 in Area 57 scheming)
- **Attitude:** Scheming, authoritative
- **Key Info:** Wears **chainmail +1** (blacker than night) and **shield +1** (studded with giant rat teeth). Plans to take over the Magical Forest by beguiling tree roots. ML 10.
- **Stats:** AC 3 [16], HD 2 (11hp), XP 20. See `the-hole-in-the-oak.md` Area 58.

### Gribbl (Priestess)
- **Species/Identity:** Gnome, adult female, stump priestess, Grimm's wife
- **Location:** Area 58
- **Attitude:** Devout, dangerous
- **Key Info:** Can cast *charm person* and *protection from evil* once per day each. ML 10.
- **Stats:** AC 7 [12], HD 2* (11hp), XP 25. See `the-hole-in-the-oak.md` Area 58.

## Wandering NPCs

### Hazrad the Unholy
- **Species/Identity:** Human magic-user, male
- **Location:** Wandering (Random Happening 17); seeks jars in Area 37
- **Attitude:** Neutral, distracted, muttering about being late
- **Key Info:** Blue robe. Amulet of infravision. Collects teeth, insect husks, and hair in jars. Low HP for his level (10hp).
- **Stats:** AC 8 [11], HD 4* (10hp), THAC0 19 [0], XP 125. Spells: *magic missile, sleep, invisibility, phantasmal force*.

## Historical / Undead NPCs

### Bozurah the Imperishable
- **Identity:** Wizard (historical, no longer present)
- **Key Info:** A vainglorious wizard of no historical import who once laid claim to the dungeon. His phantasmal illusions appear in Areas 10 and Random Happening 2. No stat block — exists only as illusions.

### Jorg the Defiler
- **Species/Identity:** Wight (undead), formerly a warrior
- **Location:** Area 32 (Hidden Crypt, in sarcophagus)
- **Attitude:** Hostile (rises to attack if disturbed)
- **Key Info:** Inscription reads "IN LIFE HE DEFIED KEZEK, IN DEATH HE SERVES". Carries the **Azure Serpent Blade** (sword +1, water breathing) and a **gold and ruby snake necklace** (2,500gp, enchanted — turns corpses into wights). Energy drain on hit.
- **Stats:** AC 5 [14], HD 3* (13hp), XP 50. See `the-hole-in-the-oak.md` Area 32.

### The Spectral Hunter
- **Species/Identity:** Incorporeal undead, formerly a member of the White League
- **Location:** Area 13 (awakened by looking in mirrors)
- **Attitude:** Demented, attacks on sight once awoken
- **Key Info:** Only harmed by magic or silver. Carries ancient silver coins (150gp), hunting horn (50gp) — if sounded by a Lawful character, summons a loyal hunting hound for 1 hour (once per week).
- **Stats:** AC 6 [13], HD 2* (10hp), XP 25. See `the-hole-in-the-oak.md` Area 13.

## Other Entities

### Huugal-Barathk
- **Identity:** Disembodied consciousness trapped in a human skull
- **Location:** Area 25 (ogre's alcove)
- **Key Info:** Awakened if skull is touched by a Neutral character. Speaks Common in a spooky whisper. Seeks to be reunited with its body in the hidden crypts of the lizard temple in the city. Grants **+1 INT and CHA** to a person who aids it.

### The Evil Tree Stump
- **Identity:** Evil sentient tree stump, "god" of the heretic gnomes
- **Location:** Area 60
- **Attitude:** Over-confident megalomaniac
- **Key Info:** Demands worship and blood sacrifices. Can charm once per round (save vs spells). Offers occult power (charm person 1/day) to those who swear their souls. Roots poke into corpses. 12 gold candle holders (250gp each).
- **Stats:** AC 6 [13], HD 6* (32hp), XP 500. See `the-hole-in-the-oak.md` Area 60.

### The Root Faces
- **Identity:** Sentient root faces covering walls of Area 4
- **Location:** Area 4
- **Attitude:** Mercenary — sell dungeon hints for 5gp each
- **Key Info:** Speak Common. Dissipate if attacked. Roll d10 for information given.
