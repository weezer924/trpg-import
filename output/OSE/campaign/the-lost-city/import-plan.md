# B4 — The Lost City: Import Plan

> **Source:** B4 — The Lost City by Tom Moldvay (TSR, 1982)
> **System:** Old-School Essentials (B/X compatible)
> **Levels:** 1–3
> **Source file:** `source/D&D Classic/Modules/B4 - The Lost City.txt` (34 pages)

## Module Structure

The module has 6 parts:
- **Part 1** (p.3–4): Introduction, DM's Background, Factions, Players' Background
- **Part 2** (p.5–8): Tiers 1 & 2 — Dungeon Level 1 (Rooms 1–12)
- **Part 3** (p.9–13): Tiers 3 & 4 — Dungeon Level 2 (Rooms 13–40)
- **Part 4** (p.17–21): Tier 5 — Dungeon Level 3 (Rooms 41–58)
- **Part 5** (p.22–25): Expanding the Adventure — Tiers 6–10 (Rooms 58–100) + Underground City
- **Part 6** (p.25–28): New Monsters (Banshee, Cynidiceans, Werefox, Polymar) + Cynidicean Encounters

## Notes

- Tier 3 split from Tier 4: Tier 3 has all three faction HQs (major RP areas), Tier 4 is burial chambers. Different enough to warrant separate files.
- Deep Levels (Tiers 6–10) are outline-only in the module — less detail per room.
- Underground City is also outline-only — brief descriptions of areas A–S + further adventure hooks.
- No `shops.md` needed — no shops in this module. Characters scavenge supplies from factions.
- No `quests.md` needed — adventure hooks are embedded in factions and region overview.
- Cynidicean encounter table (12 entries from Part 6) goes into `encounters.md`.
- New monsters (Banshee, Werefox, Polymar) get full stat blocks where they appear + in encounters index.
