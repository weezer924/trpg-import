## General Features

*(PDF p.13)*

Tier 4 holds the burial chambers of King Alexander, Queen Zenobia, and major nobles, clerics, and officials from their reign. Same construction as upper tiers. Uses Wandering Monster Table: Level 2 (see `middle-pyramid.md`).

---

## 25. Nobleman's Burial Room

*(PDF p.13)*

Scenes of warfare painted on the walls. A large wooden sarcophagus standing upright along the south wall — painted picture of a warrior in armour on the outside. A large white-furred ape squats on each side of the sarcophagus.

- **Monsters:** White Ape ×2: AC 6 [13], HD 4 (18hp), Att 2 × claw (1-4/1-4), THAC0 16 [+3], MV 120' (40'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F2), ML 7, AL Neutral, XP 75. When door opens, they beat their chests and threaten. If threats ignored, they attack. Sometimes throw rocks for 1-6 damage — these have gathered a plentiful supply.
- **Treasure (in sarcophagus):** Mummified body wearing full plate armour, holding a **sword +1**.

### 25a. Ghostly Haunts

*(PDF p.13)*

Shimmering, ghost-like figures of a man and a woman in costly clothing and golden crowns appear in the corridor. The man raises his hand: "Go no further, lest the curse of King Alexander overtake you!" The woman: "I am Queen Zenobia. Turn back; only death awaits you!"

- **Haunts:** Characters seeing them must **save vs. Spells or flee in fear** for 1-4 turns. Otherwise harmless. No physical existence — cannot be hurt by physical attacks or spells.

## 26. Tomb Annex

*(PDF p.13–14)*

Long, narrow room. Floor has a red and white checkerboard pattern of 2' × 2' squares. Court scenes on north and south walls. A 2' high opening at floor level in the east wall near the southeast corner.

This corridor was kept open when the pyramid was built to bring in the bodies of the king and queen. After funerals, sealed from outside.

### 26a. Tomb Traps

Three pendulum blades hidden in the ceiling. If a character walks into any of the three marked squares, roll 1d6 — on 1, the trap triggers. Characters caught or running through must roll DEX or lower on 1d20 to dodge. Failure: **1d10 damage**. Blades covered with sticky green goo that looks like green slime — the goo is harmless. Blades swing for 1 turn, then auto-reset.

**Ceiling blocks:** Special 10' square blocks designed to fall after the burials. One block failed to fall completely — a 2' high crawlspace remains. DM should increase tension with creaking noises.

**Rubble:** Two more stone blocks cracked into rubble when they fell. Takes 1 full turn to clear enough to proceed.

**Narrow corridor:** Final obstacle — a narrow, twisting corridor wide enough for one person. Formed by blocks that fell crookedly.

## 27. Councilor's Burial Room

*(PDF p.14)*

Door has a 2' wide hole gnawed near the floor. Paintings of a man giving advice to kings and queens. Wooden coffin in the centre with large holes gnawed in the sides.

- **Monsters:** Giant Shrew ×3: AC 4 [15], HD 1* (4hp), Att 2 × bite (1-6/1-6), THAC0 19 [0], MV 180' (60'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 10, AL Neutral, XP 13. "See" by sound echoes. Always gain initiative on first attack; +1 initiative on second. Characters of 3rd level or less must **save vs. Death Ray or flee in fear**. A silence 15' radius spell will blind them. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Treasure:** None — body and treasure already stolen.
- **Exit hole:** In door, leads to corridors.

### 27a. Trap Door

Middle of the floor: large stone slab with a bronze ring bolted into it. Opens to reveal an iron ladder leading down to room 44 on Tier 5.

## 28. High Priest's Burial Room

*(PDF p.14)*

Door is scorched, as is the end of the corridor. Outline of a man-sized humanoid etched into the scorched wall. Door slightly open.

The outline is that of a ghoul, blasted to ashes when it triggered the magical trap guarding the door. The trap is now spent — door can be opened safely.

Inside: Paintings of a priest performing ceremonies. A raised dais with an open bronze sarcophagus. Three hideous beast-like humanoids are tearing a mummified body apart.

- **Monsters:** Ghoul ×3: AC 6 [13], HD 2* (9hp), Att 3 × claw/bite (1-3/1-3/1-3), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 9, AL Chaotic, XP 25. Non-elf creatures hit must **save vs. Paralysis or be paralyzed**. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Treasure (in sarcophagus):** Jeweled necklace (1,500 gp), 2 jeweled bracelets (600 gp each) — stripped from the body and tossed aside by the ghouls.

## 29. Embalming Room

*(PDF p.14)*

Several large tables. Jars and casks around the walls. Bin in the northwest corner.

This room was used for embalming. Concealed within are:

- **Monsters:** Shadow ×5: AC 7 [12], HD 2* (11hp), Att 1 × touch (1-4 + strength drain), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 12, AL Chaotic, XP 25. Surprise on 1-5 on 1d6. Intelligent ghost-like creatures that look like real shadows. Each hit drains **1 point of Strength** (weakness lasts 8 turns). Immune to sleep and charm. **Can only be hit by magic weapons.** If party has no magic weapons, let them run — shadows will not leave this room. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Jars and casks:** Worthless embalming spices.
- **Bin:** Rotten linen winding cloths. Underneath: **potion of invisibility** and a chest with 3,000 gp.

## 31. Guard Captain's Burial Room

*(PDF p.14)*

A mummified human in plate armour holding a halberd stands at attention against the south wall. Nine skeletons holding swords grouped around it, also at attention.

- **Mummified body:** Not undead. Normal armour and weapons.
- **Monsters:** Skeleton ×9: AC 7 [12], HD 1 (4hp), Att 1 × sword (1-6), THAC0 19 [0], MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 12, AL Chaotic, XP 10. Attack on sight, fight until destroyed. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Treasure:** None.

## 32. Queen Zenobia's Burial Chamber

*(PDF p.15)*

Plain stone walls. An oblong stone box (3' high, 7' long, 3' wide) in the centre. Written on the side in several languages: "May the curse of darkness destroy all who dare desecrate my resting place."

The stone box contains the wooden coffin of Queen Zenobia.

- **Monster:** Queen Zenobia (Wight) ×1: AC 5 [14], HD 3* (24hp), Att 1 × touch (energy drain), THAC0 17 [+2], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F3), ML 12, AL Chaotic, XP 50. Attacks on sight. **Can only be hit by silvered or magical weapons.** Each hit **drains one level of experience** (including one hit die). See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Treasure:** Jeweled crown (5,000 gp), sceptre (actually a **wand of paralyzation** with 8 charges — she has forgotten how to use it since becoming undead).

## 33. False Tomb

*(PDF p.15)*

Two large gleaming sarcophagi marked "Alexander" and "Zenobia." Surrounded by large wooden chests. Room littered with broken objects: smashed thrones, broken chariot, smashed pottery, broken weapons, torn clothing. Piles of bones on the floor. Wall mosaics showing famous events from King Alexander and Queen Zenobia's reign.

**This is not the real tomb.** It is a fake to fool grave robbers. Bodies inside are royal slaves. Real burial chambers are rooms 32 and 34.

- **Sarcophagi:** Wood painted to look like gold.
- **Chests:** Four chests apparently containing 5,000 sp, 6,000 gp, 3,000 ep, 2,000 pp. In the sarcophagi: 50 gems, 12 pieces of jewellery. **All are cheap counterfeits.** Actual total value: 450 gp.
- **Detection:** Dwarves or thieves notice the fakes on 1-5 (1d6, one roll only). Others on 1-3.
- **Secret doors:** Tapping walls reveals hollow sounds where secret doors lead to hidden corridors. If party can't open them, they can break them down in 6 + 1d6 turns (noisy — roll wandering monsters normally).

## 34. King Alexander's Burial Chamber

*(PDF p.15–16)*

Bare walls. Stone box in the centre. A translucent ghost-like figure rises from the box — pale figure with glowing red eyes and a large, dark mouth.

- **Monster:** Banshee ×1: AC 3 [16], HD 5* (26hp), Att 1 × wail (1-4/round automatic), THAC0 14 [+5], MV 150' (50'), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (F5), ML 12, AL Chaotic, XP 300.
  - **Module-unique monster.** Pale ghost-like figure, roughly humanoid but longer and thinner. Glowing red eyes, large dark mouth.
  - **Wail attack:** All individuals within 30' take automatic 1-4 damage per round. Doors or walls block the sound. Attacks any living creature it sees; continues wailing until destroyed or intruders flee.
  - **Immunities:** Can only be hurt by magic or magical weapons. Immune to sleep, charm, and hold spells. **Not undead — cannot be turned.**
  - The banshee attacks as soon as the tomb door is opened. If the party wins initiative and runs out of range, they take no damage. If they get outside the room and close the door, they are safe (even if within 30').
- **Treasure (in wooden coffin inside stone box):** Jeweled crown (5,000 gp), **plate armour +1**, **sword +2**. Tomb was looted long ago except for these items.

## 35. Slave's Burial Room

*(PDF p.16)*

Room filled with skeletons. Bones lie in thick piles covering the floor — impossible to cross without walking on bones.

These are remains of household slaves belonging to the various individuals buried in the pyramid. Killed upon their masters' deaths. **Not undead — merely bones.**

## 36. Chamberlin's Burial Room

*(PDF p.16)*

Paintings of a man signing documents and organizing tax collection. A smashed wooden coffin on the floor. Six pale-skinned humans with fatal wounds standing around the coffin.

- **Monsters:** Zombie ×6: AC 8 [11], HD 2 (9hp), Att 1 × weapon (1-8), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 12, AL Chaotic, XP 20. Attack on sight. Always strike last. Immune to sleep and charm. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Treasure:** None — coffin already looted.

## 37. Giant Rat Lair

*(PDF p.16)*

Appears empty except for an odd-shaped mound in the southwest corner. Rustling and faint squeaking noises from the mound.

- **Monsters:** Giant Rat ×10: AC 7 [12], HD ½ (2hp), Att 1 × bite (1-3 + disease), THAC0 19 [0], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL Neutral, XP 6. Each has 1-in-20 chance of carrying disease. Bitten by diseased rat: **save vs. Poison or infected** — 1-in-4 chance of being fatal in 1d6 days; otherwise sick in bed (no adventuring) for 1 month. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Nest:** Conceals a small hole in the west wall, just big enough for a giant rat — leads to the corridor to the west.
- **Treasure (in nest):** 300 gp, 4 gems (500 gp each).

---

## 38. Jester's Burial Room

*(PDF p.16)*

The floor from Tier 3 (room 20a) hinges down to meet a ramp in this room.

Paintings of King Alexander and Queen Zenobia being entertained by a dwarven jester. Small coffin in a niche in the east wall. A large brass jar in each corner of the room.

- **Coffin trap (surprise):** If the lid is touched, it springs off. An oversized wooden model of the dwarven jester's head (painted brightly) jumps up on a coiled spring — a jack-in-the-box. Coffin also contains the jester's ashes.
- **Brass jars:** Look empty. Any item put completely into any jar is **instantly teleported** into a large concealed hollow beneath the coffin. The jars were the jester's master trick for making items disappear. Party can access the hollow by removing the coffin.

---

## 39. Rolling Boulder Trap

*(PDF p.16)*

Reaching the west door to room 39a triggers this trap. The wall in the north corridor opens and a 10' wide boulder rolls out at **40' per round** down the long corridor. When it stops, it blocks the corner near room 40.

- **Not meant to kill** — only scare the party. Unless they do something obviously stupid (e.g. standing and arguing), let them escape after a close shave — into a room or around a corner.
- **If it hits:** 3-30 (3d10) damage. Stops after hitting one character or reaching the end of the hall.
- **DM notes:** Describe graphically — crashing sounds echoing, stone chips flying, floor shaking.

### 39a. Noble Lady's Burial Room

*(PDF p.16)*

Sarcophagus on a 10' square dais. Slender 6' columns rise from each corner of the dais to form an arched dome above. A brass jar with dancing flames at each end. Paintings of a noble lady's life on the walls.

- **Mummified body:** Jewellery stolen. Brass jars bolted to floor, flames cannot be put out.
- **Hidden clue:** Behind one brass jar — a small bronze tube containing parchment. The parchment is a map of the southwest part of this tier marked with a large black "X" — the location of the **trap door at 27a** (leads to Tier 5).

## 40. Master Thief's Burial Room

*(PDF p.16)*

An elaborate jeweled coffin in the centre. A worm-like creature, 9' long and 3' high, with many legs and eight 2' long tentacles around its mouth, stands next to it.

- **Monster:** Carrion Crawler ×1: AC 7 [12], HD 3+1* (13hp), Att 8 × tentacle (paralysis), THAC0 16 [+3], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 9, AL Neutral, XP 75. Each tentacle hit requires **save vs. Paralysis or be paralyzed**. Paralysis removed by cure light wounds (but spell has no other effect). Without spell, paralysis wears off in 2-8 turns. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Treasure:** Jewels on the coffin worth 3,000 gp. A set of lockpicks on the body.
- **Backstory:** A master thief from King Alexander's era stole a place near the king in the afterlife — exchanged his own coffin for the nobleman who was supposed to be buried here.
