Same as upper pyramid (see `upper-pyramid.md`). Tier 3 contains the headquarters for all three factions — these can become "safe" areas for characters who join a group.

## Revolving Passage

*(PDF p.9)*

The central area of Tier 3 houses a passage on a turntable, operated by a machine-like system of weights and counter-weights.

- **Buttons (inside):** A row of 8 buttons inside each end of the passage, matching buttons in the halls. Pressing an inside button swings that end clockwise to line up with the proper hall.
- **Buttons (outside):** Next to each door is a button with a symbol. Pressing it swings the passage clockwise to line up with that door (faint grinding noises). If already lined up, nothing happens.
- **Doors:** A door to the central area opens only if the revolving passage is lined up with it.

**Monster Notes:**

- **Giant Bat:** "See" by echo-location. One bat in the group is a giant vampire bat — bitten characters must save vs. Paralysis or fall unconscious 1-10 rounds. Characters killed by the bite must save vs. Spells or become undead 24 hours later (DM chooses type). See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Brotherhood of Gorm:** Traveling to their shrine in room 24. See rooms 11–12.
- **Hobgoblin:** On a mission for the Priests of Zargon (capturing prisoners, raiding factions, etc.). See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Magi of Usamigaras:** Traveling to their shrine in room 14.
- **Rock Baboon:** Larger, more intelligent baboons. Eat anything, prefer meat. Use bones/branches as clubs. Ferocious and vicious tempers. Hunting for food. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Spitting Cobra:** 3' long grayish-white snake. Can spit venom up to 6' at victim's eyes — save vs. Poison or blinded. Alternatively may bite — save vs. Poison or die. **Blindness:** Cannot attack (DM may allow at -4). May cure with holy water eye wash + cure light wounds (spell does not restore hp if used this way). Sight may return in 1-6 turns. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Warrior Maidens of Madarua:** Traveling to their shrine in room 21.

**DM Note:** Encounter outcomes often depend on previous interactions. Characters wearing masks/necklaces of one faction will find other factions unfriendly or hostile.

## 13. Abandoned Ceremonial Chamber

*(PDF p.10)*

Ruined chapel. Tattered tapestries on walls. Dusty altar along north wall covered with rotted altar cloth. Wooden candlesticks, offering bowl, and holy symbol (shaped like an eye) on the altar. A 6' long lizard with wide skin flaps between its legs sits next to the altar.

- **Monster:** Draco ×1: AC 5 [14], HD 4+2 (20hp), Att 1 × bite (1-10), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 120' (40') / fly 210' (70'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F3), ML 7, AL Neutral, XP 125.
- **Treasure (behind altar):** Remains of a human thief with a full canteen, sack containing 400 gp and 2 gems (100 gp each).
- **Treasure (ceremonial):** Candlesticks (rare teakwood, 50 gp), bowl (25 gp), holy symbol (25 gp).

## 14. The Magi of Usamigaras

*(PDF p.10–11)*

The corridor has been painted black with tiny white stars (night sky). Iron door on the north wall to chamber 14a has a star carved into it. Opening the door rings a bell.

### 14a. The Chamber of the Magi

North and west walls hung with a large tapestry showing major constellations. Thirteen figures in rainbow-coloured robes and silver masks stand before a stone star-shaped altar. Centre figure wears a silver crown.

- **Monsters:** Mage of Usamigaras ×12: AC 9 [10], M1 (3hp), Att 1 × silver dagger (1-4), THAC0 19 [0], MV 120' (40'), SV D13 W14 P13 B16 S15 (M1), ML 8, AL Neutral, XP 10. Each has a silver star symbol on right palm.
- **NPC:** **Auriga Sirkinos**, Chief Mage: AC 9 [10], M3 (10hp), Att 1 × dagger (1-4) or spell, THAC0 19 [0], MV 120' (40'), SV D13 W14 P13 B16 S15 (M3), ML 8, AL Neutral, XP 50. S 9, I 18, W 10, D 12, C 10, Ch 15.
  - Stout; appears jolly and fun-loving. Actually a ruthless fanatic who will do anything to promote the cult and his own power. Hides this under a pleasant manner. If forced to choose between the Magi and his personal power, chooses personal power.
  - **Spells:** detect magic, hold portal, phantasmal force.
- **If attacked:** Each of the 12 first-level mages has a different spell: charm person, detect magic, floating disc, hold portal, light, magic missile, protection from evil, read languages, read magic, shield, sleep, ventriloquism. Those with attack spells cast them; those with defensive spells cast those. All then attack hand-to-hand. Auriga will flee through the door and cast hold portal on it if things go badly.
- **Hidden treasure (stone altar):** Secret compartment protected by a **poison needle trap** (save vs. Poison or die). Contains: **mace +1** and **wand of magic detection** (5 charges).
- **Treasure:** Silver masks (20 gp each), silver crown (200 gp), silver daggers (30 gp each).
- **Reaction (if party doesn't attack):** 2d6 — 9-12: asked to join; 6-8: may join, not asked; 2-5: attacked/driven off. -3 if showing signs of another faction; +2 if party proves defeating another cult.
- **Membership:** Full — magic-users, elves, thieves, and clerics. Others — lesser members only. Full initiation: place right hand palm-down on altar centre, recite pledge ("I, [name], do hereby pledge to serve and obey the great Usamigaras"). A five-pointed star is magically burned into the character's palm. Given silver masks, daggers, and rainbow-coloured robes.
- **Lesser members:** Given silver ring carved with Usamigaras's face.

## 17. Abandoned Storeroom

*(PDF p.11)*

Room filled with shelves, mostly empty, some with old crates. Shelves covered in dust and a vile-looking yellow fungus in large, thick clusters.

- **Hazard:** Yellow Mold: AC n/a, HD 2 per 10' sq (9hp per section), MV 0, SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML n/a, AL Neutral, XP 25. Covers shelves along all four walls — 6 separate sections, each 9hp. Only killed by fire. If touched, 50% chance of shooting 10' × 10' × 10' spore cloud; anyone caught takes 1-6 damage and must **save vs. Death Ray or choke to death within 6 rounds**. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Crates:** Nothing of value.

### 17a. Water Trap

When a human-sized character crosses the room, DM rolls 1d6. On 1–2, both doors slam shut and water pours in from vents high in the walls. Character must force open a door within 3 tries or find a way to breathe, or drown.

Once filled, concealed floor drains open and the trap resets automatically. If the door to room 18 is opened while water pours in, water rushes into room 18 (overturns the basket, frees the pit vipers, then drains away).

## 19. Shifting Wall

*(PDF p.11)*

Short corridor ending in an 8' tall bronze statue of a winged child with two snakes twined around its body, holding a wand and coins (Usamigaras). Firmly connected to the wall.

- **Wand pulled left:** Wall shifts westward to block the last 10' section of the hall. Stays for 3 turns, then returns to original position.
- **Wand pulled right:** Statue breathes a cloud of choking gas. Covers the 30' corridor. **1 hp damage per round** to anyone in the area. Dissipates after 2 turns (120 rounds). Only 1 gas charge (Magi recharge when necessary).

### 19a. Storeroom

*(PDF p.11)*

Room filled with crates and barrels. Four large brown beetles, each about 3' long, are breaking into them.

- **Monsters:** Oil Beetle ×4: AC 4 [15], HD 2* (9hp), Att 1 × bite + oil spray (1-6 + blisters), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL Neutral, XP 25. Can squirt oily fluid at one victim up to 5'. If hit, raises painful blisters — **-2 to hit** until cure light wounds or 24 hours pass. Spell used for blisters does not also cure damage. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Treasure:** Crates contain dried food and casks contain wine (recently stored by the Magi). Edible as long as not broken open by beetles. Food and wine are good even if crate/barrel was sprayed with beetle oil.

## 20. Abandoned Ceremonial Chamber

*(PDF p.12)*

Ruined chapel, looted and deliberately wrecked. Stone altar smashed. No ceremonial equipment. "Zargon" scrawled on one wall in large letters.

Wrecked by followers of Zargon centuries ago. Empty.

### 20a. Entrance to Tier 4

A 20' long section of the floor in the southern part of the room is hinged to swing down westward when anyone steps on it. Swings slowly down to rest against a ramp in room 38 (Tier 4). Characters can walk down into room 38.

## 21. Arch of the Warrior Maidens

*(PDF p.12)*

Two 10' tall statues of women fighters wearing masks of Madarua. They stand with spears outstretched forming an arch. The only way down the corridor is in single file under the crossed spears.

- **Hidden pressure plate:** Just beyond the arch. Each time someone steps on it, a hidden chime rings in room 23, warning the Warrior Maidens and revealing the number in the party.

### 21a. The Chamber of Madarua

*(PDF p.12)*

Door locked. Can be battered down in 1 turn (Warrior Maidens in room 23 will investigate).

Near the northeast corner: an altar covered with green and white cloth. On the altar, a 3' tall statue of a woman holding a sword and a sheaf of wheat. Large white candles on each side. Incense burning in three small braziers before the altar. White drapes on all walls, green carpet on floor. Dust-free and scrubbed clean.

- **Listening holes:** Tiny holes in the wall hidden by the white cloth. Warrior Maidens in room 23 can hear loud noises (sound carries through room 22 which also has listening holes).
- **Desecration:** If any party member desecrates the shrine, the Warrior Maidens will attack.

## 22. Treasure Room of the Warrior Maidens

*(PDF p.12)*

Empty except for a large stone vault in the middle of the east wall. The vault has a keyhole. Stone slabs too heavy to move.

- **Trap:** Any character standing in front of the keyhole who tries to open without the key and fails — a large **stone block falls from ceiling** for **2-12 damage**. Does not fall if key is used or lock is successfully picked.
- **Warrior Maidens:** Will hear loud noises from this room (see room 21a).
- **Treasure:** 600 ep, 1,000 gp, 300 pp.

## 23. Warrior Maidens of Madarua

*(PDF p.12–13)*

Ten women seated around a table. All wear bronze chain mail over green tunics, with bronze masks of Madarua.

- **Monsters:** Warrior Maiden ×7: AC 5 [14], F1 (5hp), Att 1 × sword (1-8), THAC0 19 [0], MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8 (9 with Pandora), AL Neutral, XP 10.
- **Monsters:** Warrior Maiden ×2 (veterans): AC 5 [14], F2 (9hp), Att 1 × sword (1-8), THAC0 19 [0], MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 8 (9 with Pandora), AL Neutral, XP 20.
- **NPC:** **Pandora**, Madarua's Champion: AC 5 [14], F3 (20hp), Att 1 × sword +1 (1-8+3), THAC0 16 [+3] (+2 STR 17, +1 sword), MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F3), ML 10, AL Neutral, XP 50. S 17, I 12, W 11, D 12, C 15, Ch 14.
  - Wields a **sword +1** with +2 bonus from Strength. Total attack bonus +3. Tallest and oldest of the women. Prides herself on being a simple fighter. Claims "When in doubt, attack." Actually more subtle — always tracks the odds. Raises Maiden morale to 9.
  - Carries the **key** to the treasure vault in room 22.
- **Reaction (if party doesn't attack):** 2d6 — 9-12: asked to join; 6-8: may join, not asked; 2-5: attacked/driven off. -3 if showing signs of another faction; +2 if party proves defeating another cult.
- **Membership:** Full — female fighters, dwarves, halflings, and elves. Others — lesser members only.
- **Initiation:** Character draws sword, pricks finger, smears blood on blade, places sword on altar of Madarua, swears to uphold Madarua's honour even with her own blood. A small sickle is branded on the inside of the left wrist. Full members receive bronze masks and green robes.
- **Lesser members:** Given bronze bracelet showing Madarua's face. Supplies fetched from underground city stronghold (half a day).

## 24. Door Trap / Ceremonial Chambers of Gorm

*(PDF p.13)*

### Door Trap

Iron door at the end of the corridor with three lightning bolts engraved on it.

When anyone comes within 10', door glows with crackling blue light. Door is locked. Speaking **"By the great god Gorm"** stops the glow and makes it safe to enter.

- **Electric trap:** Touching the door without the proper words deals **1-4 electrical damage** automatically. A thief can try to pick the lock while taking damage each attempt.

### 24a. The Ceremonial Chambers of Gorm

Walls, ceiling, and floor painted sky blue. Golden marble altar surrounded by golden candleholders and braziers along the middle of the east wall. On the altar: a small stone statue of a bearded man throwing a lightning bolt. Next to the statue, a golden bowl.

- **Access:** No non-members allowed. New members of the Brotherhood are initiated here.
- **Initiation ceremony:** Dress in white robe, ritually wash hands in the bowl (water), draw weapon, kneel before altar, swear in Law to uphold the Brotherhood's ideals and promote the worship of Gorm. Receive tattoo of blue lightning bolt on right shoulder.
- **Secret door (west wall):** Conceals a ladder up to room 12 on Tier 2.
