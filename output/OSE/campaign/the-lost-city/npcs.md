## Faction Leaders

### Kanadius — Grand Master of the Brotherhood of Gorm
- **Species/Identity:** Human male, Fighter 3, Lawful
- **Location:** Room 12 (Tier 2, `upper-pyramid.md`)
- **Appearance:** Tallest and oldest of the Brothers. Chain mail, blue tunic, steel helmet (fancier than the others), golden mask of Gorm.
- **Attitude:** Stern, kindly, absent-minded with trivia but sharp on important matters
- **Personality:** Prefers to outmanoeuvre opponents. Brave if fighting is necessary. Leads rather than orders. Boosts morale of all Brothers to 9.
- **Key Info:** Possesses a **helm of telepathy** (3 uses/day) — secretly tests the sincerity of characters wanting to join. If attacked and losing, may escape through secret trapdoor to room 24 (Tier 3). If he escapes, may return later with a dozen Brothers for revenge.
- **Stats:** AC 4 [15], F3 (25hp), Att 1 × sword (1-8+1), THAC0 18 [+1] (+1 STR 14), MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F3), ML 10. S 14, I 12, W 9, D 12, C 10, Ch 12.

### Auriga Sirkinos — Chief Mage of Usamigaras
- **Species/Identity:** Human (Cynidicean) male, Magic-User 3, Neutral
- **Location:** Room 14a (Tier 3, `middle-pyramid.md`)
- **Appearance:** Stout, wears silver crown, rainbow-coloured robes, silver mask of Usamigaras. Silver star symbol on right palm.
- **Attitude:** Appears jolly and fun-loving; actually ruthless and self-serving
- **Personality:** A fanatic who will do anything to promote the cult of Usamigaras and his own power. Hides this under a pleasant manner. If forced to choose between the Magi and his personal power, chooses personal power. If battle goes badly, flees and casts hold portal on the door behind him.
- **Key Info:** Commands 12 first-level magic-users (each with a different spell). Personal funds (2,500 sp + 200 gp) guarded by a trained wolf in room 16. Altar hides a **mace +1** and **wand of magic detection** (5 charges, poison needle trap).
- **Stats:** AC 9 [10], M3 (10hp), Att 1 × dagger (1-4) or spell, THAC0 19 [0], MV 120' (40'), SV D13 W14 P13 B16 S15 (M3), ML 8. S 9, I 18, W 10, D 12, C 10, Ch 15. Spells: detect magic, hold portal, phantasmal force.

### Pandora — Madarua's Champion
- **Species/Identity:** Human (Cynidicean) female, Fighter 3, Neutral
- **Location:** Room 23 (Tier 3, `middle-pyramid.md`)
- **Appearance:** Tallest and oldest of the Warrior Maidens. Bronze chain mail, green tunic, bronze mask of Madarua.
- **Attitude:** Direct, action-oriented, deceptively cunning
- **Personality:** Prides herself on being a simple fighter. Claims "When in doubt, attack." Actually more subtle than she appears — always tracks the odds. Raises Maiden morale to 9.
- **Key Info:** Wields a **sword +1** (+2 bonus from STR 17 = +3 total to damage). Carries the **key** to the Warrior Maidens' treasure vault (room 22: 600 ep, 1,000 gp, 300 pp). Commands 7 first-level and 2 second-level Warrior Maidens.
- **Stats:** AC 5 [14], F3 (20hp), Att 1 × sword +1 (1-8+3), THAC0 16 [+3] (+2 STR 17, +1 sword), MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F3), ML 10. S 17, I 12, W 11, D 12, C 15, Ch 14.

## Antagonists

### Darius — Priest of Zargon
- **Species/Identity:** Human (Cynidicean) male, Cleric 6, Chaotic
- **Location:** Room 58 (Tier 5, `base-level.md`)
- **Appearance:** Chain mail, carries a mace.
- **Attitude:** Evil, cunning, aggressive
- **Personality:** Controls the Cynidicean gamblers in room 57 through intimidation, considering all gambling money his property as "taxes." Will use spells intelligently — silence on magic-users, bless on allies, striking on his own weapon.
- **Key Info:** Twin brother of Demetrius (now a spirit in room 45). Commands 6 hobgoblins. The Cynidiceans in room 57 will join him if the party tries to steal gambling treasure. Target of Demetrius's spirit — the spirit will not rest until Darius is destroyed.
- **Stats:** AC 5 [14], C6 (22hp), Att 1 × mace (1-6+1), THAC0 16 [+3] (C6 base 17, +1 STR 13), MV 60' (20'), SV D9 W10 P12 B14 S12 (C6), ML 10. S 13, I 9, W 16, D 9, C 13, Ch 14. Spells: cure light wounds, light (or darkness), bless (or blight), silence 15' radius, striking (Expert), cause serious wounds (Expert).

### Zargon — The Monster God
- **Species/Identity:** Ancient creature (not a god), Chaotic
- **Location:** Room 100 (Tier 10, `deep-levels.md`)
- **Appearance:** 15' tall humanoid. Giant lizard head with a single red eye. Black 2' horn curving upward. Six arm-tentacles ending in razor-sharp talons. Six leg-tentacles for slithering.
- **Attitude:** Cunning predator
- **Key Info:** Worshipped as a god but is merely a cunning creature. Discovered "godhood" makes getting victims easier. Can **regenerate from its horn** (takes years between encounters, not during combat). Horn must be destroyed in the Eye of Zargon lava pit (area Q) to permanently kill it.
- **Stats:** AC 0 [19], HD 12* (80hp), Att 7 (6 × claw 1-8 / 1 × bite 3d8), THAC0 10 [+9], MV 90' (30'), SV D6 W7 P8 B8 S10 (F12), ML 12, XP 1,900.

## Spirits

### Demetrius — Spirit of the Good Twin
- **Species/Identity:** Spirit of a human Cleric 6, Lawful (possesses a living character)
- **Location:** Room 45 (Tier 5, `base-level.md`) — white robe
- **Backstory:** An elder in the cult of Usamigaras. Twin brother of Darius. Vowed to destroy the cult of Zargon and his evil brother. Was assassinated before he could begin. Made a dying wish that his spirit live on until Darius was destroyed.
- **Key Info:** Possesses a character who touches the white robe (save vs. Dragon Breath to resist). The character becomes a 6th level Lawful cleric with WIS 16, INT 10, 31hp. Knows the route to room 58 (including secret doors) but **not the traps**. Will depart once Darius is dead — the robe disintegrates and the character is restored to full hp. If the possessed character is reduced to 0 hp, the character dies.
- **Stats (when possessing):** C6, hp 31, SV D9 W8 P12 B14 S10 (C6). Spells: cure light wounds, detect evil, bless, hold person, striking (Expert), cure serious wounds (Expert).

### Queen Zenobia — The Wight
- **Species/Identity:** Undead (wight), formerly human queen, Chaotic
- **Location:** Room 32 (Tier 4, `lower-pyramid.md`)
- **Key Info:** Once the queen of Cynidicea, now an undead wight. Attacks on sight. Wears a jeweled crown (5,000 gp) and carries a **wand of paralyzation** (8 charges) but has forgotten how to use it since becoming undead.
- **Stats:** See `lower-pyramid.md` Room 32.

### King Alexander — Banshee Guardian
- **Species/Identity:** The king's burial chamber is guarded by a banshee (not Alexander himself), Chaotic
- **Location:** Room 34 (Tier 4, `lower-pyramid.md`)
- **Key Info:** The banshee guards King Alexander's grave. Attacks with an automatic wail (1-4/round, 30' range). His coffin contains a jeweled crown (5,000 gp), **plate armour +1**, and **sword +2**.
- **Stats:** See `lower-pyramid.md` Room 34.

## Notable Cynidiceans

### Brother Theodeus
- **Identity:** Leader of the "priest" Cynidiceans (Encounter #2)
- **Location:** Wandering (Cynidicean encounter table)
- **Notes:** Wears dark robes and devil mask with incense burner. Thinks he is a powerful cleric. Sees the party as demons. AC 5 [14]; C3; hp 13.

### Policrates the Herald
- **Identity:** Spokesman for the "royal court" Cynidiceans (Encounter #8)
- **Location:** Wandering (Cynidicean encounter table)
- **Notes:** Wears bright yellow robes and human mask. Will "recognise" a party member as the King or Queen of Cynidicea. AC 9 [10]; NM; hp 4.

### Priest of Zargon (Encounter #12)
- **Identity:** Active Priest of Zargon on a holy quest
- **Location:** Wandering (Cynidicean encounter table)
- **Notes:** AC 5 [14]; C3; hp 12; Spells: cure light wounds ×2. Commands 12 fanatic followers (F1, ML 12). Will try to trick the party and take them prisoner.
