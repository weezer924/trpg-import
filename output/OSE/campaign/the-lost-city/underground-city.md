## General Notes

*(PDF p.25)*

The underground city was built in the catacombs below the old city after the old city was destroyed. About **1,000 adult Cynidiceans** live here. The DM is expected to develop these areas into further adventures.

---

## Key to the Underground City

### A: Underground Lake
Water supply for Cynidicea. Deep, fresh-water lake fed by channels cut through solid rock. Many fish provide the Cynidiceans with fresh meat.

### B: The Island of Death
In the middle of the lake. Pockmarked with caves. Centre has a **Stonehenge-like group of arches** made from large stone blocks — existed before the first Cynidiceans arrived. No one knows who built it or its original purpose. Rumoured to be a place of great power.

When the underground city was built, the Cynidiceans dug up all bodies from the upper catacombs, burned them in a mass pyre, and ferried the ashes to the island for reburial. Later, the island became home to **undead** and is now avoided.

### C: City Docks
Extend into the lake, forming a harbour for the fishing fleet.

### D: Water-Filled Chasm
When the lake was first flooded, water flowed into this nearby chasm. Fishermen avoid it due to dangerous undertows and sharp rock ledges hidden just beneath the surface.

### E: Rock Bridges
Two natural rock arches forming bridges across the water-filled chasm. They look sturdy.

### F: Mushroom Fields
Cynidiceans farm mushrooms, edible fungi, and lichens. Fields are not well tended — overgrown, looking more like forests of giant mushrooms than cultivated fields.

### G: Stock Pens
Herded giant underground animals. Main meat sources (besides fish): non-poisonous giant snakes, cave locusts, and giant rats. Animals kept in stockade-like pens made from giant mushroom stalks. Poorly tended — some pens contain only dead animals (Cynidiceans forgot to feed them); in others, animals have broken down walls and roam freely among the mushroom forests.

### H: Water Channels
Channels laboriously cut through solid rock to feed the underground lake. Lead under the desert to hidden springs. 10' high and 10' wide, but water level usually only 2–3' deep. Sudden floods have been known to occur.

### I: Orpheus Park
When the city was first built, Orpheus, a wealthy citizen, had a park built in his honour. A sandy path leads in from a small beach. A tiny stream trickles through from the lake. Landscaped with giant mushrooms and strange rock formations.

### J: Main Streets
Two main streets lined with marble columns cross near the middle of the city: **Alexander Way** and **Zenobia Street** (named after King Alexander and Queen Zenobia).

### K: The Temple of Zargon
Largest building in the underground city. Contains a temple to Zargon and barred cells where **prisoners are kept** until the priests feed them to Zargon. Services are poorly attended, but the **Priests of Zargon are still the strongest power** in the underground city.

### L: Stronghold of Gorm
The Brotherhood of Gorm turned houses in this area into a stronghold surrounded by stone walls. Inside is a large building with **two stone towers**. Extra masks and necklaces for new members are stored here.

### M: Stronghold of Usamigaras
The Magi of Usamigaras made houses in this area into a stronghold. Walled area with a large central building and **guard posts at each corner**. Robes, masks, daggers, and rings for new members stored here.

### N: Stronghold of Madarua
The Warrior Maidens of Madarua turned houses in this area into their stronghold. Walled area housing a **large tower**. Bronze masks, bracelets, and robes for new members stored here.

### O: Goblin Cliff-Dwellings
On the far shore of the lake, sheer cliffs rise to a plateau. Caves in the cliffs house **goblins and hobgoblins** who climb down by ladder (some caves reachable by handholds cut into rock). Many caves connected by internal tunnels. Also house **thouls, bugbears, ogres, and trolls**.

### P: The Wasteland
Top of the plateau — a wilderness of giant stalagmites, stalactites, strange rock formations, geysers, and bubbling pools of mud.

### Q: The Eye of Zargon
In the midst of the wasteland — a **lava pit** known as the "Eye of Zargon" because the crater glows as redly as Zargon's single eye. The approach is guarded by **flame salamanders led by a lesser efreeti** (or other creatures chosen by DM). **Zargon's horn must be dropped here to permanently destroy the creature.**

### R: Entrance to the Lower Catacombs
Sealed shut by a huge stone slab, lifted only by a special winch using pulleys and heavy chains. Entrance, slab, and winch housed in a special building. The underground city was built only in the upper catacombs because the lower catacombs hold **terrible monsters**. The DM may use the lower catacombs for special adventures.

### S: Entrance to the Pyramid
Adventurers entering from the pyramid arrive at this point. Passage leads back to room 50 on Tier 5. All factions have their own secret entrances to the pyramid (not shown on map).
