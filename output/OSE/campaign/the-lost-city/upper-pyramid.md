## General Features

*(PDF p.5)*

- **Construction:** Large, smooth stone blocks. Rooms are bare stone slabs except where noted.
- **Ceilings:** Passages 10' high. Rooms 15' high.
- **Doors:** Stone slabs that push inward to open. Tend to close unless held, jammed, or spiked open.
- **Light:** None (unlit) unless otherwise stated.
- **Supplies:** Characters are out of food and water. They must find these supplies soon or die. Keep reminding the party they are hungry, thirsty, and tired.

## 1. Statue Room (Tier 1)

*(PDF p.5–6)*

The highest tier of the pyramid is a single 40' square room. Smells old and musty. Dust on the floor has been disturbed. Three huge bronze cylinders reach from floor to ceiling in the middle of the room, each with a bronze door and handle at floor level.

**Secret Entrance:** The stone slab hiding the secret entrance is held open by the dead body of a hobgoblin with a large crossbow bolt in its chest (dead several weeks, nothing of value).

- **Trap 1a — Crossbow:** In a niche opposite the secret door, a large crossbow pointed at the entrance. Not loaded (already triggered by the hobgoblin via a hidden pressure plate just inside the secret door).
- **Secret Door:** Will swing shut behind the party unless jammed open. Can be forced open normally.

**Trap 1b — Gas Room:** The door to the room swings shut unless held/jammed. Small holes in each corner release odorless, tasteless, invisible gas. Triggered by opening the door. An elf or thief has 50% chance to hear the hiss. Gas takes 1 round to build up (characters feel dizzy), then does **1 hp damage per round**. Rags or iron spikes jammed into the holes stop the gas. If both the room's door and the secret door are jammed open, gas escapes harmlessly.

**Bronze Cylinders:** Hollow bases of the three statues on top of the pyramid. Sound hollow if tapped. Each has a ladder going up (into a statue — speaking tubes and levers to move the statue's parts) and down (to room 6).

- **Trap 1c — Darts (west wall, Gorm statue):** 4 darts fire from the wall when the door handle is pulled. Att as 1 HD monster, hit only someone standing directly in front. Each dart deals 1-3 damage.
- **Trap 1d — Spears (centre, Usamigaras statue):** Harmless — already triggered (dried blood in front). Normally: 2 spears shoot out when door opened, att as 1 HD monster, 1-6 damage each. DM may reset if party leaves and returns.
- **Trap 1e — Pit (east wall, Madarua statue):** Special hinged paving stone in front of the base. Drops open when door handle is pulled. Anyone standing in front falls to room 6 — **1-6 damage**.

## 2. Storage Room

*(PDF p.7)*

Contains rotting bales of what might be cloth and dusty crates. Smells like it has been closed off for a long time.

Old storage room, long abandoned. Food in crates and clothing in bales are rotted and worthless. Otherwise empty.

## 3. Secret Room

*(PDF p.7)*

Seven bird-like creatures with long tube-like beaks. A faint glitter from the centre of the room as they fly toward the party.

- **Monsters:** Stirge ×7: AC 7 [12], HD 1* (4hp), Att 1 × beak (1-3), THAC0 19 [0], MV 30' (10') / fly 180' (60'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F2), ML 9, AL Neutral, XP 13. **+2 to hit due to speed.** Once attached, sucks blood for automatic 1-3 damage per round until victim or stirge is dead. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Treasure:** 4 gems (100 gp, 100 gp, 500 gp, 1,000 gp) lying in a pile of dust.
- **Detail:** Small hole high in the north wall — stirges use it to fly out hunting at night.

## 4. Priest's Quarters

*(PDF p.7)*

Sparsely furnished with rotting furniture: bed, desk, chest, writing table with wooden chair, wooden holy symbol shaped like a lightning bolt (Gorm). All covered in thick dust. Dead hobgoblin on floor (several weeks dead, left arm swollen and discoloured — killed by a killer bee from room 7).

- **Treasure:** Full water bottle, purse with 135 sp and 40 gp.

## 5. Fireworks Storeroom

*(PDF p.7)*

Half a dozen small crates in the centre of the room. Seated on the crates are nearly a dozen 1' tall winged people, talking a strange musical language and laughing merrily.

- **Monsters:** Sprite ×10: AC 6 [13], HD ½ (3hp), Att 1 × curse (special), THAC0 19 [0], MV 60' (20') / fly 180' (60'), SV D12 W13 P13 B15 S15 (E1), ML 7, AL Neutral, XP 6.
- **Behaviour:** Friendly if approached peacefully. Interested in everything but nothing holds their attention long. Will not join the party. DM may use them to hint about what lies ahead.
- **If attacked:** 5 sprites acting together can cast one curse spell (magical practical joke — e.g. turning armour to rust, gluing boots to floor). Will cast 1–2 curses, then flee through a 1' × 1' concealed door high in the northeast wall. If pursued, fly over green slime in room 8.
- **Treasure:** Crates contain fireworks packed in sawdust. 1-in-6 chance per crate of still being usable (100 gp resale each). Usable crate contains: 1-6 charges of flash powder, 2-8 roman candles, 1-4 skyrockets, 3-12 strings of small firecrackers. Effects: smoke, bright lights, and noise only — no damage or blindness.

### 5a. Pottery Jars

Three 4' tall pottery jars. First contains sand; others are dry and empty (once stored water). Were to be used if fire broke out in the fireworks room.

## 6. Special Storeroom

*(PDF p.7)*

A 10' radius circle at the foot of each ladder is lit by a glowing, 2' long beetle with three glowing spots.

- **Monsters:** Fire Beetle ×3: AC 4 [15], HD 1+2 (6hp), Att 1 × bite (2-8), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 120' (40'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 7, AL Neutral, XP 15. Each has three glands that glow (10' radius light). Glands continue to glow 1-6 days after removal. Hungry — will attack if disturbed. See `ose-classic-monsters.md`.
- **Contents:** Spare parts for statue machinery. Several large sealed clay pots that once held oil (mostly evaporated). Party can recover **6 flasks of oil** (burns poorly, thick smoke) — takes 2 turns to recover all.
- **Foundry:** Small foundry for fixing statue parts: forge, anvil, tongs, hammers, etc.

## 7. Treasure Room

*(PDF p.7–8)*

A 10' tall cage in the centre. Pile of coins and gems in the bottom. Giant beehive hanging from top. Several large 1' long bees buzzing around. The cage mesh is wide enough for bees to fly through. A 1' square hole high in the south wall.

The locked cage holds the treasure of the Brotherhood of Gorm (rooms 11 and 12).

- **Monsters:** Killer Bee ×5: AC 7 [12], HD ½* (2hp), Att 1 × sting (1-3 + poison), THAC0 19 [0], MV 150' (50'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 9, AL Neutral, XP 6.
  - **Poison:** Save vs. Poison or die. Bee dies after stinging. Even on successful save, stinger must be removed (takes 1 round); otherwise does 1 hp/round as it burrows deeper.
  - **Gorm masks:** Bees have learned that persons wearing a mask of Gorm are friendly. Will not attack anyone wearing the mask unless attacked first.
- **Inside the hive:** 4 more bees (HD 1, 4hp each) and a queen bee (HD 2, 9hp). Queen can sting repeatedly without dying. Hive bees only attack if hive itself is attacked.
- **Honey:** Special honey inside hive acts as half-strength potion of healing, curing 1-4 hp. All honey needed for one dose.
- **Treasure:** 2,000 sp, 500 gp, 2 gems (100 gp each), 1 piece of jewellery (700 gp).

## 11. Brotherhood of Gorm

*(PDF p.8)*

Three double bunk beds. Five men seated on the lower bunks talking. Each wears iron chain mail over a blue tunic, steel helmet, and a golden mask (long-haired, bearded man with stern gaze).

- **Monsters:** Brother of Gorm ×5: AC 5 [14], F1 (5hp), Att 1 × sword (1-8), THAC0 19 [0], MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8, AL Lawful, XP 10.
- **Reaction:** Roll 2d6 — 9-12: ask party to join; 6-8: neutral (party may join but not asked); 2-5: assume party are thieves/spies, attack or drive away.
- **If fighting starts:** Brothers in room 12 hear and rush to help.
- **Membership:** Only male fighters, dwarves, halflings, and elves of Lawful alignment may become full members. Any character may become a lesser member. Characters wanting to join are taken to room 12.
- **Equipment:** Food and water available.
- **Treasure:** Golden masks worth 100 gp each.

## 12. Grand Master of the Brotherhood of Gorm

*(PDF p.8–9)*

Three double bunk beds. Six men standing around talking. Same attire as room 11 — chain mail, blue tunics, steel helmets, golden masks.

- **Monsters:** Brother of Gorm ×5: AC 5 [14], F1 (5hp), Att 1 × sword (1-8), THAC0 19 [0], MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F1), ML 8 (9 with Kanadius), AL Lawful, XP 10.
- **NPC:** **Kanadius**, Grand Master of the Brotherhood of Gorm: AC 4 [15], F3 (25hp), Att 1 × sword (1-8+1), THAC0 18 [+1] (+1 STR 14), MV 60' (20'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (F3), ML 10, AL Lawful, XP 50. S 14, I 12, W 9, D 12, C 10, Ch 12.
  - Stern-looking but basically kindly. Prefers to outmanoeuvre opponents; brave if fighting is necessary. Somewhat absent-minded with unimportant details but not major ones. Leads rather than orders. Raises all Brothers' morale to 9.
  - **Special item:** **Helm of telepathy** (usable 3 times/day). Uses it to secretly test the sincerity of characters wanting to join.
- **Secret Trapdoor:** Behind a bed in the southeast corner. Leads down to the secret chamber next to room 24 on Tier 3. If attacked and losing, Kanadius may escape through here.
- **Revenge encounter:** If Kanadius escapes, DM may create a special later encounter — Kanadius and a dozen Brothers seeking revenge (replaces a wandering monster encounter).
- **Reaction (if party doesn't attack):** Same 2d6 roll as room 11. If first meeting, Brothers may become suspicious and attack (2-5). Neutral on 6-8. Ask to join on 9-12.
- **Initiation:** Characters are taken to room 24 (Tier 3). Simple ceremony: dress in white robe, ritually wash hands in bowl, draw weapon, kneel before Gorm's altar, swear in Law to uphold ideals of the Brotherhood. Receive blue lightning bolt tattoo on right shoulder.
- **Full members:** Given tunics, masks, and any normal armour/weapons needed.
- **Lesser members:** Given necklace with small golden mask of Gorm (20 gp).
- **Treasure:** Golden masks (100 gp each), Kanadius's fancy helmet (**helm of telepathy**).
