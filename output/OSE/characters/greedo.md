## 背景

Sculptor from Feremhaal, City of Statues. One of six dwarves carving a statue of goddess Kaast for 15 years. After 14 years, absconded with gems meant for the statue's left toe. True passion is maps, though can't draw a straight line. Dreams of drawing plans for a great manse. Still fears retribution from the cult of Kaast.
