## 背景

Butcher, gourmand, and ruthless cannibal. Devotee of Yagagphoros, goddess of flaying and anthropophagy. Raised in a burrow in the Crawback Hills, ran away to Sracka as a child. Driven by lust for new humanoid meats and love of swordplay. Cult leader promised that sufficient donations ensure a place in the eternal slaughterhouse.
