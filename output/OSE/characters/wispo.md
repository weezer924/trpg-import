## 背景

Member of the night-hunters, servants of the elf-queen of the Forest of Gong. Specializes in capture and tending of rare nocturnal birds. A vision sent by an augur drove Wispo beyond the dark borders of the ancestral home. Seeks the lost city of Toril, believed submerged beneath a far sea, and dreams of becoming ruler of an outpost there.
