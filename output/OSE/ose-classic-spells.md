## Cleric Spells

*(PDF p.78–87)*

### 1st Level Spells

#### Cure Light Wounds

**Duration:** Instant
**Range:** The caster or a creature touched

This spell has two uses:
1. **Healing a living subject:** Restores 1d6+1 hit points of damage. Cannot grant more hp than the subject's normal maximum.
2. **Curing paralysis:** Paralysing effects are negated.

**Reversed: Cause Light Wounds** — Inflicts 1d6+1 hit points of damage to a touched creature. In combat, a melee attack roll is required.

#### Detect Evil

**Duration:** 6 turns
**Range:** 120'

Objects enchanted for evil purposes or living beings with evil intentions magically glow.

- **Intent only:** Does not grant mind reading, only a general sense of evil intent.
- **Definition of evil:** The referee decides what is "evil". Potentially harmful things like traps are not "evil."

#### Detect Magic

**Duration:** 2 turns
**Range:** 60'

Enchanted objects, areas, or creatures glow. Both permanent and temporary enchantments are revealed.

#### Light

**Duration:** 12 turns
**Range:** 120'

Three usages:
1. **Conjuring light:** 15' radius. Sufficient for reading but not as bright as daylight. May be cast upon an object (light moves with it).
2. **Blinding a creature:** Cast upon its eyes. Save versus spells or blinded for the duration. A blind creature cannot attack.
3. **Cancelling darkness:** May cancel a darkness spell.

**Reversed: Darkness** — 15' radius area of magical blackness, preventing normal sight (not infravision). May alternatively blind creatures or dispel a light spell.

#### Protection from Evil

**Duration:** 12 turns
**Range:** The caster

Wards the caster from attacks by creatures of another alignment:
- **Bonuses:** +1 to saving throws against attacks or special abilities of affected creatures.
- **Affected creatures' attacks:** –1 penalty against the caster.
- **Enchanted, constructed, or summoned creatures:** Cannot attack the caster in melee (ranged attacks still possible). Broken if the caster engages such a creature in melee (save/attack bonuses still apply).

#### Purify Food and Water

**Duration:** Permanent
**Range:** 10'

Makes spoiled, rotten, poisonous, or contaminated food and water pure. Affects one of:
- **Drink:** 6 quarts.
- **Rations:** One trail ration (iron or standard).
- **Unpreserved food:** Sufficient for 12 human-sized beings.

#### Remove Fear

**Duration:** 2 turns
**Range:** The caster or a creature touched

Calms and purges fear. Magically induced fear may be dispelled (save versus spells required, modified by +1 per caster level).

**Reversed: Cause Fear** — Target within 120' flees for the duration unless it saves versus spells.

#### Resist Cold

**Duration:** 6 turns
**Range:** 30'

All creatures within range are protected:
- **Normal cold:** Unharmed by non-magical freezing temperatures.
- **Save bonus:** +2 to all saving throws versus cold-based magical or breath attacks.
- **Cold-based damage:** Reduced by 1 point per damage die (minimum 1 per die).

### 2nd Level Spells

#### Bless

**Duration:** 6 turns
**Range:** 60'

Two situations:
1. **Battle:** Allies in a 20' square area not yet in melee gain +1 to attack, damage, and morale.
2. **Ritual:** May form part of rituals of purification or consecration.

**Reversed: Blight** — –1 penalty to morale, attack, and damage of enemies in a 20' square. Save versus spells to resist.

#### Find Traps

**Duration:** 2 turns
**Range:** 30'

Trapped objects or areas within range glow with magical blue light. Both magical and mechanical traps detected. No knowledge of trap nature or deactivation.

#### Hold Person

**Duration:** 9 turns
**Range:** 180'

One or more humans, demihumans, or human-like monsters are paralysed (save versus spells):
1. **Against an individual:** Save penalised by –2.
2. **Against a group:** 1d4 individuals targeted.

**Restrictions:** Undead and human-like monsters of >4+1 HD are not affected.

#### Know Alignment

**Duration:** 1 round
**Range:** 10'

Instant knowledge of the alignment of one character, monster, object, or location within range. Most objects/locations have no alignment, but magic items or holy places may.

#### Resist Fire

**Duration:** 2 turns
**Range:** 30'

A single creature gains supernatural fire resistance:
- **Normal heat:** Unharmed by non-magical heat or fire.
- **Save bonus:** +2 to all saving throws versus fire-based magical or breath attacks.
- **Fire-based damage:** Reduced by 1 point per damage die (minimum 1 per die).

#### Silence 15' Radius

**Duration:** 12 turns
**Range:** 180'

A 15' radius area becomes utterly silent.
- **Within the area:** All sound stopped. Conversation and spell casting impossible.
- **Noise from outside:** Can be heard by those within.
- **Cast upon a creature:** Save versus spells. Failure: the silence area moves with the creature. Success: effect remains stationary; creature may move out.

#### Snake Charm

**Duration:** 1d4+1 rounds or turns
**Range:** 60'

Snakes are rendered non-hostile (swaying but not attacking).
- **HD affected:** Total HD ≤ caster's level.
- **Duration:** Against attacking snakes: 1d4+1 rounds. Otherwise: 1d4+1 turns.

#### Speak with Animals

**Duration:** 6 turns
**Range:** 30'

Communicate with one type of animal (ordinary or giant versions). Can ask questions and receive answers. Animals are no more friendly than normal (reaction roll may apply). Friendly animals may perform favours.

### 3rd Level Spells

#### Continual Light

**Duration:** Permanent
**Range:** 120'

Three usages:
1. **Conjuring light:** 30' radius, as bright as full daylight (creatures with daylight penalties are affected). May be cast on an object.
2. **Blinding a creature:** Save versus spells or blinded permanently. Blind creatures cannot attack.
3. **Cancelling darkness:** May cancel continual darkness.

**Reversed: Continual Darkness** — 30' radius magical blackness preventing both normal sight and infravision. Light sources within the area do not illuminate it. May alternatively blind creatures or dispel continual light.

#### Cure Disease

**Duration:** Instant
**Range:** 30'

Two uses:
1. **Cure a subject of any disease:** Including magical diseases.
2. **Kill green slime:** Instantly destroyed.

**Reversed: Cause Disease** — Inflicts a terrible disease (save versus spells). Effects: death within 2d12 days, –2 to attack, natural healing takes 2× time, magical healing ineffective. Cured by cure disease.

#### Growth of Animal

**Duration:** 12 turns
**Range:** 120'

One non-magical, normal animal is doubled in size and strength. Damage and carrying capacity doubled. May affect giant versions of normal animals. Intelligent animals and fantastic monsters are unaffected.

#### Locate Object

**Duration:** 6 turns
**Range:** 120'

Sense the direction (not distance) of an object:
1. **General class:** Nearest object of that type (e.g. stairway, altar).
2. **Specific object:** Must be clearly visualised. Cannot locate creatures.

#### Remove Curse

**Duration:** Instant / Permanent (curse)
**Range:** The caster or a creature touched

Instantaneously removes one curse. May allow discarding a cursed magic item.

**Reversed: Curse** — Save versus spells or suffer a deleterious effect determined by the caster. Maximum effects: –2 to saves, –4 to hit, or 50% ability score reduction. Multiple curses possible (each different effect). Referee may turn overpowered curses back on the caster.

#### Striking

**Duration:** 1 turn
**Range:** 30'

A single weapon is enchanted: +1d6 damage and treated as magical (can harm monsters only harmed by magical weapons).

### 4th Level Spells

#### Create Water

**Duration:** Permanent
**Range:** Touch

A magical fount springs from ground or wall. Produces ~50 gallons (enough for 12 humans + 12 mounts for 1 day). Above 8th level: additional 12 humans + mounts per level beyond 8th.

#### Cure Serious Wounds

**Duration:** Instant
**Range:** The caster or a creature touched

Heals 2d6+2 hp. Cannot exceed normal maximum.

**Reversed: Cause Serious Wounds** — Inflicts 2d6+2 hp damage to a touched creature. Melee attack roll required in combat.

#### Neutralize Poison

**Duration:** Instant
**Range:** The caster or a creature/object touched

Two uses:
1. **Characters:** Neutralizes poison effects. Can revive a character who died from poisoning if cast within 10 rounds.
2. **Items:** Removes poison from an item.

#### Protection from Evil 10' Radius

**Duration:** 12 turns
**Range:** 10' around the caster

Wards the caster and all allies within 10':
- **Bonuses:** +1 to saving throws.
- **Affected creatures' attacks:** –1 penalty.
- **Enchanted/constructed/summoned creatures:** Cannot make melee attacks against those warded. Broken if any warded party member engages in melee (save/attack bonuses still apply).

#### Speak with Plants

**Duration:** 3 turns
**Range:** 30'

Two uses:
1. **Normal plants:** Communication, questions, and simple favours (e.g. clearing a passageway).
2. **Monstrous plants:** Communication with plant-like or plant-based monsters.

#### Sticks to Snakes

**Duration:** 6 turns
**Range:** 120'

2d8 sticks transform into snakes obeying the caster. Revert to sticks when killed or spell ends. 50% chance of being poisonous.

*Conjured Snakes:* AC 6 [13], HD 1 (4hp), Att 1 × bite (1d4), THAC0 19 [0], MV 90' (30'), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (1), ML 7, AL Neutral, XP 10 (13 if poisonous)

### 5th Level Spells

#### Commune

**Duration:** 3 turns
**Range:** The caster

Seek knowledge from divine powers. 3 yes/no questions per casting (6 once per year). May only be cast once per week (referee may limit further).

#### Create Food

**Duration:** Permanent
**Range:** Appears in caster's presence

Conjures food for 12 humans + 12 mounts for 1 day. Above 8th level: additional 12 humans + mounts per level beyond 8th.

#### Dispel Evil

**Duration:** Concentration (up to 1 turn) or instant
**Range:** 30'

Three uses:
1. **Ward:** Concentrate and remain stationary. Enchanted/undead monsters within range banished or destroyed (save versus spells to avoid; success = flees).
2. **Target single monster:** Instantly banish/destroy one enchanted/undead monster (save versus spells at –2; success = flees).
3. **Dispel curse:** Instantly dispel a cursed item's hold on a being.

#### Insect Plague

**Duration:** Concentration (up to 1 day)
**Range:** 480'

Conjures a 60' diameter swarm of flying insects (above ground only).
- **Movement:** 20'/round, directed by caster while in range.
- **Vision:** Obscured within the swarm.
- **Creatures of 2 HD or less:** Driven away.
- **Concentration:** Caster moving or losing concentration ends the spell.
- **Underground:** Has no effect.

#### Quest

**Duration:** Until completed / Instant (remove quest)
**Range:** 30'

Commands a subject to perform a specific quest. Must not be obviously suicidal. Save versus spells to resist. Failure: must undertake the quest or fall under a curse (nature determined by referee). Spell ends when task is completed.

**Reversed: Remove Quest** — Dispels an active quest. If the original caster is higher level, 5% chance of failure per level difference.

#### Raise Dead

**Duration:** Instant
**Range:** 120'

Two uses:
1. **Restore life:** To a recently deceased human or demihuman.
   - **Time limit:** Dead no longer than 4 days per caster level above 7th (e.g. 10th level = 12 days).
   - **Weakness:** After revival: 1 hp, half movement, no heavy items, no attacks/spells/abilities for 2 full weeks of bed rest. Cannot be shortened by magical healing.
2. **Destroy undead:** Single undead monster destroyed if it fails save versus spells.

**Reversed: Finger of Death** — Single target saves versus death or dies instantly. Chaotic act; lawful casters use only in desperate situations.

## Magic-User Spells

*(PDF p.90–110)*

### 1st Level Spells

#### Charm Person

**Duration:** One or more days (see below)
**Range:** 120'

Single human, demihuman, or human-like monster saves versus spells or is charmed:
- **Friendship:** Regards caster as trusted friend/ally.
- **Commands:** If sharing a language, the caster may give commands.
- **Subject's nature:** Commands contradicting habits/alignment may be ignored.
- **Suicidal commands:** Never obeyed.

**Restrictions:** Undead and human-like monsters of >4+1 HD not affected.

**Duration:** Indefinite, with periodic saves based on INT:
- INT 3–8: New save once per month.
- INT 9–12: New save each week.
- INT 13–18: New save once per day.

#### Detect Magic

**Duration:** 2 turns
**Range:** 60'

Enchanted objects, areas, or creatures glow. Both permanent and temporary enchantments revealed.

#### Floating Disc

**Duration:** 6 turns
**Range:** 6'

Conjures a concave circular disc of magical force (3' diameter, 1" deep) that follows the caster.
- **Load:** Max 5,000 coins (500 pounds).
- **Motion:** Floats at waist height, horizontally within spell range.
- **End:** Anything supported is dropped when spell ends.

#### Hold Portal

**Duration:** 2d6 turns
**Range:** 10'

Magically holds shut a door, gate, window, or portal.
- **Knock spell:** Opens it instantly.
- **Force:** Creatures with ≥3 HD more than the caster can open it in 1 round.

#### Light

**Duration:** 6 turns +1 per level
**Range:** 120'

Three usages:
1. **Conjuring light:** 15' radius. Sufficient for reading, not as bright as daylight. May be cast on an object.
2. **Blinding a creature:** Save versus spells or blinded for duration. Blind creatures cannot attack.
3. **Cancelling darkness:** May cancel a darkness spell.

**Reversed: Darkness** — 15' radius magical blackness, preventing normal sight (not infravision). May blind creatures or dispel light.

#### Magic Missile

**Duration:** 1 turn
**Range:** 150'

A glowing dart of energy shot at a visible target.
- **Hit:** Unerring (no attack roll or save).
- **Damage:** 1d6+1.
- **Higher levels:** Two additional missiles per 5 levels (3 at 6th–10th, 5 at 11th–15th, etc.). Multiple missiles may target one creature.

#### Protection from Evil

**Duration:** 6 turns
**Range:** The caster

Wards from attacks by creatures of another alignment:
- +1 to saves, –1 to affected creatures' attacks.
- Enchanted/constructed/summoned creatures cannot melee the caster (broken if caster engages in melee).

#### Read Languages

**Duration:** 2 turns
**Range:** The caster

Read any language, coded message, map, or written instructions. Does not grant ability to speak unknown languages.

#### Read Magic

**Duration:** 1 turn
**Range:** The caster

Decipher magical inscriptions or runes:
- **Scrolls:** Understand arcane spell scrolls, enabling future activation.
- **Spell books:** Decipher another caster's spell book.
- **Inscriptions:** Understand runes or magical words on objects/surfaces.
- **Reading again:** Once read, that particular writing is always readable without this spell.

#### Shield

**Duration:** 2 turns
**Range:** The caster

Invisible field of force granting improved AC:
- Against missile attacks: AC 2 [17].
- Against other attacks: AC 4 [15].

#### Sleep

**Duration:** 4d4 turns
**Range:** 240'

Magical slumber upon creatures (undead excluded). Either:
1. A single creature with 4+1 HD.
2. A total of 2d8 HD of creatures of 4 HD or lower each.

For creatures of 4 HD or less:
- **Weakest first:** Lowest HD affected first.
- **HD treatment:** <1 HD = 1 HD; fixed hp bonus = flat HD (e.g. 3+2 HD = 3 HD).
- **Excess:** Insufficient rolled HD are wasted.

**Killing:** Helpless, can be killed instantly with a bladed weapon.
**Awakening:** Slapping or wounding wakes the creature.

#### Ventriloquism

**Duration:** 2 turns
**Range:** 60'

Make voice appear to come from any location or source (e.g. statue, animal) within range.

### 2nd Level Spells

#### Continual Light

**Duration:** Permanent
**Range:** 120'

Three usages:
1. **Conjuring light:** 30' radius. Sufficient for reading, not as bright as daylight. May be cast on an object.
2. **Blinding a creature:** Save versus spells or permanently blinded.
3. **Cancelling darkness:** May cancel continual darkness.

**Reversed: Continual Darkness** — 30' radius, prevents normal sight and infravision. Light sources don't illuminate it. May blind creatures or dispel continual light.

#### Detect Evil

**Duration:** 2 turns
**Range:** 60'

Objects enchanted for evil purposes or beings with evil intentions glow. Intent only; no mind reading.

#### Detect Invisible

**Duration:** 6 turns
**Range:** 10' per level

Invisible creatures or items in range revealed to the caster.

#### ESP

**Duration:** 12 turns
**Range:** 60'

Perceive and understand thoughts of living creatures.
- **Focus:** 1 turn in one direction to pick up thoughts.
- **Multiple creatures:** Thoughts mingle; additional turn to sort one creature's thoughts.
- **Meaning:** Understood regardless of language.
- **Obstructions:** Thin lead or ≥2' rock blocks the ability.

#### Invisibility

**Duration:** Permanent (but may be broken)
**Range:** 240'

Caster or another creature/object becomes invisible.
- **Creature:** Gear also invisible. Dropped items become visible. Attacking or casting spells breaks invisibility.
- **Object:** Invisibility is permanent.
- **Light sources:** Made invisible but emitted light is unaffected.

#### Knock

**Duration:** 1 round
**Range:** 60'

Opens stuck, barred, or locked doors, gates, chests, etc. Affects magically held doors (hold portal, wizard lock). Secret doors may be opened (must be known to caster).

#### Levitate

**Duration:** 6 turns +1 per level
**Range:** The caster

Move up and down through the air:
- **Vertical:** Up to 20'/round.
- **Horizontal:** Push against solid objects to move laterally.
- **Weight:** Normal load can be carried.

#### Locate Object

**Duration:** 2 turns
**Range:** 60' +10' per level

Sense direction (not distance) of an object:
1. **General class:** Nearest of that type.
2. **Specific object:** Known to the caster.

Cannot locate creatures.

#### Mirror Image

**Duration:** 6 turns
**Range:** The caster

1d4 illusory duplicates appear. Look and behave exactly as the caster. Any attack on the caster (hit or miss) destroys one image.

#### Phantasmal Force

**Duration:** Concentration
**Range:** 240'

Visual illusion in a 20' cube area. Three types:
1. **Illusionary monster:** AC 9 [10], vanishes if hit.
2. **Illusionary attack:** (avalanche, magic missile, etc.) Save versus spells to be unaffected.
3. **A scene:** Changes appearance or creates something new. Disappears if touched.

- **Concentration:** Required. Movement or loss of concentration ends it.
- **No real damage:** "Dead" characters fall unconscious, "petrified" characters are paralysed, etc. Effects last 1d4 turns.
- **From imagination:** Bonus to saves if caster hasn't personally seen the subject.

#### Web

**Duration:** 48 turns
**Range:** 10'

Mass of strong, sticky threads blocks a 10' cube.
- **Entanglement:** Caught creatures can't move.
- **Flammable:** Destroyed by fire in 2 rounds. Caught creatures take 1d6 fire damage.
- **Breaking free:** Normal STR: 2d4 turns. Magically augmented STR >18: 4 rounds. Giant strength: 2 rounds.

#### Wizard Lock

**Duration:** Permanent
**Range:** 10'

Magically locks a door, gate, or item with a lock.
- **Caster:** Passes freely.
- **Knock spell:** Allows passage.
- **Higher level casters:** Magic-users ≥3 levels higher pass unimpeded.
- **Temporary:** Bypassing does not destroy the lock.

### 3rd Level Spells

#### Clairvoyance

**Duration:** 12 turns
**Range:** 60'

See through the eyes of other living creatures. Focus 1 turn in one direction. May switch targets. Blocked by thin lead or ≥2' rock.

#### Dispel Magic

**Duration:** Instant
**Range:** 120'

Ends spell effects in a 20' cube area. If the effect's caster is higher level, 5% chance of failure per level difference. Magic items are unaffected.

#### Fire Ball

**Duration:** Instant
**Range:** 240'

Flame detonates in a 20' radius sphere. Damage: 1d6 per caster level (save versus spells for half).

#### Fly

**Duration:** 1d6 turns +1 per level
**Range:** The caster or a creature touched

Subject can fly at up to 360' (120'). Free movement in any direction, including hovering.

#### Haste

**Duration:** 3 turns
**Range:** 240'

Up to 24 creatures in a 60' diameter area move and act at double speed:
- Movement rates doubled.
- Double number of attacks per round.
- Number of spells cast per round is NOT doubled.
- Use of magical devices is NOT doubled.

#### Hold Person

**Duration:** 1 turn per level
**Range:** 120'

Paralysis on humans, demihumans, or human-like monsters (save versus spells):
1. **Individual:** Save at –2.
2. **Group:** 1d4 targeted.

Undead and >4+1 HD human-like monsters not affected.

#### Infravision

**Duration:** 1 day
**Range:** The caster or a creature touched

Subject gains 60' infravision.

#### Invisibility 10' Radius

**Duration:** Permanent (but may be broken)
**Range:** 120'

Selected creature and all within 10' become invisible:
- 10' area moves with chosen creature.
- Moving >10' away = visible.
- Entering after casting = not invisible.
- Gear invisible; dropped items visible; invisible light sources still emit light.
- Attacking or casting spells breaks invisibility for that subject only.

#### Lightning Bolt

**Duration:** Instant
**Range:** 180'

60' long, 5' wide electrical bolt. Damage: 1d6 per caster level (save versus spells for half). Bounces off solid barriers, travelling remaining distance toward the caster.

#### Protection from Evil 10' Radius

**Duration:** 12 turns
**Range:** 10' around the caster

Wards caster and allies within 10'. +1 to saves, –1 to affected creatures' attacks. Enchanted/constructed/summoned creatures cannot melee those warded (broken if any warded member engages in melee).

#### Protection from Normal Missiles

**Duration:** 12 turns
**Range:** 30'

Single subject gains complete immunity to small, non-magical missiles. No protection against hurled boulders or enchanted arrows.

#### Water Breathing

**Duration:** 1 day
**Range:** 30'

Subject can breathe water freely. Does not affect air breathing. No additional swimming proficiency.

### 4th Level Spells

#### Charm Monster

**Duration:** One or more days (see below)
**Range:** 120'

Charms 3d6 creatures of 3 HD or less, or 1 creature of >3 HD. Save versus spells to resist.
- Friendship, commands, subject's nature, suicidal commands: As charm person.
- **Restrictions:** Undead not affected.
- **Duration:** Indefinite with periodic saves by INT (same intervals as charm person).

#### Confusion

**Duration:** 12 rounds
**Range:** 120'

3d6 subjects within 60' of one another become confused.
- **2+1 HD or greater:** May save versus spells each round.
- **2 HD or lower:** No saving throw.

| 2d6 | Behaviour |
|:---:|-----------|
| 2–5 | Attack caster's group |
| 6–8 | No action |
| 9–12 | Attack subject's group |

#### Dimension Door

**Duration:** 1 round
**Range:** 10'

Caster or a creature is instantly transferred up to 360'. Destination by known location or offsets (totalling ≤360'). Fails if destination is solid. Unwilling target saves versus spells.

#### Growth of Plants

**Duration:** Permanent
**Range:** 120'

Vigorous growth in an area up to 3,000 sq ft (e.g. 150'×20'). Plants become thick, overgrown, thorny jungle. Only very large creatures can force through.

#### Hallucinatory Terrain

**Duration:** Until touched
**Range:** 240'

Conjures an illusory terrain feature or hides an existing one. Must fit within range. Negated if touched by an intelligent being.

#### Massmorph

**Duration:** Permanent
**Range:** 240'

Human-sized creatures in a 240' diameter area appear as a copse of trees/orchard. Even creatures moving among them are deceived. Subjects leaving the area are no longer veiled. Caster may dismiss at any time.

#### Polymorph Others

**Duration:** Permanent
**Range:** 60'

Living subject changed into another creature type:
- **HD:** New form's HD ≤ 2× subject's HD.
- **Hit points:** Retained.
- **Abilities:** Truly becomes the new form (all special abilities, behaviour, intelligence acquired).
- **Specific individuals:** Cannot duplicate.
- **Reversion:** If killed, returns to original form.
- **Unwilling:** Save versus spells to negate.

#### Polymorph Self

**Duration:** 6 turns +1 per level
**Range:** The caster

Transform into another being:
- **HD:** New form ≤ caster's level.
- **Stats:** Retains own intelligence, hp, saves, attack probabilities.
- **Physical capabilities:** Acquired (strength, physical attacks, movement modes).
- **Non-physical abilities:** NOT acquired (immunities, breath weapons, spell casting).
- **Casting spells:** Not possible while polymorphed.
- **Specific individuals:** Cannot duplicate.
- **Reversion:** If killed, returns to original form.

#### Remove Curse

**Duration:** Instant / Permanent (curse)
**Range:** The caster or a creature touched

Removes one curse. May allow discarding a cursed magic item.

**Reversed: Curse** — Save versus spells. Maximum effects: –2 saves, –4 to hit, 50% ability score reduction. Multiple curses possible (different effects). Referee may turn overpowered curses back.

#### Wall of Fire

**Duration:** Concentration
**Range:** 60'

Immobile, opaque curtain of shimmering violet fire.
- **Size:** Up to 1,200 sq ft, any shape.
- **Location:** Cannot appear where objects are.
- **<4 HD monsters:** Cannot pass.
- **≥4 HD monsters:** 1d6 damage passing through. Double damage to undead or cold-using/accustomed creatures.
- **Concentration:** Persists while caster concentrates and doesn't move.

#### Wall of Ice

**Duration:** 12 turns
**Range:** 120'

Immobile, translucent wall of ice.
- **Size:** Up to 1,200 sq ft, any shape.
- **Location:** Must rest on solid surface. Cannot appear where objects are.
- **<4 HD monsters:** Cannot pass.
- **≥4 HD monsters:** Can break through for 1d6 damage. Double damage to fire-using/accustomed creatures.

#### Wizard Eye

**Duration:** 6 turns
**Range:** 240'

Invisible magical eye for remote viewing.
- **Movement:** Up to 120'/turn within range.
- **Vision:** Normal and infravision.
- **Barriers:** Blocked by solid barriers; can pass through holes ≥1" diameter.

### 5th Level Spells

#### Animate Dead

**Duration:** Permanent
**Range:** 60'

Turns bones/bodies into undead skeletons or zombies. Obey caster's commands. No special abilities from life. Persist until destroyed or dispel magic cast. Number of HD animated = caster's level.
- **Skeletons:** AC 7 [12], HD equal to life.
- **Zombies:** AC 8 [11], HD one greater than life.
- **Classed characters:** Levels don't count as HD (e.g. 5th level fighter → 2 HD zombie: 1 HD as normal human +1).

#### Cloudkill

**Duration:** 6 turns
**Range:** 30'

Poisonous fog, 30' diameter.
- **Movement:** 60'/turn (20'/round), driven by wind or away from caster.
- **Sinking:** Heavier than air; pours into dens/sinkholes.
- **Damage:** 1 hp/round of contact.
- **<5 HD creatures:** Save versus death each round or die.

#### Conjure Elemental

**Duration:** Permanent (until dismissed or slain)
**Range:** 240'

Summons a 16 HD elemental (air, earth, fire, or water). Requires large volume of appropriate element. Concentration required to command. May dismiss at any time. If caster moves >half speed or concentration broken, elemental becomes free-willed and attacks the caster. May be dispelled by dispel magic or dispel evil. Max one elemental per plane per day.

**Air Elemental:** AC –2 [21], HD 16\* (72hp), Att 1 × blow (3d8), THAC0 8 [+11], MV 360' (120') flying, SV D2 W3 P4 B3 S6 (16), ML 10, AL Neutral, XP 2,300. Size 32'×8'. Whirlwind sweeps <2 HD (save vs death). Mundane damage immunity. Extra 1d8 vs flying creatures.

**Earth Elemental:** AC –2 [21], HD 16\* (72hp), Att 1 × blow (3d8), THAC0 8 [+11], MV 60' (20'), SV D2 W3 P4 B3 S6 (16), ML 10, AL Neutral, XP 2,300. Size 16' tall. Cannot cross water wider than own height. Mundane damage immunity. Extra 1d8 vs grounded creatures.

#### Feeblemind

**Duration:** Permanent
**Range:** 240'

Arcane spell caster (magic-user or elf) saves versus spells at –4 or becomes an imbecile, unable to think or cast spells.

#### Hold Monster

**Duration:** 6 turns +1 per level
**Range:** 120'

Paralysis (save versus spells):
1. **Individual:** Save at –2.

Undead not affected.

#### Magic Jar

**Duration:** Special
**Range:** The caster

Life-force transferred to a receptacle (inanimate object within 30').

**From the magic jar:**
- **Possession:** Attempt to possess a creature within 120'. Save versus spells to resist. Failure: caster's life-force enters victim.
- **Returning:** May return to own body at any time (ends spell).
- **Magic jar destroyed:** Caster dies.
- **Own body destroyed:** Life-force stranded in jar.

**While possessing a victim:**
- **Control:** Full control, but cannot make victim cast spells.
- **Magic jar destroyed:** Life-force trapped in possessed body.
- **Possessed victim killed:** Life-force returns to jar.
- **Dispel evil:** Forces life-force back to jar.
- **Own body destroyed:** Life-force stranded in possessed body.

#### Telekinesis

**Duration:** Concentration (up to 6 rounds)
**Range:** 120'

Move objects/creatures by thought.

#### Teleport

**Duration:** Instant
**Range:** 10'

Subject vanishes and reappears at a chosen location. Gear included (up to max load). Unwilling subjects save versus spells. Destination may be any distance but must be known and at ground level.

| Knowledge | Ground Level | Too High | Too Low |
|:---------:|:------------:|:--------:|:-------:|
| Scant | 01–50 | 51–75 | 76–00 |
| Moderate | 01–80 | 81–90 | 91–00 |
| Exact | 01–95 | 96–99 | 00 |

- **Ground level:** Success.
- **Too high:** 1d10×10' above destination. If solid, instant death; otherwise, falling damage.
- **Too low:** Appears in ground, killed instantly.

Knowledge: Scant (visited once/twice, scried, or described), Moderate (visited often, scried weeks), Exact (detailed in-person study).

#### Anti-Magic Shell

**Range:** The caster

Anti-magic barrier around the caster. No spells or spell effects pass through (in or out). Caster may dismiss early.

#### Control Weather

**Duration:** Concentration
**Range:** 240 yards around the caster

Outdoors only. No movement allowed. One weather condition:
- **Calm:** Clears bad weather (side-effects like mud remain).
- **Extreme heat:** Dries snow/mud (including transmute rock to mud). Half movement.
- **Fog:** Visibility 20'. Half movement. May cause getting lost.
- **High winds:** Half movement. No missile fire or flight. +50% sailing speed with wind. Sandstorm possible (visibility 20').
- **Rain:** –2 to missile attacks. Mud in 3 turns, halving movement.
- **Snow:** Visibility 20'. Half movement. Water may freeze. Mud after thaw.
- **Tornado:** Directed at 120'/round. See stats below. Ships: 75% chance of 12 hull damage.

*Tornado:* AC 0 [19], HD 12\* (54hp), Att 1 × blow (2d8), THAC0 10 [+9], MV 360' (120') flying, SV D6 W7 P8 B8 S10 (12), ML 10, AL Neutral, XP 1,900. Whirlwind sweeps <2 HD (save vs death). Mundane damage immunity. Extra 1d8 vs flying creatures.

**Range:** 30'

**Reversed: Remove Geas** — Dispels active geas and penalties. 5% chance of failure per level the caster is below the geas caster.
