## Combat

### Initiative

Each side rolls **1d6**. Highest acts first.

- **Ties**: Roll again or resolve simultaneously.
- **Slow weapons**: Always act last in round.

### Morale and Loyalty

Roll **2d6**.

- **Monsters**: Check morale when first death on their side, or half killed/incapacitated.
- **Retainers**: Check loyalty when confronted with extraordinary danger.
- **Result**: Higher than morale/loyalty score → surrenders or flees.
- **Two successes**: No more morale checks for that monster.
- **Morale 2**: Never fights (unless cornered).
- **Morale 12**: Never checks morale.

### Movement in Combat

| Situation | Movement |
|-----------|----------|
| Outside of melee | Up to encounter movement rate (1/3 base) |
| Fighting withdrawal | Half encounter move, backwards from melee |
| Retreat | Full encounter move, flee melee. Cannot attack; opponent +2 to hit, ignores shield AC |
| Spell casting | No movement |
| Moving and attacking | May combine in a single round |

### Combat Round Sequence

Per combat round (10 seconds):

1. Declare spells and melee movement
2. Initiative: Each side rolls 1d6
3. Winning side acts:
   - a. Monster morale
   - b. Movement
   - c. Missile attacks
   - d. Spell casting
   - e. Melee attacks
4. Other sides act: In initiative order

### Attack Rolls

Roll **1d20**.

- **Ranges**: Melee within 5', missile beyond 5'.
- **Melee modifiers**: STR modifies attack rolls.
- **Missile modifiers**: DEX modifies attack rolls. +1 at short range, –1 at long range.
- **1s and 20s**: 1s always miss, 20s always hit.

### Damage

- **PC attacks**: 1d6 (optional rule: by weapon). STR modifies melee damage.
- **Monster attacks**: As indicated in description.
- **Minimum 1 damage**: A successful attack always deals at least 1 damage.
- **Death**: At 0 hit points or less.

### Spell Casting (in Combat)

- **Sole action**: No move or other action.
- **Disrupting**: If caster is hit or fails a save, spell is lost.
- **Concentration**: Broken by other action or being distracted.

### Situational Modifiers

| Situation | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Attacks from behind | Shield AC bonuses negated |
| Blind characters | Cannot attack |
| Cover (total) | Impossible to hit |
| Cover (partial) | –1 to –4 penalty to hit |
| Invulnerable monsters | Optionally hit by other invulnerable or 5+ HD monsters |
| Paralysed characters | Auto-hit (just roll damage) |
| Spacing | 2–3 characters side-by-side in 10' passage |
| Subduing intelligent foes | Blunt blows, subdual damage noted separately; 0 hp = surrenders |
| Unarmed attacks | Melee, 1d2 damage + STR |
| Unstable surfaces | No spells; –4 missile attacks |
| Water | Attack and damage penalty; most missiles don't work underwater |

## Checks and Saves

### Ability Checks

Roll **1d20**. Lower or equal = success.

- **Difficulty**: –4 (easy) to +4 (very difficult).
- **1s and 20s**: 1s always pass, 20s always fail.

### Saving Throws

Roll **1d20**. Higher or equal = save succeeds.

## Dungeons

### Darkness

- **Torches and lanterns**: 30' radius light.
- **Infravision**: Non-human monsters: 60'.
- **Surprise**: Light sources negate surprise.

### Doors

- **Listening**: Base 1-in-6 chance.
- **Shutting**: Doors may close after PCs pass.
- **Stuck**: Chance depends on STR. Failed attempt alerts monsters.

### Movement (Dungeon)

- **Exploring the unknown**: Base movement rate per turn.
- **Familiar areas**: Referee may allow faster rate.

### Resting (Dungeon)

- **Frequency**: One turn every hour.
- **Penalty** (if no rest): –1 to hit and damage.

### Searching

1-in-6 chance. 10' x 10' area. Takes one turn.

### Traps

- **Triggering**: 2-in-6 chance on triggering action.
- **Types**: Room trap, treasure trap. PCs find room traps by searching.

### Wandering Monsters (Dungeon)

Typically 1-in-6 every 2 turns.

## Wilderness

### Finding Food

- **Foraging**: Alongside travel. 1-in-6 chance of food for 1d6 humans.
- **Hunting**: Sole activity of day. 1-in-6 chance of encountering animals.

### Losing Direction

Roll 1d6:

| Terrain | Chance |
|---------|--------|
| Clear, grasslands | 1-in-6 |
| Barren, hills, mountains, woods | 2-in-6 |
| Desert, jungle, swamp | 3-in-6 |
| Coastal waters | 2-in-6 |
| Sea (with navigator) | 2-in-6 |
| Sea (without navigator) | 6-in-6 |

### Movement (Wilderness)

- **Miles/day**: 1/5 of base movement rate.
- **Broken, desert, forest, hills**: 33% slower.
- **Jungle, mountains, swamp**: 50% slower.
- **Maintained roads**: 50% faster.
- **Forced march**: 50% faster, rest 1 day after.

### Resting (Wilderness)

- **Frequency**: One day out of seven.
- **Penalty**: –1 to hit and damage.

### Wandering Monsters (Wilderness)

Typically rolled once per day:

| Terrain | Chance |
|---------|--------|
| City, clear, grasslands, settled | 1-in-6 |
| Barren, desert, forest, hills | 2-in-6 |
| Jungle, mountains, swamp | 3-in-6 |

## Encounters

### Surprise

Each side rolls **1d6**. Result of 1 or 2 = surprised.

### Encounter Distance

- **Dungeon**: 2d6 × 10 feet.
- **Wilderness/waterborne**: 4d6 × 10 yards (1d4 × 10 yards with surprise).

### Monster Reactions

Roll **2d6** (apply interacting PC's CHA modifier):

| 2d6 | Result |
|----:|--------|
| 2 or less | Hostile, attacks |
| 3–5 | Unfriendly, may attack |
| 6–8 | Neutral, uncertain |
| 9–11 | Indifferent, uninterested |
| 12+ | Friendly, helpful |

### Conclusion

1 turn passes: rest, regroup, bind wounds.

## Downtime

### Buying Equipment

Availability depends on location.

### Hiring Retainers

Roll **2d6**. Locating: taverns or posting notices.

- CHA: Apply hiring PC's modifier.
- Generosity: –2 to +2 based on offer.
- Bad reputation: –1 or –2.

| 2d6 | Result |
|----:|--------|
| 2 or less | Ill will (–1 to further rolls) |
| 3–5 | Offer refused |
| 6–8 | Roll again |
| 9–11 | Offer accepted |
| 12+ | Offer accepted, +1 loyalty |

### Hiring Specialists or Mercenaries

- Locating: Taverns or posting notices.
- Availability depends on residents in area.
- Pay rate depends on type.

### Learning Arcane Spells from a Mentor

- **When**: Upon gaining a level.
- **Number**: Spells in spell book increased to match number memorizable per day.
- **Time**: About one week.

### Magical Research

- **Spells**: Per spell level: 2 weeks + 1,000gp.
- **Items duplicating spells**: Per spell level per charge: 1 week + 500gp.
- **Other items**: Referee decides time and cost.
- **Chance of failure**: 15% or higher.

### Resting

Healing: 1d3 hp per day of complete rest.

## Sequences

### Dungeon Turn Sequence

Per dungeon turn (10 minutes):

1. Wandering monsters
2. Party decides course of actions
3. Description: Referee describes what happens
4. End of turn: Update time records (light, spell durations, rest)

### Wilderness Day Sequence

1. Party decides course of travel
2. Losing direction
3. Weather (when waterborne)
4. Wandering monsters
5. Description: Referee describes regions, sites of interest; players declare actions
6. End of day: Update time records (rations, spell durations, rest)

### Encounter Sequence

1. Surprise
2. Determine encounter distance
3. Initiative (unsurprised sides only)
4. Actions: Unsurprised sides decide response; encounter plays out
5. Conclusion: One turn has passed

## Class Abilities

### Cleric

- **Combat**: Blunt weapons, all armour, shields.
- **Divine magic**: Pray for spells.
- **Turning the undead**: See turning table.

### Dwarf

- **Combat**: Small/normal-sized weapons, all armour, shields.
- **Detect construction tricks**: 2-in-6.
- **Detect room traps**: 2-in-6.
- **Infravision**: 60'.
- **Listening at doors**: 2-in-6.

### Elf

- **Arcane magic**: Memorize from spell book.
- **Combat**: All weapons, all armour, shields.
- **Detect secret doors**: 2-in-6.
- **Immune to ghoul paralysis**.
- **Infravision**: 60'.
- **Listening at doors**: 2-in-6.

### Fighter

- **Combat**: All weapons, all armour, shields.

### Halfling

- **Combat**: Size-appropriate weapons, all armour (tailored), shields.
- **Defensive bonus**: +2 AC vs large creatures.
- **Hiding**: 90% in woods, 2-in-6 in dungeons.
- **Listening at doors**: 2-in-6.
- **Missile weapons**: +1 bonus to attack.

### Magic-User

- **Arcane magic**: Memorize from spell book.
- **Combat**: Dagger, no armour.

### Thief

- **Back-stab**: +4 to attack, 2× damage.
- **Combat**: All weapons, leather armour.
- **Read languages**: 80% chance from 4th level.
- **Scroll use**: 90% chance from 10th level.
- **Thief skills**: See thief skills table.
