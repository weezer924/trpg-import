## About This Book

21 pre-generated 1st level characters: one of each alignment (Lawful, Neutral, Chaotic) × each class (Cleric, Dwarf, Elf, Fighter, Halfling, Magic-User, Thief). Ability scores generated via 3d6-in-order with prime requisite reassignment. Hit points rolled with re-roll 1s and 2s. Equipment assigned by flavor rather than random gold.

**Uses**:
- **Player characters**: Pick by illustration/background, not just stats.
- **Retainers**: Request 1d4 gp/day + half treasure share.
- **Rival adventurers**: Quick NPC party — pick 7 by alignment (one per class).

Each character has a **minor magical trinket** in addition to standard gear.
