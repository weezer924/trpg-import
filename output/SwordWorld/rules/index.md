## Core Mechanics at a Glance

- **Resolution dice**: 2d6. Roll + bonus ≥ target = success.
- **Auto-success / auto-failure**: Double 6s always succeed (often crit). Double 1s always fail.
- **Ability scores**: Dexterity, Agility, Intelligence, Strength, Life Force, Mental Power. Human range 4–24. Bonus = score ÷ 6 (rounded down).
- **Skills, not classes**: Characters accumulate levels in multiple *skills* (Fighter, Thief, Sorcerer, Shaman, Priest, Sage, Ranger, Bard + general skills). Skill levels 0–10.
- **Adventurer Level**: Highest single adventurer-skill level a character holds. Drives many rolls.
- **Mental Power**: Spent to cast magic. Recovered via sleep or magical recovery.
- **Races** (PC): Human, Dwarf, Grassrunner, Elf, Half-Elf.
- **Rune Master skills** (collective term): Sorcerer, Shaman, Priest, Dark Priest, Dragon Priest. Last two are NPC-only.

## Key Setting Notes

- **Forcelia**: World shared across Sword World 1.0 supplements (Alecrast, Lodoss, Crystania).
- **The three worlds**: Spirit world, fae world, material world. Fae races (Dwarf, Grassrunner, Elf) originated in the fae world.
- **Gods of Light vs Gods of Darkness**: Ancient divine war cost all gods their physical bodies; they now act through priests.
- **Kastuul**: Fallen ancient magical kingdom, source of modern sorcery (high ancient magic).
