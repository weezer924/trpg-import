## Faction Overview

> PDF p.21-22

For over three hundred years, the Principality of New Antioch（新安提阿公国） has stood defiantly as the focal point of the Church and the Faithful at the very edge of the shadow cast by the Gate of Hell. It is the **Home of All Our Hopes**, a bulwark against Heretic forces and the first defence against the Devil's might.

The ancient city of Antioch was destroyed by a mysterious demonic weapon in **1545**; the Faithful never gave up their positions in the ruins. The 1559 Sword Congress of Vienna decreed the city's reconstruction and mandated all Faithful nations to deliver a yearly tithe. New Antioch has withstood **eight great sieges**; its famed walls with their **seventy-seven mighty towers** have never been breached, partly thanks to hundreds of metallic battle shrines（anchorites）embedded in the walls.

In recent years, the Duke has dispatched small forces into the tortured landscape of **No Man's Land**, the desolate stretch between New Antioch and the Heretic domains. These warbands gather intelligence, search for artefacts, raid Heretic forces, secure strongpoints, and loot battlefields for centuries of lost coin and weaponry. Granted only a commission from the Duke, they must raise their own funds and muster their own forces.

**战术风格**：均衡型 Faithful faction，依赖 `FIRETEAM` 协同（独有 *Concentrated Attack* 触发 Bloodbath）与稳健科技（Machine Armour、Bolt-Action Rifle、Sniper Rifle、Flamethrower）。Variants 改写整体取向：Papal States 神迹精英、Éire 游击轻装、Alba 近战冲击、Abyssinia 短程齐射 + Flanking。

## Warband Creation

> PDF p.23

- **Starting Funds**：**700 👑** to recruit a New Antioch Warband for a campaign（→ Starting a Warband，Pass 13 `matches/roster-template.md`）。
- **Faction Identity**：所有 New Antioch Warbands 都是 **Faithful**（与 Heretic 对立的阵营标签）。
- **Mandatory Composition**：必须包含 **1 Lieutenant**（Papal States variant 可改为必须包含 1 Trench Cleric）。
- **Single-Match 简化用法**：v0.1 单场对战可按双方约定 👑 总预算（参见 `rules/08-scenarios.md` Scenario Format）。

## Faction Special Rules

> PDF p.23

以下规则适用于 New Antioch Warbands（**含所有 New Antioch Variant Warbands**）：

### New Antioch Fireteams

→ `rules/03-keywords-glossary.md#fireteam`

- 一个 New Antioch Warband 可包含**至多 2 个 Fireteams**（Stosstruppen variant 升至 3 个）。
- 每个 Fireteam 由 Warband 中任意 **2 个模型**组成，自动获 `FIRETEAM` Keyword（**不额外花 👑**）。
- 在 Warband Roster 上标记哪些模型组成 Fireteam（→ Pass 13 `matches/roster-template.md`）。

### Concentrated Attack（New Antioch Fireteams 独有）

> PDF p.23

- **触发**：若 Fireteam 中一名模型击中目标，且该目标已被同 Fireteam 另一名模型在**同一联合 Activation 早些时候**击中过——
- **效果**：可花 **3 BLOOD MARKERS** 将第二次攻击的 **Injury Roll 转为 Bloodbath Roll**（**即使目标未 Down**）。
- → `rules/02-comprehensive-rules.md` Injury Rolls / Bloodbath；`rules/03-keywords-glossary.md#blood-marker`

## New Antioch Battlekit

> PDF p.26

以下 Battlekit 仅 New Antioch 可用（带 **[•]** 标记）。其余 Battlekit 规则见 `rules/05-battlekit.md`。

### Engineer Body Armour

```yaml
battlekit: engineer-body-armour
type: armour
slot: armour
stipulation: Combat Engineer only
cost: 45
faction: new-antioch
keywords: ["-2 INJURY MODIFIER", "NEGATE SHRAPNEL"]
```

> New Antioch Combat Engineers are clad in specially designed reinforced armour.

**Ballistic Box Armour**：Add **-1 INJURY DICE** to Injury Rolls for a model that has Engineer Body Armour that has been hit by an attack with the `SHRAPNEL` Keyword — **even if the attacking weapon has the `IGNORE ARMOUR` Keyword**.

→ `rules/03-keywords-glossary.md#shrapnel` · `rules/03-keywords-glossary.md#ignore-armour` · `rules/03-keywords-glossary.md#negate-keyword`

### Heavy Ballistic Shield [•]

```yaml
battlekit: heavy-ballistic-shield
type: shield
slot: shield
stipulation: Models wearing Machine Armour only, Shield Combo
cost: 15
faction: new-antioch
keywords: ["COVER"]
```

> Made of aramid fibre mesh and orichalcum steel alloy. So heavy that only Heavy Mechanized Infantry can carry them — too cumbersome for fast-paced melee, mainly used for protection during their ponderous advance.

→ `rules/03-keywords-glossary.md#cover`

### Machine Armour [•]

```yaml
battlekit: machine-armour
type: armour
slot: armour
stipulation: ELITE & Mechanized Heavy Infantry only, Limit 1 excluding Mechanized Heavy Infantry
cost: 50
faction: new-antioch
keywords: ["-3 INJURY MODIFIER"]
```

> Combining the rarest of divine metal alloys and the latest technology of New Antioch, machine armour makes its wearer a virtual tank.

**Bulky**：Change the base size of a model that has Machine Armour to **40mm** unless it is already 40mm or larger. In addition, a model with Machine Armour **cannot be equipped with a Trench Shield** and has a **Charge Bonus of D3"** instead of D6".

**Standfast**：When a model that has Machine Armour suffers a **Down** result on the Injury Table, it is treated as a **Minor Hit** result instead.

> ⚠️ **Errata 注解**：`TOUGH` 与 Machine Armour 的 Standfast 叠加按字面顺序——首次 OoA → TOUGH 改 Down → Standfast 再改 Minor Hit；二次 OoA 因 TOUGH 仅触发首次而正常出局。
> → `errata/rules-commentaries.md#q5--tough-与其他防-down-规则的叠加顺序`

> ⚠️ **MCP 注解**：Machine Armour 自动将基座升至 40mm。按 `→ matches/coordinate-system.md` §5.2，40mm 单边接近 1.5"，**不**升至 `[2, 2]`（仍属 `[1, 1]` 上限边缘）。本文件单位 profile 中 `base_dimensions_mm` 写 `[40, 40]` 但 `base_size` 保持 `[1, 1]`。

→ `rules/03-keywords-glossary.md#tough` · `rules/02-comprehensive-rules.md`（Charge / Injury Roll Table）

## Elite Warband Entries — The Officer Corps

> PDF p.27-29

### Lieutenant

> **Faction**: New Antioch · **Type**: Elite · **Cost**: 70 👑 · **Limit**: 1 (mandatory)

```yaml
profile: lieutenant
faction: new-antioch
type: elite
cost: 70
movement: 6           # 6"/Infantry
ranged: +2            # +2 DICE
melee: +2             # +2 DICE
armour: 0
keywords: [NEW ANTIOCH, ELITE, LEADER, TOUGH]
base_size: [1, 1]
base_shape: circle
base_dimensions_mm: [32, 32]
```

**Battlekit slots**：A Lieutenant can have any Battlekit from the New Antioch Armoury Tables.

**Special rules**:

- **Hold Your Fire! ACTION**：The Lieutenant orders their warband to stop shooting, forcing the enemy to move first. Pick 1 enemy model in the Lieutenant's Line of Sight that the opponent is allowed to Activate. The Lieutenant's Activation then ends, and the opponent **must** Activate the model you picked.
  - 距离测量与 LOS → `→ matches/coordinate-system.md` §3 / §6

**Lore**（PDF p.27 节选）:
> Lieutenants are the leaders of small squads or platoons of New Antioch's soldiers, playing a fundamental role on the frontlines. They are steadfast in their duty, preserving the unity and morale of their troops amid relentless conflict. A lieutenant may be a noble family's favoured son or daughter, or have risen through the ranks by impressing their superiors with their exceptional strategic acumen and audacious bravery.

> ⚠️ **Errata 注解**：装备 Machine Armour 的 Lieutenant 在首次 OoA 时的结算顺序见 `→ errata/rules-commentaries.md#q5--tough-与其他防-down-规则的叠加顺序`。

### Sniper Priest

> **Faction**: New Antioch · **Type**: Elite · **Cost**: 50 👑 · **Limit**: 0–2

```yaml
profile: sniper-priest
faction: new-antioch
type: elite
cost: 50
movement: 6
ranged: +2
melee: -1
armour: 0
keywords: [NEW ANTIOCH, ELITE]
base_size: [1, 1]
base_shape: circle
base_dimensions_mm: [25, 25]
```

**Battlekit slots**：A Sniper Priest can have any Battlekit from the New Antioch Armoury Tables.

**Special rules**:

- **Absolute Faith**：Sniper Priests do not use their eyesight to aim at their enemies. The **opposing player cannot spend BLOOD MARKERS** to add -DICE to a Ranged Attack by a Sniper Priest.
- **Aim ACTION**：Take a Risky Success Roll with **+2 DICE**. On Failure, nothing happens and the Sniper Priest's Activation ends. On Success / Critical Success, **+2 DICE** to all Ranged Attack Success Rolls made by the Sniper Priest for the rest of the Activation.
  - → `rules/02-comprehensive-rules.md`（Risky Success Roll / Ranged Attack Modifiers）；`rules/03-keywords-glossary.md#blood-marker`

**Lore**（PDF p.28）:
> These devotees of the Church ritually blind themselves as a devotion to God, relying solely on their faith to strike His enemies. Stories have been told that during the Siege of the Monastery of Saint Lux, a Sniper Priest killed a target three miles away.

### Combat Engineer

> **Faction**: New Antioch · **Type**: Troops · **Cost**: 80 👑 · **Limit**: 0–2

```yaml
profile: combat-engineer
faction: new-antioch
type: troops
cost: 80
movement: 6
ranged: +0
melee: +1
armour: -2            # Engineer Body Armour included
keywords: [NEW ANTIOCH, NEGATE MINED]
base_size: [1, 1]
base_shape: circle
base_dimensions_mm: [25, 25]
mandatory_battlekit:
  - engineer-body-armour
  - shovel
```

**Battlekit slots**：A Combat Engineer **always has** Engineer Body Armour (→ [New Antioch Battlekit](#new-antioch-battlekit)) **and** a Shovel. **This Battlekit cannot be removed or lost throughout the campaign for any reason**. The Injury Modifier for the Armour（**-2**）is already included in the Profile above. They can also have any Battlekit from the New Antioch Armoury Tables, **apart from Armour**.

**Special rules**:

- **Battlefield Demolition**：Ignore the Effect of the `HEAVY` Keyword for **1 Satchel Charge** that a Combat Engineer has. A Combat Engineer still cannot have **more than 1 Weapon (of any type)** with the `HEAVY` Keyword.
- **Set Mine ACTION**：A Combat Engineer can take a Set Mine ACTION if in contact with a terrain piece **≤ 8" × 8"** that doesn't have the `MINED` Keyword. Take a Success Roll with **+2 DICE**. On Success / Critical Success, the terrain piece gains the `MINED` Keyword.
- **Defuse Mine**：When moving into contact with a Marker or terrain piece with the `MINED` Keyword, you can declare the Combat Engineer will try to defuse the mine before it detonates. Take a **Risky Success Roll**. Failure → mine detonates and Activation ends. Success / Critical Success → mine does not detonate and the Marker / terrain piece loses `MINED`. **A model that itself set a mine can defuse it automatically** when moving into contact with that terrain piece (no Risky Success Roll).
- **Fortify ACTION**：Take a Risky Success Roll. Success / Critical Success → the Combat Engineer has the `COVER` Keyword until they move away from their current position.
  - → `rules/03-keywords-glossary.md#mined` · `rules/03-keywords-glossary.md#cover` · `rules/03-keywords-glossary.md#heavy`
  - → `rules/05-battlekit.md` Satchel Charge / Shovel

**Lore**（PDF p.32）:
> Combat Engineers specialise in destroying bunkers, discovering minefields, and building battlefield emplacements. Clad in steel body armour and armed for close combat, they brave the most perilous frontlines. Casualties within their ranks are exceedingly high, yet they consider it an honour to fight and die for New Antioch and the Church.

**Special rules**:

- **Expert Medic**：Add **+2 DICE** to the Risky Success Roll when a Combat Medic carries out a **Treat ACTION** with their Medi-kit. (→ Medi-kit Treat ACTION 见 `rules/05-battlekit.md`)
- **Finish the Fallen**：Due to their knowledge of anatomy, medics inflict debilitating injuries. Add **+1 INJURY DICE** to Injury Rolls for Melee Attacks made by a Combat Medic if **the target is Down** and does **not** have the `BLACK GRAIL` or `DEMONIC` Keywords.
  - → `rules/03-keywords-glossary.md#black-grail` · `rules/03-keywords-glossary.md#demonic`

**Lore**（PDF p.34）:
> New Antioch Combat Medics are highly-trained specialist troops. Many are devout followers of Saint Michael, the Archangel. Combat Medics serve a brutal dual duty — emergency battlefield treatment aimed at returning soldiers to the fight as swiftly as possible, while also standing shoulder to shoulder with them on the front lines. Their misericordia knives are equally useful for on-the-spot surgery and the rapid dispatch of fallen enemies.

### Papal States Intervention Force

> PDF p.35-36

The Papal States operate under the command of the Supreme Pontiff of Rome (rather than the Duke of New Antioch). They are sometimes dispatched to the front lines to perform a specific duty — hunting down dangerous Heretic leaders, or recovering artefacts of great spiritual importance. These warbands are typically relatively small but have access to extremely elite soldiers and specialist equipment, led by utterly devoted warrior-monks and priests handpicked by the Pope.

#### Papal States Special Rules

- **Far from Home**：A Papal States Intervention Force Warband **cannot include Trench Moles**.
- **Lector**：A Papal States Warband **must include 1 Trench Cleric**, but **does not have to include a Lieutenant**. A Trench Cleric in a Papal States Warband has the `LEADER` Keyword and the following additional ability:
  - **Arise and be Healed! ACTION**：Take a Risky Success Roll for the Trench Cleric. Failure → nothing happens, Activation ends. Success / Critical Success → pick the Trench Cleric or 1 friendly model within **3"** of the Trench Cleric. The model can stand back up at **no cost to their movement**, and you can remove up to **D3 BLOOD MARKERS and/or INFECTION MARKERS** from the model.
- **Specialist Force**：You have **500 👑 and 11 ☼** to recruit a Papal States Intervention Force Warband for a campaign. A Papal States Warband gains **4 ☼** each time it calls for Reinforcements. In a campaign, their Threshold Value is reduced by **200 👑**. When recruiting models as a latecomer for a campaign, or for a one-off game, after agreeing upon the size of the game, **reduce 👑 by 200** and **increase ☼ by 11**.
- **Supreme Blessing**：When recruiting a Papal States Warband, you **must give the Supreme Pontiff's Crucifix to one model in the Warband**. The Supreme Pontiff's Crucifix taken when the Warband is created is **free** and does not cost any ☼ (replacement after loss must be paid for normally).
- **Swiss Guard**：The Lieutenant and **up to 4 models** in a Papal States Warband can have the `NEGATE FEAR` Keyword at **no additional cost in 👑**.

#### Papal States Armoury & Battlekit

##### Supreme Pontiff's Crucifix

```yaml
battlekit: supreme-pontiffs-crucifix
type: equipment
slot: equipment
stipulation: Limit 1
cost_glory: 3        # 3 ☼
faction: new-antioch-papal-states
keywords: []
```

> This holy gift has been blessed by the Supreme Pontiff themselves and inspires the wearer to overcome any adversity.

**Inspiring Relic**：The bearer of this relic **does not end their activation after a failed Risky Success Roll**.

> → 与 main warband 招募差异速览：去 Lieutenant（改 Trench Cleric 强制必选）；去 Trench Moles；总预算 500 👑 + 11 ☼（非 700 👑）；多达 5 个模型免费 `NEGATE FEAR`；强制装备一件免费 Supreme Pontiff's Crucifix。

### Éire Rangers

> PDF p.37-38

The Emerald Isle, Éire, an ancient stronghold of the Church and a famed centre of learning, is home to the Éire Rangers. The island has long endured devastating raids by the Heretic navy. After a hundred years of brutal conflict, its warriors have become masters of skirmish warfare and guerrilla resistance.

Elite **Fianna** warrior-hunters are especially adept at guerrilla tactics. They adorn themselves with grisly trophies in the manner of their pagan ancestors. A rare latent **berserker** trait runs in a few warrior bloodlines of Éire; with the chemical communion of the blood of the Meta-Christ, such fighters enter a state of battle-rage where no bullet or blade seems to cause them any harm.

#### Éire Rangers Special Rules

- **Anointed Ammunition**：Models in an Éire Rangers Warband can have **Armour-Piercing Bullets** (→ `rules/05-battlekit.md`) at a cost of **5 👑 each**. The Armour-Piercing Bullets have **Limit: 2**.
- **Berserker**：The **Lieutenant or one of the Fianna**（Shock Troopers）in an Éire Rangers Warband can be upgraded to a **Berserker for +15 👑**. A Berserker **cannot have Armour**（can have Shields）. In addition, a Berserker has the `NEGATE FEAR` Keyword, and `BLOOD MARKERS` are **never placed on them** under any circumstances.
- **Carnyx**：**One Musical Instrument** in an Éire Rangers Warband can have the `FEAR` Keyword at **no additional cost in 👑**.
- **Fianna**：The Fianna are famed warrior-hunters. **Shock Troopers** in an Éire Rangers Warband can have the `INFILTRATOR` and `SKIRMISHER` Keywords for **+10 👑 per model**.
- **Followers of Saint Patrick**：The Patron of an Éire Rangers Warband is always a **Learned Saint**（v0.2+ campaign，→ `rules/06-campaign-rules.md` 占位）。
- **Hit & Run Tactics**：Add **-1 DICE** to the roll for a Melee Attack that targets a model from an Éire Rangers Warband that is **making a Retreat**.
  - → `rules/02-comprehensive-rules.md`（Retreat ACTION / Melee Attack Modifiers）
- **Light Infantry**：以下限制适用于 Éire Rangers Warband：
  - Only **1 Mechanized Heavy Infantry** model；但可达 **4 Combat Engineers**，Satchel Charges 的 Limit 改为 **4**。
  - Cannot include more than **3 models with a Great Sword/Axe**. Mechanized Heavy Infantry models **cannot have a Great Sword/Axe**.
  - **Exception**：Great Swords/Axes 例外——其他**仅 Mechanized Heavy Infantry** 可携带带 `HEAVY` 关键词的 Ranged Weapons 与 Melee Weapons。**Combat Engineers** 可携带 Satchel Charges。
  - **Only Mechanized Heavy Infantry** can have Reinforced Armour or Machine Armour.
- **Loose Formation**：The Lieutenant in an Éire Rangers Warband **replaces their *Hold Your Fire!* ability with the `SKIRMISHER` Keyword** at no change to their cost in 👑.
- **Strong in Faith**：An Éire Rangers Warband can include **0–2 Trench Clerics**（非 default 0–1）。In addition, Trench Clerics in an Éire Rangers Warband **replace** *Onward Christian Soldiers!* with the following two abilities:
  - **Arise and be Healed! ACTION**：（同 Papal States Lector 版本，详见上方 Papal States Special Rules）
  - **Away Serpents! ACTION**：Pick 1 enemy model within **12"** of the Trench Cleric and order them to crawl on their belly like a snake. Take a Risky Success Roll. Add **-1 DICE** to the roll **if the enemy model is mounted on a base that is 40mm or greater**. Failure → nothing happens, Activation ends. Success / Critical Success → the enemy model **is taken Down**.
    - ⚠️ **Errata 注解**：以 *Away, Serpents!* 把敌方打 Down 会在其旁放置 `BLOOD MARKER`（"taken Down" = "Down 结果"同义）。→ `errata/rules-commentaries.md` Rules Q10。

> → 与 main warband 招募差异速览：Lieutenant 失 *Hold Your Fire!* 得 `SKIRMISHER`；Trench Clerics 升至 0–2 且换两个新 ACTION（Arise / Away Serpents）；Mechanized HI 限 1；Combat Engineers 升至 4、Satchel Charges Limit 4；可购 AP Bullets / Berserker / Fianna 升级；Carnyx 给 Musical Instrument 加 `FEAR`。

### Kingdom of Alba Assault Detachment

> PDF p.39-40

Hailing from the Scottish Highlands where the Church is strong, these men and women come to New Antioch in search of glory, forgiveness of their sins, and dreams of acquiring valuable loot — but above all to get their hands on some Devil-worshippers up-close and in personal combat. The Heretic submarine fleet harries the shores of the Kingdom constantly. As is tradition in Alba, these warbands favour **close quarters combat and fierce charges** over defence and long-range attacks; they wield **Lochaber axes** and their famous **claymores** with great skill. To deal with the rugged terrain of their homeland, the Crown of Alba produces a **highly mobile version of machine armour**.

#### Alba Special Rules

- **Bagpipes**：**One Musical Instrument** in a Kingdom of Alba Warband can be upgraded to a set of **Bagpipes** at **no additional cost in 👑**. Friendly models **within 8"** of a model with Bagpipes have the `NEGATE FEAR` Keyword.
- **Brave**：Add **+1 DICE** to the Success Rolls for a **Morale Check** for a Kingdom of Alba Warband.
- **Celtic Machine Armour**：Models in a Kingdom of Alba Warband with Machine Armour have a **Charge Bonus of D6"**（**not reduced to D3"** by Machine Armour的 *Bulky* 规则）。
- **Claymore Smiths**：**Great Swords/Axes cost 7 👑 instead of 12** for models from a Kingdom of Alba Warband.
- **Cold Steel**：**Halve the cost of a Melee Weapon the first time it is purchased** for a Kingdom of Alba Warband. 例：第一把 Sword/Axe 花 **2 👑**（不是 4），第二把起恢复 4 👑。
- **Dum-Dum Ammunition**：Models can have **Dum-Dum Bullets** (→ `rules/05-battlekit.md`) at a cost of **5 👑 each**. Dum-Dum Bullets have **Limit: 3**.
- **Highland Strength**：The **Lieutenant and any Shock Troopers** in a Kingdom of Alba Warband have the `STRONG` Keyword at **no additional cost in 👑**.
- **Lightly-armoured**：**Only the Lieutenant and Mechanized Heavy Infantry models** can have Reinforced Armour or Machine Armour.
- **Melee-focused**：Mechanized Heavy Infantry in a Kingdom of Alba Warband have a **Melee Characteristic of +1 DICE** and a **Ranged Characteristic of +0 DICE**（**与 default 的 Ranged +1 / Melee +0 互换**）。
- **Rampant Charge**：Models in a Kingdom of Alba Warband have the **`IGNORE DEFENDED OBSTACLE`** Keyword. → `rules/03-keywords-glossary.md#ignore-modifier`（`IGNORE [MODIFIER]` 的 faction-specific 实例）
- **Strained Supply**：**Automatic Shotguns, Grenade Launchers, Machine Guns, Sniper Rifles, and Submachine Guns** in a Kingdom of Alba Warband have a **Limit of 1**（main warband 各有更高 Limit）。

### Expeditionary Forces of Abyssinia

> PDF p.43-46

The Expeditionary Forces of Abyssinia represent the forces of the Solomonic Throne of Ethiopia. While the warriors of Africa also defend their homeland against the Legions of Mammon, they still faithfully send reinforcements to New Antioch **once every seven years**.

Most Ethiopian army regulars hail from **Oromo and Amhara**. They typically wear a traditional white **shamma** robe and **kammish** shirt-tunics; many go to battle barefoot. Their training emphasises **short-range marksmanship** to decimate enemy ranks before the elite **Chewa** finish the fight in close combat. They favour a **half-moon formation** to outflank and envelop an enemy. The **Holy Warriors**, soldiers dedicated to warrior-saints and martyrs, treat the wounded — among the few Faithful capable of countering even the power of the Black Grail, if they get to the victim early enough.

#### Abyssinia Special Rules

- **Abyssinian Healers**：Can include **0–2 Combat Medics**（非 default 0–1）; **Misericordia has Limit: 2** instead of Limit: 1.
- **Chieftain Panoply**：Mechanized Heavy Infantry in an Expeditionary Forces of Abyssinia Warband **cannot have Machine Armour**（只能 Reinforced Armour → 85 👑 固定成本）。
- **Faith of Ethiopia**：The sect of the Sniper Priests does not operate in the Solomonic Dynasty. **Cannot include Sniper Priests**.
- **Holy Warriors**：Can include **0–1 Trench Cleric and 0–2 Holy Warriors**. **Use the Trench Cleric Warband Entry for Holy Warriors**. 除 Trench Cleric 默认规则外，Holy Warrior 还有以下异能：
  - **Blessed Psalm ACTION**：A Holy Warrior can take a Blessed Psalm ACTION. Remove **1 BLESSED MARKER** from the Holy Warrior and place it beside a friendly model. **No Success Roll required**.
  - **Arise and be Healed! ACTION**：（同 Papal States 版本 — Risky Success Roll，Success → 3" 内自己或友方模型站起、移除 D3 BLOOD / INFECTION MARKERS）
- **Short-Range Marksmanship**：Add **+1 DICE** to the Success Rolls for Ranged Attacks made at **Short Range** by the **Lieutenant and any Yeomen**, unless the attacking Weapon is a **Grenade** or has the `HEAVY` Keyword.
  - Short Range 定义 → `→ matches/coordinate-system.md` §3.2（≤ 半射程） · `rules/02-comprehensive-rules.md`（Ranged Attack Modifiers）
- **Vanguard Forces**：Cannot include Trench Moles. Instead, **up to 4 Yeomen** can have the **Flanking** ability for **+5 👑 each**.
  - **Flanking**：When deployed for the first time in a game, a model with this ability can be deployed normally in its own deployment zone **or** in contact with **any edge of the battlefield** and **at least 8" away from any enemy models**. Flanking 模型在 `INFILTRATOR` 之后部署、在其他所有模型之后。**若 scenario 规则要求 INFILTRATOR 模型须正常部署**，则 Flanking 模型亦须正常部署。
- **Warrior Nobles**：**Shock Troopers and models with the `ELITE` Keyword** can have the **Chewa** ability for **+5 👑 each**.
  - **Chewa**：Add **+1 DICE** to the Success Roll for a Melee Attack made by a model with the Chewa ability for **each other friendly model within 1" of the target**, up to a **maximum of +2 DICE**.
- **Weapons of Mobile Warfare**：Maximum **3 Ranged Weapons with the `HEAVY` Keyword**, not counting Satchel Charges.
